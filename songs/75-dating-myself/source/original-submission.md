# Dating Myself — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A woman takes herself on a proper date and does not apologise for it once. Reservation under her own name, gold earrings, a candle, a corner booth, dessert. Then peonies on a Wednesday, a matinee alone with both armrests, and a polite no to a stranger in the lobby. The joke is that she means it: she is seeing someone.

**Mood:**
Silky, funny, unhurried. Warm rather than defiant. The confidence of someone who is not performing for anyone, filmed like a soft-lit romance where the love interest is the person in the mirror.

**Message:**
"I stopped waiting for a plus one to make an evening count."

**Genre:**
Contemporary R&B with neo-soul warmth. Think: SZA x Cleo Sol x Jorja Smith — electric piano, fretless bass, finger snaps, and a harmony stack that does the emotional work the belt would do in a pop record.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 94 BPM, 4/4
- Key: C major (open, warm, unbothered)
- No modulation. The lift in the final chorus is harmony width and a vibraphone doubling the hook, not a key change — the song never gets loud, it gets fuller.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, low and amused in the verses, sung in the pocket in the choruses with a smile in the tone.
- Backing vocals: thick female harmony stacks in thirds and sixths, a wordless counter-line under the hook, a filtered vocal chop answering the second chorus, snapped and chanted layers in the post-chorus.

**Instrumentation:**
- Silky electric piano (the spine, first bar to last)
- Round fretless bass, switching to upright in the bridge
- Finger snaps and a soft rimshot backbeat
- Muted electric guitar on the offbeat
- Warm analogue pads
- Low strings from the second pre-chorus
- Vibraphone doubling the hook in the final chorus
- Light hand percussion in the post-chorus, and restaurant room tone, cutlery and low conversation as ambience

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: the booking, the earrings, the long walk over
- Verse 1: the meal, the phone face down, the room watched like a film
- Pre-chorus: what a Friday alone used to mean and what it means now
- Chorus: the hook, silky and wide, delivered relaxed
- Verse 2: flowers with no occasion, a matinee, a polite no
- Bridge: the honest caveat — this is not a speech about not needing anyone
- Final chorus and outro: fullest harmonies, two desserts, then home to a lamp she left on for herself

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – electric piano, brushed rim, no drums]

```
Made the reservation under my own name,
Table for one, and the host didn't blink.
Gold earrings out for an ordinary Tuesday,
Walked the long way over just to think.
Nobody's evening to carry but my own,
Nobody waiting on me at the door.
```

- Scene: A quiet upscale bistro on a weeknight, and the walk to it.
- Shots: Her hand on a phone making a booking (screen composited). Close-up of gold earrings going in at a bathroom mirror. Her coat going on. Her walking a long city block in no hurry, hands in pockets, taking a corner she didn't need to take.
- Cut to: A host stand. She says two words. The host nods and picks up one menu, not two.
- Emotion: Calm anticipation. Not bravado — she is genuinely looking forward to it.
- Lighting: Blue evening street, warm pooled light through the restaurant window.

### [verse 1 – snaps, fretless bass, the pocket]

```
Ordered the one thing I'd never get for two,
Olives on the side and a small glass of red.
Waiter came around and asked me if I was waiting,
I said, no, I'm all here, go ahead.
```

- Scene: The corner booth.
- Shots: A menu closing decisively. Olives arriving on a small plate. A small glass of red poured, the light through it. The waiter's hand gesturing at the empty seat opposite, her shaking her head once with a smile.
- Emotion: Ease. The empty chair is not a wound.
- Lighting: Single candle plus warm practicals, deep shadow in the booth.

```
Phone in my bag with the screen facing down,
No proof required and no one to impress.
Watched the whole room like the opening of a movie,
And I liked the girl who came in that dress.
```

- Scene: The room, seen the way she sees it.
- Shots: The phone sliding face-down into a bag and staying there. A slow pan of the restaurant in shallow focus — a couple arguing softly, a birthday candle at another table, a waiter carrying four plates. Her reflection in the dark window over the booth.
- Her reaction: She watches everything with open, unhurried attention, then catches her own reflection and holds it for a beat.
- Lighting: Warm interior, the window reflection cooler.

### [pre-chorus]

```
Used to think a Friday on my own was a loss,
Something you survive, something you hide.
Now I light the candle and I take my time,
And I don't leave until I decide.
```

- Scene: A memory, then the present.
- Shots: Two seconds, cooler grade — her younger, eating standing up over a sink with the TV on, coat still on. Hard cut back to the booth: her hand cupping the candle flame, then settling back.
- Emotion: The distance between the two women is the whole song.
- Lighting: Memory flat and grey-blue; present warm and low.

### [chorus – wide, silky, in the pocket]

```
I'm dating myself, and honestly it's going great,
She shows up on time and she never makes me wait.
Reservation for one, and I said it out loud,
Corner booth, candle lit, and I'm proud.
I'm dating myself, no notes and no complaints,
Nobody's bad mood that I have to translate.
Ordered the dessert and I finished the plate,
I'm dating myself, and honestly it's going great.
```

- Scene: The date in full, shot exactly like a romance.
- Shots: Two-shot framing of one person — the classic across-the-table setup with the reverse angle on an empty, beautifully lit chair. Her laughing at something on her own. Dessert arriving with one spoon. The spoon going down on a clean plate.
- Emotion: Delight. The camera treats her the way a love interest is normally treated.
- Lighting: Golden, soft, romantic-comedy warm.

### [verse 2 – pulled back to keys and snaps]

```
Peonies on a Wednesday, no occasion,
Paid the full price and I carried them home.
Put them where the sofa sees them in the morning,
Not the hall where they'd be blooming for someone unknown.
```

- Scene: A florist, a street, a living room.
- Shots: Her pointing at peonies through glass. Paper being wrapped and taped. Her carrying them home across a crossing, the blooms above the traffic. Her setting them on a low table opposite the sofa, then moving them two inches so they line up with where she actually sits.
- Emotion: Small, deliberate self-regard. The two-inch adjustment is the whole point.
- Lighting: Overcast daylight outside, warm lamp inside.

```
Matinee at four, back row, lights low,
Both of the armrests mine and I cried at the end.
Somebody kind asked me out in the lobby,
I said, I'm seeing someone, and I'm loyal, my friend.
```

- Scene: An almost-empty cinema and its lobby.
- Shots: A wide of the auditorium with one person in the back row. Both her elbows spreading onto both armrests. Her face lit by the screen, tears she does not wipe. In the lobby, a faceless man saying something warm; her smiling, saying a short sentence, and walking on with genuine kindness.
- Emotion: Not a brush-off. She is happy for him to have asked and completely unavailable.
- Lighting: Screen light in the auditorium; flat bright lobby light.

### [pre-chorus 2]

```
Used to call it lonely when the place went quiet,
Used to fill it up with anyone I could find.
Now the quiet is the part I look forward to,
And I don't rush a single thing that's mine.
```

- Scene: Her flat, evening, no one else there.
- Shots: A record going on. A bath running. Her sitting on the floor with her back against the sofa, doing nothing at all. The peonies in frame behind her.
- Emotion: The quiet has stopped being an accusation.
- Lighting: One lamp and the last of the daylight.

### [chorus]

(repeat)

- Scene: Repeat of the date world, fuller and more confident.
- Shots: The reverse on the empty chair again, this time with the candle burned lower. Her tipping generously. Her walking out past a full restaurant with her coat over her arm, everyone else in pairs.
- Emotion: Ownership.
- Lighting: Warmer, richer, more saturated.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: The evening between the date and home, no lyrics.
- Shots: Rain starting on the pavement outside the restaurant. Her under an awning waiting it out, entirely content. The electric piano solo cut to her walking, a bus going past, her reflection sliding across the glass. A close-up of a receipt being folded and put in a pocket. One held wide of an empty city crossing.
- Lighting: Wet street, neon reflections, one warm shop window.

### [bridge – keys, upright bass, voice, almost spoken]

```
This is not a speech about not needing nobody,
I would love a hand to hold when the winter comes.
But I'm not going hungry at a table for two
Just to say that I wasn't the only one.
So whoever you are, you can take your sweet time,
I'm not standing at the window keeping score.
I'm not waiting to start, I already started,
And you'd be joining something good, not fixing something poor.
```

- Scene: Home, honest, unlit for effect.
- Shots: Her sitting on the edge of the bath with her shoes off, still in the dress. A wide of the flat with one lamp and the door open. Her looking at the empty half of the bed without any drama in her face, then pulling the duvet across it herself.
- Emotion: The one place the song admits it would like company — and refuses to make that a shortage.
- Lighting: One lamp, warm, deep shadow, nothing romantic.

### [final chorus – fullest harmonies, two desserts]

```
I'm dating myself, and honestly it's going great,
She shows up on time and she's never running late.
Reservation for one, and I said it out loud,
Corner booth, candle lit, and I'm proud.
I'm dating myself and I'm easy to date,
Nobody's silence that I have to translate.
Ordered two desserts and I finished them straight,
I'm dating myself, and honestly it's going great.
```

- Scene: The booth again, another night, no explanation.
- Shots: Two desserts arriving at one place setting. The waiter grinning. Her raising her glass to the empty chair, unembarrassed. A wide of the whole restaurant with her booth the brightest thing in it.
- Emotion: Full, funny, generous.
- Lighting: The warmest and widest lighting in the video.

### [post-chorus – snaps, hand percussion, chant]

```
Going great, going great,
Table for one and I'm never running late.
Going great, going great,
Flowers on a Wednesday and nothing to celebrate.
Going great, going great,
Nobody to answer to and nobody to wait.
Going great, going great,
I'm dating myself and it's going great.
```

- Scene: A rhythmic montage of the small rituals.
- Shots: Cut on the snaps — earrings in, candle lit, olives, paper wrapped around flowers, both armrests, a record dropping, a bath tap, a lamp. Fast, warm, tactile, no faces needed except two.
- Emotion: Pleasure in the routine.
- Lighting: Consistent warm gold across every cut.

### [outro – solo electric piano, one voice]

```
Walked home slow with the peonies wrapped in paper,
Keys in the door and the lamp already on.
Poured one glass and I put on a record,
And I stayed up late with the best company I've known.
```

- Scene: Home, late, alone, good.
- Shots: Her walking a night street with the peonies in the crook of her arm. Keys in a door. A lamp already on because she left it on for herself that morning. One glass poured. A needle dropping. Final shot: her on the sofa with her feet up and the flowers in frame, eyes closing, a small private smile.
- Emotion: Peace, and a joke she is enjoying alone.
- Lighting: Warm lamp, deep night outside the window.
