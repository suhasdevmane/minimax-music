# We Look Good Together — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
Two people who are extremely aware that they are a good-looking couple and enjoy it out loud. Matching fits, a corner window used as a mirror every Friday, a crosswalk crossed like a runway, a room going quiet when they walk in. It would be unbearable if it were not funny, and it is funny because both of them know exactly what they are doing.

**Mood:**
Cocky, warm, deadpan. Swagger with a real relationship under it. The joke is always shared, never at anyone's expense.

**Message:**
"Yes, we know. We're enjoying it. And the version nobody photographs is better."

**Genre:**
Modern funk-pop. Think: Bruno Mars x Silk Sonic x Dua Lipa's disco records — slap bass out front, wah guitar, a three-piece brass section, and a band that ducks under the punchlines.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 116 BPM, 4/4
- Key: E minor, so the swagger has some grit rather than sounding cheerful
- The lift is a brass stack per chorus and an octave-up brass section in the final

**Vocal setup:**
- Singer A, female lead: verse one, the calling half of the chorus
- Singer B, male: verse two, the answering half of the chorus
- Both in unison on every hook line, in harmony through the final chorus
- Group vocals from the second pre-chorus; a group chant on the post-chorus

**Instrumentation:**
- Slap bass (the lead instrument, first bar to last, with a solo in the break)
- Clipped wah electric guitar; clavinet trading with it in the break
- Live kick and snare, tight hats, tambourine and cowbell on the choruses; a tight three-piece brass section with a stack added per chorus
- Clean electric guitar and soft Rhodes carrying the bridge with no brass at all
- Ambience: a door, keys, a lock, late-afternoon street noise, one laugh

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: keys, mirror, green light, both voices trading half lines
- Verse 1: getting dressed, the argument about the jacket, the Friday window
- Pre-chorus: counting into the corner
- Chorus: the strut, the whole hook
- Verse 2: the party, the doorman, somebody's ex, and what nobody can copy
- Instrumental: the walk as a full set piece
- Bridge: Tuesday, no filter, flour in her hair
- Final chorus: rain, sun, an airport line, widest arrangement
- Post-chorus: the chant
- Outro: the lock, the shoes, the photo that does not get posted

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – slap bass and hi-hat, both voices]

```
Mirror by the door and the keys in my hand,
Green light on the corner, whole block understands.
Turn the collar up, do the thing with the shades,
Baby, look at what we made.
```

- Scene: The last ten seconds inside the apartment before they leave.
- Shot: A full-length mirror by the front door with both of them in it, checking each other rather than themselves; keys lifted off a hook.
- Cut to: A crosswalk signal going green at the end of the block, seen from the doorway; a collar being turned up; sunglasses going on in one move.
- Emotion: Anticipation and total self-possession. Neither of them is nervous about anything.
- Lighting: Warm interior lamp on the mirror, hard late-afternoon sun in the doorway behind them.

### [verse 1 – her, getting dressed]

```
I picked the jacket and you picked the shoes,
We argued for an hour and the argument was the truth.
You came out the bedroom doing that stupid little spin,
And I said, "absolutely not," then I let you win.
There's a window on the corner where the light hits right,
And we take the same photo there every Friday night.
Two coffees, one straw, and a plan we didn't make,
And the afternoon's a scene we didn't have to stage.
```

- Scene: The getting-ready hour, played as comedy with a straight face.
- Shot: A bed covered in rejected clothes; her holding two jackets up against him; him doing an entirely unnecessary spin in a doorway.
- Cut to: The corner window — a shop front used as a mirror — and a phone held up for the same framing they use every week; then two coffees with one straw between them on a wall.
- Her reaction / Emotion: Exasperated, delighted, entirely in charge and pretending not to be.
- Lighting: Hard low sun through apartment windows; the corner window shot is direct golden-hour bounce off glass.

### [pre-chorus – counting in]

```
Count it in, one, two, hit the corner clean,
Bass in the chest and the whole street lean.
Are you ready, are you ready, don't break stride,
Chin up, shoulders back, and glide.
```

- Scene: The four beats before the strut starts.
- Shot: Two pairs of feet stopping at a curb in step, seen from ground level.
- Cut to: A crosswalk light counting down; both of them looking at each other sideways; the first step off the curb landing on the downbeat.
- Emotion: A shared cue. They have done this before.
- Lighting: Long shadows straight down the crosswalk; the street painted in blocks of color behind them.

### [chorus 1 – the strut]

```
Baby, we look good together, and we know it,
Every window in the city is a stage and we show it.
Green light, gold hour, and the whole block slows,
We didn't come to the party, we're the reason it goes.
Baby, we look good together, say it with your chest,
Two of us on one sidewalk and the sidewalk is a set.
You can look, you can love it, you can put it on your screen,
Baby, we look good together, and we know it.
```

- Scene: The walk. One block, shot like a runway.
- Shot: Tracking backwards ahead of both of them at waist height, the block passing behind, everyone else slightly slower than them.
- Cut to: Their reflection running along a row of shop windows; a bus passing and both of them appearing in its glass for one frame; a stranger visibly turning around.
- Emotion: Joy, not superiority. They are laughing between lines.
- Lighting: Full gold hour down the length of the street; the city color-blocked in reds, greens and yellows behind them.

### [verse 2 – him, the party]

```
Doorman did a double take and held the door too long,
Somebody's ex went quiet in the middle of a song.
You walked in like the floor was on your payroll,
And I held both the drinks and let the whole room go slow.
They can copy the fit, they can borrow the pose,
Take it frame for frame off a picture that we posted.
But they can't get the part where you laugh at my joke,
The one that isn't funny and you laugh at it the most.
```

- Scene: The arrival, told entirely from his point of view and played deadpan.
- Shot: A doorman's face doing a small double take; a door held open two beats longer than necessary; a room turning in slow motion.
- Cut to: Him standing still holding two drinks while she crosses the floor; a group at the bar looking at a phone, then looking up, then trying the same pose badly.
- Emotion: Pride, amusement, and one line of real tenderness at the end that he does not sell.
- Lighting: Interior party lighting, saturated color gels, one hard key on her as she crosses.

### [pre-chorus 2 – the second count]

```
Count it in, one, two, hit the corner clean,
Bass in the chest and the whole street lean.
Are you ready, are you ready, don't break stride,
Chin up, shoulders back, and glide.
```

- Scene: The same count, now inside the party, on a floor instead of a curb.
- Shot: Repeat the ground-level feet shot in identical framing, this time on a polished floor with other shoes around them.
- Cut to: Both of them counting each other in with a look and nothing else.
- Emotion: The same joke, escalated.
- Lighting: Color gels, a mirror ball throwing spots across both faces.

### [chorus 2 – bigger]

```
Baby, we look good together, and we know it,
Every window in the city is a stage and we show it.
Green light, gold hour, and the whole block slows,
We didn't come to the party, we're the reason it goes.
Baby, we look good together, say it with your chest,
Two of us on one sidewalk and the sidewalk is a set.
You can look, you can love it, you can put it on your screen,
Baby, we look good together, and we know it.
```

- Scene: The party version of the strut, then back out onto the street.
- Shot: The two of them dancing in a small clear circle that the room makes without being asked; a phone screen filming them from the crowd.
- Cut to: A hard match cut from the party floor to the same two steps back on the crosswalk at night, neon replacing gold.
- Emotion: Peak swagger, and the first hint that it is a performance they both enjoy putting on.
- Lighting: Party gels to wet neon street. The two grades are deliberately opposite and cut hard.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: No lyrics. The walk as a full set piece.
- Shots: The slap-bass break played over a locked-off wide of the empty crosswalk; both of them crossing it in three different outfits in three cuts on the same framing; the corner window from directly opposite, their reflections arriving and leaving; a brass shout chorus cut against four fast reflections in four different surfaces — glass, a car door, a puddle, a phone screen.
- Emotion: Pure style, no story.
- Lighting: Golden hour, night neon and one overcast frame, in that order, so the outfit changes read as different days.

### [bridge – no brass, Rhodes and clean guitar]

```
Take the gold off, hang the good coat on the chair,
Tuesday, no filter, and there's flour in your hair.
Nobody's watching and there's nothing to prove,
And I still catch myself just looking at you.
That's when we look the best, with the lights off the floor,
Don't tell the internet, but I like this version more.
```

- Scene: The turn. A Tuesday kitchen, no styling, no audience.
- Shot: Jewelry going into a dish; the good coat going over the back of a chair and staying there; a kitchen counter with flour on it.
- Cut to: Her with flour in her hair not knowing it; him seeing it and not saying anything; both of them in old clothes with the overhead light on, which is the worst light in the video and the best they look.
- Emotion: The whole song's argument, said quietly.
- Lighting: One flat overhead kitchen light, deliberately unflattering, warm. No gels, no gold, no neon.

### [final chorus – widest]

```
Baby, we look good together, and we know it,
Every window in the city is a stage and we show it.
Green light, gold hour, and the whole block slows,
We didn't come to the party, we're the reason it goes.
Baby, we look good together, in the rain, in the sun,
In the line at the airport at a quarter past one.
You can look, you can love it, you can put it on your screen,
Baby, we look good together, and we know it.
```

- Scene: The same two people in four unglamorous places, still enjoying it.
- Shot: The crosswalk in the rain with one umbrella; the same crosswalk in flat midday sun; an airport line at a quarter past one with both of them exhausted and still posing.
- Cut to: A last full strut down the block with the brass at its widest, everyone else out of focus.
- Emotion: The joke has become the relationship. Both are fine with that.
- Lighting: Rain-wet reflections, hard flat sun, airport fluorescents, then gold again for the last strut.

### [post-chorus – the chant]

```
Look at us, look at us,
Every shop window agrees with us.
Look at us, look at us,
Nobody had to tell us, we just knew.
```

- Scene: The chant, over reflections only.
- Shot: A rapid sequence of their reflection in shop glass, a car window, a puddle, a shop mirror, a phone screen, cut on the beat.
- Cut to: The two of them walking away from camera down the block, still in step.
- Emotion: A shared in-joke played to the whole street.
- Lighting: Last of the gold, long shadows, everything reflective.

### [outro – bass alone, a lock, a laugh]

```
Turn the lock, kick the shoes down the hall,
Take the picture, don't post it, this one isn't theirs.
Baby, we look good together,
And we know it.
```

- Scene: Home. The door closing on the whole performance.
- Shot: A lock turning from inside; two pairs of good shoes kicked down a hallway; the good coat already on the chair from the bridge.
- Cut to: Final shot — a phone taking one photo of the two of them on the sofa in old clothes, the screen showing it, and a thumb not pressing anything. Held to the fade.
- Emotion: Quiet ownership. The last image is the only private one in the film.
- Lighting: Warm hallway lamp, one soft source, everything else dark.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the first step off the curb landing on the downbeat of *"baby, we look good together"* — a runway walk made from a crosswalk.
- **The transition:** three outfit changes cut on the same locked-off crosswalk framing during the instrumental, which is the outfit-transition format built directly into the video.
- **The caption line:** "We didn't come to the party, we're the reason it goes."
- **The turn quote:** "Don't tell the internet, but I like this version more."
- **The last shot:** a photo taken and deliberately not posted — the only frame in the video nobody else is allowed to see.
