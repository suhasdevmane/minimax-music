# Ring on a Rainy Day — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A wedding that the weather ruined and that the weather made. He proposed in the rain in a seafront parking lot because he could not wait one more day; a year later the sky does exactly the same thing to the wedding, and the couple are the only two people at the venue who are not upset about it. A duet: her verse is the proposal, his verse is the morning of the wedding, and both voices meet on the hook.

**Mood:**
Warm, wet, grateful, quietly funny. Big-hearted wedding ballad with a lump in the throat rather than a lump in the strings. Joy under a leaking roof.

**Message:**
"Everything good I have arrived in bad weather, so let it rain."

**Genre:**
Modern wedding ballad / cinematic acoustic pop duet. Think: Lewis Capaldi x Kacey Musgraves, with the string writing of a Sam Smith ballad and the plain-spoken male half of a Dan + Shay record.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 82 BPM, 4/4
- Key: E♭ major, wide and warm, comfortable for both voices
- Final chorus lifts a whole step to F for the modulation, tambourine and full kit

**Vocal setup:**
- Singer A (female): lead on the intro, verse 1, the pre-choruses and the whole of the hook.
- Singer B (male): lead on verse 2 and the second half of the bridge, harmony a third below from chorus 1 onward.
- Backing: layered choir-style stacks on the final chorus, a wide singalong crowd on the post-chorus.

**Instrumentation:**
- Grand piano (the spine, first bar to last)
- Steel-string acoustic guitar (fingerpicked verses, open strums in choruses)
- Live string section: violins, viola, cello
- Solo violin (the instrumental break)
- Church-organ style pad under the choruses
- Brushed kit in the verses, soft-mallet backbeat in the choruses, floor tom into the modulation
- Tambourine and handclaps (pre-chorus two, final chorus)
- Low electric bass, never forward
- Ambience: rain on a canvas roof, far-off thunder, the small creaks of a marquee

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: two people looking at a forecast and choosing anyway
- Verse 1: the proposal, seen from her side, wet and shaking and perfect
- Pre-chorus: the venue in crisis, the couple entirely calm
- Chorus: the big warm statement, both voices, arms open
- Verse 2: his morning, the collar, the thunder, saying it twice
- Bridge: her mother's perfect sunny wedding and the house she had to leave; his answer
- Final chorus: modulated, barefoot, a river under the tent floor
- Outro: the last song, nobody leaving, the forecast kept its word

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – piano and rain only, half-smiling]

```
The forecast said a hundred percent by four,
So we bought the cheap umbrellas at the corner store.
You said that we could wait for a kinder sky,
I said the sky has had its whole life to try.
```

- Scene: A gas-station convenience store on a coast road, the day before.
- Shot: Close on a phone weather screen, a blue bar at one hundred; then her face, unbothered.
- Cut to: Two clear plastic umbrellas being pulled off a wire rack, a bored cashier, the door chime.
- Her reaction / Emotion: Amused, decided. She is not asking his opinion so much as telling him the answer.
- Lighting: Flat overcast daylight through a wet shopfront window, fluorescent inside.

### [verse 1]

```
You went down on the wet in a parking lot by the water,
Gray coming sideways off the sea behind your back.
The box would not open and your hands were shaking,
And I was crying before you got the question out.
Your knee left a print in the gravel and the rain,
My mascara took a shortcut down my chin.
And you laughed like the weather was a friend of yours.
```

- Scene: A gravel seafront parking lot, a low grey sea, one year earlier.
- Shot: Wide, both of them small in frame, the weather enormous behind them; he goes down on one knee into standing water, then macro on the ring box hinge with his thumbnail slipping and rain beading on the velvet.
- Cut to: An insert straight down on the wet gravel with a knee-shaped print filling with water; then her face in close-up, mascara tracking to her jaw, grinning through it; then his head tipped back, laughing at the sky.
- Her reaction / Emotion: Hands over her mouth, already gone. Ruined and radiant. This is the frame the whole song is written to.
- Lighting: Silver, flat storm daylight with no sun anywhere; rain in the foreground catching what light there is, faces slightly underlit by the wet ground.

### [pre-chorus]

```
They moved the chairs inside, they moved the flowers twice,
The tent man never showed and the aisle turned to clay.
Everyone kept apologizing for the sky,
And the two of us were the only ones okay.
```

- Scene: Wedding day, ninety minutes before. Controlled chaos at a garden venue.
- Shots: Two men carrying a stack of white folding chairs at a run; a planner with a clipboard held over her hair; jars of wildflowers being relocated for the second time; the aisle runner sinking into mud.
- Cut to: The couple, separately, both perfectly still in the middle of it — she at a window upstairs, he under an awning.
- Her reaction / Emotion: Serene. Everyone else is performing a crisis around two people who are not in one.
- Lighting: Grey-green garden light, one warm interior window behind her.

### [chorus – both voices, wide]

```
You gave me a ring on a rainy day,
So let it rain, let it come, let it stay.
Let it soak through the silk and ruin the shoes,
There is nothing in this garden I would trade for you.
The band's under the awning and the cake's in the hall,
And your hand is on my back and I don't care at all.
Every good thing I have came the same way,
You gave me a ring on a rainy day.
```

- Scene: The ceremony outdoors under a canopy of raised umbrellas, then the reception improvising itself around the weather.
- Shots: A wide crane over the seated guests, every one of them holding an umbrella, a field of black and clear domes with the couple bare-headed at the centre; then a four-piece band crammed under a stretched awning with cables taped to a beam.
- Cut to: Her hem dragging through wet grass and satin shoes carried by a bridesmaid rather than worn; a cake carried indoors by two people walking very carefully; close on his hand flat on her back as they turn and her eyes closing for one beat.
- Emotion: Full-throated joy, then a contentment that reads as triumph.
- Lighting: Overcast but lifted, a warm key from a rented lamp under the canopy; string bulbs and lamp light fighting a grey afternoon and winning.

### [verse 2 – male lead]

```
I ironed a shirt at seven and it lost by ten,
Stood at the end of the aisle with my collar going soft.
My father held his jacket like a roof over my mother,
And your uncle raised a golf umbrella like a flag.
Then the doors came open and none of it mattered,
Thunder took the second half of what I meant to say,
So I said it again louder, and I'd say it in worse.
```

- Scene: The groom's morning, the top of the aisle, and the vows.
- Shots: An iron hissing on a board in a small rented room; the same shirt three hours later, collar wilting, shoulders dark with rain; then his eyeline down the aisle as the far gate opens and the sound of the field drops away.
- Cut to: An older man holding his suit jacket spread over his wife's hair; a huge striped golf umbrella going up in the third row like a banner; then a crack of thunder, the guests flinching in unison, the officiant pausing, and his mouth moving, then moving again, bigger.
- Emotion: Nerves turning into affection for everyone in the field, then the one moment of real intensity in the video. He is shouting a promise over weather.
- Lighting: Morning window light indoors, flat wet daylight outdoors, a hard sky flash on the thunder and back to soft grey.

### [pre-chorus 2]

```
The photographer kept waiting for a break in it,
Then the light went strange and gold behind the gray.
Everyone kept apologizing for the sky,
And we were the only two who wanted it this way.
```

- Scene: Portraits, attempted.
- Shots: A photographer crouched under a rain cover checking the sky every four seconds; the couple waiting, holding hands, unhurried.
- Cut to: The sky doing the thing it does after a storm — a low gold seam under the cloud, everything suddenly lit like a painting.
- Emotion: Vindication, quiet and huge.
- Lighting: The best light in the entire video, and it lasts nine seconds.

### [chorus 2 – bigger, both voices in unison]

```
You gave me a ring on a rainy day,
So let it rain, let it come, let it stay.
Let it soak through the silk and ruin the shoes,
There is nothing in this garden I would trade for you.
The band's under the awning and the cake's in the hall,
And your hand is on my back and I don't care at all.
Every good thing I have came the same way,
You gave me a ring on a rainy day.
```

- Scene: The reception going properly, everyone soaked and past caring.
- Shots: Guests dancing in coats; a grandmother in a clear rain bonnet clapping on the beat; the flower girl stamping in a puddle on purpose.
- Cut to: A wide of the marquee from outside, glowing, rain streaking the lens.
- Emotion: Communal. The song widens from the couple to the room.
- Lighting: Warm interior against a blue-grey exterior — the classic wet-window glow.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: The rain and one violin. No dialogue, no lyrics, sixteen bars.
- Shots: Water running off a canvas seam in a rope; the abandoned aisle runner; a pair of satin shoes upside down on a chair; the string quartet playing under the awning with their cases held over their instruments.
- Cut to: Slow-motion of guests running from the house to the tent with jackets over their heads, laughing.
- Emotion: Held breath, tenderness, the middle of a long good day.
- Lighting: Late afternoon, the gold seam gone, lamps taking over.

### [bridge – half-time, one voice at a time]

```
My mother married in a heatwave, not a cloud to spare,
An album full of sunshine and a house she had to leave.
So keep the painted blue, keep the perfect afternoon,
I will take a roof that leaks above a man who doesn't.
And I will take the woman who kicked her heels off early,
Who never checked her hair and walked out into it.
```

- Scene: An old wedding album on a side table, then his answer from across the tent.
- Shots: Square photographs, a cloudless nineteen-eighties sky, a young bride squinting into the sun; then her satin shoes abandoned under a chair and her bare feet stepping off the boards into wet grass.
- Cut to: Her face in the present, tender and unsentimental; a wide of the leaking marquee seam above her; then him watching her walk out into the rain and following without hesitating.
- Emotion: The turn of the song. Perfect weather guarantees nothing. Then: chosen, twice, on purpose.
- Lighting: Warm lamp on the album with cool grey through the tent behind her; backlight from the doorway putting both of them in silhouette.

### [final chorus – modulated, fullest arrangement]

```
You gave me a ring on a rainy day,
So let it rain, let it come, let it stay.
Let it flatten every curl that took an hour to do,
There is nothing in this garden I would trade for you.
There's a river running under the floor of the tent,
And we're barefoot in the middle of it, soaked and content.
Every good thing we have came the same way,
You gave me a ring on a rainy day.
```

- Scene: The first dance outdoors in a wet garden, then the flooded marquee floor.
- Shots: The two of them alone on grass with the band playing from the tent mouth, her hair completely flat and the dress heavy with water; then a low angle across a wooden dance floor with an inch of water moving over it, feet stamping, spray on the beat.
- Cut to: Guests spilling out of the tent to join them, shoes in hands; then the couple at the centre of the flooded floor, foreheads together, absolutely still while everything else moves.
- Emotion: Release, every restraint in the video dropping at once, then the quiet inside the loudest moment.
- Lighting: Warm tent light from behind with rain lit like sparks against it; bulbs reflecting in the standing water and doubling every light in the room.

### [post-chorus – singalong]

```
So let it rain, so let it rain,
On the folding chairs and the paper chain.
So let it rain, so let it rain,
We are not going in, we are not going in.
```

- Scene: The whole party outside, refusing to go back in.
- Shots: A paper chain sagging and dripping between two poles; folding chairs standing in puddles; forty people singing at the sky.
- Cut to: The planner giving up on the clipboard and joining in.
- Emotion: Comic, communal, cathartic.
- Lighting: Full night now, tent glow and one work light on a stand.

### [outro – piano, guitar, rain]

```
The last song finished and nobody moved,
Fifty people dancing in a garden turned to mud.
The forecast said a hundred percent by four,
And it kept its word, and so did we.
```

- Scene: The end of the night.
- Shots: The band packing up while the crowd keeps dancing to nothing; mud over every shoe and hem in frame.
- Cut to: The final shot — the two of them last in the garden, the tent dark behind them, rain still coming, holding each other and not dancing to anything.
- Emotion: Settled. A whole life implied.
- Lighting: One lamp left on a pole, everything else in blue darkness.
