Global Metadata
Basic Attributes: bpm is 108. key is E major, and scale is major. Afrobeats with a warm pop sheen: log drums, palm-muted guitar, round bass and a shaker-led groove.
Global Emotional Progression: The track opens on rooftop air, distant traffic and a single clean guitar figure with a shaker, the lead voice unhurried and half-smiling, and the log drum entering alone on the last intro line as a promise of the groove to come. The first verse rides the full Afrobeats pocket at low volume, log drum and shaker with a round bass line and a guitar answering every second line, the vocal conversational and dry with the story told almost as gossip. The pre-chorus lifts by adding a soft synth marimba and a rising vocal stack, the kick dropping out for two bars so the words sit forward. The chorus opens wide and warm rather than loud, layered harmonies on the title, brass swells behind, and the groove at its most danceable. A short chanted post-chorus follows with call-and-response gang vocals and a heavier low tom pattern. The second verse warms further, adding a talking-drum figure and light hand percussion, the story moving out of a bedroom and into a market and then a party. The second chorus adds a counter melody in the guitar. The instrumental break is sixteen to twenty-four bars of percussion conversation between log drum, talking drum and shaker with the guitar riff floating over it and a filtered vocal chop of the hook. The bridge drops the drums to guitar, bass and a single sustained pad, the vocal close and honest at the emotional floor of the song, then the percussion returns one layer at a time. The final chorus is night rather than afternoon, the widest arrangement of the record with full harmonies and brass, and the outro settles back to the rooftop, the guitar figure and the shaker, ending on one line and the log drum fading out.

Dynamics are contained in the verses and open dramatically in the choruses. Generous plate and hall reverb on atmospheric elements, with the lead vocal kept close and dry for intimacy.
