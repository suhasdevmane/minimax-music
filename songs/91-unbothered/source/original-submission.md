# Unbothered — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
The art of not caring, danced. Somebody wrote three paragraphs about her and she read them, laughed, made coffee and put a song on. The whole record is one day of choosing her own afternoon over somebody else's argument.

**Mood:**
Warm, easy, sunlit. Confidence with no edge in it — nobody gets dragged, nobody gets named. The joke is never at anyone's expense; it is just that she has somewhere better to be.

**Message:**
"Unbothered is not a wall I built. It's a door I close without the guilt."

**Genre:**
Afrobeats with a warm pop sheen. Think: Tems x Tyla with the guitar-forward brightness of a Wizkid record — log drum, palm-muted guitar, round bass, shaker.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 108 BPM, 4/4
- Key: E major (bright, open, guitar-friendly)
- No modulation. The last chorus lifts by arrangement and by moving from afternoon to night.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, conversational and dry in the verses, warm and open rather than belted in the choruses.
- Backing vocals: layered harmonies on the title line; call-and-response gang vocals on the chant.

**Instrumentation:**
- Log drum (the heart of the record); round melodic electric bass
- Clean palm-muted guitar figure — opens the song, answers every second verse line, carries the outro
- Shaker and light hand percussion throughout; talking drum from the second verse
- Soft synth marimba on the pre-choruses; brass swells behind the choruses
- One sustained pad for the bridge
- Ambience: rooftop air, distant traffic, a far-off motorbike horn, ice in a glass, market voices, a scooter

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: rooftop, six o'clock gold, the log drum arriving on the last line
- Verses: the screenshot and the coffee, then the market and the party
- Pre-chorus: peace as a muscle, kick out for two bars
- Chorus: the widest, warmest, most danceable moment; the post-chorus is a chant
- Instrumental: percussion conversation, log drum against talking drum
- Bridge: drums gone, the honest floor of the song
- Final chorus and outro: night on the same rooftop, then one line and a fade

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – rooftop, guitar and shaker]

```
Rooftop speaker, six o'clock gold,
Ice in a glass and a story I've been told.
Somebody's typing something long about me,
And the log drum came on, so I let it be.
```

- Scene: A city rooftop at golden hour, a portable speaker, washing lines on the roofs beyond.
- Shot: Ice moving in a glass in extreme close-up, condensation running, gold light through it.
- Cut to: Her lying back on a sun-warmed roof with the phone face-down on her stomach, eyes closed.
- Emotion / Lighting: Entirely unhurried. Low gold sun, long shadows, warm skin tones.

### [verse 1 – the screenshot and the coffee]

```
They sent me a screenshot at ten in the morning,
Three paragraphs deep with no kind of warning.
I read it, I laughed, I went and made my coffee,
Then I put on the song that gets my hips talking to me.
My cousin said, "Aren't you gonna reply?"
I said, "To what — a stranger having a hard July?"
They want me in the comments, want me in the fight,
But my afternoon is booked and my chest is light.
Some things are weather, they pass over the roof,
I'm not gonna argue with a cloud about proof.
```

- Scene: Morning kitchen, bright and cluttered, a fan turning.
- Shots: A phone lighting up on the counter with a long wall of composited text; her reading it, one eyebrow up, then a single genuine laugh; the kettle; her dancing two small steps at the counter while the coffee brews.
- Cut to: A video call propped against the sugar tin, her cousin mid-question; her shrugging with both hands full.
- Emotion / Lighting: Amused, never wounded. Hard morning sun through a window, everything warm.

### [pre-chorus]

```
Peace is a muscle and I work it every week,
Learned it from my aunties in the kitchen when they speak.
When the noise gets loud I turn the bass up higher,
Let them run their mouth — I am not lending them a fire.
```

- Scene: The kitchen turning into a memory of a kitchen.
- Shots: Three older women mid-conversation at a table, laughing and unbothered, seen from a child's height; her hand reaching for a speaker dial and turning it up.
- Emotion / Lighting: Inherited calm. Memory shot warmer and slightly softer than the present.

### [chorus – the widest, warmest groove]

```
Unbothered, untouchable, in my lane,
Sun on the back of my neck, I'm not complaining.
Say what you want, put it in a frame,
I'll be right here moving just the same.
Low stress, light feet, easy on my mind,
Whatever you're carrying, leave it behind.
Unbothered, untouchable, in my lane,
Look at me dancing while you're saying my name.
```

- Scene: A sunlit street. The dance is the point.
- Shots: Her dancing down the middle of a quiet residential street in full sun; neighbours joining for two beats each and dropping out; a low angle on feet and shadow; a wide with washing lines and painted walls behind her.
- Emotion / Lighting: Joy with no showing-off in it. Full midday sun, saturated colour, no filter.

### [post-chorus – chant]

```
Shoulders down, shoulders down,
Nothing in this city gonna hold me down.
In my lane, in my lane,
Unbothered, untouchable, in my lane.
```

- Scene: The chant as a four-count dance move.
- Shots: Four dancers in a line dropping their shoulders on the beat; a mirror-frame repeat with her in front; a close-up of the move at half speed for the challenge.
- Emotion / Lighting: Simple, repeatable, shareable. Flat bright sun, no shadow drama.

### [verse 2 – market, flowers, the party]

```
Market on a Saturday, colours in a row,
Bought myself the flowers, nobody had to know.
The auntie with the peppers said, "You look light today,"
I said, "I put some heavy things down along the way."
Cold drink after, hibiscus and lime,
Walked the long block home because the day was mine.
Then at nine that same person walked into the party,
Whole room turning, waiting on a story.
I said "hey," made room, went back to the beat,
And the floor stayed hot underneath my feet.
```

- Scene: A Saturday market, then a house party at night.
- Shots: Stacked colour on market stalls; her paying for flowers with both hands; the pepper auntie's face, warm and knowing; a hibiscus drink in the sun; her walking home with flowers under one arm at a deliberately slow pace.
- Cut to: Night. A crowded front room. A figure entering, the room's heads turning; her glancing over, nodding once, and turning back to the dance.
- Emotion / Lighting: Generous, then anticlimactic on purpose. Market in full daylight; party in warm lamp and one red bulb.

### [pre-chorus 2]

(repeat)

- Scene: The party version of the same lift.
- Shots: Her hand on the speaker dial again, this time in the party; two friends closing ranks around her without being asked.
- Emotion / Lighting: Protected without needing protection. Lamp warm, faces close.

### [chorus 2]

(repeat)

- Scene: The party floor at full heat.
- Shots: The street dance from chorus one recreated indoors, more people, less space; a slow-motion insert of the flowers on a table behind the dancers.
- Emotion / Lighting: Bigger, sweatier, still light. Red bulb and lamp, no overheads.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: Pure percussion, pure movement, no story.
- Shots: Hands on a log drum in close-up; a talking drum being squeezed and struck; feet on a wooden floor; the shaker in silhouette against a window; a wide of the whole room moving on one beat.
- Lighting: Party warm cut against a single hard window shaft.

### [bridge – drums out, honest]

```
Don't get it twisted, I feel it all,
I just stopped performing it for people in the hall.
I let it hurt on a Tuesday with the curtains closed,
Then I woke up Wednesday and I picked the good clothes.
They call it cold, I call it a choice,
I gave my best years to the loudest voice.
Unbothered is not a wall I built,
It's a door I close without the guilt.
```

- Scene: The stillest part of the video. Her bedroom, curtains closed, then opened.
- Shots: Her sitting on the end of a bed in a dim room, entirely still, no dancing; a close-up of her face doing nothing; then a hard cut to her opening the curtains and choosing something bright from a rail; the last two lines shot on a real door closing gently.
- Emotion / Lighting: Honest, unperformed, then decided. Curtain dark to full daylight in one cut.

### [final chorus – night on the rooftop]

```
Unbothered, untouchable, in my lane,
Moon on the rooftop and I'm still not complaining.
Say what you want, put it in a frame,
I'll be right here moving just the same.
Low stress, light feet, easy on my mind,
Whatever you're carrying, leave it behind.
Unbothered, untouchable, in my lane,
Look at me dancing while you're saying my name.
```

- Scene: The intro rooftop at night, now full of people.
- Shots: The same speaker, the same roof, string lights up; twenty people dancing the chorus move; a drone rising off the roof over the whole neighbourhood with the music carrying.
- Emotion / Lighting: Communal and easy. Moonlight, string lights, warm skin, deep blue sky.

### [post-chorus 2]

(repeat, everyone)

- Scene: The chant with the whole roof doing it.
- Shots: The four-count move from every angle; her not at the front; a child on the edge of frame doing it half a beat late.
- Emotion / Lighting: Handed around. String lights and phone torches.

### [outro – back to one glass]

```
Rooftop speaker, midnight gold,
Ice in the glass and a story getting old.
Somebody's typing, I will never even see,
The log drum's playing, so I let it be.
```

- Scene: The roof emptying out.
- Shots: The same glass as shot one, ice almost gone, warm light instead of gold; her phone face-down on the ledge with the screen glowing under it; the final shot is her lying back on the roof exactly as in the intro, eyes closed, one hand keeping time on her stomach, as the log drum fades.
- Emotion / Lighting: Settled. String lights, deep blue, one warm lamp.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the street dance in full midday sun with neighbours joining for two beats each and dropping out.
- **The challenge:** the four-count *"shoulders down"* move from the post-chorus, shot at half speed once so people can learn it. It works in a kitchen, a lift or a market aisle.
- **The caption line:** *"Unbothered is not a wall I built. It's a door I close without the guilt."*
- **The quote:** *"To what, a stranger having a hard July?"*
- **The soft moment:** *"You look light today." "I put some heavy things down along the way."*
