# Barefoot on the Boulevard — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
Two in the morning on a wide northern city boulevard. The heels come off,
the shoes go in her fist, and the walk home becomes the best part of the
night. A funk strut record about the moment a woman stops waiting to be
collected.

**Mood:**
Confident, funny, warm. Not defiant — it never argues with anybody. The joy
is in the walking.

**Message:**
"I stopped waiting for a lift a long time ago. The walk was always the good
bit."

**Genre:**
Modern funk with a live rhythm section, clavinet, slap bass and a
three-piece horn section. Think: Jungle x Vulfpeck x Dua Lipa's funk-pop
side — pocket first, pop top line over it.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 112 BPM, 4/4
- Key: A minor
- No modulation. The final chorus lifts by putting the horns up an octave
  and adding a gang vocal on the extra couplet.

**Vocal setup:**
- Singer A (female lead) throughout, solo.
- Verse two is spoken for its first two lines and tightens into a full rap
  cadence from the third — bass, drums and clavinet only underneath, guitar
  scratches answering each phrase.
- Backing: stacked female harmonies on the choruses, a gang vocal on the
  final chorus's extra couplet.

**Instrumentation:**
- Slap and pop electric bass (the lead instrument)
- Clavinet, dry and clipped
- Tight live kit with a rimshot backbeat
- Three-piece horns: trumpet, tenor saxophone, trombone
- Wah guitar sixteenths and single-note scratches
- Bright organ pad on the choruses
- Fingerclicks, Rhodes and upright bass for the bridge
- Tambourine on the final chorus
- Ambience: wet street, a car stereo, a busker's amp, a passing tram, a
  market being wheeled out, shutters, birdsong

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: wet street, a clavinet riff, buckles coming undone
- Verse 1: the shoes lose, the pavement wins
- Pre-chorus: the first step, the cold, the realisation
- Chorus: the strut, horns, the boulevard as a stage
- Verse 2: spoken then rapped — half a mile of city, taken
- Instrumental: slap-bass feature, clavinet trade, horn shout chorus
- Bridge: the drums fall away and the song admits what it is about
- Final chorus: the whole band, a group of them now, horns an octave up
- Post-chorus: the horn riff and the safest line in the song
- Outro: dawn over the market roof, feet ruined, entirely satisfied

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – clavinet, wet street, no drums]

```
Wet street shining like it's just been laid,
Orange in the puddles where the tram lines fade.
Somebody's boot lid open with the bass turned round,
And I'm undoing buckles, and here comes the sound.
```

- Scene: A wide city boulevard just after the clubs empty. It has rained.
- Shot: Low and level with the road: wet setts, tram rails throwing orange
  light, one puddle taking up half the frame.
- Cut to: A car with its boot lid up and the speakers turned to face out;
  three people around it doing nothing in particular.
- Her reaction: Mahima sits, hooks a finger into an ankle strap, and starts
  undoing it before the bass has even landed.
- Lighting: Sodium orange, wet reflections, the only bright thing a shop's
  security light.

### [verse 1 – the shoes lose]

```
Two in the morning and my shoes have gone traitor,
Four inches of glamour and a blister like a saucer.
So I sit on a bollard with the straps in my teeth,
And the paving is freezing and the freezing is relief.
```

- Scene: A bollard at the edge of the boulevard.
- Shots: A heel held up to the light like evidence; a blister in close-up,
  unglamorous and funny; the straps gripped in her teeth while she works the
  buckle; the first bare sole meeting cold wet paving, macro.
- Emotion: Comic exasperation turning into physical relief.
- Lighting: Streetlight from above, hard shadow, wet ground bouncing orange
  back up.

```
Nobody's watching and the whole street can see,
I stand up and the city rearranges round me.
```

- Scene: The stand-up.
- Shots: A slow low push-in as she rises; the buildings appearing to swing
  behind her as the camera arcs; a single held beat before she moves.
- Emotion: The hinge of the whole song.
- Lighting: The first frame where she is the brightest thing in it.

### [pre-chorus 1]

```
There's a bassline coming out of a car door,
There's a busker packing up and starting one more.
I take the first step and the cold goes right through,
And I have never in my life felt as good as I do.
```

- Scene: The first step.
- Shots: A car door open with the stereo audible; a busker who has already
  packed his amp plugging it back in; her bare foot landing, then the second;
  her face, entirely uncomplicated delight.
- Emotion: Pure pleasure, no irony.
- Lighting: Warmer — the busker has a light, the car has an interior lamp.

### [chorus 1 – full band, horns]

```
Barefoot on the boulevard, heels in my hand,
Wet tram rails and the whole street's a band.
Every shop window's a mirror I can use,
Every kerb is a catwalk and I'm making the news.
Barefoot on the boulevard, two in the morning,
The city is my dance floor and it's only just warming.
Don't need a lift and I don't need a plan,
Barefoot on the boulevard, heels in my hand.
```

- Scene: The strut, straight down the middle of the empty boulevard.
- Shots: Tracking backwards ahead of her at hip height, heels swinging by
  their straps; her reflection running along a row of dark shop windows; a
  kerb walked like a beam; a tram passing empty behind her with its lights on.
- Emotion: Total ownership, no aggression in it.
- Lighting: Orange sodium, wet reflections, the tram's interior white light
  crossing the frame.

### [verse 2 – spoken, then rapped]

```
Right, shoes in the left hand and the phone in the right,
Half a mile of orange lamps and I am taking the lot tonight.
Past the tram stop, past the cabs with their engines still humming,
Past the lads on the corner who go quiet when they see me coming.
```

- Scene: The length of the boulevard, covered fast.
- Shots: Both hands in one frame, shoes left, phone right; a tram-stop
  shelter with one person asleep sitting up; a rank of cabs with engines
  running and windows fogged; three lads on a corner who stop talking, and
  the camera does not stay on them a second longer than it has to.
- Emotion: Momentum, awareness, no fear on display.
- Lighting: A run of orange lamps in perspective down the street.

```
There's a girl walking quick with her keys through her fist,
So I fall in beside her and we chat till the fear is dismissed.
Cobbles like a drum kit and the kerb stones like a snare,
I am not going home yet, I am going everywhere.
```

- Scene: The best forty seconds in the video.
- Shots: A young woman walking too fast with keys laced through her fingers;
  Mahima falling into step beside her, saying something ordinary; both their
  shoulders dropping; the two of them walking on together in wide shot; then
  bare feet on setts cut like a drum pattern, each footfall on a snare hit.
- Emotion: Solidarity handled lightly. No speech about it, no eye contact
  required.
- Lighting: The pair lit by a bus shelter's white light, warmer than the
  street.

### [pre-chorus 2 – bass and voice only]

```
I take the next step and the cold's nothing new,
And there's nobody walking this city like I do.
```

- Scene: Two shots, both very simple.
- Shots: A single bare foot landing on a wet metal tram rail; her face in
  profile moving through frame.
- Emotion: Certainty.
- Lighting: Half-lit, the rail throwing light up under her.

### [chorus 2]

- Scene: The strut again, wider and busier.
- Shots: The same backward-tracking frame but with more of the street alive
  behind her; a bus going past with two passengers in it; the shop-window
  reflection now containing two of them.
- Emotion: Building.
- Lighting: Same, plus the first bus headlights.

### [instrumental – slap-bass feature, clavinet trade, horn shout]

- Scene: The boulevard as a dance floor, no lyrics.
- Shots: Bare feet dancing on a wet zebra crossing, filmed from the road;
  a street sweeper's brushes turning in slow motion; the busker playing to
  nobody and being joined; a hand-held spin under a tram wire with the lights
  streaking; a wide from a footbridge of the whole empty boulevard with one
  small figure dancing in the middle of it.
- Lighting: Orange, wet, the widest and most cinematic moment of the video.

### [bridge – fingerclicks, Rhodes, almost no drums]

```
I spent years in shoes that somebody else chose,
Standing very still in a corner in a pose.
Held my breath through a hundred nights like this,
Waiting on a lift, waiting to be asked, waiting to be missed.
Now the straps are in my fist and the street is in my chest,
And the walking is the best part, never was the dress.
```

- Scene: Memory, then present.
- Shots: Flashbacks kept short and cold — a younger version of her standing
  perfectly still at the edge of a room, weight on one hip, smiling on
  request; a phone in a clutch bag, unanswered; a coat being held for her by
  somebody out of frame. Then present: her fist tightening on the straps; a
  long still wide of her standing alone in the middle of the road.
- Emotion: The song stops performing and says the thing.
- Lighting: Flashbacks are a cold interior white; present is warm orange.

### [final chorus – whole band, gang vocal]

- Scene: Not alone any more.
- Shots: Six women walking abreast down the middle of the road, shoes
  swinging from every hand; the girl with the keys among them, hands now
  empty; the group taking a bus lane like a stage; a wide from behind at road
  level; the horns' octave lift cut to a jump.
- Emotion: Full release, communal.
- Lighting: Brightest of the night — every streetlight, a lit shopfront, one
  set of headlights.

### [post-chorus – horn riff]

```
Heels in my hand and the horns coming in,
Cold on the soles and it feels like a win.
Heels in my hand and the long road home,
Never felt safer than walking alone.
```

- Scene: The group thinning out at a crossroads, everyone going their own
  way.
- Shots: Hands raised in goodbye without anyone turning round; her alone
  again on a long straight road; heels swinging in one hand, in step with the
  horn riff.
- Emotion: Alone by choice, which is the whole point.
- Lighting: The lamps beginning to lose to the sky.

### [outro – dawn, clavinet, market]

```
Sky going pale over the market roof,
Straps in my fist and a mile of proof.
Feet are a state and the morning's begun,
Barefoot on the boulevard, and I'm not done.
```

- Scene: Dawn at the top of the boulevard, the market being set up.
- Shots: A pale sky over a market roof; stall frames going up and crates
  being wheeled; her feet, filthy and unbothered, in close-up; her walking on
  past the market into the light; final shot, a locked-off wide of the
  boulevard with the streetlights switching off in a run down the street.
- Emotion: Wrecked, delighted, unfinished.
- Lighting: The streetlights lose; daylight takes the frame.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the first bare sole meeting cold wet paving, cut on
  the first chorus hit.
- **The challenge:** the shoes-off strut — post the moment you gave up on
  your shoes, heels swinging, with the horn riff over it.
- **The caption line:** *"Never felt safer than walking alone."*
- **The quote:** *"And the walking is the best part, never was the dress."*
- **The solidarity beat:** falling into step with the girl holding her keys
  through her fist, and neither of them making a thing of it.
- **The last visual:** the streetlights switching off one after another down
  the whole boulevard as she keeps walking.
