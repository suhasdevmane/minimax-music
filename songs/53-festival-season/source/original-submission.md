# Festival Season — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
One festival weekend, start to finish. Friday's wristband and half-built tent, the rain at four that nobody ran from, the flags on poles so the lost ones can find the crew, a stranger's shoulders on Saturday night, the sunrise set at four a.m., and the Sunday bus back with mud on her knees. It is not about a boy. It is about the version of herself she only meets in a field.

**Mood:**
Euphoric, muddy, glitter-on-the-cheekbones happy. The energy of a drop landing while ten thousand people go quiet first.

**Message:**
"It's festival season, and we're not going home."

**Genre:**
Festival EDM-pop / big-room dance pop. Think: Calvin Harris x Dua Lipa on the verses, Swedish House Mafia x Fred again.. on the drops, with a crowd-chant post-chorus built for a recap reel.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 128 BPM, 4/4
- Key: F♯ minor (euphoric-melancholy, big-room)
- No modulation; the final chorus lifts by adding the octave lead, the brass-stab layer and the crowd underneath.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, playful and close in the verses, belted and raspy on the drops.
- Backing vocals: stacked female harmonies on the chorus; gang-style chant on every post-chorus; male and female layers on the final hook.

**Instrumentation:**
- Layered supersaw synths (filtered pad in the intro and bridge, fully open on the drops)
- Heavy sidechained sub-bass
- Four-on-the-floor kick, offbeat hats, stadium claps
- Plucky arpeggio synth (verses, outro)
- Bright octave lead synth (second chorus onward)
- Brass-like stab layer (final chorus)
- Soft piano and warm analogue pad (bridge)
- Gang-style chant vocals
- Textures: crowd murmur, far-off soundcheck, wind across a field, white-noise risers, confetti-cannon impacts, sub drops, crowd cheers

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: noon on Friday, the tent half up, bass in the ground
- Verses: mud and glitter, flags and sandwiches; Saturday on a stranger's shoulders
- Pre-chorus: the low end rolling in, ten thousand people going quiet, the count-in
- Chorus: the drop, the field on fire, we're not going home
- Bridge: four a.m., the sunrise set, the girl she found in the field
- Final chorus: the sun coming up, leaving with the light
- Outro: Sunday, tent down, the bus, the wristband that stays on

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – close, playful, over a filtered pad and a distant kick]

```
Wristband on and the tent's half up,
Warm cider in a paper cup.
Bass in the ground before we see the stage,
Three days off from acting my age.
```

- Scene: Friday noon. A campsite field filling up, tents going up in rows, a wristband being snapped onto her wrist.
- Shot: Close-up of the wristband clicking shut, then her looking up at the field. A tent half-collapsed with her friends fighting the poles.
- Cut to: A paper cup of cider, warm, held up to the sun. Her feet feeling the bass through the grass before the stage is even in view.
- Emotion: Anticipation, the grin of three days with no responsibilities.
- Lighting: Flat bright noon, overexposed sky, dust in the air.
