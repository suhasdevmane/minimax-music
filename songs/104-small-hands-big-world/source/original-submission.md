# Small Hands, Big World — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:** A father kneels to his six-year-old daughter's height and sees
how much courage and imagination she carries in a body that still fits in
his arms. She draws a house with seven windows, lines her plush animals up
across the hallway, falls in the playground and gets back up. He is proud of
her, and a little humbled by her, and he tells her so. The song is about a
child who is small and sees the world big, and about a father who learns
from her.

**Mood:** Tender, proud, unhurried. Warm kitchen light, a porch at dusk, a
hallway floor. Never sugary, never pleading. The emotion is earned by
specific things she does, not by announcing how he feels.

**Message:** "You may be small enough to fit inside my waiting arms, but
there is something powerful in all your little charms."

**Genre:** Cinematic country-pop ballad. A plain-spoken, acoustic-led
record with a steel guitar that sits under the voice the way a hand sits
under a child's back, a brush-kit pulse that comes in on the chorus, and
a wide, close-miked finish that feels like a family room on a Sunday
evening. Sound comparisons only: the honest, small-room warmth of a
late-night porch record, the open-chorus lift of a big-sky radio ballad,
and the close, conversational vocal of a singer-songwriter's kitchen
demo.

**Tempo & Key:** 70 BPM, 4/4, G major. The song stays in G throughout. The
lift comes from the arrangement opening up (strings, a fuller band and a
wider stereo image) on the bridge and final chorus, not from a
modulation.

**Vocal setup:** Lead male, Singer A, a warm, lived-in baritone in his late
thirties. Close to the microphone in the verses, opening into a grainier
upper chest voice on the choruses. One low male harmony a third beneath the
lead on the choruses. A soft wordless pad of female and child-register
voices joins the final chorus. No second lead singer.

**Instrumentation:**

- Acoustic guitar with a restrained steel-guitar line (carries the song)
- Upright bass for the woody low end
- Light brush kit, entering on the first chorus
- Soft kick under the chorus backbeat
- Banjo-like plucked guitar colouring the second verse
- Soft bass
- String pad and piano bed under the third verse and the bridge
- Fiddle-style line lifting the final chorus
- Wordless choir on the final chorus
- Ear candy: a pencil scratching on paper under the first line, a child's
  laugh sampled faintly in the second verse

**Mood arc:**

- Intro: one guitar, a steel swell, the father describing a drawing.
- Verse 1: confiding, brushes at the edge, the pencil and the puzzles.
- Chorus 1: the first full strum and the title phrase, warm and plain.
- Verse 2: lighter, banjo plucks, the hallway animals made playful.
- Verse 3: tender, strings and pad, the falls and the courage.
- Pre-chorus: tightens and rises, "you rescue me each day."
- Chorus 2: the same hook with the low harmony added.
- Bridge: the turn. Piano, strings, almost spoken. The father's promise.
- Chorus 3: every layer back, fiddle and wordless choir, "brave heart."
- Outro: guitar and voice alone, the title phrase repeated softly to the end.

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – one guitar, a steel swell]
```
You hold a pencil like a captain
Gripping hard the wheel,
You draw a house with seven windows
And a garden of a field.
```
- Scene: A kitchen table in late afternoon. A six-year-old girl, Ellie,
  pigtails, red jumper, a pencil behind one ear, bent over a sheet of paper
  with her tongue in the corner of her mouth. Her father, Kai, is across
  the table, looking down at the drawing.
- Shot: Overhead, then a slow push toward the paper. The pencil in her fist,
  gripped hard. The house begins to take shape, seven windows counted out
  in pencil.
- Cut to: Her face, close, concentrating.
- Emotion: Absorbed, serious, a little proud of herself.
- Lighting: Warm window light from the left, a soft kitchen glow, dust in
  the air.

### [verse 1 – confiding, the pencil and the puzzles]
```
You say, this one is for Daddy,
This one's where the flowers grow,
Then you add a little rainbow
To a sky I used to know.
You solve your tiny puzzles
With a focus fierce and bright,
You turn the pieces over
Till the picture comes to light.
You may be small enough to fit
Inside my waiting arms,
But there is something powerful
In all your little charms.
```
- Scene: The same kitchen. Ellie slides the drawing across to Kai and points
  at the flowers and then the rainbow. Kai, kneeling on the floor beside
  her chair so their eyes are level, nods. Later, on the rug, she works a
  wooden jigsaw, turning each piece over until it clicks.
- Shot: Kai at her height, medium two-shot, his face soft and listening.
  Then a close insert of her small hands turning puzzle pieces.
- Cut to: Her hands, then Kai's face as he watches her try again.
- Emotion: Kai, quietly moved. Ellie, determined, not looking up.
- Lighting: Warm kitchen light, then soft daylight from a rug-side window.

### [chorus 1 – the first full strum]
```
Small hands, big world,
Big dreams in a little girl.
You carry more light
Than the stars in the night.
You teach my heart to see
Who I was and who I can be.
Small hands, big world,
You are my precious girl.
You make me laugh,
You make me strong,
You show me where my heart belongs.
And every day I understand
A better life begins
With your small hands in my hands.
```
- Scene: The first big chorus. The brush kit comes in and the steel guitar
  rises. Kai and Ellie stand at the front door, Ellie with a drawing rolled
  under one arm, and walk out to the porch, her hand in his.
- Shot: Wide two-shot as they walk out. Her small hand held in his big one.
  On "small hands, big world," a slow push-in to their joined hands.
- Cut to: Kai's face, a single breath, eyes bright.
- Emotion: Open, warm, plain. He is not performing it; he is proud.
- Lighting: The porch at the start of dusk, a warm lamp behind the door,
  the sky going blue-grey past the rail.

### [verse 2 – the hallway animals]
```
You lined your animals up
Across the hallway floor,
The lion was the teacher,
The rabbit guarded the door.
The little bear was sleeping,
The elephant was late,
And you explained the whole plan to me
With a serious little face.
You organise the whole world
In your wonderfully bright way,
You make a home from anything,
A game from any day.
Your mind is full of questions,
Your spirit full of flight,
You find a hundred possibilities
Inside a single night.
```
- Scene: The hallway, lined with plush animals in a row across the floor.
  Ellie is explaining the plan to Kai, who has crouched in the doorway. A
  lion at one end, a rabbit guarding a door drawn from a cardboard box, a
  bear asleep under a blanket.
- Shot: Kai crouched in the doorway at Ellie's eye level, listening with
  deliberate seriousness, nodding at each animal she names. Then a low
  wide shot down the hallway with the animals lined up.
- Cut to: Ellie, pointing, one finger raised, deadly serious.
- Emotion: Playful, affectionate, Kai amused and respectful at once.
- Lighting: Soft hallway light from a single ceiling lamp, a warm pool on
  the floor, the rest of the corridor in gentle shadow.

### [verse 3 – the falls and the courage]
```
Sometimes you fall and scrape your knee
And tears begin to rise,
You look at me, then stand again
With courage in your eyes.
I kiss the place that hurts you,
But you're stronger than the pain,
You take a breath and run again
Into the sun and rain.
I wish I could protect you
From every difficult road,
Carry every burden
And lighten every load.
But life will have its lessons,
And you'll learn them as you grow,
I'll be beside you, cheering
Every step you choose to go.
```
- Scene: A playground in the late afternoon. Ellie runs, trips, goes down
  on the wet tarmac and scrapes a knee. She looks up toward Kai on the
  bench, tears starting, then gets up on her own and runs back across the
  playground into a patch of rain and sunlight. Kai kneels by the bench
  for the kiss on the knee, then stays put and cheers her on.
- Shot: Medium shot of the fall, then Kai's close profile on the bench as
  she looks at him. A wide shot of her running into the rain. A close
  shot of Kai kneeling and kissing the knee, then standing with his hands
  on his hips, grinning.
- Cut to: Her face, eyes wet, then hard with resolve.
- Emotion: Tender, restrained. The fear is his, not hers. Her courage is
  the point.
- Lighting: Overcast, then a break of low sun through rain, a wet
  playground shining.

### [pre-chorus – the rescue]
```
You don't know it, little darling,
But you rescue me each day,
You bring my tired heart back home
In your own special way.
```
- Scene: Back in the kitchen. Kai at the sink, the dishes done, Ellie
  beside him on a step stool, drying a cup with great care.
- Shot: A tight shot of his hands in the water, then Ellie's face looking
  up at him as he turns to say the line. He tips his head toward her,
  unhurried.
- Cut to: Her face, mouth slightly open, not quite understanding and
  delighted all the same.
- Emotion: The father, lightly undone. The daughter, oblivious and bright.
- Lighting: Warm kitchen light, the window going dark behind them.

### [chorus 2 – the hook with the low harmony]
```
Small hands, big world,
Big dreams in a little girl.
You carry more light
Than the stars in the night.
You teach my heart to see
Who I was and who I can be.
Small hands, big world,
You are my precious girl.
You make me laugh,
You make me strong,
You show me where my heart belongs.
And every day I understand
A better life begins
With your small hands in my hands.
```
- Scene: The same porch as chorus 1, now full night. Ellie is asleep on a
  blanket on the porch swing with her drawing on her chest. Kai sits beside
  her, one hand on the swing chain, the other on her hand, watching the
  stars come out.
- Shot: Static wide of the porch, the swing, and the stars above. A close
  shot of their hands together on the blanket on "with your small hands in
  my hands." The stars brighten slowly through the chorus.
- Cut to: Kai looking up at the stars, then down at her.
- Emotion: Gentle, awed, plain. The hook as a lullaby.
- Lighting: Deep blue night, the porch lamp a single warm point, the stars
  sharp and small.

### [bridge – the turn]
```
If the world tells you you're too small,
Remember what I know,
The strongest rivers start as drops,
The tallest trees once grew low.
A seed can split the earth apart,
A spark can light the sky,
And you have more inside your heart
Than you can see tonight.
So dream as wide as oceans,
Walk as far as you can see,
Be gentle with the world, my child,
But never hide your wings.
And if you ever lose your way,
I'll help you understand,
You'll always have your father's voice
And a place within his hands.
```
- Scene: Ellie is older in the frame only by the imagination of the song:
  there is no time jump, the scene stays with the child. Kai sits with her
  on the bedroom floor after bedtime, the wall covered in her drawings, the
  house with seven windows at the centre. He speaks slowly, close to her,
  and she listens with her chin on her knees.
- Shot: A static, low-angle two-shot on the floor, then a slow drift to
  Kai's face speaking. Cutaways to the drawings on the wall: the seven
  windows, a seed, a spark.
- Cut to: Ellie, eyes on him, one small nod.
- Emotion: The song's centre. Kai almost speaks rather than sings. Quiet
  promise, nothing grand.
- Lighting: One bedside lamp, hard warm shadows, the room otherwise dark.

### [chorus 3 – every layer back]
```
Small hands, big world,
Brave heart in a little girl.
You carry more hope
Than the wildest wind can blow.
You teach my heart to see
The best that life can be.
Small hands, big world,
You are my precious girl.
You make me laugh,
You make me strong,
You are the reason I belong.
And every day I understand
A better life begins
With your small hands in my hands.
```
- Scene: A montage of the day in one chorus: Ellie on the swing, Ellie
  drawing at the table again, Ellie at the bottom of the stairs with her
  shoes on the wrong feet, Kai kneeling to put them right. The final
  chorus returns with the full band and the fiddle line.
- Shot: Quick cuts on the phrases, roughly one per line, each held on a
  small gesture. End on Kai kneeling with the shoes, tying a double knot.
- Cut to: Ellie's face, looking down at her shoes, then at him.
- Emotion: Bright, full, the tenderness out in the open. A wordless choir
  under the title phrase.
- Lighting: Warm daylight through the front window, the stairwell lit from
  above, the house full of afternoon.

### [outro – guitar and voice alone]
```
Small hands, big world,
My forever little girl.
No matter where you stand,
You'll always hold my hand.
```
- Scene: The porch at dusk again, the same blanket. Ellie is on the step,
  her back against Kai's knee, her drawing on her lap. He sits with his
  hand resting on her shoulder. Nothing else happens.
- Shot: A slow, locked-off wide of the two of them against the last of the
  light. The camera holds through the final phrase and the fade.
- Cut to: Black on the last note of the guitar.
- Emotion: Stillness, belonging, nothing left to prove.
- Lighting: The last blue of dusk, the porch lamp, the sky almost dark.

## Viral moments

- The hook frame: a six-year-old's hand held in a grown man's hand on a
  porch step, the title phrase on screen, "small hands, big world."
- The challenge: "draw your house with the most windows" — post a child's
  drawing and tag a parent, with the chorus playing under it.
- The caption line: "With your small hands in my hands." Short enough to
  sit under any photo of a kid and a parent.
- The quote: "You may be small enough to fit inside my waiting arms, but
  there is something powerful in all your little charms."
