# Airplane Mode — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
Two people drive far enough out of the city that the signal dies, put both phones flat on a cabin table, and leave them there for two days. Nothing dramatic happens. That is the point. The song is about what she can hear once nothing is reaching her — including a question she has been avoiding for a year, which she answers out loud because there is nowhere else to look.

**Mood:**
Warm, unhurried, quietly euphoric. Not a wellness lecture and not a detox joke — a love song about presence, with one small ache in it because Monday is real.

**Message:**
"I wasn't tired. I was only being reached."

**Genre:**
Chill pop with lo-fi drums. Think: Jungle x Novo Amor x the soft rhythm-section feel of a Khruangbin record, everything mixed a little soft at the edges, nothing ever pushed loud.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 98 BPM, 4/4
- Key: G minor (warm rather than sad — the minor here is dusk, not grief)
- No modulation. The final chorus lifts by width: stacked harmonies, a low string bed, a soft tambourine.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, low and unhurried, talking more than singing in the verses, open and full in the choruses, near-whispered in the outro.
- Backing vocals: stacked female harmonies in the last chorus only; the first two choruses are a single voice, which is what makes the last one land.

**Instrumentation:**
- Clean electric guitar playing natural harmonics and a falling figure — the signature of the record, first bar to last
- Warm analogue pad breathing under everything, blooming in the choruses
- Round soft-attack bass; lo-fi drums with a filtered kick, brushed snare and a dusty top end
- Rhodes-style keys answering the vocal from verse two, leading the instrumental break
- Light hand percussion, shaker, a soft tambourine in the final chorus only
- Low string bed rising under the bridge
- Textures: road noise, a radio dissolving into static, water, wind in pines, a distant bird, a kettle beginning to whistle very low, tape flutter across the whole mix

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: the signal dropping bar by bar on a road out of the city
- Verse 1: arriving, the cabin's objects, the two phones going down
- Pre-chorus: the week coming off her shoulders
- Chorus: the release — wide, but never loud
- Verse 2: the lake, the cards, the question she answers
- Instrumental: outdoors, water and wind, one empty bar
- Bridge: Monday exists, the ridge has a signal, they choose not to walk up
- Final chorus: the fullest arrangement, warm rather than triumphant
- Outro: the drive down, the bars returning like weather, one last refusal

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – guitar harmonics over road noise, no drums]

```
Two hours out of the city and the bars start dropping,
One, then none, and the little map goes plain.
You said we could turn back, I said keep driving,
And the radio gave up somewhere past the county line.
```

- Scene: A car on a two-lane road heading out of a city at the end of an afternoon.
- Shot: Windshield POV, the last of the suburbs going past, then trees.
- Cut to: The phone in the cradle losing bars one at a time (screen composited), then the map going blank grey.
- Her reaction / Emotion: A small decision made and not discussed.
- Lighting: Late-afternoon sun through a dirty windshield, real, no grade.

### [verse 1a]

```
The key was under a rock exactly where they said it was,
The kettle's older than the both of us and whistles like a train.
There's a drawer of someone's postcards and a deck with two cards missing,
And a window with nothing in it but the water and the pines.
```

- Scene: Arriving. The cabin's objects, catalogued.
- Shots: A hand lifting a flat rock and finding a key; the door swinging in on a room nobody has been in for a month; a battered kettle going on a stove; a drawer of postcards fanned out; a deck of cards with obvious gaps; the window and the lake.
- Emotion: Curiosity, the pleasure of somewhere unfamiliar.
- Lighting: Low golden side light through small windows, dust in the air.

### [verse 1b]

```
You put your phone down on the table, screen flat, and slid it over,
I put mine beside it and we never said a word.
Two whole days and nobody needs us, nobody knows,
And the quiet came in through the screen door like a guest.
```

- Scene: **The founding image of the video.** Two phones going flat on a wooden table.
- Shots: His hand placing a phone screen-down and pushing it to the middle of the table; her hand setting hers beside it; a wide of the table with the two phones and nothing else on it; the screen door standing open onto the lake with the light moving on the floor.
- Emotion: Relief, mutual, unspoken.
- Lighting: Warm interior, cool blue lake light coming through the door.

### [pre-chorus 1]

```
There's a whole week in my shoulders that I'm setting on the floor,
There's a hundred things unanswered and they'll all still be there.
Let them wait, let them wonder where I've gone,
I have got somewhere better to be and it is right here.
```

- Scene: Her physically putting the week down.
- Shots: Her sitting on the porch steps and rolling her shoulders once; a bag dropped in a corner and not unpacked; her taking her shoes off and leaving them by the door; a long slow look at the water.
- Emotion: Decompression, in real time.
- Lighting: Golden hour on the porch, everything long-shadowed.

### [chorus 1 – wide, never loud]

```
Put the world on airplane mode,
It's just you and me tonight.
No little lights, no half a conversation,
Nobody pulling on my sleeve to be somewhere else.
Let the sky keep whatever it was going to say,
I am not taking anything today.
Put the world on airplane mode,
It's just you and me tonight.
```

- Scene: The evening. Nothing happens and it is the best footage in the film.
- Shots: The two of them on the porch with mugs; a woodstove being lit badly and then well; the lake going flat and silver; a slow drift across the table with the two phones still exactly where they were left; her laughing at something off-camera.
- Emotion: Contentment, no urgency anywhere in the frame.
- Lighting: Sunset into blue hour into firelight, all practical.

### [verse 2a]

```
Saturday the lake was cold enough to make me shout,
You went in like a lunatic and stayed in twice as long.
We played that broken deck all afternoon and made the missing up,
You cheated, badly, and I let you have it anyway.
```

- Scene: Day two. Physical, funny, bright.
- Shots: Her going into the lake to her waist and shouting; him running straight past her and diving; both of them on the dock with towels and blue lips; the incomplete deck laid out on the porch boards with two hand-drawn cards among them; him palming a card with absolutely no skill; her seeing it and saying nothing.
- Emotion: Play. The lightest sequence in the video.
- Lighting: Hard midday sun off water, real flare, no filtration.

### [verse 2b]

```
Then you asked me something small that I had spent a year avoiding,
And I answered it out loud because there was nowhere else to look.
Turns out I have plenty to say when nothing interrupts,
Turns out I was never tired, I was only being reached.
```

- Scene: The turn of the whole song. A conversation on the dock with legs in the water.
- Shots: A wide two-shot from behind, both of them sitting on the dock, no cuts for the whole first line; then her face in profile, thinking longer than a music video usually allows; then his face listening; then the two of them again, closer together than they were.
- Emotion: Something real being said, and nothing bad happening.
- Lighting: Late afternoon, low and warm, the lake bright behind them.

### [pre-chorus 2]

```
There's a whole year in my jaw that I am putting down as well,
There's a version of me answering at midnight in my head.
She can wait, she can sit outside the door,
I'm not letting her back in until the drive home instead.
```

- Scene: Quieter than the first pre-chorus, almost spoken.
- Shots: Her alone at the window at dusk; a very brief city insert — a lit desk at midnight, a hand on a keyboard, shot cold and unappealing — then straight back to the cabin; her closing the inner door on the porch.
- Emotion: A boundary being drawn on purpose.
- Lighting: Blue dusk inside, one warm lamp.

### [chorus 2]

Repeat of chorus 1 — the same evening, later, more firelight.

- Scene: Night two. The stove, the cards, the postcards, the table.
- Shots: Firelight on the ceiling boards; the two phones on the table with the fire moving on their backs; her feet on his lap on the couch; a slow push out through the screen door to the dark lake.
- Emotion: Deeper contentment. Lighting: firelight and one lamp only.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: Outdoors, no people for the first half.
- Shots: Water moving against the dock pilings; wind through the tops of the pines; a bird crossing the frame; the kettle starting to steam on the stove; the two of them asleep in chairs; a single held wide of the cabin from the water with one window lit.
- Lighting: Blue hour, then full dark with one interior light.

### [bridge – no drums, guitar and pad and voice]

```
Monday's coming for us both with all its little teeth,
There's a signal on the ridge if we walk up and let it in.
But the fire's going and the kettle's going and you're reading me the postcards
From people we will never meet, from summers we were not in.
So the ridge can keep its one bar and the week can hold its breath,
I'll take the last of the quiet and I'll take it here with you.
```

- Scene: The one moment of real temptation, and the refusal.
- Shots: A ridge above the cabin at night with a single distant light on it; her looking up at it through the window; him reading a postcard aloud with the fire behind him; her turning away from the window and sitting back down; a hand putting another log on the stove.
- Emotion: Choice, made gently, without a speech.
- Lighting: Fire and one lamp; the ridge shot is the only cold frame in the sequence.

### [final chorus – stacked harmonies, low strings, soft tambourine]

```
Put the world on airplane mode,
It's just you and me tonight.
No little lights, no half a conversation,
Nobody pulling on my sleeve to be somewhere else.
Let the sky keep whatever it was going to say,
I'll pick it up on Monday, not today.
Put the world on airplane mode,
It's just you and me tonight.
```

- Scene: The last night, then the first light of the last morning.
- Shots: The two of them outside in blankets with the whole sky above them; a slow tilt up from the porch to the stars; the fire going down to embers; morning mist coming off the lake; the table with the two phones, untouched, in daylight.
- Emotion: Full, warm, a little sad because it is nearly over.
- Lighting: Starlight and embers, then cold clean dawn.

### [post-chorus]

```
Airplane mode, airplane mode,
Nothing coming in and nothing going out.
Airplane mode, airplane mode,
Just the water and the woodsmoke and your mouth.
```

- Scene: Four slow images, not fast cuts — the opposite of a chant.
- Shots: The lake surface; smoke leaving the chimney; the two phones; the two of them close in the doorway.
- Lighting: Dawn, soft and blue-grey.

### [outro – road noise, single guitar]

```
We drove back down on Sunday and the bars came back like weather,
Three at once, then a hundred, then a hum.
You reached over and you slid my phone beneath the seat,
And said not yet, and I said not yet, and we drove on.
```

- Scene: The drive home, and one last refusal.
- Shots: The cabin door being locked and the key going back under the rock; the car pulling out; the phone in the cradle beginning to light up as the signal returns (screen composited); his hand reaching over, taking it out of the cradle and sliding it under the passenger seat; final locked-off shot through the windshield of the road ahead, both of them quiet, holding through the fade.
- Emotion: Peace, and one small act of defiance.
- Lighting: Flat morning light, real, no grade.

## Viral moments

- **The two phones on the table.** They are in frame, untouched, in every act of the video, and the shot never changes angle. The whole concept in one image.
- **The hook.** *"Put the world on airplane mode"* is a caption, a status and a challenge in four words.
- **The line for captions.** *"Turns out I was never tired, I was only being reached."*
- **The challenge.** Post your own airplane-mode weekend: first frame the phone going flat on a table, last frame the signal coming back.
- **The quote.** *"Let the sky keep whatever it was going to say."*
- **The last gesture.** Sliding the phone under the seat instead of answering it — the shareable ending.
