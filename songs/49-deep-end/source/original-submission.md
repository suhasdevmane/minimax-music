# Deep End — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A rooftop pool party at sunset. Forty people stand in the shallow end holding their glasses at their chins and nobody's hair is wet. She has been that person for two summers: careful, dry, exactly far enough away. Then someone walks up the steps at the far end and she decides, for once, to go in headfirst. The deep end is the metaphor and the location at the same time.

**Mood:**
Euphoric and slightly melancholy, the way good house records are. Flirty but never explicit; the charge is in the decision, not the contact.

**Message:**
"The shallow end is safe and it is boring. I would rather be the one who jumped."

**Genre:**
Classic house. Think: Fred again x Robin S x Jamie xx — piano stabs, deep sub-bass, a filtered vocal hook and a drop that stays in a minor key instead of resolving sweet.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 124 BPM, 4/4
- Key: A minor (euphoric without going saccharine; the drop lands hard in minor)
- No modulation; the final drop lifts by arrangement, layered stacks and a second piano an octave up.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, low and almost spoken in the verses, wide open on the drops.
- Backing vocals: stacked harmonies on every chorus; the title chopped, pitched and gated as an instrument in the post-choruses and the build.

**Instrumentation:**
- Bright acoustic piano playing house stabs (filtered in the intro, fully open on the drop, doubled an octave up at the end)
- Deep round sub-bass
- Four-on-the-floor kick, crisp clap on two and four, open hats, shaker
- Warm analogue pad, plucky counter-melody synth
- Chopped and pitched vocal samples of the hook
- Textures: rooftop ambience, water lapping, traffic far below, a crowd bed, a swept underwater filter, one splash and a rush of bubbles

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: the record heard through a wall, the sun coming down the building
- Verse 1: the party observed from the shallow end, and two summers of being careful
- Pre-chorus: the underwater lights, the shoe off, the count
- Chorus: the drop, the decision, the hook
- Verse 2: the conversation, the rule, and the line that dismantles it
- Bridge: dry piano, no reverb, the honest reason
- Final chorus and outro: the dive, wet hair, a city that carried on without her

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro — filtered piano, water, no drums]

```
Sun coming down the side of the building like it's pouring,
Turning the whole pool the color of a coin.
Everybody's on the edge with their feet in and their phones out,
Nobody in past the second step.
I've been the queen of the second step.
```

- Scene: A rooftop pool at seven in the evening, high above a city.
- Shot: The sun sliding down a glass tower and hitting the surface of the water, the whole pool going gold, slow drift.
- Cut to: Feet in a row along the pool edge, ankle deep, phones up, nobody past the second step.
- Emotion: Detached and amused. She is watching a room she has been in a hundred times.
- Lighting: Low direct sun, hard gold on water, long shadows across hot tile.

### [verse 1 — soft kick, sub-bass, filtered piano]

```
Seven o'clock and the tiles are still warm from the sun,
Half the city down below us and the evening just begun.
Forty people in the shallow end with glasses at their chins,
Nobody's hair is wet, and nobody goes in.
```

- Scene: The party, catalogued.
- Shot: A slow lateral track along the shallow end at chest height: glasses held above the waterline, careful hair, everybody talking to somebody over somebody's shoulder.
- Lighting: Gold going amber, the water lit from above only.

```
I have been that girl for two whole summers, careful, dry,
Perfect hair, perfect distance, perfect lie.
Then you came up the steps at the far end of the blue,
And you didn't look away, and neither did I.
```

- Scene: The introduction of Kai, from across the water.
- Shot: Mahima at the pool edge in a sun lounger, phone face-down, glass in hand, immaculate, not in the water.
- Cut to: Kai coming up the roof stairs at the far end, towel over one shoulder, seeing her, not looking away.
- Emotion: The first thing in two summers to interrupt the routine.
- Lighting: He is backlit by the last of the sun; she is in the shadow of the building.

### [pre-chorus 1 — filter opening, drums cut on the last line]

```
Somebody turned the lights on underneath the water,
The whole roof went quiet and the whole pool went blue.
I've got one shoe off and one hand on the rail,
And the drop is only six feet, so
```

- Scene: Dusk arriving in one cut.
- Shot: The underwater pool lights coming on all at once, the whole surface going electric blue, everything above it dropping into shadow.
- Emotion: The held breath before a decision.
- Lighting: The pool becomes the only light source in the video from here on.

### [chorus 1 — the drop]

```
Meet me at the deep end, I'm done with wading in,
Standing in the shallow with a glass up to my chin.
Six feet of nothing underneath and neon on the blue,
Count me down from three and I'll come up looking at you.
Meet me at the deep end, I'm done with wading in,
Everybody's careful, I've been careful for a while,
So kick the whole year off and let it sink under the tile,
Meet me at the deep end, I'm going in.
```

- Scene: The party finally moving.
- Shot: The drop lands on a wide of the whole roof: neon coming on, people turning toward the water, the deep end suddenly the centre of the frame.
- Emotion: Euphoric, but not sweet. It is a minor key drop and the video should feel like one.
- Lighting: Underwater lights, pink and blue neon on the parapet, the sky gone violet.

### [post-chorus 1 — chopped hook]

```
Deep end, deep end, water going gold to blue,
Deep end, deep end, I am not staying dry for you.
Deep end, deep end, hair down and hands up high,
Deep end, deep end, meet me at the deep end.
```

- Scene: Four rhythmic cuts on the chopped vocal.
- Shot: A hair tie pulled out; a hand hitting the water flat; the underwater light strobing; a wide of the pool with everyone's hands up.
- Lighting: Hard neon, high contrast, wet skin.

### [verse 2 — bass and hats, dub delay]

```
You asked me what I'm waiting for, I said I've got a fear,
The last one left me sitting on an edge for half a year.
You said the shallow end is where the loud ones stay,
Then you turned around and walked the other way.
```

- Scene: The conversation, shot as two people talking at the edge of a party.
- Shot: The two of them at the rail with the city behind, close two-shot, the party out of focus.
- Cut to: Kai walking away from her along the pool edge toward the deep end, unhurried, not looking back.
- Emotion: Not a rejection — an invitation with no pressure in it. That distinction is the whole scene.
- Lighting: Neon on both their faces, the pool light coming up from below.

```
Somebody laughed and somebody held a phone up in the air,
I put my glass down on the tile and pulled the pins out of my hair.
Two whole summers keeping every single thing in reach,
Gone in about the time it takes to breathe.
```

- Scene: The decision, in three objects.
- Shot: A glass set down on hot tile, macro, condensation ring spreading.
- Emotion: Calm. She is not being persuaded; she has decided.
- Lighting: Blue from below, neon from the side.

### [pre-chorus 2 — longer, higher build]

```
Everybody's phone is up, the underwater lights are on,
The whole roof went quiet and the whole pool went blue.
I've got both shoes off now and no hand on the rail,
And the drop is only six feet, so
```

- Scene: The whole roof watching.
- Shot: Forty phones up, a ring of small lights around the pool, from her point of view at the deep end.
- Emotion: Everything held still. The last bar has no drums; the video should have no motion either.
- Lighting: Only the pool.

### [chorus 2 — counter-melody, the dive]

```
(the chorus again, and the dive lands on the first beat)
```

- Scene: The dive.
- Shot: One slow-motion shot from water level: her going in cleanly, the surface breaking, the neon shattering across it.
- Emotion: The frame the entire video exists for.
- Lighting: Underwater light directly on her; everything above the surface is a blur of neon.

### [instrumental — breakdown, sixteen bar build]

- Scene: Underwater and above, alternating.
- Shots: Her surfacing and pushing her hair back, wide. The rest of the party going in after her, one at a time and then all at once. Legs and bubbles from below. A slow drone rise off the roof showing one lit blue rectangle in a dark city. Back down through the build to a tight shot of her face at the surface, breathing, waiting for the drop.
- Lighting: Blue and neon; the drone shot is the only wide of the city in the video.

### [bridge — dry piano, no reverb]

```
I've spent a long time with my feet on solid ground,
Reading every room before I ever made a sound.
The shallow end is safe and it is boring and it's small,
And I have been so careful that I hardly lived at all.
So if this is a mistake, then it's a mistake I choose,
And I'd rather be the girl who jumped than the girl who didn't move.
```

- Scene: The quietest part of the loudest song.
- Shot: Her floating on her back in the middle of the pool, ears under, the sound of the party gone, shot from directly above.
- Emotion: Honest, not self-pitying. She is stating a fact about herself.
- Lighting: Only the pool light; her face is the brightest thing in the frame.

### [final chorus — the biggest drop]

```
(the chorus, with "Hair already ruined and there's neon on my skin")
```

- Scene: The whole roof in the water.
- Shot: A wide of a pool with forty people in it, phones abandoned along the edge, everyone's hair wet.
- Cut to: The two of them in the deep end, treading water, talking, the party going on around them.
- Emotion: Joy with a room in it. Keep the two of them at a distance from each other; the song is about the decision, not the kiss.
- Lighting: Peak neon, the brightest frame of the video.

### [post-chorus 2 — filtering away]

```
(the chopped hook again, filtering down)
```

- Scene: Late, thinning out.
- Shot: The pool from the far corner, half as many people, the light softer, someone getting out and wrapping up in a towel.
- Lighting: Neon starting to be turned off, one section at a time.

### [outro — one wet piano chord]

```
Wet hair on a rooftop with a towel round my shoulders,
City carrying on below us like it always does.
You said, what took you so long, and I said, nothing, I'm here,
Meet me at the deep end, I'm already in.
```

- Scene: The very end of the night.
- Shot: Her on a lounger with a towel round her shoulders, hair flat and wet, no makeup left, looking out at the city.
- Final shot: The empty pool, still lit, the surface settling flat, one towel and one glass at the edge, locked-off, held through the fade.
- Emotion: Settled. Not triumphant, not romantic-comedy. Just changed.
- Lighting: Pool light only, the city as background.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the dive in slow motion from water level, neon shattering on the surface, on *"I'm going in."*
- **The challenge:** the shallow-end joke — a whole party stood in waist-deep water with glasses at their chins, then one person goes under. Cut on the drop.
- **The turn:** *"You said the shallow end is where the loud ones stay, then you turned around and walked the other way."*
- **The caption line:** *"I'd rather be the girl who jumped than the girl who didn't move."*
- **The last frame:** an empty lit pool at the end of the night with one towel at the edge.
