# Wine and Whispers — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
One night in, one bottle, and the exact volume at which people tell the
truth. The song is a conversation getting quieter and more honest as it goes:
it starts on a safe confession, gets to the year he never talks about, gets
to the thing she is most afraid of, and ends with something said so low it
has to be said into the air beside somebody's jaw. Then they push the table
back and dance with nothing playing.

**Mood:**
Low-lit, adult, unhurried, warm. Flirtation that has already been settled and
has moved on to something riskier: being known.

**Message:**
"Some things you can only say at this volume."

**Genre:**
Jazz-pop lounge with a small-room trio feel. Think: Norah Jones x Laufey,
with the muted-trumpet intimacy of a Chet Baker record and a modern pop lyric
sitting on top of it.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 92 BPM, 4/4, softly swung
- Key: E♭ major (warm, classic horn key, sits low and easy in her chest voice)
- No modulation and no big lift. The final chorus is the fullest arrangement
  on the record and still never the loudest.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, so close to the microphone that the breath
  is part of the arrangement; nearly spoken in the pre-choruses and the
  bridge.
- Backing vocals: light stacked harmonies under the hook, a low harmony stack
  on the post-chorus, a wordless vocal over the outro.

**Instrumentation:**
- Upright double bass (opens the record alone, plays to the last bar)
- Brushed drum kit, never a stick
- Warm jazz piano, comping in the verses, alone in the bridge
- Muted trumpet: phrase answers, the whole instrumental solo, a countermelody
- Soft string pad in the choruses only
- Room tone, rain on a closed window, a cork, a glass set down on wood

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: the room, the candles, the bottle, the phones in a drawer
- Verse 1: the safe confessions, both of them still performing a little
- Pre-chorus: the light stays off, the volume comes down
- Chorus: the hook, harmony opening rather than volume rising
- Verse 2: the confessions stop being charming and start being true
- Instrumental: a full muted-trumpet solo at conversation volume
- Bridge: the quietest passage, the biggest thing said
- Final chorus: the same room, everything now known
- Outro: the table pushed back, a dance with no music left

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – bass alone, brushes after a bar, rain outside]

```
Two candles burned to nothing and the wax on the floor,
The good bottle open that we swore we'd save for more.
Phones in a drawer where we can't hear them ring,
And the rain out there doing its own quiet thing.
```

- Shots: A living room after midnight seen from the doorway. Macro on two candles burned down to stubs with wax spilled over a saucer onto the boards. A bottle standing open on the floor with the cork beside it and a label turned away from camera. A drawer sliding shut on two dark phones. A window with rain running down the outside of it and the room reflected in the glass.
- Emotion and lighting: Settled anticipation; the entire film is lit by candles and one lamp with a scarf over it, nothing overhead, ever.

### [verse 1]

```
You start with a small one, something safe and half a joke,
You say you nearly cancelled, sat outside and never spoke.
So I tell you that I looked you up before we met,
Went four years back on a Sunday, not embarrassed yet.
```

- Shots: The two of them on the floor with their backs against the sofa, a foot of space between them. Him talking with a glass balanced on his knee, laughing at himself. A two-second flash of a parked car outside a restaurant, a man not getting out of it, cold blue. Her covering her face with one hand and admitting hers anyway, still laughing.
- Emotion and lighting: Warm and easy; candlelight from below, both faces half in shadow.

```
There's a trumpet somewhere low, doing something to the room,
Making everything we say sound truer than it should.
Your glass goes down an inch, my voice goes down a tone,
And the loudest thing in here is what we haven't shown.
```

- Shots: A turntable in the corner with a stack of sleeves on the floor beside it, seen only in a wide, never in macro. A slow drift across the room past the candles to the two of them. Her glass, his glass, one lower than the other. A held two-shot where neither of them says anything for four bars.
- Emotion and lighting: The first shift; the room getting smaller in the framing, tighter lens, shallower focus.

### [pre-chorus]

```
Don't turn the big light on, leave it where it is,
Everything looks kinder at the edge of a wick.
Say the next one closer, say it near my ear,
The only thing worth having is the thing you're scared to hear.
```

- Shots: A hand reaching for a wall switch and stopping. Macro on a candle wick at the moment the flame leans. Her turning her head so her ear is nearer his mouth than her eyes are to his. Extreme close-up on a mouth moving with no sound design behind it.
- Emotion and lighting: The volume dropping; one candle in frame, everything else black.

### [chorus – harmony opens, volume does not]

```
Wine and whispers, keep the lights down low,
Tell me something true, then something slow.
The street can have the shouting, the street can have the rain,
In here we say it quiet and we say it plain.

Wine and whispers, the hours going soft,
Nothing in this room is getting turned off.
Move a little closer, let the candles go,
Wine and whispers, keep the lights down low.
```

- Shots: The room from above, the two of them small in a pool of candlelight on a dark floor. A wide of the window with the wet street beyond it, a car passing, someone shouting distantly. Back inside: the space between the two of them measurably smaller than it was. A candle guttering and being ignored.
- Emotion and lighting: The song's warmest frame; deep amber, hard falloff into black, the window the only cool light in the film.

### [verse 2]

```
Somewhere past the second glass you stop performing,
And you tell me about a year you never say aloud,
How you slept on a friend's floor and called it fine,
And how you still won't sit with your back to a crowd.
```

- Shots: His face with the laughing gone out of it, close, one candle. A two-second flash of an unfamiliar living room floor with a folded blanket on it, cold morning light, no people. Present: him picking his seat deliberately, back to the wall, and her noticing him do it.
- Emotion and lighting: The turn from charming to true; the light unchanged and the faces completely changed.

```
Then you ask me what I'm scared of and I nearly lie,
And I say the real one and it comes out small,
That I'm easy to leave and I've been left before,
And you set your glass down and don't argue at all.
```

- Shots: Her opening her mouth on the easy answer and stopping. A long hold on her face while she decides. The line said very quietly, in one take, no cutaway. His hand setting a glass down on the boards without a sound, and staying there.
- Emotion and lighting: The most exposed moment in the film; the closest lens in the film, candlelight only on one side of her face.

### [pre-chorus 2]

```
Don't turn the big light on, we're better in the dark,
Everything looks braver at the edge of a spark.
Say the next one closer, say it near my ear,
The only thing worth having is the thing you're scared to hear.
```

- Shots: The lamp with the scarf over it, glowing dull orange. Macro on a match being struck to relight a candle, the flare filling the frame for one beat. Both of them closer than any previous shot. Her ear, his mouth, nothing else in focus.
- Emotion and lighting: Braver; one new flame, the room fractionally brighter for the first time.

### [chorus]

(repeat)

- Shots: The same overhead as chorus one with the pool of light smaller and the two of them nearer its centre. The rain harder on the glass. Two glasses side by side on the floor, both nearly empty.
- Emotion and lighting: Fuller and closer; the trumpet countermelody visible as a slow tilt across the ceiling.

### [instrumental]

- Shots: The muted-trumpet solo carried by the room rather than a performer. Wax running down a candle in macro. The rain on the glass in macro, the street lights broken up in it. A wide of the two of them not talking, her head on his shoulder, both looking at the window. The turntable arm reaching the end of a side and lifting itself, seen once in a wide from across the room.
- Emotion and lighting: Suspended time; slow drifting camera, no cuts on the beat.

### [bridge – piano and voice, the quietest passage]

```
There's a volume you can only say the big things at,
And it's lower than a room, it's lower than that.
So I say it to the air beside your jaw,
The one I've never said to anybody before.
You don't answer with a speech, you just breathe out slow,
And you say it back to me at the same level, low.
```

- Shots: Extreme close-up: her mouth beside his jaw, the words not audible even in the mix. His eyes closing. His chest rising and falling once, slowly. His mouth at her ear, answering. Neither face fully in frame at any point in this section — this is the only passage in the video shot entirely in fragments.
- Emotion and lighting: The heart of the film; a single candle, most of the frame black.

### [final chorus – strings and trumpet, still soft]

```
Wine and whispers, keep the lights down low,
Tell me something true, then something slow.
The street can have the shouting, the street can have the rain,
We said the whole thing quiet and it came out plain.

Wine and whispers, the hours going soft,
The candle's on its last and we're not moving off.
Push the table back and let the small hours go,
Wine and whispers, keep the lights down low.
```

- Shots: The two of them standing up for the first time in the film. A coffee table being pushed aside with a foot. A rug rolled back with two hands. The last candle down to a puddle with a flame still on it. Both of them standing in the cleared space, not dancing yet.
- Emotion and lighting: Resolution; the dimmest and warmest the room gets, the window still cool behind them.

### [post-chorus]

```
Low, low, keep the lights down low,
Nothing in this room has anywhere to go.
Low, low, keep the lights down low,
Wine and whispers, and the rest of it slow.
```

- Shots: Four repeating beats: the candle, the window, two feet on bare boards, a hand at a back. Same four, same order, on each repetition.
- Emotion and lighting: Restful; unchanged.

### [outro – bass, brushes and a wordless vocal]

```
Sofa pushed back and the rug rolled aside,
Your hand at my back and the last of the wine.
No music left but we're still going round,
And the loudest thing in here is not a sound.
```

- Shots: The two of them dancing very slowly in the cleared space with nothing playing on screen. A wide from the doorway, the same framing as the first shot of the film. His hand flat on her back. The last candle going out on its own, the room dropping to window light. Final shot: the two of them still turning in the near-dark, barely moving.
- Emotion and lighting: Complete; the film ends darker than it began, which is the point.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the overhead of two people in a pool of candlelight on a dark floor on *"keep the lights down low."*
- **The challenge:** the whispered-confession cut — post the bridge and invite people to film the thing they would only say at that volume, mouth to camera, no audio.
- **The caption line:** *"There's a volume you can only say the big things at."*
- **The quote:** *"The only thing worth having is the thing you're scared to hear."*
- **The still:** the last candle going out with two people still turning behind it.
