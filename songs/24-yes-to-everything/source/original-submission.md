# Yes to Everything — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A first date with a woman who plans everything and a man who suggests everything. She tells herself one drink and home by ten. He asks if she's ever been up a ferris wheel at night, and she hears herself say yes. Then karaoke, then the fair as it's closing, then the top of the wheel, then pancakes at 3 a.m., then sunrise on the car park — and, in the end, yes to the second date. The realisation in the bridge: she wasn't saying yes to the wheel, the song or the syrup. She was saying yes to him.

**Mood:**
Euphoric, funny, breathless, a night that keeps opening another door. Big-room dance-pop with a heart.

**Message:**
"Tonight I'm saying yes to everything."

**Genre:**
Dance-pop / festival pop with a drop chorus and a male pop-rap feature verse. Think: Dua Lipa "Levitating" bounce x Calvin Harris drop-chorus euphoria x a Jack Harlow-style playful feature, with the catalogue's cinematic pop-R&B polish.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 124 BPM, 4/4
- Key: B major (bright, hands-up, no modulation needed)
- The final chorus lifts by arrangement — highest supersaw voicing, both voices, the second date written into the hook.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, quick and conversational in the verses, near-spoken and tender in the bridge, belted and joyful in the choruses.
- Feature: male pop-rapper (Singer B) on the third verse — bright, melodic, playful, US pop-rap cadence; harmonies and "yes" ad-libs under the final chorus; the spoken question in the outro.
- Backing vocals: stacked female harmonies on every "yes"; a pitched vocal chop of the word "yes" in the post-choruses; male and female harmonies on the final hook.

**Instrumentation:**
- Sidechained supersaw chords (filtered pad in the verses, full on the drops)
- Rubbery synth bass and a deep sub-bass
- Four-on-the-floor kick, crisp clap, tight hi-hats
- Bright synth plucks doubling the chorus melody
- Rising synth arpeggio in the pre-choruses
- Pitched "yes" vocal chop (post-choruses, instrumental)
- Brass-style synth stab on the downbeats (second and final choruses)
- Warm pad and soft piano (bridge, outro)
- Textures: distant fairground organ, a car door, a cab meter tick, a karaoke crowd cheer, a coin-slot clunk, one ferris wheel bell

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: seven o'clock, one drink, home by ten — and the first yes slips out
- Verse 1: the planner meets the suggester; the menu dare, the cab, the karaoke bar
- Pre-chorus: "I'm the one who says maybe" — and she's tired of maybe
- Chorus: the drop — yes to the wheel, the mic, the worst idea you've got
- Post-chorus: the yes chant
- Verse 2: karaoke, the run for the gate, the top of the wheel, the 3 a.m. diner
- Rap verse: his list of what's next, and the ask for the second date
- Bridge: sunrise on the lot — every yes was a yes to him
- Final chorus: yes to the second date, yes to the third
- Outro: ten o'clock came and went; "what do you think — yes, yes, yes"

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – filtered pad, soft clap, no kick]

```
Seven o'clock, I told my girls I'd be home by ten,
Told myself, one drink, then leave.
You said, "You ever been up a ferris wheel at night?"
And I heard my own mouth go, "Yeah, why not, let's see."
```

- Scene: A bar patio at 7 p.m., early evening light, a fairground's ferris wheel visible over the rooftops a few blocks away, not yet lit.
- Shot: Close on her phone in her lap under the table — a group chat with her girls (composited), her thumb typing "home by 10". Then a medium two-shot: her sitting very upright with one drink, him leaning back, easy.
- Cut to: The ferris wheel's lights switching on in the distance behind his shoulder as he asks. Her face: a beat of "absolutely not", then her mouth saying yes before her brain does.
- Emotion: Polite, contained, and then the first crack in the plan.
- Lighting: Golden-to-blue dusk, warm patio bulbs.

### [verse 1]

```

```

- Scene: The restaurant table. Her phone face-up with a ride app open (composited), a countdown she keeps glancing at. He orders something with a name she can't pronounce; a small plate arrives; he slides it across.
- Shot: Close-up on her fork hovering, his raised eyebrow, then her eating it and refusing to show a reaction.
- Cut to: Her thumb cancelling the ride on her phone (composited) without looking at the screen.
- Emotion: Competitive, amused, the plan bending.
- Lighting: Warm restaurant tungsten, candle on the table.

```
Now there's a cab outside and the meter's running slow,
And you said, "There's a bar with a mic and a bad crowd."
Every sensible cell in my body said no,
But the word that came out of me was pretty loud.
```

- Scene: A yellow cab at the kerb, the meter ticking (composited digits as a soft glow). He's half in the door, holding it, asking. She's on the pavement with her arms crossed.
- Shot: A slow push-in on her face as every "no" she's ever said lines up behind her eyes.
- Cut to: Her saying "yes" so loudly the cab driver looks round. She gets in.
- Emotion: The first big yes, surprising herself.
- Lighting: Street sodium, the cab's dome light.

- Shot: Her talking to the window, listing her own rules on her fingers; him grinning in the reflection.
- Cut to: The cab pulling up outside a karaoke bar with a buzzing sign (abstract glow, no text). Her hand on the door handle. The kick drops out. She pushes the door open on "here we go".
- Emotion: Self-aware, then decided.
- Lighting: Passing neon, then the bar's pink and green spill.

- Scene: A fast montage of the night's yeses cut on the beat: the karaoke stage, the ferris wheel lights, a spinning ride, a mic in her hand, a bad idea being pointed at.
- Shot: Her on the karaoke stage in the bad crowd with the mic, jacket off, hair down for the first time, the whole room bouncing. Every "yes" lands on a jump cut to a new place.
- Cut to: "No to the plan, no to the clock, no to the ten" — three inserts: her list crumpled, a wall clock (face composited), her phone's 10 p.m. alarm dismissed (composited).
- Emotion: Euphoria. The plan is gone and she's glad.
- Lighting: Strobing bar light, pink and green, the wheel's white bulbs.

- Emotion: The turn: quiet, certain, a little scared, then not.
- Lighting: Pale gold sunrise, long shadows, the wheel a dark silhouette.
