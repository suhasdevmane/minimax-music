# Eyes on Me — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A crowded house party. She wasn't even going to come. Then, from the staircase, she feels somebody looking, and she knows exactly who. Instead of walking over and making it easy, she decides to make him keep looking: the long way round the room, a laugh at nothing, a window seat where he'll find her when his nerve kicks in. The bridge turns it: she's been watching him watching her since she walked in. The eyes on him were hers the whole time.

**Mood:**
Slow, confident, playful, a little smug. Flirtation as a game she's already won. PG-13, never explicit; the whole song is a glance.

**Message:**
"You're already looking. I'm just going to make it worth it."

**Genre:**
Modern pop-R&B with a late-night slow groove. Think: SZA x Doja Cat's softer side x Victoria Monét, with a half-rapped second verse in a US R&B cadence, the kind of talk-sing that reads as a smirk. Finger snaps, sub-bass, Rhodes.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 94 BPM, 4/4
- Key: B♭ minor (dark, sultry, room for the sub)
- No modulation; the final chorus lifts through harmonies and an octave on the hook.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, close and amused in the verses, full and seductive in the choruses; the second verse is half-rapped, rhythmic and dry, melody returning on its last two lines; the bridge is near-spoken.
- Backing vocals: stacked harmonies in the choruses; a call-and-answer in the post-chorus; wordless echoes in the outro.

**Instrumentation:**
- Rhodes electric piano (the spine; filtered in the intro, full in the verses, stabs under the rap verse, alone in the bridge)
- Deep sliding sub-bass and clean 808s
- Finger snaps on two and four (throughout)
- Muted guitar chop (verses)
- Rising synth pad and a bright synth top line (pre-choruses, choruses)
- Ticking hi-hat, clap layered on the snaps, tambourine shaker
- Saxophone-like synth lead (instrumental)
- Ambient textures: party chatter, glass clink, a muffled kick through a wall, a door closing

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: the staircase, the feeling of being looked at
- Verse 1: watching him pretend not to watch
- Pre-chorus: the long way round the room
- Chorus: the glance held for a whole chorus
- Verse 2 (half-rapped): reading him line by line: the fake phone check, the frozen laugh
- Bridge: the twist: she was looking first
- Final chorus: standing next to him, calling it even
- Outro: half past two, his jacket on her shoulders

```

Red cup, red dress, I wasn't even gonna come.
Then I felt it from the staircase like a hand across the room,
Somebody's looking, and I know exactly who.
```

- Scene: A house party, mid-flow. A hallway, a kitchen full of people, a cousin on door duty.
- Shot: Her coming in the front door in a red dress, everyone else in black and denim, a red cup pressed into her hand.
- Cut to: Her halfway up the staircase, looking down over the whole room, and then the feeling: her head turns slowly, in slow motion, to the speaker corner.
- Her reaction: The smallest lift of one eyebrow. She knows.
- Lighting: Warm lamp and string lights, a bit of coloured LED from the living room; the staircase in shadow.
