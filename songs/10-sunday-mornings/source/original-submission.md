# Sunday Mornings — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A whole relationship measured in Sunday mornings. The first one is awkward: she wakes before him in a bed that isn't hers and doesn't move. By the sixth he knows her coffee order. By the twentieth there's frost on the balcony rail. By the thirtieth she's making him soup. By the fiftieth there's a cat neither of them planned, a plant with a name, and pancakes shaped like nothing. Then one Sunday she does the math on every Monday she'd ever have to leave and realises she doesn't want a single day without him, not just the Sundays.

**Mood:**
Warm, sunlit, lazy, in love. The good kind of ordinary. A song that feels like sheets that smell of someone.

**Message:**
"I don't need the big days. Give me the small ones with you in them. All of them."

**Genre:**
Indie-pop with a sunlit bedroom-pop softness. Think: Clairo x Maggie Rogers x early Vampire Weekend jangle, with a Phoebe Bridgers-style spoken bridge. Jangly guitar, soft claps, mellotron.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 100 BPM, 4/4
- Key: D major (bright, open, guitar-friendly)
- No key change; the final chorus lifts through harmonies and an octave doubling of the hook rather than a modulation.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, sleepy and close in the intro and verses, open and joyful in the choruses, near-spoken in the bridge.
- Backing vocals: stacked harmonies in the choruses; wordless echoes in the post-chorus and outro.

**Instrumentation:**
- Jangly electric guitar (the spine; picked intro figure, strummed verses, double-tracked choruses)
- Warm round bass guitar
- Mellotron (flute pad in verses, strings in choruses and the instrumental)
- Soft kick, shaker, real snare from the first chorus, tambourine from the second verse
- Soft handclaps (pre-chorus, choruses, instrumental)
- Low upright-style piano (second verse, bridge)
- Second chiming guitar counter-line (choruses)
- Ambient textures: room tone, distant birdsong, a mug set down on wood

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: the first morning, not moving, ceiling cracks
- Verses: Sundays one, six, twenty, thirty, fifty; the relationship growing in small objects
- Pre-chorus: the week's demands stacking up, Sunday as the day that asks nothing
- Chorus: big, warm, singable; the small-things list
- Bridge: the morning she does the math and wants every day
- Final chorus: same hook, now asking for Mondays and Wednesdays too
- Outro: back to the first morning, reversed: now she's the one still sleeping

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – hushed, one guitar and mellotron, half-awake]

```
The first one, I woke up before you and I didn't move,
Counting the cracks in a ceiling that wasn't mine.
You mumbled something about coffee in your sleep,
And I thought, "okay, I could get used to this in time."
```

- Scene: A sunlit bedroom, early, white sheets, a window with bare early-spring branches outside.
- Shot: Close on her face on the pillow, eyes open, very still; his arm heavy across her.
- Cut to: Her POV of the ceiling: a hairline crack, then another; the camera slowly tilts to the window.
- Her reaction: A tiny smile she tries not to let happen when he mumbles.
- Lighting: Pale morning light, soft, slightly cool; the first Sunday is the least golden of the video.

### [verse 1]

```
Sunday number one, we were shy about the light,
You kept the curtains closed like the morning might stare.
I wore your flannel to my knees and I burned the eggs,
You ate them anyway and swore you didn't care.
```

- Scene: Same bedroom, curtains half-drawn; then a small kitchen off the bedroom.
- Shot: Him pulling the curtain across, sheepish. Her in his flannel shirt to her knees, at the stove, smoke rising off a pan.
- Cut to: Him eating burnt eggs with a straight face, then breaking into a laugh.
- Emotion: Shy, funny, the beginning of ease.
- Lighting: Curtain-filtered light, warm stripes.

```
Sunday number six, you already knew my order,
Two sugars and a splash, and you didn't have to ask.
I stood in the doorway while you poured out two,
Thinking, "I could stand here all my life."
```

- Scene: Six weeks later. The window now shows full green leaves.
- Shot: Over his shoulder: two mugs, two sugars, a splash of milk, done without looking.
- Cut to: Her in the doorway, leaning on the frame, watching him, the light behind her.
- Emotion: The quiet moment of realising she's known.
- Lighting: Full late-spring sun, the room brighter than the first Sunday.

### [pre-chorus]

```
Monday's got alarms, and Tuesday's got the train,
Wednesday wants an answer, Thursday wants my name.
But Sunday doesn't ask me to be anyone,
Just barefoot on your balcony, that's all.
```

- Scene: Quick weekday inserts, then the balcony.
- Shot: An alarm clock slapped, a train window, an email inbox (composited), a form with a signature line (composited, illegible). Then: her bare feet on a warm balcony floor.
- Cut to: Her leaning on the balcony rail in the flannel, coffee in hand, eyes closed.
- Emotion: The week as noise, Sunday as silence.
- Lighting: Weekday inserts cool and flat; the balcony warm and gold.

### [chorus – big, warm, singable]

```
Give me Sunday mornings, messy hair and coffee rings,
Your voice before you're really awake, a window open, small things.
Give me Sunday mornings, no alarm, no place to be,
Sun across the pillow and your arm across me.
Give me every Sunday, and the Sundays after that,
Give me Sunday mornings, I don't need more than that.
```

- Scene: The bedroom and balcony, full sun, a montage of small things.
- Shots: Her messy hair on the pillow from above; a coffee ring on the nightstand; his mouth half in the pillow saying something; the window pushed open; sun in a stripe across the sheets; his arm across her waist.
- Emotion: Joy without drama; the whole point of the song.
- Lighting: The warmest light so far, slight lens flare from the window.

### [verse 2]

```
Sunday number twenty, there was frost along the rail,
Two mugs, one blanket, and the paper split in two.
Sunday number thirty, you were sick, I made the soup,
You said it tasted like a home, and I said, "that's you."
```

- Scene: The same balcony in winter, then the bedroom with him under the duvet, ill.
- Shot: Frost on the balcony rail; the two of them under one blanket on the balcony chairs, each holding half a newspaper.
- Cut to: Her carrying a bowl of soup to the bed; him pale, wrapped up, taking it and saying something that makes her laugh.
- Emotion: Care; the relationship now has weather in it.
- Lighting: Winter light, white and low; the bedroom warm from a lamp.

```
Sunday number fifty, there's a cat we didn't plan,
Pancakes shaped like nothing either one of us can name.
There's a plant on the sill now, and the plant's got a name,
And the girl who counted ceiling cracks has stopped counting anything.
```

- Scene: A year in. The window shows full summer again. A cat on the bed.
- Shot: A grey cat walking across the duvet and sitting on his chest. A pan of misshapen pancakes; both of them squinting at one, arguing what it is.
- Cut to: A pothos on the windowsill; her lying back on the pillow looking up at the ceiling and not counting, eyes soft.
- Emotion: Home.
- Lighting: Summer sun, the fullest gold of the verses.

### [pre-chorus]

```
Monday's got alarms, and Tuesday's got the train,
Wednesday wants an answer, Thursday wants my name.
But Sunday doesn't ask me to be anyone,
Just barefoot on your balcony, that's all.
```

- Scene: The weekday inserts again, faster, then the balcony in summer.
- Shot: The same four inserts cut tighter. Her bare feet on the balcony, the cat winding around her ankles.
- Emotion: The same relief, more earned.
- Lighting: Inserts cool; balcony gold.

### [chorus]

```
Give me Sunday mornings, messy hair and coffee rings,
Your voice before you're really awake, a window open, small things.
Give me Sunday mornings, no alarm, no place to be,
Sun across the pillow and your arm across me.
Give me every Sunday, and the Sundays after that,
Give me Sunday mornings, I don't need more than that.
```

- Scene: The small-things montage again, now with a year's worth of Sundays flickering through the window.
- Shots: The window shown through four seasons in one push-in; his sleepy face; the coffee rings now several on the nightstand; the cat asleep between them.
- Emotion: Bigger, fuller; the harmonies stack.
- Lighting: Warm, gold, the window the brightest thing in frame.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: A silent Sunday, the guitar hook and mellotron over it.
- Shots: Her dancing in socks in the kitchen with a spatula while he watches from the doorway; the two of them on the balcony feet up; the cat chasing a sunbeam; a slow push-in to the window as the leaves outside change colour in a single continuous shot; a drop to her alone on the bed in the leaf-dappled light, him asleep beside her.
- Lighting: Full gold, then the dappled green of the bridge.

### [bridge – near-spoken, then building]

```
Then one Sunday you were sleeping, and the light came through the leaves,
And I did the math on all the Mondays I would ever have to leave.
And I don't want a single one without you in it,
Not a Tuesday, not a Thursday, not a minute.
```

- Scene: The bedroom, dappled light through leaves on the sheets, him asleep.
- Shot: Her propped on one elbow watching him sleep, the light moving on his face.
- Cut to: Her looking at the window, at the door, at her keys on the nightstand, doing the math.
- Emotion: The realisation, quiet and enormous.
- Lighting: Dappled, moving, green-gold.

```
I used to think that Sunday was the day I got to rest,
Turns out Sunday's just the day I get to see you best.
So I woke you up to tell you, and you said, "go back to sleep,"
Then you pulled me in and said, "I know, I've known for weeks."
```

- Scene: Same bed.
- Shot: Her shaking his shoulder; him not opening his eyes, mumbling; then his arm pulling her in, and his eyes opening just enough to smile.
- Emotion: The turn: he already knew.
- Lighting: The dappled light steadying into full sun as the band comes back.

### [final chorus – full width, octave on the hook]

```
Give me Sunday mornings, messy hair and coffee rings,
Give me Monday mornings too, the alarms and everything.
Give me Sunday mornings, and the Wednesdays in between,
Sun across the pillow and your arm across me.
Give me every Sunday, and the Sundays after that,
Give me all the mornings, I don't need more than that.
```

- Scene: Now the weekdays get the Sunday treatment.
- Shots: A Monday alarm going off and both of them laughing under the duvet instead of getting up; a Wednesday coffee on the balcony in work clothes; the window through all four seasons again; his arm across her on a weekday with a shirt and tie hanging on the door.
- Emotion: The whole week is theirs now.
- Lighting: Warmest and widest of the song.

### [post-chorus]

```
Messy hair, coffee rings,
Cat on the bed, the window open, small things.
Messy hair, coffee rings,
Fifty Sundays down, and I'm not counting anything.
```

- Scene: The small-things list as a fast, loving inventory.
- Shots: Hair on the pillow; the coffee rings; the cat; the open window; her face, not counting.
- Emotion: Contentment.
- Lighting: Gold.

### [outro – guitar and mellotron, soft]

```
Sunday number one, I didn't move, I didn't dare,
Sunday number fifty, I'm the one still sleeping there.
So if you wake before me, don't move, there's nowhere to be,
It's Sunday, it's Sunday, and you get to look at me.
```

- Scene: The opening shot reversed. He's awake first; she's asleep.
- Shot: His face on the pillow, awake, very still, watching her sleep. His arm across her. The window: spring again, bare branches with the first buds.
- Final shot: The ceiling crack, and the camera tilting to the window, birdsong, a held chord.
- Emotion: Full circle.
- Lighting: Pale spring morning, like the intro, but a degree warmer.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** her messy hair on the pillow from above with a coffee ring in the corner of the frame, *"messy hair and coffee rings"* on screen.
- **The challenge:** "Sunday number ___": couples post their own Sunday count with one small thing in frame (the mug, the cat, the plant).
- **The caption line:** *"Give me Sunday mornings, I don't need more than that."*
- **The quote:** *"Turns out Sunday's just the day I get to see you best."*
- **The window:** the single push-in where the leaves change season in one continuous shot is the shareable visual.
- **The turn:** *"I know, I've known for weeks."*
