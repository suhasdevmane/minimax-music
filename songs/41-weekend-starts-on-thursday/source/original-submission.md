# Weekend Starts on Thursday — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
Quitting the week early. It's Thursday, four fifty-nine, and one text from him turns a grey office into the start of the weekend. Friends, the first drink on a rooftop, a taxi built for four holding six, and the realisation that the weekend was never about the day of the week, it was about who she's with.

**Mood:**
Giddy, funny, glittering. The feeling of a laptop lid closing. Leaving-work-early energy, disco lights on a rooftop, a countdown chant you can shout in a lift.

**Message:**
"The calendar doesn't decide when the weekend starts. You do. And he does."

**Genre:**
Dance-pop / nu-disco with a countdown chant. Think: Dua Lipa x Doja Cat "Say So" x a little Jessie Ware glitter, with a Lizzo-sized grin on the chorus.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 124 BPM, 4/4
- Key: G major (bright, open, made for claps)
- Final chorus lifts a whole tone to A major for the last dance on the rooftop.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, bored and dry in the office intro, brightening through the verse, belting the chorus with a grin, playful and honest in the bridge.
- Backing vocals: stacked harmonies in choruses; gang-style shouted countdown in the post-chorus; hum and wordless echoes in the outro.

**Instrumentation:**
- Live-feeling disco bass (octaves, walking lines) as the engine
- Four-on-the-floor kick and layered handclaps
- Dry funk rhythm guitar; wah guitar in verse 2
- Disco strings (stabs in choruses, riff in the break)
- Brass hits on the chorus hooks
- Bright analog synth lead and talkbox-style synth (break, final chorus)
- Warm pad and electric piano for the bridge
- Textures: ticking clock, laptop-shut click, keyboard hum, elevator ding, taxi doors, distant traffic, a crowd cheer in the break

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: grey office, clock stuck, then the first laptop shuts
- Verse 1: the week as a comedy of small miseries, then his one-word text
- Pre-chorus: the elevator ride down, everybody in, the countdown
- Chorus: rooftop, glasses up, the weekend starts now
- Post-chorus: the countdown chant, five to one
- Verse 2: the rooftop bar, the wrong lyrics, the tie headband, six in a taxi
- Bridge: the alarm and the meeting exist, but it was never about the day, it's him
- Final chorus: lifted key, last dance at dawn
- Outro: Monday, sunglasses on, humming in the elevator, counting down to Thursday

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – dry, close, over a ticking clock and a filtered bass loop]

```
4:59 and the clock is stuck,
Fluorescent lights and I'm out of luck.
Then a laptop shuts across the room,
And the whole floor starts to hum.
```

- Scene: An open-plan office, grey carpet, grey light, the wall clock frozen at one minute to five.
- Shot: Close-up of her face lit by a monitor, chin on her hand, eyes on the clock. The clock's second hand seems to hesitate.
- Cut to: Across the room, one laptop lid snaps shut. Heads lift like meerkats.
- Her reaction: The tiniest smile, eyebrows up.
- Lighting: Flat fluorescent white, slightly green; the monitor glow on her face.

```
Somebody's phone lights up with a plan,
Somebody's already got a drink in hand.
Printer's jammed and I don't even care,
I can hear the weekend on the stairs.
```

- Scene: The office waking up. A phone buzzes on a desk, a colleague pulls a can from a mini fridge, the printer flashes red.
- Shot: Quick cuts on the claps: phone, can, printer light, then a wide of the floor as people start standing.
- Cut to: A stairwell door swinging open, laughter echoing up.
- Emotion: Contagious anticipation.
- Lighting: Still fluorescent, but a warmer slice of late-afternoon sun creeps across the carpet from a window.

### [verse 1]

```
Monday was a marathon, Tuesday was a blur,
Wednesday I was talking to a plant that didn't care.
Coffee number seven going cold beside my screen,
Spreadsheet in my eyeballs, I forgot what colours mean.
```

- Scene: A comedy flashback of the week, one shot per day.
- Shots: Monday: her jogging between meeting rooms with a stack of folders. Tuesday: a time-lapse of her at the desk, the light moving across the wall. Wednesday: her talking earnestly to a small desk plant that leans away. Seven paper cups in a row beside a monitor full of cells.
- Cut to: Her face reflected in the monitor, a spreadsheet grid ghosted over her eyes.
- Emotion: Deadpan, self-aware, funny.
- Lighting: Each day a little flatter and greyer than the last.

```
Then a text from you, one word, "out?", no question mark,
And my heart does a drum roll like the party's about to start.
Heels under the desk, I'm swapping out my flats,
Grab my jacket, grab the girls, and we are not coming back.
```

- Scene: The phone on the desk lights up (screen composited in the edit). One word.
- Shot: Extreme close-up of the phone, then her face changing, then under the desk: flats off, heels on, in one fluid movement.
- Cut to: Her standing, jacket swinging over her shoulder, pointing at two friends across the floor who are already standing.
- Emotion: A switch flipped. The whole song changes colour here.
- Lighting: The sun slice reaches her desk exactly on the text.

### [pre-chorus]

```
Lock the screen, kill the lights, let the inbox wait,
Everybody grab a hand, we are not gonna be late.
Elevator going down, counting every floor,
Ten, nine, eight, seven, we're already out the door.
```

- Scene: The exodus. Screens locking one by one, a hand hitting the light switch, the floor going dim behind them.
- Shot: A chain of hands pulling each other into a packed elevator. The floor numbers ticking down (composited).
- Cut to: Inside the lift, everyone shouting the countdown at the numbers, faces squashed together.
- Emotion: Rising, ridiculous, joyful.
- Lighting: Elevator light warm and gold compared to the office; the doors open onto orange evening.

### [chorus – big, glittering, singable]

```
Weekend starts on Thursday when I'm with you,
Friday is a rumour, Saturday can wait too.
First drink on the rooftop, sunset bleeding through,
Weekend starts on Thursday when I'm with you.

Tell the boss I'm sorry, tell the week we're through,
Clocking out my worries, got better things to do.
Glasses up, glasses up, one more for the crew,
Weekend starts on Thursday when I'm with you.
```

- Scene: The rooftop bar at sunset, the city going orange and pink behind them.
- Shots: The lift doors opening straight onto the rooftop (a cheat cut). Him at the bar with two drinks, turning as she arrives. The first sip with the sunset behind. A slow-motion wide of the whole crew raising glasses on "glasses up." Her singing the hook straight to him, close.
- Emotion: Release, the grin she was holding in all day.
- Lighting: Golden hour into magenta; fairy lights along the bar switching on during the chorus.

### [post-chorus – countdown chant]

```
Five, four, three, two, one,
Thursday night, here we come.
Five, four, three, two, one,
The week is done, the week is done.
```

- Scene: The crew counting down on their fingers, on the beat, straight at camera.
- Shots: Five hands, then four, three, two, one finger, cut on each number. A glass slammed down on "done."
- Emotion: The shoutable bit.
- Lighting: Magenta sky, string lights, lens flare.

### [verse 2]

```
Rooftop bar and the speakers play a song from when we were seventeen,
You know every word, you sing it wrong, it's the best thing I have ever seen.
Somebody ordered the sharing plate, nobody's sharing, it's a war,
Your tie is now a headband and I'm not sure what the tie was for.
```

- Shots: Him singing at the top of his lungs with the wrong words, her filming him, laughing. A sharing plate being fought over with forks like fencing. His tie now tied around his forehead.
- Emotion: Tipsy, funny, in love with the whole night.
- Lighting: Blue hour, fairy lights, the bar's neon sign spilling pink.

```
Taxi waiting with the meter on, six of us in a car for four,

Neon on your cheekbones, my mascara's holding on for dear life,
This is the night we'll be quoting back every time that Monday picks a fight.
```

- Scene: The street outside. A taxi at the kerb, the meter running, six people trying to fit.
- Shots: The driver's face in the rear-view mirror, deadpan, then a small laugh. Her on his lap in the back, the city neon sliding across his face. Close-up of her eye, mascara smudged, still laughing.
- Emotion: Chaos, warmth.
- Lighting: Street neon, passing headlights, the taxi's interior light.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: The night in motion, no lyrics. A string riff and a talkbox synth.
- Shots: The taxi from above driving through a lit grid of streets. Her heels in her hand on a pavement. A late food stop, chips shared out of one paper bag. The crew walking in a line down the middle of an empty street. A clap breakdown: the crew clapping on a rooftop, then a filter sweep into the bridge.

### [bridge – honest, playful, then building]

```
There's an alarm set for seven, there's a meeting I forgot,
There's a version of tomorrow where I'm paying for this a lot.
But it was never about the calendar, never about the day,
Every hour feels like the weekend when you're looking at me that way.
```

- Scene: The night rooftop, almost empty now, the two of them on the edge with the city going quiet.
- Shots: Her phone showing an alarm set for seven (composited), her turning it face-down with a shrug. Him looking at her. A long two-shot.
- Emotion: The turn. The song stops being about Thursday and becomes about him.
- Lighting: Pre-dawn blue, one string of lights still on.

```
So I'll take the Monday headache, I'll take the Tuesday blues,
As long as every Thursday is a Thursday spent with you.
The week can have my mornings, it can have my nine to five,
But the nights belong to us and that's the part where I'm alive.
```

- Scene: The sky brightening behind them as the arrangement builds.
- Shots: Her head on his shoulder, then both standing as the claps come back. The crew reappearing one by one behind them with the last drinks.
- Emotion: Sure of it.
- Lighting: The first orange line on the horizon.

```

```

- Scene: Monday morning, the office elevator, the same faces from the lift, all in sunglasses.
- Shots: Her in the elevator with sunglasses and a coffee, humming, the floor numbers going up. A glance at her phone: a text from him (composited, one word). The doors opening onto the grey office, and her walking in with a smile the office doesn't deserve.
- Emotion: Contentment, a secret.
- Lighting: Elevator warm, office grey, her smile the brightest thing in the frame.
