# Falling Slowly, Landing Soft — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A woman who was dropped badly by her last love is terrified of falling again. She keeps her coat on, keeps a hand on the door, keeps a hundred exits mapped in her head. The new man never pushes. He waits, sits a little further off, learns her silences, leaves the porch light on. By the time she looks down, she has already fallen — and he has caught her so gently she never felt the ground.

**Mood:**
Tender, guarded-then-open, slow-motion romance. Soft light, soft landings. A love song for the ones who flinch.

**Message:**
"I was falling slowly, and you made me land soft."

**Genre:**
Piano ballad / cinematic pop with strings and minimal drums. Think: Adele "Make You Feel My Love" restraint x Lewis Capaldi "Someone You Loved" piano weight x Birdy softness, with the modern pop-R&B polish of the catalogue.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 80 BPM, 4/4
- Key: E♭ major (warm, resolved, no lift needed — the whole song is a landing)
- The final chorus widens with full strings and layered harmonies rather than modulating; the lift is dynamic, not harmonic.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, near-spoken and breathy in the verses and bridge, open and warm in the choruses, never harsh.
- Backing vocals: stacked female harmonies in the choruses; male and female harmonies on the final hook; wordless echoes in the post-chorus and outro.

**Instrumentation:**
- Piano (the spine, first bar to last)
- Cello doubling the piano bass; warm rounded sub-bass
- String section: violins, viola, cello (pad in the intro, full in the choruses, widest in the final chorus)
- Harp arpeggios (second chorus, post-chorus)
- Minimal drums: brushed kick, brushed snare, soft toms, finger snaps, light shaker
- Textures: soft room air, faint tape hiss, reverse piano swells, string risers

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: guarded, coat on, hand on the door
- Verse 1: the old fall, the flinch, the man who waits
- Pre-chorus: the hundred exits, then looking down
- Chorus: the realisation, tender and wide — she already fell, and it didn't hurt
- Verse 2: the small kindnesses, the porch light, the habit of checking exits fading
- Bridge: the turn — she is not scared of the height anymore
- Final chorus: fullest arrangement, still soft at the centre
- Post-chorus / outro: land soft, coat off, don't run, don't shout, just be there

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – hushed, piano and a faint string pad]

```
I kept my coat on for the first three dates,
Hand on the door in case I had to leave.
I said, "I don't do the falling anymore,"
You said, "That's fine, I'll wait while you breathe."
```

- Scene: A quiet café at dusk, pale pastel walls. She sits opposite him with her coat still buttoned, her hand resting on the strap of her bag, one eye on the door.
- Shot: Medium two-shot from the side, her side lit, his face partly turned away and unhurried.
- Cut to: Close-up on her hand on the door handle as they leave, then his hand stopping a polite distance from hers, not touching.
- Emotion: Guarded, braced, a little ashamed of being braced.
- Lighting: Soft grey window light, muted pastels, no hard shadows.

### [verse 1]

```
The last one dropped me from a height I can't name,
I hit so hard I learned to walk on glass.
When you reached for me, I flinched before you touched,
You smiled and said, "We've got time, there's no rush."
```

- Scene: A memory in desaturated grey — a faceless man's back walking away down a hallway, a door left open. Then present day, a park path, he reaches to brush a leaf off her shoulder and she flinches.
- Shot: The flinch in slow motion, extreme close-up on her shoulder and his hand stopping in the air.
- Cut to: His face, no offence taken, a small smile, hands going back into his pockets.
- Emotion: Old fear surfacing; his patience meeting it.
- Lighting: Memory cold and flat; present soft overcast daylight.

```
You never asked why my back's against the wall,
You brought me tea and sat a little further off.
You learned the way I go quiet when I'm scared,
You got quiet too, till the quiet wasn't hard.
```

- Scene: Her flat, evening. She sits on the floor with her back literally against the wall. He sets a mug of tea beside her and sits a metre away, also on the floor.
- Shot: Wide static shot, the two of them and the gap between them, the tea steaming.
- Cut to: A slow dissolve where the gap shrinks a little, then a little more, across several evenings (same framing, different clothes).
- Emotion: Being learned rather than pushed.
- Lighting: One warm lamp, blush and cream tones.

### [pre-chorus]

```
I built a hundred exits, drew them in my head,
Every door had a plan, every plan had a name.
Then I looked down one morning, feet not touching ground,
And I realised I'd been falling all along.
```

- Scene: Her point of view scanning a room — doors, windows, the stairwell — each one lingering for a beat. Then a morning: she is standing in the kitchen, looks down, and the floor is not there; she is a foot above it, weightless.
- Shot: A slow tilt down her body to her bare feet hanging above the tiles.
- Cut to: Her face, startled, then the beginning of a laugh.
- Emotion: The plans dissolving; the discovery.
- Lighting: Morning pastel light through thin curtains, the first warmth of the video.

### [chorus – wide, tender, singable]

```
I was falling slowly, you made me land soft,
Feather on a pillow, coat I finally took off.
Braced for the crash, braced for the drop,
I was falling slowly, you made me land soft.
No sirens, no shatter, no sorry on the floor,
Just your hand in my hair, your voice saying, "more."
I was falling slowly, but I never felt the ground,
You made me land soft, you caught me coming down.
```

- Scene: The hero image — a park lawn under a soft grey sky. She stands with her back to him, arms crossed over her chest, and lets herself fall backwards in slow motion. He catches her. Feathers drift down around them from nowhere.
- Shot: Side-on slow motion of the fall, wide; then an overhead of her lying in his arms on the grass, feathers settling on her coat.
- Cut to: Her taking her coat off for the first time in the video and letting it drop; his hand in her hair; her laughing.
- Emotion: Relief so complete it looks like joy.
- Lighting: Muted pastels, soft diffused daylight, the feathers catching the light.

### [verse 2]

```
You memorised my coffee, then you memorised my fears,
Don't take it personal when I need a night alone.
Porch light on like a question I can answer
Any hour I decide to come home.
```

- Scene: A takeaway coffee with her order written on the cup (no legible text — a scribble). A night where she stays at her own place; he texts something short and kind (composited). His porch light glowing at dusk, then at midnight, then at two in the morning.
- Shot: Three cuts of the same porch light at three hours, the sky changing behind it.
- Cut to: Her walking up the path at the third hour, the light still on, the door unlocked.
- Emotion: Safety that doesn't demand anything.
- Lighting: Warm porch light against blue dusk deepening to night.

```
I still check the exits, only out of habit,
Like reaching for a wall in a room full of light.
You don't try to fix me, you just stand closer,
And the closer you stand, the further I can fall.
```

- Scene: A bright room; she reaches out a hand for a wall that isn't there and laughs at herself. He stands beside her, does nothing, just stands there.
- Shot: Medium two-shot, the space between them a hand's width now.
- Cut to: A tiny trust fall in the living room — a foot of drop, his hands ready, both of them grinning.
- Emotion: Habits loosening, playfulness arriving.
- Lighting: Full soft daylight, cream and sage.

### [pre-chorus]

```
So I stopped counting doorways, stopped rehearsing goodbye,
Let the hundred exits blur into one open sky.
I looked down this morning, feet not touching ground,
And I laughed, cause I'd been falling all along.
```

- Scene: The same point-of-view scan of the room from the first pre-chorus, but now the camera never lingers on the doors; it drifts up to the window and the pale sky.
- Shot: Slow tilt up through the window to open sky, clouds moving.
- Cut to: Her feet above the tiles again, and this time she laughs out loud.
- Emotion: Freedom, amusement at her old self.
- Lighting: Bright pastel morning.

### [chorus]

(repeat)

- Scene: The trust fall again, but now in different places — the park, a beach at low tide, a rooftop at dusk. Every time she falls, he's there. Feathers every time.
- Shot: A series of slow-motion catches, each one from a new angle, cut on the hook.
- Cut to: Her coat left hanging on a park bench, forgotten.
- Emotion: Trust becoming reflex.
- Lighting: Muted pastels shifting with each location, always soft.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: Wordless montage in slow motion. A single feather falling the whole length of a stairwell. Her lying on the grass looking up while feathers drift down onto her face. His hands, open, waiting, palm up. The porch light at dawn, finally switched off because she's inside.
- Lighting: The softest light in the video; near-white pastels; a slow fade to a single lamp for the bridge.

### [bridge – bare piano, then building]

```
I used to think love meant bracing for the fall,
Arms crossed over my chest, waiting for the sound.
You never let me hit, you never let me break,
You kept your arms wide open till I came down.
```

- Scene: Night, his living room, one lamp. She stands in the middle of the room with her arms crossed tight over her chest, eyes closed, the old bracing pose. He stands behind her, arms wide open, waiting.
- Shot: Static wide, then a slow push-in on her closed eyes.
- Cut to: Her arms slowly uncrossing.
- Emotion: The last of the fear, spoken aloud for the first time.
- Lighting: Single warm lamp, soft shadows.

```
Here's the thing I never said to anyone before,
Not scared of the height, not scared of the drop.
If I'm falling for you, then I'm falling for good,
I know the way you catch, I'll land soft.
```

- Scene: She turns to face him. She says it to his face. She falls forward this time, not backward — eyes open — and he catches her.
- Shot: Close-up on her face saying the line, then a wide of the forward fall in slow motion.
- Cut to: Both of them on the floor, laughing, the lamp light on them.
- Emotion: Resolve, declaration, the turn of the song.
- Lighting: The lamp warming as the strings come in.

### [final chorus – fullest strings, layered harmonies, still soft]

```
I was falling slowly, you made me land soft,
Feather on a pillow, coat I finally took off.
No bracing for the crash, no bracing for the drop,
I was falling slowly, you made me land soft.
No sirens, no shatter, no sorry on the floor,
Just your hand in my hair, your voice saying, "more."
I was falling slowly, now I'm lying on the ground,
You made me land soft, and I'm staying where I'm found.
```

- Scene: The park lawn again, but now she runs at him and jumps; he catches her and they both go down onto the grass, a small avalanche of feathers. They lie there. She doesn't get up. The coat is nowhere.
- Shot: Wide slow motion of the jump and the fall, then a slow overhead pull-up of the two of them on the grass surrounded by feathers.
- Cut to: Their faces close, both grinning, her hair across his arm.
- Emotion: Total, easy trust.
- Lighting: The brightest pastel daylight of the video, soft haze.

### [post-chorus]

```
Land soft, land soft, you made me land soft,
Coat off at the door, both hands off the lock.
Land soft, land soft, you made me land soft,
Never felt the ground, only felt you catch.
```

- Scene: His front door. Her coat hung on the hook by the door, for good. Her hand leaving the lock without turning it. A feather on the doormat.
- Shot: Macro on the coat hook, the lock, the feather, cut on each "land soft."
- Cut to: Her walking away from the door into the flat without looking back at it.
- Emotion: Settled, home.
- Lighting: Warm evening, hallway lamp.

### [outro – piano and one held string]

```
If you see me falling, don't run, don't shout,
Don't bring a net, don't brace, don't work it out.
Just be exactly where you've been this whole time,
Cause I was falling slowly, and I landed soft.
```

- Scene: Dawn, the park, empty. She is lying on the grass alone, eyes closed, calm, a single feather on her chest. His shadow enters the frame and lies down next to her.
- Shot: Overhead, static, the two of them side by side on the grass.
- Cut to: Final frame — her hand finding his on the grass. Hold.
- Emotion: Peace, no fear left.
- Lighting: Pale dawn, the softest pastels, a slow fade.

- **The challenge:** "land soft" trust falls; partners film a backwards fall and the catch, feathers optional, with the hook line on screen.
- **The caption line:** *"I was falling slowly, you made me land soft."*
- **The quote:** *"You don't try to fix me, you just stand closer, and the closer you stand, the further I can fall."*
- **The turn:** *"If I'm falling for you, then I'm falling for good."*
- **The last frame:** her hand finding his on the grass at dawn, no text.
