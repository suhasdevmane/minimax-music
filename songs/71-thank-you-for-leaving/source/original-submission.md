# Thank You for Leaving — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A genuine, unbitter thank-you to the person who left. He walked away on a train platform one winter night and she thought that was the end of her. It was the beginning. The song is the letter she'd send if she still had his number: no spite, no score-settling, just the honest admission that his leaving is the reason she finally arrived at herself. The plant she bought half-dead the week he left is the whole story in one object.

**Mood:**
Warm, calm, grateful. A ballad that smiles instead of crying. Gentle in the verses, wide-open and thankful in the choruses.

**Message:**
"You thought you were the ending. You were only the ride. Thank you for leaving — I finally arrived."

**Genre:**
Piano pop ballad with cinematic strings. Think: Adele's warmth x Olivia Dean's easy conversational phrasing x the string lift of a Lewis Capaldi final chorus, but with the bitterness taken out and gratitude put in its place.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 84 BPM, 4/4
- Key: D♭ major (warm, rounded, forgiving)
- No key change; the final chorus lifts in dynamics, strings and harmonies rather than pitch.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, close and matter-of-fact in the verses, open and warm in the choruses, a smile audible in verse two.
- Backing vocals: stacked female harmonies in the choruses; male and female layers on the final hook; airy echoes on the post-chorus and outro.

**Instrumentation:**
- Felt piano (main spine, from the first bar to the last)
- Warm sub-bass / soft round 808
- Light acoustic guitar (from verse two)
- Cinematic strings: cello first, violins later
- Soft synth pads
- Drums: soft kick, brushed snare, light hats, gentle shaker, a full live-feeling kit in the choruses
- Ambient textures: room hum, cardboard rustle, a distant train-departure tone, platform ambience

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: on the floor with a box of his things, calm, no tears
- Verses: the platform, the bare apartment, the plant; then the plant growing, the sink, the sister
- Pre-chorus: the months counted off, March to August, the silence turning into room
- Chorus: big, warm, thankful, "I finally arrived"
- Bridge: the honest test of bitterness, the spare chair, homes and trains
- Final chorus: fullest, the plant touching the ceiling, coffee instead of a handshake
- Outro: the box left on the platform, the plant and the place and the sky kept

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – alone at the piano, close and calm]

```
I found the box of your things last week,
Sat on the floor and I didn't even weep.
Just a jacket, a charger, a ticket stub,
And a version of me I've stopped dreaming of.
```

- Scene: A small, bright apartment. Bare floorboards. She sits cross-legged with an open cardboard box.
- Shot: Overhead, then close on her hands lifting a jacket, a phone charger, a folded ticket.
- Cut to: Her face, dry-eyed, curious, like she's looking at someone else's things.
- Emotion: Calm. A small surprise that it doesn't hurt.
- Lighting: Soft daylight from one window, warm and clean.

### [verse 1]

```
The night you left, the platform was cold,
You didn't look back, and I didn't hold.
I stood there shaking with my hands in my sleeves,
Thinking nobody survives when the whole train leaves.
```

- Scene: Memory, winter. A night train platform, near-empty, breath visible.
- Shot: Wide from behind her, a man walking away down the platform toward a carriage door, never turning, his face never shown.
- Cut to: Her hands pulled up into her coat sleeves, shaking; the train pulling out; the platform empty.
- Emotion: The old pain, seen from a distance now.
- Lighting: Cold sodium orange and blue, steam, hard shadows.

```
I moved to a place with a crack in the wall,
One chair, one plate, one mattress, that's all.
Bought a plant from the corner store, half dead,
Put it by the window and I went to bed.
```

- Scene: The new apartment, early days. Almost empty.
- Shot: Slow pan: one chair, one plate on the counter, a mattress on the floor, a crack running up the wall.
- Cut to: A corner-shop bucket of sad plants; she picks the worst one. She sets it on the windowsill and lies down on the mattress in her coat.
- Emotion: Numb, practical, surviving.
- Lighting: Grey daylight, then a single streetlight through the window at night.

### [pre-chorus]

```
And I hated you in March, I'll be honest, it was rough,
By June I was running, and by August I was up.
Turns out the silence you left me had room
For all of the things I never got to bloom.
```

- Scene: Time-lapse across a season in the same apartment.
- Shot: The windowsill: the plant gaining a leaf between cuts. Her in March on the floor with her head in her hands; in June lacing running shoes at dawn; in August dancing while cooking.
- Cut to: The crack in the wall being painted over.
- Emotion: Rising, month by month.
- Lighting: Grey to gold as the months pass.

### [chorus – big, warm, thankful]

```
Thank you for leaving, I finally arrived,
You closed a door and I walked out into the light.
You took the girl who waited by the phone,
And left me with a woman who can make it on her own.

Thank you for leaving, I mean it, no spite,
I'd shake your hand on that platform tonight.
You thought you were the ending, you were only the ride,
Thank you for leaving, I finally arrived.
```

- Scene: The present. She walks out of the apartment building into full daylight.
- Shot: Tracking from the front as she comes down the steps, sunlight hitting her face, a real smile.
- Cut to: A flash of the old her on the platform, then the new her on a bright street; her hand extended as if to shake, then dropping and laughing.
- Emotion: Open, grateful, big.
- Lighting: Full morning sun, warm, slightly overexposed.

### [verse 2]

```
The plant grew a leaf in the middle of May,
I said, look at you, out loud, to a plant, okay.
Learned how to fix the sink from a video,
Learned how to fall asleep with the radio low.
```

- Scene: The apartment filling up. Domestic, funny.
- Shot: Close on a new leaf unfurling; her talking to it, catching herself, shrugging. Her under the sink with a wrench and a phone propped up playing a tutorial, water spraying, her laughing.
- Cut to: Night, a small radio glowing on the floor beside the mattress, her asleep.
- Emotion: Warm, self-amused, at ease.
- Lighting: Bright afternoon; warm lamp at night.

```
Ran into your sister at the grocery store,
She hugged me and said, you seem lighter than before.
I said, I am, and I didn't have to try,
I didn't ask about you, and she didn't say why.
```

- Scene: A supermarket aisle, daytime.
- Shot: Two women meeting by the fruit; a surprised, real hug. The sister holds her at arm's length, says something kind.
- Cut to: Her nodding, meaning it. A beat where the question could be asked and isn't. They part with a wave.
- Emotion: Tender, clean.
- Lighting: Flat supermarket white, made warm by the faces.

### [pre-chorus]

```
And I hated you in March, I'll be honest, it was rough,
By June I was running, and by August I was up.
Now the flat's got a rug and a friend with a key,
And a window full of green that's growing just for me.
```

- Scene: The apartment now: a rug, a lamp, books, a second chair.
- Shot: A friend letting herself in with her own key, holding takeaway; the two of them on the rug.
- Cut to: The window, now crowded with green, the first plant tallest in the middle.
- Emotion: Full, easy.
- Lighting: Golden late afternoon.

### [chorus]

(repeat)

- Scene: The city in daylight; her walking through it like she owns her own life.
- Shot: A crossing, a park, a coffee window; she is alone and not lonely.
- Cut to: The platform again, empty, seen in daylight from a bridge above, harmless now.
- Emotion: Bigger and warmer than the first chorus.
- Lighting: Full sun.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: Wordless montage of the year.
- Shots: The plant across the seasons on the sill, fast. Her painting the wall. Her running along a river at dawn. The spare chair being carried up the stairs. The friend and her laughing over dinner. A pause on her alone at the window, watering the plant, calm.
- Lighting: Every season, ending in gold.

### [bridge – piano and voice, then building]

```
If you're wondering if I'm bitter, come and see,
There's a spare chair at the table now that wasn't there for me.
I don't play our song, I don't drive down your street,
I don't wish you badly, I just wish you well, and I mean it.
```

- Scene: The kitchen table, evening, two chairs.
- Shot: Close on her at the table, talking almost straight to camera. Her hand resting on the back of the second chair.
- Cut to: A car passing a street sign without turning; a playlist skipped without a flinch.
- Emotion: Honest, steady, kind.
- Lighting: Warm lamp, soft shadows.

```
Some people are a home, and some people are a train,
You pull out of the station and you don't come back again.
I'd have waited on that platform till my hair turned grey,
So thank God, thank you, thank the day you pulled away.
```

- Scene: Memory and present intercut.
- Shot: The winter platform, the train pulling away; her standing there, then a match cut to the same spot in summer with her walking past it without stopping.
- Cut to: Her face in the present, eyes closed, a breath, then a laugh.
- Emotion: Release. Gratitude arriving for real.
- Lighting: Cold blue for the memory, warm gold for the present, the cut landing on the word "thank."

### [final chorus – fullest, strings, layered harmonies]

```
Thank you for leaving, I finally arrived,
The plant's touching the ceiling and I'm doing alright.
You took the girl who waited by the phone,
And left me with a woman who can make it on her own.

Thank you for leaving, I mean it, no spite,
I'd buy you a coffee on that platform tonight.
You thought you were the ending, you were only the ride,
Thank you for leaving, I finally arrived.
```

- Scene: The apartment at its fullest, the plant enormous.
- Shot: Wide from the door: the whole room alive, the plant's leaves brushing the ceiling, her in the middle of it.
- Cut to: Her on the platform at night with two coffees, setting one down on a bench and walking away with the other.
- Emotion: Joy without an edge.
- Lighting: Golden, wide, warm.

### [post-chorus – lighter groove, echoes]

```
Oh, I arrived, oh, I arrived,
Watered myself and I came back alive.
Oh, I arrived, oh, I arrived,
Thank you for leaving, look at me thrive.
```

- Scene: Her watering the plant, then herself: a glass of water, a deep breath.
- Shot: Close on the watering can, the soil, then her drinking, sun on her face.
- Cut to: Her spinning once on the rug, arms out, the room going by.
- Emotion: Light, playful.
- Lighting: Bright morning.

### [outro – piano alone]

```
I found the box of your things last week,
Left it on the platform for the city to keep.
Kept the plant, kept the flat, kept the sky,
Thank you for leaving, I finally arrived.
```

- Scene: The platform, daylight, the last shot.
- Shot: Her setting the cardboard box down on the bench, straightening, and walking away up the stairs into daylight without looking back.
- Cut to: The windowsill, the plant in full sun, curtain moving.
- Emotion: Done. Clean.
- Lighting: Clear morning light, white and warm.
