# When the World Gets Loud — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes, rewritten from the submitted draft into a tighter ballad. The story and central images are kept; the length is not. Kept as source material.

**Theme:** A father comes home from a loud day of phones, traffic, deadlines and a meeting he had to smile through. He does not explain it. His small daughter does not ask for the report either. She takes his hand and pulls him into the kitchen, where the music is on and she is dancing in the hall. Later she brings in a flower with a bent stem from the fence and sets it in a glass of water by the window, and she checks on it at bedtime and at dawn until it stands up. The quiet he comes back to is her, not a fix for anything.

**Mood:** Tender, tired, warm and steadying. The day is real and the relief is earned. Nothing is sugary; the father is not cured, only met.

**Message:** "You don't have to fix anything to be the quiet someone comes back to."

**Genre:** Emotional piano-and-strings ballad. Sound-described comparisons only: a small-room, late-evening record where a warm piano leads, a low string bed sits under the chorus, a brushed snare appears once the song opens, and the voice is close enough to hear the breath.

**Tempo & Key:** 68 BPM, 4/4, D minor. The lift comes from dynamics and orchestration, not a key change: cellos enter at the first pre-chorus, the chorus widens, and the final chorus is the fullest the record gets.

**Vocal setup:** Lead male, Singer A, a warm baritone in his late thirties. A single male harmony a third beneath the lead on each chorus. A soft wordless pad of female and child-register voices joins the final chorus. Airy unison doubles of the lead drift under the outro. No Singer B.

**Instrumentation:**

- Upright piano, carrying the whole song from the first chord to the final decay
- Low string bed under every chorus
- Cellos entering with the first pre-chorus
- Soft kick and brushed snare, entering with the chorus only and dropping out for the instrumental and the bridge
- Single acoustic guitar, second verse only
- Brass bed and choir pad on the final chorus
- Distant rain at the top of the song, a glass set down on wood in verse two, a single sustained string note under the last line of each verse

**Mood arc:**

- Intro: one piano chord, far-off rain, a man admitting the day was loud.
- Verse 1: sparse piano and a low pad; the phone, the traffic, the meeting, then the front door and the hand.
- Pre-chorus: cellos enter; she does not ask how it went, she spins him until he loses the thread.
- Chorus: gentle, low strings, a brushed snare, a male harmony; the quiet he comes back to.
- Verse 2: small acoustic guitar; the bent flower, the glass, the window, the checking at dawn.
- Instrumental: sixteen bars of solo piano over held strings, no drums.
- Bridge: nearly spoken; the men who never learned to care, and the strength in softness, then the strings return.
- Final chorus: fullest and warmest; choir pad and brass; the lift lands on the shelter line.
- Post-chorus: drums stripped back; the glass by the window, and the dancing in the hall.
- Outro: one piano and one voice, the flower standing up, decaying into room tone.

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

Characters on screen: **Kai**, the father, a man in his late thirties with short dark hair and a warm, tired face, in a plain grey work coat that he takes off in the hallway. **Ellie**, his daughter, about four, with wavy dark hair and a yellow cardigan. The flower is a single stem in a clear glass.

### [intro – one chord, the day outside]
```
Some days start with thunder
Before the sun is high,
A hundred voices asking
And no good way to reply.
```
- Scene: A grey dawn over a city street. Kai stands at a kitchen window in his grey work coat, phone in his hand, not yet looking at it.
- Shot: Medium close-up through the window glass, rain streaking the pane, the phone face down on the counter beside a cold mug.
- Cut to: Kai's reflection in the glass, then his hand flattening on the sill.
- Emotion: Overwhelmed before the day has begun. He is not dramatic about it; he is just worn already.
- Lighting: Cold grey dawn, a single warm kitchen light behind him that he has not switched on yet.

### [verse 1 – the phone, the meeting, the front door]
```
The phone goes off at seven with a problem I can't name,
The traffic sits and doesn't move and then it moves again.
There's a man who wants an answer and a number that won't close,
And a meeting in an hour where I'll say that it's all fine.
I keep a face for all of it, I've worn it till it fits,
I can carry a whole bad week and never let it slip.
Then I come in through the front door, drop my bag against the wall,
And there's music in the kitchen and you dancing in the hall.
You don't stop to look at me, you're too far into the song,
You just reach out for my hand and pull me where you are.
```
- Scene: Quick cuts through the working day: a car park, a traffic jam, a conference room with a laptop he does not look at. Then the front door of the house at evening. Kai sets his bag down, shrugs off the grey coat in the hallway.
- Shot: Handheld in the car park, then a static wide of the hallway with the front door open to the dark street behind him.
- Cut to: Ellie in the kitchen doorway, dancing barefoot in the yellow cardigan, music from a radio on the counter.
- Emotion: The day is still on him, but a small joy starts to surface. He is not ready to smile yet.
- Lighting: Cool street light outside the door, then warm amber from the kitchen spilling down the hallway.

### [pre-chorus – the thread]
```
You don't ask me how it went,
You don't need the whole report,
You just take my hand and spin me
Till I've lost the thread of it.
```
- Scene: Ellie takes his hand in the hallway and turns him in a slow circle. She does not ask a single question.
- Shot: Medium two-shot, the camera orbiting slowly around the pair, Kai's coat now off and hanging over his arm.
- Cut to: Kai's face, a breath going out of him, his shoulders dropping an inch.
- Emotion: Being let off the hook without being asked to explain anything. Relief arriving before he has words for it.
- Lighting: Warm hallway light, the kitchen beyond it blurred and gold.

### [chorus – the quiet I come back to]
```
When the world gets loud,
You're the quiet I come back to.
When I can't hear myself,
I just stand here and I watch you.
You don't fix a single thing,
You don't know there's any weight,
You just put your hand in mine
And the shouting goes away.
```
- Scene: The kitchen. Ellie stands on a step stool at the counter, humming and rinsing a cup, Kai leaning in the doorway watching her with his arms folded.
- Shot: Slow push-in on Kai's face from the doorway, then a reverse on Ellie at the sink, the water running.
- Cut to: Their hands joined over the counter, the radio turned low.
- Emotion: Stillness. Not healing yet, just the ache easing when he simply stands and looks at her.
- Lighting: Warm low kitchen light, the rest of the house dim.

### [verse 2 – the flower in the glass]
```
You brought a flower in to me from somewhere near the fence,
The stem was bent, the petals torn, it didn't make much sense.
You said that it was only tired and wanted somewhere small,
So we filled a glass with water and we set it by the wall.
You checked on it at bedtime and you checked on it at dawn,
You turned it to the window so it knew which way was warm.
By Thursday it was standing and you never said a thing,
You just carried on with breakfast like it's normal to be mending.
I have spent my whole life hammering at problems till they break,
And you fixed a broken flower with a glass and three days' faith.
```
- Scene: Ellie holds up a bent flower from the garden fence, petals torn. She fills a glass with water and sets it on the windowsill. Over the following days the stem straightens and the flower stands.
- Shot: Macro on the stem and petals, then a wide of the sill at bedtime, then a dawn close-up of the glass turned toward the light, a time-lapse feel.
- Cut to: Kai at the table over breakfast, seeing the flower standing for the first time, and Ellie not even looking up from her cereal.
- Emotion: Wonder with no fuss. The lesson is in the patience, not the words, and he sees himself in the problem he cannot break.
- Lighting: Soft morning window light for the flower, warm lamp at bedtime, pale early dawn in the close-up.

### [pre-chorus – the thread, again]
```
You don't ask me how it went,
You don't need the whole report,
You just take my hand and spin me
Till I've lost the thread of it.
```
- Scene: The same hallway, later in the evening. Ellie takes his hand again and turns him in a circle, now with her cardigan sleeves swinging.
- Shot: Reuse the orbit from the first pre-chorus, tighter, with the flower in the glass visible on the sill in the background.
- Cut to: Kai laughing quietly with his eyes closed.
- Emotion: The small rituals repeating. Being met every evening is the thing that keeps him going.
- Lighting: Warm hallway light, the window behind them a deep blue dusk.

### [chorus – the quiet I come back to, again]
```
When the world gets loud,
You're the quiet I come back to.
When I can't hear myself,
I just stand here and I watch you.
You don't fix a single thing,
You don't know there's any weight,
You just put your hand in mine
And the shouting goes away.
```
- Scene: Ellie on the living room rug building a small tower of blocks, Kai on the sofa with his eyes half closed, the flower glass on the low table in front of him.
- Shot: Reverse angle from the sofa, Ellie in the foreground, Kai soft in the background. Hold the frame, then a slow tilt to the glass.
- Cut to: Ellie reaching up to put her small hand into his, and his hand closing around it.
- Emotion: The shouting in his head goes quiet. He stops performing and simply rests.
- Lighting: Warm lamp light, the room softly shadowed, the window dark.

### [bridge – the strength in softness]
```
I don't know how to tell you
That the thing you do is rare,
That there are grown men twice your size
Who have never learned to care
The way you cared about a flower
With a stem that wouldn't hold.
So if anybody ever says
Your softness makes you small,
They have never been the broken thing
You carried down the hall.
They have never been the tired man
You turned towards the sun.
```
- Scene: Kai sits alone on the edge of Ellie's bed in the dark, looking at the flower in the glass on her windowsill. Ellie asleep. He stands and picks up a small hairbrush from the floor, puts it back, carefully.
- Shot: Static medium shot with the window light cold across his face, a slow hold on the flower, then Ellie asleep under a blanket, wide and still.
- Cut to: Kai in the hallway pausing with his hand on the wall, a small breath before he goes on.
- Emotion: Tenderness that is almost too much to say out loud. The bridge is spoken, not sung, in spirit: a father finally naming what his daughter has taught him.
- Lighting: Cold blue window light on the bridge, a single soft night lamp in the hall, hard shadows on the walls.

### [chorus – the shelter, lifted]
```
When the world gets loud,
You're the quiet I come back to.
When I can't hear myself,
I just stand here and I watch you.
I came here to be the shelter,
To stand between you and the rain,
And you've been the one who's quietly
Been taking it away.
```
- Scene: The front step of the house in soft rain. Kai stands under the porch light holding an umbrella over Ellie, who holds the glass with the flower in it, carefully, both hands.
- Shot: Wide two-shot from across the path, the rain falling in the porch light, then close on Ellie's hands around the glass.
- Cut to: Kai taking the umbrella down and letting it rain on his shoulders while Ellie laughs at him.
- Emotion: The role reverses. He has been the shelter, and she quietly becomes the one holding the weather off him.
- Lighting: Warm porch light against the blue wet street, rain caught in the light.

### [post-chorus – the glass by the window]
```
The world can keep on shouting,
The night can rise and fall,
There's a glass beside the window
And you dancing in the hall.
```
- Scene: Back indoors. Kai leaves the grey coat on the hook and the door swings closed. Ellie dances in the hallway in her yellow cardigan, arms out, the glass safe on the sill.
- Shot: Slow tracking shot along the hallway, from the front door to the kitchen, Ellie spinning in the middle of the frame.
- Cut to: Kai leaning on the doorframe, smiling properly for the first time in the film.
- Emotion: Joy that is light and unhurried. The noise is still out there, and it is simply not in the house.
- Lighting: Warm amber from the kitchen, soft fill from the hallway lamp, the front door dark.

### [outro – the flower standing up]
```
So let it all get louder,
Let the thunder do its worst.
There's a flower on the windowsill
And it is standing up.
So let the phone keep ringing,
Let the traffic have its say,
I've got a little hand to hold
And a whole hall left to play.
```
- Scene: Morning. The phone is on the counter, face down, ringing and then stopping. Kai and Ellie stand at the sink together; she pours the water, he holds the glass steady. The flower is upright on the sill.
- Shot: Slow wide of the kitchen with the sun coming through the window, then a close-up of the glass and the stem, then a final frame of their two hands on the glass.
- Cut to: Black, with the piano decay and room tone under the last held note.
- Emotion: Calm. The world is still loud, and they are not frightened of it anymore.
- Lighting: Clear early sunlight through the window, warm and even, the room tone soft and golden.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** a father in a grey work coat in the hallway, a small girl in a yellow cardigan spinning him by the hand, the caption "When the world gets loud, you're the quiet I come back to."
- **The challenge:** post the hallway spin with the line "you don't ask me how it went" on screen, and invite parents and kids to film their own come-home moment, one minute, no words, just a hand.
- **The caption line:** "You don't fix a single thing." The most shareable sentence in the song, and a line for any caregiver who has ever come home overwhelmed.
- **The quote:** "There's a flower on the windowsill and it is standing up." Pairs with a time-lapse of a bent flower straightening in a glass, a slow, quiet video that needs no music cut.
