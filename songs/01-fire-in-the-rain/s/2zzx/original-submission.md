## Style direction

- Genre: Modern cinematic pop with dark R&B, pop-rock, and light rhythmic percussion.
- Tempo: 96 BPM, with a half-time bridge and a final chorus lift to 104 BPM.
- Key: F♯ minor, modulating to A major for the final chorus.
- Vocal: Emotional young-adult lead vocal; intimate and breathy in the verses, powerful and slightly raspy in the chorus. Add layered male/female harmonies on the final hook.
- Instrumentation: Piano, warm sub-bass, atmospheric synth pads, muted electric guitar, low 808s, handclaps, cinematic strings, and a wide electric-guitar lift in the final chorus.
- Mood arc: Secretive attraction → passionate romance → betrayal and confusion → emotional revelation → self-respect and liberation.
- Viral hook: **"Now I miss you, but I choose me / No more chains where my wings should be."**
