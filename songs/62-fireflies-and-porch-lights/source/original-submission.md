# Fireflies and Porch Lights — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
Two kids, two houses, one fence and a jar with a nail-punched lid. A summer
that never became a relationship and shaped her anyway. The song ends on her
own porch years later, in a different town, leaving the light on for somebody
else's kids.

**Mood:**
Warm, unhurried, front-porch. Nostalgia without self-pity. The ache is real
but the ending is generous.

**Message:**
"Nothing ever happened, and it changed my whole life."

**Genre:**
Contemporary folk-pop / Americana. Think: Noah Kahan x Kacey Musgraves, with
a Lord Huron string swell on the last chorus.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 94 BPM, 4/4. Key: G major (open strings, singable, sits mid-chest)
- No key change; the lift is a twelve-string layer, a fiddle countermelody
  and the full string section arriving on the final chorus.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead: young adult female, close and conversational, almost spoken in the
  bridge, full and open on the title line.
- Backing: stacked harmonies on the title, a group harmony on the last chorus,
  wordless echoes in the outro.

**Instrumentation:**
- Fingerpicked steel-string acoustic guitar (the spine), twelve-string layer
- Mandolin-style tremolo high above the guitar; fiddle from the second chorus
- Upright bass, brushed kit, shaker, tambourine, stomp and clap
- Strings (violas, cello) from the first pre-chorus; low piano in the last chorus
- Textures: crickets, a ticking sprinkler, a screen-door hinge, a bug-light
  hum, one pass of distant thunder

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: the porch, the hinge, the light coming on before anyone notices
- Verse one: the jar, the fence, the chasing, two kids at fourteen
- Pre-choruses: ten more minutes, and nobody going in
- Chorus: the hook, the borrowed light, the line that carries the song
- Verse two: the last August, the packed truck, the jar emptied
- Instrumental: the empty porch and the empty lawn
- Bridge: her own porch, her own light, somebody else's kids
- Final chorus and post-chorus: she lets it go on purpose
- Outro: the same hinge, and the light left on till morning

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro — porch ambience, one guitar, no drums]

```
Screen door banging on a hinge that never got fixed,
Cut grass and citronella in the air.
Somebody's sprinkler ticking two yards over,
And the light comes on before we know it's there.
```

- Scene: A wooden porch on a small suburban street, the last twenty minutes of daylight.
- Shot: Macro on a screen-door hinge, paint layers, the spring stretching and letting go; the door slaps twice and settles. Cut to: A wide of the porch from the lawn, two chairs, a bug light, nobody in frame yet, and the bulb flicking on by itself as the sky drops a stop.
- Emotion: Anticipation with no event attached to it. The feeling of a summer evening starting.
- Lighting: Blue dusk on the lawn, one warm tungsten bulb on the porch, citronella smoke visible in the beam.

### [verse 1 — bass and mandolin in]

```
We had a jar with a lid my dad punched with a nail,
Six holes and a blade of grass for a bed.
You'd come across the fence at half past eight,
With grass stains and whatever your mother said.

We were bad at catching, better at the chasing,
Barefoot on a lawn that hadn't cooled off yet.
My knees in the dirt and your hand in the dark,
Two kids and a hundred nights we hadn't spent.
```

- Scene: The summer they were fourteen. Two houses, one fence, one lawn.
- Shot: A hand holding a mason jar up to the light; six punched holes throwing six dots on a cheek; a blade of grass curled inside on the glass floor. Cut to: A boy swinging a leg over a low chain-link fence, landing badly, laughing; the two of them running the lawn barefoot at half speed, arms out, hands closing on nothing.
- Emotion: Uncomplicated joy. Nothing in this stanza is loaded yet.
- Lighting: Deep blue lawn, the porch bulb behind them as a warm halo, fireflies as practical points of light only.

### [pre-chorus 1 — brushed snare, first strings]

```
Mama's voice through the screen saying, ten more minutes,
Ten more minutes, and then ten more again.
Porch light doing that thing where it hums,
And neither of us ever going in.
```

- Scene: The negotiation every kid on that street knows by heart.
- Shot: Shot from outside looking in through the mesh: a mother's silhouette at the kitchen sink, distorted by the screen, calling without turning round. Cut to: The bug light in macro, moths against it, the filament hum visible as flicker; then two pairs of bare feet on the boards, not moving toward the door.
- Emotion: Small conspiracy. The first time either of them chose each other over an adult.
- Lighting: Warm interior spill through the screen mesh, everything outside it going cooler by the second.

### [chorus 1 — twelve-string, full kit, cello]

```
Fireflies and porch lights, you and me and July,
Two houses and a fence and a whole lot of sky.
We caught them in a jar and we let them go at nine,
Because a light that small is only borrowed for a time.
I can still hear the hinge, I can still smell the grass,
I can still feel fourteen when the summer gets like this.
Nothing ever happened and it changed my whole life,
Fireflies and porch lights, you and me and July.
```

- Scene: The full summer, told as one long evening on one lawn.
- Shot: A slow crane up from the grass to a wide of both houses, both porch lights on, the fence running between them, the sky still holding blue at the top of frame. Cut to: The jar opened, the lid turned back, and everything in it going up at once past two faces.
- Emotion: The biggest, simplest feeling in the song. Play it as joy, not as loss.
- Lighting: The widest and warmest of the video so far; the only moment where the lawn is lit as well as the porch.

### [verse 2 — guitar and voice, one low string note]

```
Last August you were taller and you drove,
And I had a summer job and plans for the fall.
You knocked on the screen at half past eight anyway,
And we sat on the steps and hardly talked at all.

Then you kissed me by the gate like it was nothing,
And your dad's truck was already packed to leave.
I let the last one out of the jar that night,
And I stood in the yard till there was nothing to see.
```

- Scene: Three years later. Same porch, same hour, everything different.
- Shot: A knuckle on a screen door in close-up, the same hinge, the same slap; the two of them on the steps a foot apart, not touching, both looking at the lawn. Cut to: A packed truck at the curb with a tarp roped over it; a kiss at the gate held for less than two seconds; her walking back across the lawn alone with the empty jar swinging from one hand.
- Emotion: Not a breakup. Something smaller and worse: an ending nobody names.
- Lighting: Same tungsten bulb, but the lawn now reads cold and the fireflies are almost gone. One pass of distant lightning on the horizon.

### [pre-chorus 2 — quieter than the first]

```
No voice through the screen now, nobody counting,
Ten more minutes and the whole house still.
Porch light doing that thing where it hums,
And neither of us saying what we feel.
```

- Scene: The same frame as the first pre-chorus, emptied out.
- Shot: The kitchen window from outside, dark, no silhouette at the sink. Cut to: The bug light again in macro, one moth, then none; two pairs of feet on the boards, one pair in driving shoes now.
- Emotion: Both of them waiting for the other one to say it. Neither does.
- Lighting: Interior dark, the porch bulb the only source in frame, hard falloff to black.

### [chorus 2 — fiddle countermelody]

```
Fireflies and porch lights, you and me and July,
Two houses and a fence and a whole lot of sky.
We caught them in a jar and we let them go at nine,
Because a light that small is only borrowed for a time.
I can still hear the hinge, I can still smell the grass,
I can still feel fourteen when the summer gets like this.
Nothing ever happened and it changed my whole life,
Fireflies and porch lights, you and me and July.
```

- Scene: The same crane and the same lawn, but cut against the present.
- Shot: Repeat the crane from chorus one, matched exactly, and this time one of the two porch lights is off and the fence has a new panel in it. Cut to: Fast intercutting of four summers on the same square of grass, the two of them getting taller in each, one cut per bar.
- Emotion: Overwhelmed. This is the only chorus that hurts.
- Lighting: Identical grade to chorus one for the crane, so the missing light is the only thing the eye catches.

### [instrumental] — scene block only

- Scene: The porch with nobody on it. The video's only silence.
- Shot: The two empty chairs; the jar upside down on the rail with the lid beside it; the hinge, still; a spider working in the corner of the bug light. Cut to: The lawn from the upstairs window, mown short, no fireflies, a sprinkler ticking across it in the dark.
- Emotion: Absence, held long enough to be uncomfortable.
- Lighting: The porch bulb alone, no fill, deep black beyond the boards.

### [bridge — half-time, guitar and voice]

```
I've got a porch of my own on a street you never knew,
Two chairs and a bulb I leave on after dark.
When the heat leaves the grass I still go out at eight
And let the evening take its time to start.
Some kids came through with a jar and a nail-punched lid,
And I let them have the yard, the way somebody did.
```

- Scene: A different porch, a different town, present day, unmistakably hers.
- Shot: A new hinge that works; two chairs, one of them used; a mug on the rail; her sitting with her feet on the boards, adult, comfortable. Cut to: Two kids cutting across her lawn with a jar, freezing when they see her; she lifts a hand, they carry on, and she watches them from the rail without saying anything.
- Emotion: The turn. Not wistful — settled, and quietly funny in the way she does not intervene.
- Lighting: Warmer and softer than the childhood porch, more sources, less contrast; the first time the video looks like the present is a nice place.

### [final chorus — full strings, group harmony]

```
Fireflies and porch lights, you and me and July,
Two houses and a fence and a whole lot of sky.
I don't need it in a jar, I don't need it to stay,
A light like that was only ever passing anyway.
I can still hear the hinge, I can still smell the grass,
I can still feel fourteen when the summer gets like this.
Nothing ever happened and it changed my whole life,
Fireflies and porch lights, you and me and July.
```

- Scene: Both porches at once, cut together as if they were on the same street.
- Shot: The crane from chorus one a third time, and now both lights are on again because one of them is hers; the two lawns match-cut on the rise.
- Cut to: The kids on her lawn opening their jar and letting it go, shot from her eyeline at the rail.
- Emotion: Resolution without a reunion. Nobody comes back and it is still a happy ending.
- Lighting: The fullest, warmest grade in the video; the lawn, the porch and the sky all lit for the first time together.

### [post-chorus — stomp and clap]

```
You and me and July,
Two kids and a jar and a sky.
You and me and July,
Let it go, let it go, let it fly.
```

- Scene: The chant, played as the kids' scene rather than hers.
- Shot: Handheld low on the grass with the two kids running through the sprinkler, hands opening, the jar abandoned on its side.
- Cut to: Her clapping the rhythm on the porch rail with one hand, half-smiling, not singing.
- Emotion: Light, fast, uncomplicated. The release valve after the last chorus.
- Lighting: Sprinkler mist catching the porch light, hard backlight, everything sparkling.

### [outro — fingerpicking, crickets]

```
Screen door banging on a hinge that never got fixed,
Somebody out there is fourteen tonight.
I'll leave it on, I'll leave it on till the morning,
Fireflies and porch lights, you and me and July.
```

- Scene: Her porch, late, everyone gone home.
- Shot: The old hinge from shot one and the new hinge cut back to back, matched framing, the old one paint-thick and the new one clean.
- Cut to: Her going in, the screen door slapping twice behind her, and the porch light left on over two empty chairs.
- Emotion: Peace, and a small deliberate act of generosity.
- Lighting: One warm bulb in a black frame, held until the audio fades.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the jar lid turned back and everything inside going up at once past two faces, shot at 120 frames per second.
- **The matched crane:** the same rise off the same lawn three times, one porch light missing the second time. Free to notice, devastating when you do.
- **The challenge:** film your own porch, your own street, your own dusk, and cut on *"you and me and July"* — tag the neighbour you spent it with.
- **The caption line:** *"Nothing ever happened and it changed my whole life."*
- **The quote:** *"A light like that was only ever passing anyway."*
