# Mirror, Mirror — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
Getting ready for a night out as a ritual of power. One bathroom, one mirror ringed with bulbs, one girl who turns forty minutes of eyeliner, curls and lipstick into a coronation. The mirror is her hype girl, her witness and the only thing in her life that has never lied to her. By the end of the song she walks out of the door into a crowd that already knows the answer to the question in the hook.

**Mood:**
Cheeky, certain, loud, sparkly. Confidence as joy, not as a fight. Nothing to prove, everything to enjoy.

**Message:**
"The mirror never gave me anything. It only shows back what I bring. And I bring it every single night."

**Genre:**
Hyperpop-leaning pop with pitched vocal chops and distorted bass. Think: Charli XCX x Doja Cat with a Dua Lipa chorus, get-ready-with-me energy, mirror-selfie shine.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 130 BPM, 4/4
- Key: A minor (sharp, glossy, a little dangerous)
- Final chorus lifts toward the relative major feel (C) with the widest layers of the track.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, quick and conversational in the verses, big and grinning in the choruses, almost spoken in the bridge.
- Backing vocals: pitched-up vocal chops answering every "mirror, mirror"; stacked harmonies in the choruses; a crowd-chant layer on the final post-chorus.

**Instrumentation:**
- Distorted saw bass (the spine; clean and rubbery in verses, crushed in choruses)
- Pitched vocal chops (the second lead; "mirror" stutter in the intro, "who's the baddie" bounce in the post-chorus)
- Tight electronic kit: four-on-the-floor kick, crushed clap, bright hi-hats, finger snaps in the verses
- Hyperpop supersaw stabs on the beat in the choruses
- Filtered synth chord pad (intro, bridge)
- Detuned synth lead (instrumental break)
- Soft sub-bass under the bridge
- Textures: bathroom-reverb room tone, a running tap, a lipstick cap clicking, glitch stutter edits, filtered risers, reverse claps, club haze and a distant crowd in the outro

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: steam on the mirror, a wiped palm, "there she is"
- Verses: the ritual, item by item; then the wrong name on the phone, the dress, the girls at the door
- Pre-chorus: ask the glass, it's never lied; the second time admits it saw her cry
- Chorus: the big cheeky question the mirror already knows the answer to
- Post-chorus: the chant, doors up, it's me
- Bridge: the turn; the mirror is only glass, she brought everything it shows
- Final chorus: she takes the mirror's answer with her out of the door
- Outro: the club, the bass in her chest, every shiny surface in the city agreeing

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – close, playful, over a filtered pad and a stuttering chop]

```
Lights on, phone down, speakers loud,
9:30, I'm a one-woman crowd.
Steam on the glass, I wipe it with my palm,
There she is. "Hey, you. Stay calm."
```

- Scene: A small bathroom, the light switch flicked, a phone put down on the counter, a speaker turned up. The mirror is fogged from the shower.
- Shot: Close on a palm wiping a clean arc through the steam. Her face appears in the arc, towel on her head, no makeup yet.
- Cut to: Her eyes meeting her own in the glass, a little grin, an eyebrow.
- Emotion: Anticipation. The night hasn't started and she already likes it.
- Lighting: Warm bulbs around the mirror, the rest of the room soft and steamy.

### [verse 1]

```
Hair up in a towel and a song I know by heart,
Every night out is a ritual, and this is the part
Where the bathroom's a temple and the bulbs are a crown,
And nobody gets to tell me to turn it down.
```

- Scene: The ritual begins. She sings along to the speaker, off-key and happy.
- Shot: Medium on her at the mirror, towel turban, singing into a hairbrush for one line, then getting to work.
- Cut to: The row of bulbs from below so they ring her head like a crown.
- Emotion: Play. This is her favourite part of the night.
- Lighting: Bulb-warm, a little glitter catching in the steam.

```
Eyeliner wing so sharp it could cut,
First try, no shake, that's how I know it's my luck.
Curls falling out of the pins one by one,
I'm not even dressed yet and I'm already the fun.
```

- Scene: The eyeliner. One clean wing, first try. She looks at it, then at herself, impressed.
- Shot: Extreme close-up of the pen drawing the wing in one stroke, then her eye opening.
- Cut to: Pins pulled from her hair one at a time on the beat, curls dropping.
- Emotion: Small victories, taken seriously.
- Lighting: Same warm bulbs, macro detail.

```
Gloss on the counter, glitter on the floor,
A dress on the hook that I've been saving for.
Hype on the speaker, I'm singing off-key,
The mirror's my hype girl, and she's staring at me.
```

- Scene: The counter is chaos: gloss, brushes, a fallen pot of glitter. A black dress hangs on the back of the door.
- Shot: A slow pan across the counter mess, then up to the dress on its hook.
- Cut to: Her pointing at her own reflection, mouthing the line, the reflection pointing back.
- Emotion: Delight. She and the mirror are a team.
- Lighting: Warm, glitter sparkle on the tiles.

### [pre-chorus]

```
Ask the glass, it's never lied,
It's the only one that's always on my side.
One deep breath, the red goes on slow,
Say it with me, mirror, you already know.
```

- Scene: She picks up the red lipstick. Everything slows.
- Shot: The lipstick cap clicking off in close-up, the first stroke going on slow, her breath held.
- Cut to: Her lips pressed together once, then her eyes lifting to the mirror.
- Emotion: The moment before. A dare to herself.
- Lighting: The bulbs dim slightly, the red is the brightest thing in frame.

### [chorus – big, glossy, shout-along]

```
Mirror, mirror, tell me who's the baddie,
Don't act shy, you've been looking at me.
Mirror, mirror, say it to my face,
Nobody in this city's gonna take my place.
```

- Scene: The drop. Lights flare, the music hits, she performs the chorus straight to the mirror.
- Shot: Wide, her dancing in the bathroom in a towel and a full face, the bulbs pulsing on the beat.
- Cut to: Her reflection filmed as if it were a second person, a shot-reverse-shot between her and herself.
- Emotion: Full grin. Nothing to prove and proving it anyway.
- Lighting: Bulbs at maximum, strobing subtly with the kick.

```
I'm the whole event, I'm the reason they came,
Every bulb around you is spelling my name.
Mirror, mirror, tell me who's the baddie,
You already know, and baby, so does everybody.
```

- Scene: She climbs onto the edge of the tub to see her whole self in the mirror.
- Shot: The bulbs blur into bokeh as the camera racks focus from them to her face.
- Cut to: A quick flash-forward of a club door, then back to the bathroom on the last line.
- Emotion: Certainty. She's laughing at how sure she is.
- Lighting: Bulb-warm to a one-frame flash of club colour and back.

### [post-chorus – chant]

```
Say it, say it, who's the baddie,
Say it to my face, say it back at me.
Say it, say it, who's the baddie,
Lights up, doors up, it's me, it's me.
```

- Scene: The beat strips down. She chants at the mirror, bouncing on her toes.
- Shot: Jump cuts on every "say it": a different pose each time, in the same frame.
- Cut to: Her pointing at the mirror on "it's me, it's me", the reflection pointing back.
- Emotion: Playground energy, a private hype session.
- Lighting: Bulbs, steady and warm.

### [verse 2]

```
Phone lights up, it's a name I don't need,
Someone who used to say I was too much to be seen.
Cute. I flip it face down on the sink,
This red on my lips is louder than anything he thinks.
```

- Scene: The phone on the counter lights up. A name she knows. She reads it, exhales through her nose, and flips the phone face down on the sink.
- Shot: The phone glowing (a blank lit screen, the name composited), her eyes flicking to it and back to the mirror.
- Cut to: Her hand turning the phone over in one clean motion, then reapplying the red.
- Emotion: A beat of the old feeling, then a decision. The red wins.
- Lighting: A cold flash of phone light on her jaw, then warm again.

```
Zip on the dress, it's black and it's mean,
Heels that could walk straight out of a magazine.
Perfume on the wrist, on the neck, in the air,
Rings on every finger and a clip in my hair.
```

- Scene: The dress comes off the hook. The zip goes up. The heels go on.
- Shot: A zip closing in extreme close-up on the beat, then heels stepping onto tile.
- Cut to: Perfume misted into the air and walked through, rings sliding on, a clip snapping in.
- Emotion: Armour going on, piece by piece, all of it fun.
- Lighting: Warm, with a hallway light spilling in from the door.

```
The girls at the door, all three of them screaming,
Phone up, flash on, this is the reveal scene.
Keys, gloss, one last kiss to the glass,
Don't wish me luck, wish the city luck, I'm out the door at last.
```

- Scene: The doorbell. Three friends in the hallway lose their minds when she opens the bathroom door.
- Shot: The reveal from the friends' side, phone flashes going off, all three screaming.
- Cut to: Her turning back for one last look, blowing the mirror a kiss, grabbing keys, and out.
- Emotion: Joy, shared. The mirror gets the last kiss.
- Lighting: Phone-flash pops in a warm hallway.

### [pre-chorus]

```
Ask the glass, it's never lied,
Even on the nights I cried, it stayed on my side.
One deep breath, the door's about to go,
Say it with me, mirror, you already know.
```

- Scene: The front door. Her hand on the handle, one beat of quiet before the noise.
- Shot: A two-second memory, colder: her at the same mirror on a worse night, mascara run, the mirror still there. Then back to now.
- Cut to: The door opening onto the street, streetlight and taxi headlights.
- Emotion: The mirror saw the bad nights too. It stayed.
- Lighting: A cold memory flash, then warm hallway, then the blue-orange street.

### [chorus]

(repeat)

- Scene: The taxi, four girls in the back, windows down.
- Shot: Her face in the taxi window's reflection, city lights sliding across it, singing the chorus.
- Cut to: The wing mirror, the rear-view mirror, a shop window, a phone screen: every reflective surface on the ride gets her.
- Emotion: The mirror has left the bathroom and is now the whole city.
- Lighting: Streetlight orange, neon, headlights, passing.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: The walk to the door. No lyrics, all attitude.
- Shots: Heels on wet pavement in slow motion. The four girls in a line, coats open. A bouncer's rope. A mirrored lobby wall that multiplies her a dozen times. The club door, still closed, bass leaking through it. A hard cut to black on the pad.
- Lighting: Street to lobby to a strip of club light under the door.

### [bridge – almost spoken, then building]

```
Lean in close, I'll tell you the truth,
The mirror is just glass, it's got nothing to prove.
It only shows back what I carry inside,
And I've been carrying it through every bad night.
```

- Scene: The club bathroom, quieter, a different mirror. She leans in close to it.
- Shot: Extreme close-up, her forehead almost touching the glass, speaking to herself.
- Cut to: The reflection alone, no her in frame, just the glass and the bulbs.
- Emotion: Honesty. The mirror was never magic.
- Lighting: Cooler, greener club-bathroom light, one warm bulb.

```
The girl who cried at it, she's still in there,
She just learned to walk in like she owns the air.
So when the doors swing open and the whole floor turns,
That's not a trick of the light, that's the thing I earned.
```

- Scene: She straightens up, fixes one curl, and walks toward the door back to the floor.
- Shot: Her back to camera, the door ahead, the bass getting louder as she gets closer.
- Cut to: Her hand on the door on "swing open."
- Emotion: Resolve, but grinning.
- Lighting: The strip of coloured light under the door widening.

### [final chorus – widest, lifted, crowd double]

```
Mirror, mirror, tell me who's the baddie,
Don't act shy, you've been looking at me.
Mirror, mirror, I don't need you to say,
I took your answer with me when I walked away.
I'm the whole event, I'm the reason they came,
Every light in this club is spelling my name.
Mirror, mirror, tell me who's the baddie,
You already know, and baby, so does everybody.
```

- Scene: The doors swing open onto the floor. The whole crowd turns.
- Shot: The reveal from inside the club, the doors opening, her in the frame, light behind her.
- Cut to: Her walking in, the crowd parting, the friends behind her, a mirrored wall along the floor catching all of it.
- Emotion: Arrival. The question doesn't need the mirror anymore.
- Lighting: Club colour, strobes on the kick, a mirrored wall multiplying everything.

### [post-chorus]

```
Say it, say it, who's the baddie,
Say it to my face, say it back at me.
Say it, say it, who's the baddie,
Lights up, doors up, it's me, it's me.
```

- Scene: The crowd chants it with her. The whole floor is in on it.
- Shot: Jump cuts through the crowd, hands up, on every "say it."
- Cut to: Her pointing at the mirrored wall on "it's me, it's me", a hundred reflections pointing back.
- Emotion: The private hype session has become a public one.
- Lighting: Strobes, colour, haze.

### [outro – low, pleased, half-time]

```
Lights low, phone up, the bass in my chest,
Half past twelve and I'm still the best dressed.
Somebody's staring, I'll let him stare,
Every mirror in this building knows I'm here.
Catch me in the window, catch me in the chrome,
Every shiny thing in this city's gonna walk me home.
Mirror, mirror, you were right about me,
You were right about me.
```

- Scene: Later. The floor in a slower haze, her phone up filming the room, someone across the bar looking. She lets him.
- Shot: Slow motion, close, the bass visible in the shake of a glass on the bar.
- Cut to: Her in the club bathroom mirror one last time at the end of the night, lipstick a little worn, grinning. She taps the glass twice. Final shot: the street outside, her reflection sliding along a dark shop window as she walks home with her girls, heels in hand.
- Emotion: Satisfied. The mirror was right.
- Lighting: Club haze to warm bathroom bulb to blue street.
