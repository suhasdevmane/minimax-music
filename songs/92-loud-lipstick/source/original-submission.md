# Loud Lipstick — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A red lipstick as armour and a night out as a victory lap. Two years of being agreeable, beige and quiet end at half six on a wet Manchester evening when she finds an old lipstick at the bottom of a case. UK register throughout — pre-drinks, the queue in the drizzle, the cloakroom, the club toilets, the night bus home.

**Mood:**
Loud, funny, defiant, warm. Pop-rock with a terrace chant. Nobody is the villain; the enemy is the habit of making yourself smaller.

**Message:**
"It was never the colour. It was two years of quiet finally paid."

**Genre:**
Modern British pop-rock with a chanted chorus. Think: Wolf Alice x Sam Fender guitars with a Rina Sawayama-sized hook — big overdriven chords, live drums, gang vocals.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 130 BPM, 4/4
- Key: A major (open chords, guitar-friendly, singable at full belt)
- No modulation. The last chorus lifts by adding an octave-up guitar and the crowd vocals at full size.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, dry and conversational in the verses, full belt on the choruses with the rasp left in.
- Backing: gang-vocal crowd doubling the title; shouted terrace vocals on the post-chorus.

**Instrumentation:**
- Two electric guitars — palm-muted in the verses, overdriven and ringing in the choruses, a solo in the break
- Live drums with a dry forward snare and a crash on every chorus bar; bass locked to the guitar
- Clean spring-reverb arpeggio: opens the song, returns for the bridge and outro
- Tambourine from the pre-choruses; gang vocals and floor stomps on the post-chorus
- A filtered club kick under the second verse; octave guitar on the last chorus
- Ambience: rain on glass, bathroom tile reverb, an extractor fan, a lipstick lid clicking, a queue in the rain, a heavy door, floor stomps, a night bus

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: half six, steamed mirror, the lid comes off
- Verses: two years of beige, then the club toilets and the fire door
- Pre-chorus: the queue, the cloakroom, the bass through the floor
- Chorus: the biggest and simplest thing on the record; the post-chorus is a terrace chant
- Instrumental: guitar solo, half-time breakdown, snare-roll build
- Bridge: one clean guitar, the school hall, the report card
- Final chorus and outro: full size, then the night bus at ten past three

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – bathroom reverb, clean arpeggio]

```
Half six, bathroom light, rain down the pane,
Wiped the steam off the mirror with the side of my hand.
One stripe of red and she's back again,
The girl I put in a drawer, and she's turned up with a plan.
Right then.
```

- Scene: A small flat bathroom at half six on a wet evening, the light too bright, the window black with rain.
- Shot: Her hand dragging across a steamed mirror, the streak revealing one eye.
- Cut to: Extreme macro of a red lipstick being wound up, then one stripe drawn across the bottom lip.
- Emotion / Lighting: Ordinary, then decided. Harsh bathroom white, rain-black window, red the only colour.

### [verse 1 – two years of beige]

```
Two years in beige and I called it peace,
Kept my voice at the volume of a library seat.
Said sorry to a chair, said sorry to the rain,
Held the door for people who'd not hold it again.
Then Steph texted — pre-drinks, half seven, her place —
And I found the red at the bottom of a case.
Rolled it in my fingers like a little bit of proof
That I used to be the girl who took up the room.
The lid came off with a click like a lock,
And the one in the mirror said, "Right then, off we pop."
```

- Scene: A montage of the beige two years, shot deliberately flat, then the flat on the night itself.
- Shots: Her in a grey office in grey clothes saying nothing in a meeting; apologising to a chair leg she has walked into; holding a door for three people who do not look at her; a beige coat on a hook, worn thin.
- Cut to: The phone lighting up with a group chat (composited); her upending a make-up case onto a duvet and the red rolling out.
- Emotion / Lighting: Numb, then a flicker. Flashbacks desaturated to nearly monochrome; the flat warm and lamp-lit.

### [pre-chorus 1 – the queue]

```
Cab on the corner, heels on the kerb,
Queue in the drizzle and I don't say a word.
Cloakroom ticket, coat off, hair down,
Bass through the floor coming up through the ground.
Right then. Let's go.
```

- Scene: Arriving. Wet pavement, orange street light, a door with a queue.
- Shots: A heel landing in a puddle on the kerb; the queue in fine rain under an awning; a cloakroom hatch and a paper ticket; the beige coat handed over and taken away.
- Emotion / Lighting: Anticipation, no nerves. Sodium orange outside, red spill from the doorway.

### [chorus – terrace-sized]

```
Loud lipstick, louder me,
Kicked the door off the quiet and I set it free.
Two years small, now the whole road knows,
Red on the rim of the glass wherever it goes.
You can hear me in the queue, you can hear me in the cab,
I'm not sorry for the space and I'm not giving it back.
Sing it with your chest till the ceiling agrees,
Loud lipstick, louder me.
```

- Scene: The dance floor at full power, cut against the street outside.
- Shots: Her walking into the room as the crash lands; a whole floor of people jumping on the beat; a red lipstick mark on a glass rim in macro; the crowd shouting the hook straight at camera.
- Emotion / Lighting: Full release. Red and white club light, hard shadows, real strobe used sparingly.

### [post-chorus – stomp and clap]

```
Louder, louder, louder me,
Louder, louder, let the whole street see.
Red on the rim, red on the rim,
Louder, louder, let the night begin.
Hands up if you've ever been told to calm down,
Hands up, hands up — we are not calming down.
```

- Scene: The chant as a stomp-and-clap block, shot like a gig rather than a club.
- Shots: Feet stomping on a wooden floor; hands going up in a wave on the last two lines; her at the front with the crowd behind, all shouting the same words.
- Emotion / Lighting: Communal, funny, defiant. House lights half up so faces are visible.

### [verse 2 – the toilets and the fire door]

```
Half eleven, toilets, mirror full of us,
One I'd never met said, "Your colour is mad."
I said, "Take it — go on, do your top lip,"
She came out braver, and she kept it, and that's it.
Somebody's ex was stood by the fire door,
I said "Alright, mate," and I meant nothing more.
Two years back that would have been my whole night,
Now it's just a face in a bit of red light.
Then the DJ dropped the one we all know,
And the floor came up like it wanted a go.
```

- Scene: A crowded club toilet, then a propped fire door, then the floor.
- Shots: Six women crammed at a mirror being unreasonably kind to each other; the lipstick handed over and used; the stranger looking at herself differently; the lipstick going into the stranger's bag, not back into Mahima's.
- Cut to: A faceless figure by the fire door; a two-second nod; her turning back before the shot is finished with them.
- Emotion / Lighting: Generous, then anticlimactic on purpose. Toilet fluorescent and red spill; the fire door in green exit light.

### [pre-chorus 2]

```
Sweat on the ceiling, hands in the air,
Somebody starts it and the whole room's there.
Feet on the speaker stack, hair coming down,
Bass through the floor coming up through the ground.
Right then. Let's go.
```

- Scene: The room at maximum heat.
- Shots: Condensation actually dripping from a low ceiling; a hundred hands going up; her climbing onto a speaker stack with two friends holding her ankles.
- Emotion / Lighting: Peak. Red, white, steam in the beams.

### [chorus 2]

(repeat)

- Scene: The same chorus from up on the stack and from the stage.
- Shots: Her eyeline over the whole room; the crowd from behind her shoulder; a hard cut to a small stage with a mic stand and a red wash.
- Emotion / Lighting: Bigger, sweatier, still funny. Full red wash, white backlight.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: Guitar solo, half-time breakdown, build. No story, all room.
- Shots: A guitarist in silhouette against the red wash; the crowd in half-time slow motion; a bar towel, a spilled drink, a stack of glasses with red marks on every rim; the snare-roll build shot entirely on faces.
- Lighting: Red wash to a single white key light, then everything at once.

### [bridge – one clean guitar]

```
It was never the colour, it was never the shade,
It was two years of quiet finally paid.
You can take my lipstick, take the whole case,
I would still walk in like I own the place.
I was loud at nine years old in a school hall,
Somebody said "bring it down," so I did — that's all.
They wrote it on a report card — "too much, too loud" —
And I have been apologising to a paper crowd.
Well, the volume is back and it isn't for you,
It's for the girl in the toilets and the nine-year-old too.
```

- Scene: The club empties out of the frame and a school hall replaces it.
- Shots: Her alone on the small stage with the house lights up and the room half empty; a flashback to a nine-year-old girl singing far too loudly in a school hall while an adult hand lowers, palm down; a report card in a drawer (composited); the drawer closing.
- Emotion / Lighting: Quiet, then decided. House lights flat and unflattering; the school hall in flat daylight; the band slams back in on the last line and the red returns.

### [final chorus]

(repeat, biggest)

- Scene: The stage, the crowd, the whole night at once.
- Shots: Her on the stage with the mic, the crowd shouting every word; the stranger from the toilets in the front row wearing the lipstick; confetti of nothing but light; a wide from the back of the room over every raised hand.
- Emotion / Lighting: Triumphant and shared. Full red and white, no strobe on the last eight bars.

### [post-chorus 2]

(repeat, crowd)

- Scene: The chant handed entirely to the room.
- Shots: The stomp from floor level; hands up in a wave; her not singing for two bars, just watching them do it.
- Emotion / Lighting: Given away. House lights half up, faces lit.

### [outro – night bus]

```
Ten past three, night bus, window steamed,
Red on the cup and the chips on my knee.
Lipstick's gone, some girl has got it now,
Louder me, and I'm keeping that somehow.
Loud lipstick, louder me.
```

- Scene: The top deck of a night bus at ten past three.
- Shots: A steamed window with the city smeared behind it; a paper cup with a red mark on the rim; a bag of chips balanced on her knee; her drawing a small line in the steam with one finger and leaving it.
- Emotion / Lighting: Content, tired, entirely herself. Bus fluorescent, orange street light passing in bars across her face.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the lid coming off the lipstick with a click, cut hard to the crash of the first chorus and the room jumping.
- **The challenge:** *"Hands up if you've ever been told to calm down."* Film the two-line stomp-and-clap block with your own answer on screen; it works at a gig, in a kitchen or on a night bus.
- **The caption line:** *"It was never the colour. It was two years of quiet finally paid."*
- **The knife line:** *"They wrote it on a report card, too much, too loud, and I have been apologising to a paper crowd."*
- **The soft moment:** the lipstick going into the stranger's bag instead of back into her own.
