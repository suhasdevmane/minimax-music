# Passport Stamps — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A life measured in passport stamps, and the discovery that the stamps are not the point — the people are. Lisbon is a girl with paint under her nails. Tokyo is a boy at a vending machine at two in the morning. Oaxaca is a kitchen and a language she does not have. Berlin and Marrakech and Istanbul are one person she keeps meeting, until he flies home in March and she flies on to Peru. By the bridge she can say what home is, and it is not a country.

**Mood:**
Sunlit, forward-moving, generous. Not restless and not running away — the song explicitly refuses that reading. Travel as accumulation rather than escape.

**Message:**
"I don't count countries, I count who I met."

**Genre:**
Global pop with real world percussion. Think: the hand-drum bed of a Rosalía track x the daylight of a Kacey Musgraves chorus x a Bomba Estéreo groove, sung in plain US English. Nylon-string guitar, djembe and congas, bright synths, a four-on-the-floor kick under real drums.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 116 BPM, 4/4
- Key: G major (open, sunlit)
- No modulation. The final chorus widens instead, and swaps the city names in the hook for the ones from verse two.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, close and conversational in the verses, wide and open in the choruses, near-spoken in the bridge.
- Backing vocals: stacked female harmonies in the choruses, a wordless line over the second chorus, a chanted stack on the post-chorus.

**Instrumentation:**
- Bright nylon-string acoustic guitar (main spine, a repeating figure)
- Hand-drum ensemble: djembe, congas, shaker, tambourine, a talking drum in the break
- Round electric bass
- Four-on-the-floor kick under the choruses
- Bright plucked synth arpeggio and a synth lead doubling the vocal
- Stadium claps
- Low string pad from the end of verse two through the bridge
- Ambient textures: a low airport concourse hum, a distant departure chime, a departure-board flutter, and the hard mechanical thud of a passport stamp

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: an airport before sunrise, a stamp, a stranger who doesn't ask her name
- Verse 1: three cities, three people, one line each
- Pre-chorus: her mother asking when she's going to settle down
- Chorus: the hook, wide and sunlit, the cities living in her body
- Verse 2: the recurring one — Berlin, Marrakech, two stamps side by side in Istanbul, then two different flights
- Bridge: the shoebox under the bed, the old passport kept, the definition of home
- Final chorus: the widest, with the second verse's cities in the hook
- Post-chorus: chanted, a stamp on every downbeat
- Outro: the same airport, the same stranger, and this time she knows her

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – guitar, shaker, concourse hum]

```
Ink on the page and an airport at dawn,
Boarding pass in my teeth, half my sleep gone.
A stranger stamps the book, never asks me my name,
One more little square that says I came.
```

- Scene: An international departures hall before sunrise. Half the lights on, cleaners still working, almost nobody in the queue.
- Shot: Macro on a passport open on a counter and a stamp coming down hard; the ink square landing among others.
- Cut to: Her in the queue with a paper coffee cup and a bag on one shoulder, tired, patient, alone.
- Her reaction: A small nod to the officer that isn't returned.
- Lighting: Cold blue pre-dawn through the glass, hard fluorescent overhead.

### [verse 1]

```
Lisbon was a girl with paint under her nails,
Yellow tram at midnight, she taught me how to fail.
Tokyo was a boy by a vending machine,
Two cans of hot coffee, no words in between.
```

- Scene: Three cities, three people, one vignette each, all shot as memory.
- Shots: Lisbon — a girl with paint on her hands laughing on the step of a yellow tram at night, the tram pulling away. Tokyo — a boy in a coat by a lit vending machine on an empty street, two hot cans coming out, one handed over, neither of them speaking.
- Emotion: Fondness without nostalgia. These are people, not postcards.
- Lighting: Lisbon warm sodium; Tokyo cold neon on wet tarmac.

```
Oaxaca was a kitchen and a stranger's hands,
Feeding me like family in words I couldn't understand.
Every border I crossed, somebody crossed it with me,
I carry all of them like ink you can't see.
```

- Scene: A courtyard kitchen full of people, one of them a woman feeding her.
- Shots: Hands putting a plate in front of her. The room talking over her head in a language she doesn't have. Her laughing anyway and being talked to anyway.
- Cut to: A slow tilt across her own forearms and hands, unmarked — the ink you can't see.
- Emotion: Being taken in.
- Lighting: Warm afternoon through a doorway, smoke and steam in the light.

### [pre-chorus]

```
My mom keeps asking when I'm gonna settle down,
I said, "Mama, I'm settled, just in a lot of towns."
Nothing's chasing me out, I'm chasing every bit of it,
Nobody comes back the same, and that's the point.
```

- Scene: A video call from a hostel bunk or a station bench.
- Shot: Her mother's face on a phone screen (composited), affectionate and unconvinced; her laughing and shaking her head.
- Emotion: Warm friction. This is not a song about running away and it says so.
- Lighting: Whatever light she's in — a bunk lamp, a platform strip light.

### [chorus – wide, sunlit, full percussion]

```
Passport stamps and a heart full of places,
Every page is a name, every name has a face.
Lisbon in my laugh and Tokyo in my walk,
A little Oaxaca in the way that I talk.
Passport stamps and a heart full of places,
I don't count countries, I count who I met.
Everywhere I land, I leave a piece, take one,
Passport stamps and a heart full of places, I've only just begun.
```

- Scene: The daylight montage. Movement in every frame.
- Shots: A train window with a country going past. A market at full noon. A beach at the wrong end of the day. A mountain road from the back of a truck. Every third cut is a face, not a place — the girl, the boy, the kitchen, a bus driver, a hostel receptionist.
- Emotion: Joy, and the specific joy of being in motion with other people.
- Lighting: The brightest and widest the video gets; real sun.

### [verse 2]

```
Berlin was a bass line in a basement till four,
Marrakech was the same boy waiting at my door.
We got two stamps side by side in Istanbul,
Window seat and aisle seat, and that was beautiful.
```

- Scene: The recurring one. He is the only person in the video who appears more than once.
- Shots: A basement club, low ceiling, a bass bin, the two of them in the crowd not dancing so much as standing in it. A riad doorway in Marrakech at dusk, him already there. A counter in Istanbul, two passports handed over together, two stamps landing.
- Emotion: The pleasure of being expected somewhere.
- Lighting: Berlin red and dark; Marrakech dusty pink; Istanbul flat airport white.

```
Then he flew home in March and I flew on to Peru,
Cried at the gate, then laughed, cause that's what I do.
Some people are a chapter and some are just a line,
But every single one of them is written down my spine.
```

- Scene: A departure gate. Two directions.
- Shots: Two boarding passes in one hand, then one going to him. Her crying properly, unattractively, at a gate, and then laughing at herself and wiping her face. A departure board flipping over.
- Emotion: Loss handled well. Not a tragedy, and not nothing.
- Lighting: Gate-lounge fluorescent, unflattering, honest.

### [pre-chorus]

```
Sometimes it's Tuesday and I wake up in a bed,
Don't know the city, or the language in my head.
Nothing's chasing me out, I'm chasing every bit of it,
Nobody comes back the same, and that's the point.
```

- Scene: A room she doesn't recognise, first thing.
- Shot: Ceiling, then a window, then a street sign out of focus that she can't read; her sitting up on the edge of the bed and laughing at herself.
- Emotion: Disorientation with no fear in it.
- Lighting: Whatever comes through an unfamiliar curtain.

### [chorus]

```
Passport stamps and a heart full of places,
Every page is a name, every name has a face.
Lisbon in my laugh and Tokyo in my walk,
A little Oaxaca in the way that I talk.
Passport stamps and a heart full of places,
I don't count countries, I count who I met.
Everywhere I land, I leave a piece, take one,
Passport stamps and a heart full of places, I've only just begun.
```

- Scene: The montage again, faster, and now with her in more of the frames rather than behind the camera.
- Shots: Other people's hands taking her phone to take her picture. Her in a group at a table. Her being pulled into a dance she doesn't know.
- Emotion: Bigger, less solitary.
- Lighting: Saturated afternoon into evening.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: No lyrics. The percussion break.
- Shots: Feet on six different surfaces cut on the drums — cobbles, sand, tiled station floor, a metal gangway, a dirt road, an escalator. Hands on a drum in a street band. A stamp coming down in slow motion. A window seat with a wing and cloud. A door opening onto a room she's never seen.
- Lighting: Every shot a different light, cut hard against each other.

### [bridge – guitar and pad, near-spoken]

```
There's a shoebox under my bed at mama's house,
Boarding passes, ticket stubs, a napkin with a map drawn out.
The old book ran out of pages, they gave me a new one,
But the first one stays in the box, cause that's where I come from.
```

- Scene: Her childhood bedroom at her mother's house. The only still, quiet place in the video.
- Shots: A shoebox pulled out from under a bed. Boarding passes, stubs, a napkin with a map biro'd onto it (composited, never legible). Two passports side by side, one battered and full, one new and empty.
- Emotion: Tenderness toward her own history.
- Lighting: A single bedside lamp, everything else off.

```
Home was never a country, it was never a street,
Home is every person who walked a mile with me.
When they ask me where I'm from, I say it's complicated,
I'm from everywhere I've loved and everyone who waited.
```

- Scene: The faces from the whole video, returning.
- Shots: Quick returns to the Lisbon girl, the Tokyo boy, the Oaxaca kitchen, the recurring one at the gate, her mother on the phone — each one held a beat longer than it was the first time.
- Emotion: The turn. The answer to the question the song has been asked twice.
- Lighting: Each memory as it was; the bedroom lamp between them.

### [final chorus – widest, city names changed]

```
Passport stamps and a heart full of places,
Every page is a name, every name has a face.
Berlin in my hips and Istanbul in my eyes,
A little bit of Peru in the way I say goodbye.
Passport stamps and a heart full of places,
I don't count countries, I count who I met.
Everywhere I land, I leave a piece, take one,
Passport stamps and a heart full of places, I've only just begun.
```

- Scene: Everything at once, the widest and brightest.
- Shots: All the cities, all the faces, all the transport, cut on the beat, ending on her walking into a departures hall with the new passport in her hand.
- Emotion: Complete, and not finished.
- Lighting: Full daylight throughout.

### [post-chorus – chanted, a stamp on every downbeat]

```
Stamp it, one more page,
Every face is a place I'd never trade.
Stamp it, one more page,
Heart full of places, I'm on my way.
```

- Scene: Stamps, in rhythm.
- Shots: Four stamps coming down on four downbeats, four different desks, four different hands. Between them, four faces.
- Emotion: Delight, rhythm.
- Lighting: Hard, flat, high-contrast counter light.

### [outro – guitar and shaker, the airport again]

```
Ink on the page and an airport at dawn,
Somebody new in the seat where you were gone.
The stranger stamps the book and this time knows my name,
And I smile, cause I came.
```

- Scene: The same departures hall, the same hour, a year on.
- Shots: The same counter, the same officer. This time she looks up and says something. Her face. Final shot: the new passport closing on the counter, her hand taking it, and her walking out of frame toward the gates. Hold on the empty counter.
- Emotion: Peace and continuation.
- Lighting: The same cold pre-dawn blue as the intro, unchanged.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the stamp coming down in macro slow motion with *"Passport stamps and a heart full of places"* on screen.
- **The challenge:** post your own passport spread or a face from each trip, one per beat, to the post-chorus chant.
- **The caption line:** *"I don't count countries, I count who I met."*
- **The turn:** *"Home was never a country, it was never a street."*
- **The quote:** *"Some people are a chapter and some are just a line, but every single one of them is written down my spine."*
- **The bookend:** the officer who doesn't ask her name in the intro and knows it in the outro.
