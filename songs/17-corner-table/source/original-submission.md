# Corner Table — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A whole love story told through one café's corner table across a year. September: she pretends to read a paperback so she has a reason to sit there while he works two tables over. November: he orders her coffee before she reaches the counter. December: the street lights go up and they share the table. January: he stops coming, and she keeps showing up anyway. March: he walks back in with a reason and no way of having called her, because in a year of ten o'clocks they never swapped numbers. The barista sees all of it and never says a word.

**Mood:**
Quiet, warm, patient. A slow burn with a smile in it. Understated rather than dramatic; the big feeling is in the small repeated gesture of showing up.

**Message:**
"It was never the table. It's who keeps showing up."

**Genre:**
Understated indie-pop with a live-band feel. Think: Maggie Rogers x Bleachers restraint, with a Kacey Musgraves-level attention to small detail and a Phoebe Bridgers-style wit in the verses.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 110 BPM, 4/4
- Key: C major, warm and plain
- No modulation; the final chorus lifts through arrangement (strings entering for the first time) rather than key.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, close and conversational in the verses, open and warm in the choruses, near-spoken at the top of the bridge.
- Backing vocals: soft stacked harmonies in the choruses, a wordless line over the instrumental, the last outro line doubled.

**Instrumentation:**
- Warm clean electric guitar (the spine, a two-bar picked figure)
- Upright piano
- Soft round electric bass
- Drums: soft kick, brushed rim click, light hi-hat, soft snare from the first chorus
- A second, lightly overdriven guitar for chorus counter-lines
- Low string pad under the second chorus
- Restrained real strings (violins, one cello line) in the final chorus only
- Café textures: room tone, milk steamer, cups on saucers, a shop bell

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: the table, the chair, the barista; a habit described with affection
- Verse 1: September, the paperback, two people pretending
- Pre-chorus: telling herself it's nothing while showing up every week
- Chorus: the hook, sure and warm, the year seen through one window
- Verse 2: November he knows her order; December they share the table; January he's gone
- Pre-chorus 2: stubborn, the foam going flat, she stays anyway
- Instrumental: winter in the café, the wordless line, the shop bell
- Bridge: March, the door, his reason, her laugh, "sit down"
- Final chorus: strings, the napkin, the number, the hook twice
- Post-chorus: the barista and two leaves in the foam
- Outro: the intro again with two coffees; "it's who keeps showing up"

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – guitar figure, café room tone, close voice]

```
Same chair, same window, same wobble in the leg,
Same latte going cold beside a book I never read.
The girl behind the counter draws a leaf into the foam,
She's watched this whole thing happen and she's never said a word.
```

- Scene: A small independent café, early, the corner table by the window. Establish the geography once: the door with a bell, the counter, the corner table, the window onto a narrow street.
- Shot: A locked-off wide from the counter's point of view, the corner table empty, then her sitting down into frame. Close-up of the chair leg rocking on the tile. A latte set down beside a closed paperback.
- Cut to: The barista's hands pouring a leaf into the foam, then a glance up at the corner table and back down, no expression.
- Emotion: Fond, settled, a habit she loves and hasn't examined.
- Lighting: Soft overcast daylight through the window, warm interior lamps.

### [verse 1]

```
September, I was hiding in a paperback,
Same page for an hour, eyes on the door instead.
You came in shaking rain out of your jacket,
Sat two tables over and I lost my place.
```

- Scene: September. Rain on the window. She has the paperback open and hasn't turned a page.
- Shot: Over her shoulder onto the book, her thumb on the same page; her eyes lift to the door every time the bell rings. The door opens and Kai comes in shaking rain off a jacket, orders, and takes the table two along by the wall socket, laptop out.
- Cut to: Her looking down at the book and realising she has no idea where she was.
- Emotion: Caught out by herself, a private smile.
- Lighting: Grey rain light outside, warm inside, a lamp on the wall behind him.

```
I pretended I was reading, you pretended you were working,
Both of us just stealing looks across the room.
Then you asked me what the book was, and I couldn't tell you,
Because I'd never made it past the first line.
```

- Scene: The same morning, later. Two people not looking at each other.
- Shot: A slow two-shot with the empty table between them; her glancing up as he glances down, his glancing up as she looks back at the page. Then him standing to get a refill and pausing at her table to ask about the book.
- Cut to: Her turning the book over to check the cover, then laughing.
- Emotion: Awkward, charmed, honest.
- Lighting: Same as above; the rain has eased.

### [pre-chorus]

```
And every week I told myself, this isn't anything,
Just coffee and a window and a habit I can't kick.
But I kept coming back at ten, and you kept coming in at ten,
And the girl behind the counter kept on smiling like she knew.
```

- Scene: A compressed run of October mornings, always ten o'clock.
- Shot: Jump cuts on the same locked-off wide: she sits down; he comes in; she sits down; he comes in; different clothes, same seats. A clock on the wall reading ten every time.
- Cut to: The barista behind the counter, watching the door open on him, a small smile she hides by turning to the machine.
- Emotion: Denial that is really anticipation.
- Lighting: Autumn light getting lower and warmer week by week.

### [chorus – open, warm, the full band]

```
Same corner table, different me, and you still sat down,
Watched a whole year through that window, and you're still around.
I came in pretending, now I'm not pretending anymore,
Same corner table, and I'm not who I was before.
Same corner table, different me, and you still sat down.
```

- Scene: The chorus is the window as a calendar. The table stays; the world outside it changes.
- Shot: A long slow push-in on the corner table through the window from the street, the seasons dissolving behind the glass: rain, falling leaves, string lights, snow, blossom. She is at the table in each; he is opposite her in most.
- Cut to: Him pulling out the chair opposite and sitting down, from her point of view, in different light each time.
- Emotion: Warm certainty.
- Lighting: The full cycle of a year in one push-in.

### [verse 2]

```
November, you got to the counter before I did,
Said, oat milk, extra hot, and turned around and grinned.
I didn't know you'd been paying attention,
I'd been so careful to look like I wasn't.
```

- Scene: November. She comes in with her coat still buttoned and he's already at the counter.
- Shot: Him at the counter, ordering, then turning with the grin; her stopped in the doorway with the bell still ringing. Close-up on the barista sliding the cup across with the leaf already in the foam.
- Cut to: Her taking the cup, pretending it's nothing, failing.
- Emotion: Found out, delighted.
- Lighting: Cold blue outside, deep warm inside, steam catching the window light.

```
By the time the lights went up along the street outside,
We were sharing one table and pretending it was small.
Then January, the door kept opening and it was never you,
And she wiped your side of the table twice and didn't say a thing.
```

- Scene: December into January. Two cups on the corner table, elbows almost touching. Then one cup.
- Shot: From the street, string lights being strung across the road, the two of them laughing inside behind the glass. Hard cut to January: snow, the same table, one cup, her eyes going to the door every time the bell rings and it's someone else.
- Cut to: The barista wiping the empty side of the table, a beat, wiping it again, walking away.
- Emotion: The warmest and the coldest verse in the song, back to back.
- Lighting: December gold and coloured lights; January flat white and blue.

### [pre-chorus 2]

```
And every week I told myself, it wasn't anything,
Just coffee and a window and a chair across from mine.
But I kept coming back at ten, and the door stayed shut at ten,
And the foam went flat, and I let it, and I stayed.
```

- Scene: The same jump-cut run as pre-chorus one, but now the door doesn't open.
- Shot: The locked-off wide: she sits down, the door stays shut; she sits down, the door stays shut. The clock reading ten. Close-up on the latte, the leaf in the foam slowly flattening.
- Cut to: Her not leaving. Coat still on, still there at the end of the shot.
- Emotion: Stubborn, quietly heartbroken, loyal to something she can't name.
- Lighting: Winter light, grey and low.

### [chorus]

(repeat)

- Scene: The year-in-a-window push-in again, but it stops at winter and holds on the empty chair a beat too long before the last line.
- Shot: Same push-in, but the dissolve slows through January and she is alone in the frame for the last two lines. On the final "you still sat down" the chair opposite is empty.
- Emotion: The hook sung with hope rather than proof.
- Lighting: Ends in flat winter light.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: The café in winter with no lyrics. The guitar figure and piano; the wordless vocal line.
- Shots: The espresso machine steaming in slow motion. Her at the corner table reading, actually reading now, a page turning. The barista drawing a leaf in a cup for another customer. Snow on the window from inside. The bell above the door, still, then ringing on the last chord.
- Lighting: Cold light from the window, one warm lamp over the counter.

### [bridge – spring, near-spoken, then building]

```
March, and the window's open, there's blossom on the street,
I stopped bringing the book, I stopped saving you the seat.
Then the bell above the door, and the rain-shaken jacket,
And you say, it's a long story, is anybody sitting here.
```

- Scene: March. The window propped open; blossom blowing along the pavement outside. She has a coffee and nothing else; her bag is on the chair opposite.
- Shot: A close-up on the open window and the blossom. Her hands around the cup, no book. The bell rings; she doesn't look up this time; a shadow falls across the table; Kai in the same jacket, rain in his hair, standing at the chair.
- Cut to: Her looking up slowly.
- Emotion: Everything at once, held very still.
- Lighting: The first real sun of the year, low and gold through the open window.

```
You say, my dad got sick, I went home, I didn't have your number,
And I laugh, because a year of ten o'clocks and I never gave it to you.
I could say a hundred things about the winter,
But I push the sugar over and I say, sit down.
```

- Scene: The same moment, continuous.
- Shot: His face as he explains, tired and honest, cropped close. Her laugh breaking out of her, hand over her mouth. Then a slow tilt down to her hand pushing the sugar bowl across the table and her moving the bag off the chair.
- Cut to: The barista at the counter, who has stopped what she was doing and is holding a cup halfway to a saucer.
- Emotion: Relief, forgiveness that doesn't need to be said, the whole winter let go in one gesture.
- Lighting: Sun on the table, motes in the air.

### [final chorus – strings enter, the fullest arrangement]

```
Same corner table, different me, and you still sat down,
Watched a whole year through that window, and you're still around.
I came in pretending, now I'm not pretending anymore,
Here's my number on a napkin, should have done it long before.
Same corner table, different me, and you still sat down.
Same corner table, different me, and you still sat down.
```

- Scene: The push-in through the window one last time, and this time it runs all the way through to spring and holds on the two of them.
- Shot: The year dissolves again, faster, and settles on March: the two of them at the table, sun on the glass. Close-up on her writing a number on a paper napkin and sliding it across (no legible digits). Him pocketing it without looking, grinning.
- Cut to: The chair pulled out; him sitting down; her watching it happen.
- Emotion: Full, warm, earned.
- Lighting: Spring sun, the warmest frame in the video.

### [post-chorus – guitar, piano and voice]

```
The girl behind the counter draws two leaves into the foam,
Sets them down between us, doesn't say a word, and goes.
She's watched the whole thing happen from the first page to the last,
She never had to read it, she was always there.
```

- Scene: The barista's moment.
- Shot: Close-up on her hands pouring two cups, a leaf in each. Her walking them to the corner table, setting them down between the two of them, and turning away without a word. Then, from behind, the smallest shrug of her shoulders as she goes.
- Cut to: The two cups side by side on the table, steam rising, the closed paperback pushed to the edge.
- Emotion: Gentle wit; the witness gets her line.
- Lighting: Warm, soft.

### [outro – the intro figure alone]

```
Same chair, same window, same wobble in the leg,
Two coffees going warm beside a book I'll never read.
It was never the table, it was never this street,
It's who keeps showing up, it's who keeps showing up.
```

- Scene: The intro reframed: the same locked-off wide from the counter, but there are two people at the table.
- Shot: The chair leg rocking on the tile, his foot steadying it. The two cups. The book, closed, pushed aside. Then the wide holding on both of them talking, the street bright outside.
- Cut to: The final frame is the same wide as the first frame of the video, held, with two people in it instead of one.
- Emotion: Settled, certain, small and enormous.
- Lighting: Spring morning, clean and warm.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the year-in-a-window push-in (chorus one) with *"same corner table, different me, and you still sat down"* on screen; a natural vertical cut for café and slow-burn edits.
- **The challenge:** the locked-off wide with jump cuts on the same table across weeks: people post their own "same seat, same time" ritual, a café, a bus stop, a library desk, with the pre-chorus under it.
- **The caption line:** *"It was never the table. It's who keeps showing up."*
- **The knife and the laugh:** *"I didn't have your number / a year of ten o'clocks and I never gave it to you"* as a duet-able audio for couples who took too long.
- **The witness:** the barista's two leaves in the foam as its own four-second clip.
