# Class of Forever — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
Graduation night in a small American town. The last bell rings and nobody
leaves. One class goes from the emptying corridor to the parking lot to the
football field at midnight, swears to meet in the same spot in ten years, and
the song is honest about the odds.

**Mood:**
Loud, warm, slightly frantic. The joy of a night you already know you will be
describing for the rest of your life. Grateful, with a lump in its throat.

**Message:**
"I won't ask you for a decade. Just stay out on this field with me right now."

**Genre:**
Anthemic pop-rock / heartland alternative. Think: Bleachers x The 1975 guitar
ring, a Taylor Swift narrative eye, a Springsteen final chorus.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 116 BPM, 4/4. Key: B♭ major (bright, gang-vocal friendly)
- No key change; the lift comes from a second drum kit, a gang vocal and an
  added piano layer in the final chorus.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead: young adult female, conversational in the verses, full-throated and
  slightly raw on the hook.
- Backing: stacked harmonies in the choruses, a real gang vocal of eight to
  twelve voices on the last chorus and the post-chorus chant.

**Instrumentation:**
- Clean chiming electric guitar with long quarter-note delay (the intro voice)
- Warm overdriven rhythm guitar; a third layer for the countermelody
- Melodic electric bass mixed forward; dry kick and snare, floor toms, ride
- Hammond-style organ under the choruses, piano only in the final chorus
- Textures: a real school bell, guitar feedback, sprinkler hiss, field
  ambience, crowd stomp, morning birdsong

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: the last bell, and nobody moving
- Verses: the locker sticker and the trophy case, then midnight on the fifty
- Pre-choruses: everyone holding their keys and refusing to start the car
- Chorus: caps, floodlights, the hook, the whole class at once
- Instrumental: the drive from the school to the field
- Bridge: the honest turn — half of them will not come back, and half will
- Final chorus and post-chorus: the loudest moment, the crowd chant
- Outro: sunrise, empty bleachers, one car left in the lot

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro — school bell, chiming guitar, low tom]

```
Last bell rang and nobody moved,
Half of us just stood there in the hall.
Somebody's speaker in the parking lot,
Playing the same four chords for us all.
Nothing but floor wax and fluorescent light,
And now they want the keys and the building back.
```

- Scene: A high school corridor at four in the afternoon on the last day.
- Shot: A long lens down the empty corridor, fluorescent tubes, a floor so polished it doubles everything; a dozen students standing still in it, not talking. Cut to: The propped-open double doors at the end and a car stereo playing in the lot beyond, seen as a rectangle of daylight.
- Emotion: Anticlimax. Nobody knows what the last bell is supposed to feel like.
- Lighting: Flat institutional fluorescent, one hot blade of daylight from the doors.

### [verse 1 — kick and snare in]

```
I scrape a sticker off the locker door,
It leaves the shape of it, four years of glue.
Coach is holding the gym doors open,
Saying, go on, get out, like he isn't crying too.

There's a dent in the trophy case from sophomore year,
Nobody ever said whose fault it was.
I put my hand flat on the cold glass,
And I say thank you to a building, just because.
```

- Scene: The building itself, item by item, before it becomes somebody else's.
- Shot: Macro on a thumbnail working at a sticker edge; the ghost of it left behind in adhesive; her breath on the metal. Then the gym doorway, the coach holding it open with one arm and looking hard at the floor. Cut to: The trophy case, a spidered crack low in the glass, her palm flat against it and the reflection of the corridor behind her.
- Emotion: Affection for an ugly building, arriving as a surprise.
- Lighting: Fluorescent throughout, deliberately unromantic; the only warmth is the daylight bleeding in from the gym.

### [pre-chorus 1 — hats open, tambourine]

```
Nobody's engine is actually running,
Everybody's keys are in their hand.
We keep saying that we're gonna go now,
Then nobody goes. I think we understand.
```

- Scene: The parking lot at golden hour. Twenty cars, nobody driving.
- Shot: A slow track along a row of open car doors, kids sitting sideways in driver's seats with their feet on the tarmac. Cut to: A close-up of a hand turning a keyring over and over without putting the key anywhere.
- Emotion: Everyone waiting for someone else to end it.
- Lighting: Low gold sun straight down the row, long shadows, heat shimmer off the roofs.

### [chorus 1 — full band, caps up]

```
We're the class of forever, no goodbye's gonna stick,
Throw it up, let it fall, we were here and it was quick.
Fifty caps in the floodlights, half of them come down wrong,
Somebody's crying, somebody's laughing, somebody starts the song.
We've got no five-year plan, we've got the whole of the night,
And a field full of people I will know for life.
Turn the headlights on the grass, take one more pic,
We're the class of forever, no goodbye's gonna stick.
```

- Scene: The football field under floodlights, gowns over jeans, caps going up.
- Shot: Low and wide from the grass as fifty mortarboards leave frame at once; hold on the empty sky for a beat; then they come back down badly and everyone ducks. Cut to: Handheld inside the crowd, spinning, faces, hugging, one boy on someone's shoulders.
- Emotion: Total release. This is the loudest the video ever gets.
- Lighting: Sodium floodlights, deep blue sky behind, headlights raking across from the parked cars.

### [verse 2 — rim clicks, floor tom, clean arpeggio]

```
Midnight on the fifty and the sprinklers kick on,
Nobody runs for it, we let it soak us through.
He's enlisting in the fall, she's flying out on Tuesday,
And I'm staying right here, and that's alright too.

We swear on a helmet somebody stole from the shed,
Same spot, ten years, whoever can come.
Then we sit in the wet grass till the sky goes gray,
And nobody says the word done.
```

- Scene: Long after the ceremony. Gowns gone, shoes off, the field to themselves.
- Shot: The sprinklers coming up in sequence across the fifty-yard line; nobody moves; the water crossing them in slow motion. Cut to: Three of them lying on their backs in the wet grass; a stolen helmet passed hand to hand; each one puts a palm on it and says nothing.
- Emotion: Different futures sitting comfortably next to each other for one more hour.
- Lighting: Floodlights switched off; only the scoreboard glow, phone torches and the first gray of dawn.

### [pre-chorus 2 — bigger build]

```
Somebody's mom keeps flashing headlights at the gate,
Somebody's brother says he'll drive us all around.
We keep saying that we're gonna go now,
And then nobody goes. Nobody makes a sound.
```

- Scene: The gate, and the adult world politely waiting.
- Shot: Headlights flashing twice through the chain-link from the parking lot, seen from the field. Cut to: A wide of the whole group ignoring it, and one girl waving without turning around.
- Emotion: The first time anybody has ever wanted to be told to come home later.
- Lighting: Two hard headlight beams through fence diamonds, everything else near-black.

### [chorus 2 — countermelody guitar, group vocal on the last line]

```
We're the class of forever, no goodbye's gonna stick,
Throw it up, let it fall, we were here and it was quick.
Fifty caps in the floodlights, half of them come down wrong,
Somebody's crying, somebody's laughing, somebody starts the song.
We've got no five-year plan, we've got the whole of the night,
And a field full of people I will know for life.
Turn the headlights on the grass, take one more pic,
We're the class of forever, no goodbye's gonna stick.
```

- Scene: The chorus replayed, but now it is the middle of the night and the light is theirs, not the school's.
- Shot: Cars reversed onto the running track with headlights aimed at the grass; the whole class dancing in the beams; long silhouettes thrown fifty feet. Cut to: A phone held up for a group photo, everyone piling in, someone jumping in late.
- Emotion: Bigger and looser than chorus one, and slightly wilder.
- Lighting: Only headlights and phone screens; hard white key, black surround.

### [instrumental] — scene block only

- Scene: The drive from the school to the field, cut as a single fast sequence.
- Shot: Five cars leaving the lot in a line; a trunk full of shoes and gowns; feet out of a rear window; the school sign shrinking in a wing mirror; someone standing up through a sunroof with a cap in each hand. Cut to: The empty corridor from the intro, now dark, with one janitor's light on and the floor still shining.
- Emotion: Motion and noise, then two seconds of stillness that hurts.
- Lighting: Streetlight strobing through car interiors; the corridor lit by one exit sign.

### [bridge — one clean guitar and voice, then toms]

```
I know how this goes, I have watched it before,
The group chat goes quiet by the second fall.
Half of us won't drive back when the ten years land,
And half of us will, and half is not that small.
So I won't ask you for a decade or a vow,
Just stay out on this field with me right now.
```

- Scene: Her alone at the fifty, talking to the group without looking at them.
- Shot: A slow locked-off wide, her sitting up in the grass, the others out of focus behind her. Cut to: Four faces in close-up, one at a time, listening and not answering.
- Emotion: The most honest thirty seconds in the song. No irony, no bravado.
- Lighting: Blue pre-dawn, the scoreboard finally switched off mid-line.

### [final chorus — gang vocal, biggest arrangement]

```
We're the class of forever, no goodbye's gonna stick,
Throw it up, let it fall, we were here and it was quick.
Fifty caps in the wet grass, we can leave them where they land,
Somebody's crying, somebody's laughing, somebody takes my hand.
We've got no five-year plan, we've got the rest of the night,
And a field full of people I will know for life.
Kill the headlights, one more song before we split,
We're the class of forever, no goodbye's gonna stick.
```

- Scene: The last stand. Everyone up, arms round shoulders, singing at the sky.
- Shot: A drone rising off the field: a ring of people, caps scattered in the wet grass, the headlights going out one pair at a time as it climbs. Cut to: Ground level, a hand finding hers in the middle of the line.
- Emotion: Peak. This is the frame people screenshot.
- Lighting: Headlights killed on the seventh line, leaving only dawn; the video's warmest light arrives from the horizon, not from the school.

### [post-chorus — crowd chant, stomp]

```
Oh, no goodbye's gonna stick,
Oh, we were here and it was quick.
Same spot, same gate, same ten years,
Oh, no goodbye's gonna stick.
Hands up if you're never getting over it,
Oh, no goodbye's gonna stick.
```

- Scene: The chant, shot like a crowd rather than a cast.
- Shot: Handheld in the middle of the group at chest height, faces above the lens, hands going up on the fifth line. Cut to: The stolen helmet upside down in the grass with car keys thrown into it.
- Emotion: Ridiculous, sincere, entirely committed.
- Lighting: First direct sun across the top of the bleachers.

### [outro — one clean guitar, birdsong]

```
Sun's coming up on the empty bleachers,
Somebody's shoes still out on the grass.
I'm the last one in the parking lot,
And I'm not in a hurry, and I'm not sad.
```

- Scene: Everyone gone. One car.
- Shot: Empty bleachers in full morning light; a single pair of white sneakers left on the fifty; a slow pan to the parking lot with one car in it. Cut to: Her sitting on the hood, gown folded on her lap, not looking at her phone.
- Emotion: Calm. Nothing unresolved.
- Lighting: Clean gold morning, sprinkler mist still hanging over the grass.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** fifty caps leaving frame at once under floodlights, held on the empty sky for a full beat before they fall back badly.
- **The headlights shot:** cars reversed onto the running track with their beams on the grass and the whole class dancing in them.
- **The challenge:** post your own last-day corridor or field with the caption line over it, and tag the people you swore the ten years with.
- **The caption line:** *"We've got no five-year plan, we've got the whole of the night."*
- **The quote:** *"I won't ask you for a decade or a vow. Just stay out on this field with me right now."*
