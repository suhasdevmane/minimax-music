# Glow Up Season — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
The post-breakup glow up, played as a festival rather than a revenge plot. He left; she assumed the light went with him. Instead she got a haircut, ran the river loop, walked into the gym she was scared of, bought the red dress she'd been saving in her cart, signed a lease with a view and painted the walls a colour he'd have hated. The song is a montage of small wins that add up to one big one: she is not glowing *at* him, she is just glowing.

**Mood:**
Sunlit, bouncy, cheeky, unbothered. Dance-pop with a drop and a grin. The joy is real and earned, never bitter.

**Message:**
"He thought he took the light with him. Turns out I was the light."

**Genre:**
Bright dance-pop with a drop chorus and a male pop-rap feature on the bridge. Think: Dua Lipa x Doja Cat's pop side, with a bridge that could sit on a Jack Harlow record — smooth, warm, unbothered.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 122 BPM, 4/4
- Key: A major
- Final chorus lifts one step to B major with the fullest arrangement.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, playful and conversational in the verses, belted and bright in the chorus.
- Singer B: male pop-rapper, laid-back and confident, takes the bridge alone (twelve bars) and harmonises under the final chorus.
- Backing vocals: stacked female harmonies in the chorus, a chant post-chorus, male and female layers on the final hook.

**Instrumentation:**
- Bouncing synth bass and a four-on-the-floor kick
- Bright supersaw chords and a plucky lead synth
- Tight hi-hats, shakers, claps on every beat in the pre-chorus
- Disco-style guitar chops (second verse onward)
- Brass-style synth stabs (second and final chorus)
- Chopped, pitched vocal hook (post-chorus and instrumental)
- Sub-bass, kick and claps only under the rap bridge
- Warm pads (pre-choruses and outro)
- Ear candy: alarm-clock synth blip, camera-shutter click on the beat, reverse swells, filter sweeps

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: six a.m., alarm, trainers, blinds open, a decision
- Verses: the montage of small wins, funny and specific
- Pre-chorus: what he said versus what happened, delivered with a raised eyebrow
- Chorus: the drop, the sun, the hook
- Post-chorus: the chant, built for the edit
- Bridge: the male rapper describes her from across the room; the beat rebuilds
- Final chorus: key change, everyone in, hands up
- Outro: a rooftop at last light, calm, tomorrow is Monday and she's fine

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – filtered kick, synth blip, half-awake and amused]

```
6 a.m. and I didn't hit snooze,
Laced up the white ones, got nothing to lose.
Playlist loud and the blinds pulled wide,
This is the year I stop hiding the light.
```

- Scene: A small bedroom just before sunrise. Phone alarm on the nightstand.
- Shot: Close-up on her hand reaching for the phone and, instead of snoozing, turning the alarm off and sitting up. White trainers by the door, laces being tied.
- Cut to: Her yanking the blinds open; dawn light hits the room and the filter opens on the beat.
- Emotion: Sleepy, then a small private grin. A decision being made.
- Lighting: Blue pre-dawn to warm gold in one cut.

### [verse 1]

```
Booked the salon on a Tuesday afternoon,
Told her, "take it shorter, I've got somewhere to be soon."
Watched the old length fall like it was never mine,
Walked out lighter with the sun on my mind.
```

- Scene: A bright salon, a Tuesday, almost empty.
- Shot: Her in the chair, the stylist holding up a length of hair with a questioning look; she nods, bigger than the stylist expected. The first cut. Hair falling to the floor in slow motion.
- Cut to: Her walking out onto the street with the new length, shaking it out, sun on her face.
- Emotion: Nervous for one beat, then delighted.
- Lighting: Clean white salon light, then hard afternoon sun.

```
Group chat blowing up, they said, "who is she,"
Same girl, new light, that's the whole story.
Ran the long loop by the river, lungs on fire,
Every mile a little further from the liar.
```

- Scene: A group chat exploding (composited); a river path at dawn.
- Shot: Her phone on a café table lighting up with reactions; her laughing at it. Then the river path: white trainers, breath in the cold, a long tracking shot as she runs.
- Cut to: A faceless figure in a memory (the ex, back to camera, walking away down a corridor) that she literally runs past and out of frame.
- Emotion: Amused, then determined.
- Lighting: Café daylight; the river in cold blue turning gold.

```
Gym at seven, I was scared of the room,
Now the room knows my name and my favourite tune.
Little wins stacking like a tower of gold,
I'm twenty-four and I've never felt this bold.
```

- Scene: A gym at 7 a.m.
- Shot: Her hesitating at the gym door the first time (a flash), then present day: walking in, a nod from the front desk, her song playing, a fist bump from a regular. Gym mirror: a set finished, a small nod to herself.
- Cut to: A stack of tiny moments on the beat — a plant bought, a bill paid, a stretch, a laugh.
- Emotion: Pride without performance.
- Lighting: Bright, clean, morning.

### [pre-chorus]

```

```

- Shot: Her mouthing the first line at her reflection with an eyebrow up, mock-serious. Claps on every beat: lipstick, earring, hair, keys.
- Cut to: The front door opening onto a street flooded with sunrise.
- Emotion: Cheeky, rising.
- Lighting: Warm interior, then blown-out golden street.

### [chorus – the drop, big and bright]

```
It's glow up season, and I'm the sun,
Look at me shining, yeah, look what I've become.
New hair, new dress, new door, new number,
Turned my whole winter into a summer.
It's glow up season, and I'm the sun,
Nobody's shadow, I'm the only one.
Watch me rise, watch me run,
It's glow up season, and I'm the sun.
```

- Scene: The city in full sun; she walks like the street is hers.
- Shot: Wide, tracking from the front; she's in the middle of a crosswalk with the sun behind her, lens flare on the drop. On "new hair, new dress, new door, new number," four fast cuts: the haircut, the red dress on a hanger, a new front door with new keys, a phone screen with a new number being saved (composited).
- Cut to: Her friends catching up to her, arms around her shoulders.
- Emotion: Full joy, arms open.
- Lighting: Saturated, sunny, warm.

### [post-chorus – chant, chopped vocal]

```
Shine, shine, that's the season,

Shine, shine, that's the season,
It's glow up season.
```

- Scene: Four beat-synced impact shots for the edit.
- Shots: Sunlight through her fingers; a spin in the red dress; a splash of water from a bottle in the gym; her laughing straight to camera.
- Emotion: Play.
- Lighting: Bright, high-contrast sun.

- Emotion: Held, holding.

### [chorus]

(repeat)

- Scene: The rooftop at golden hour, everyone dancing.
- Shot: Her dancing with friends; the city behind; a friend filming her on a phone. On "new hair, new dress, new door, new number," the four objects again but in the new home. A drone pull-back on "watch me rise."

### [post-chorus]

(repeat)

### [instrumental]

- Scene: The drop instrumental as a pure montage. The gym, the river, the salon, the paint roller, the rooftop, cut to the vocal chop; a snare build with faster cuts; a filter sweep into a single wide shot of her on the rooftop edge with the city behind, breathing, then the breakdown: a door opens and a man walks into the party.
- Lighting: All the day's lights in sequence.

### [bridge – male rap feature, stripped beat, then rebuild]

```
Saw her cross the room and the whole vibe shifted,
Not the dress, not the hair, it's the way she's lifted.
Heard some guy walked out, thought he took the light,
Now she's running every morning, sleeping every night.
Main character energy, no script, no cue,
She don't need a co-star, she's the whole view.
Every L he handed her, she flipped to a lesson,
Every door he slammed became a door to a blessing.
So if you see her shining, don't ask who she's with,
She's with herself, and honestly, that's the gift.
Glow up season, tell your friends,
Once the sun comes out, it doesn't set again.
```

- Scene: The rooftop party from a newcomer's point of view: a friend-of-a-friend (Kai) arriving late, spotting her across the roof.
- Shot: Kai to camera at the edge of the party, low-key, a drink in hand, narrating. Cutaways to her in the middle of the crowd: laughing, holding court, the red dress, sunset on her. On "running every morning, sleeping every night," quick flashes of the river and the peaceful sleep. On "every door he slammed," the faceless ex's back walking out of a door, then the same door reframed as her new front door opening in sun.
- Cut to: On "glow up season, tell your friends," Kai turning to the person next to him and pointing her out; she catches it and laughs, unbothered, and the beat rebuilds.
- Emotion: Admiration, not pursuit. He's describing a phenomenon.
- Lighting: Blue hour arriving, string lights on.

### [final chorus – key change, everyone in]

It's glow up season, and I'm the sun,

New hair, new dress, new door, new number,

- Lighting: String lights and the last pink in the sky.

### [post-chorus]

(repeat)

### [outro – pad, plucky lead, soft kick, calm]

```
Rooftop, last light, still warm on my face,
Didn't need him back, I just needed the space.
Tomorrow's a Monday and I'm up at six,
Glow up season, and this is just the start of it.
No countdown, no comeback, nothing to prove,

```

- Scene: The party has gone in; she's alone on the roof edge with a blanket, the last light on her face.
- Shot: Slow push-in on her face, eyes closed, warm. Her phone beside her, screen dark, not picked up. A wide shot of her small against the sky. Final shot: back in the bedroom, the alarm set for six (composited), the white trainers by the door, and she smiles and turns off the lamp.
- Emotion: Peace. Not a victory lap, just a life.
- Lighting: Last warm light to a soft lamp to dark.
