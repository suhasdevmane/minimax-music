# Diamond Season — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
Pressure made her a diamond, and now it is diamond season. She spent years on the night shift cleaning the glass over other people's jewellery; the video takes her from that case, down into a dark seam of rock, and out onto a gala staircase. The second verse is rapped by the same female lead.

**Mood:**
Cold, glittering, controlled, then euphoric. Not revenge — arrival. The bridge deliberately refuses the "suffering is beautiful" reading.

**Message:**
"I did not need the pressure to be worth the light. But the pressure came, and I did not break, and that is mine."

**Genre:**
Cinematic dance-pop with a rapped verse. Think: Dua Lipa x Zara Larsson production with a Doechii-style half-time verse — glass plucks, four-on-the-floor, a wide instrumental drop.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 126 BPM, 4/4
- Key: B minor (cold, glittering, room for a big drop)
- No modulation. The final chorus lifts with a choir pad and an octave counter-melody.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female. Sung and conversational in verse one and the choruses; **rapped in verse two**, colder and more precise, over half-time drums.
- Backing vocals: stacked harmonies on the title; a chanted layer over the drop.

**Instrumentation:**
- Glass-like synth pluck (the signature) — alone in the intro, full arpeggio in the choruses, alone again in the outro
- Four-on-the-floor kick and deep side-chained sub-bass; wide saw stacks on the chorus chords
- Tight closed hats and a half-time snare under the rapped verse
- Rising filtered arpeggio and snare builds on the pre-choruses; choir pad from the second one
- Soft piano and one sustained pad for the bridge
- Ambience: fluorescent hum, a shop shutter, cloth on glass, a jeweller's loupe click, a velvet tray sliding, a deep distant rumble

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: a shop after closing, no drums, one pluck, a bet made out loud
- Verses: the four Cs on a staff-room poster, then the rapped ten-year account
- Pre-chorus: two thousand degrees, a mile of stone, one dead stop
- Chorus: full dance-pop; the post-chorus is the drop with a chant over it
- Instrumental: arpeggio through a filter sweep, a drums-out drop, a snare-roll return
- Bridge: piano only, the refusal to romanticise the pressure
- Final chorus and outro: the widest version, then back to the glass and the hum

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – no drums, one glass pluck]

```
Night shift, glass case, cloth in my hand,
Wiping other people's diamonds off the stand.
Somebody said, "That's as close as you get,"
I said, "Give it ten years — and I'll take that bet."
```

- Scene: A jewellery shop after closing, shutters half down, one row of lights still on.
- Shot: Macro of a soft cloth moving across glass, the reflection of a lit ring travelling under it.
- Cut to: Her in a staff polo shirt, alone in the shop, and a faceless colleague in the doorway saying the line on their way out.
- Emotion / Lighting: Flat, unbothered, quietly certain. Fluorescent white, everything slightly green, the jewellery the only warm thing in frame.

### [verse 1 – the four Cs]

```
Twenty-two, two jobs and a folding bed,
Four Cs on a poster and they lived in my head.
Cut, colour, clarity, carat — in that order,
Said them like a prayer while I locked the door.
Everybody's diamond came out of the dark,
Nobody asks a stone what it cost to start.
They only see the setting, they never see the mine,
Never see the hands that put the shine on the shine.
So I kept my head down and I let it press,
Diamonds don't get made out of comfort and rest.
```

- Scene: Her life at twenty-two, cut against the shop.
- Shots: A folding bed against a wall in a rented room; a laminated four-Cs poster taped to a staff-room wall, the corners curling; her mouthing the four words while pulling a shutter down; a bus at six in the morning; a second uniform in a bag.
- Cut to: The first glimpse of the seam — a dark rock face, one work lamp, a figure far away in it.
- Emotion / Lighting: Tired, methodical, no self-pity. Staff-room fluorescent, dawn bus window, then near-black with one lamp.

### [pre-chorus 1]

```
Two thousand degrees and a mile of stone,
Nobody heard a thing — I was down there alone.
Now the lid comes off and the room goes still,
Count it down and let the light spill.
Here we go.
```

- Scene: Underground. The pressure made literal.
- Shots: A tight tunnel of rock lit by one lamp; dust falling; her palm flat on the rock face; a hairline crack opening and a single point of white light coming through it.
- Emotion / Lighting: Compressed, then the first light. Near-black, one lamp, one white point.

### [chorus 1 – the case opens]

```
Pressure made me, now it's diamond season,
You can hate the way I shine — you don't get a reason.
I was carbon in the dark with a weight on my chest,
Now the light goes right through me and comes out the best.
Turn me, turn me, catch the edge,
Every cut you ever gave me is a facet in the end.
Pressure made me, now it's diamond season,
Diamond season, diamond season.
```

- Scene: The display case, the velvet tray and the prisms.
- Shots: A glass case lid rising on a hydraulic hinge; a velvet tray sliding into a spotlight; prisms throwing bands of colour across a white wall and across her face; her turning slowly under a light, the refraction moving over her.
- Emotion / Lighting: Cold euphoria. Hard white key light, real prism colour, deep black surround.

### [post-chorus – the drop]

```
Carbon, carbon, hold the line,
Carbon, carbon, give it time.
Pressure, pressure, hold me down,
That is how a diamond gets its crown.
Diamond season, diamond season,
Ten years cooking — here's the reason.
```

- Scene: The drop is the transformation cut.
- Shots: Four hard match cuts on the beat: cloth to loupe, uniform to gown, fluorescent to spotlight, rock face to gala staircase. Then a wide of her at the top of a black staircase.
- Emotion / Lighting: Impact. Every cut brighter than the last.

### [verse 2 – rapped, half-time]

```
Ten years pressing, ten years quiet, ten years carbon in the seam,
Every "no" was a hundred atmospheres, every knockback was a squeeze.
They said the girl with the cloth ain't the girl on the tray,
Now the girl with the cloth got the loupe and the say.
Cut me at an angle where the light has to bend —
That is not luck, that's geometry, friend.
Clarity is nothing but the flaws I outgrew,
Colour is the temper that the fire ran through.
Carat is the years, so go on, count the weight,
I don't shine for a room — the room can catch up late.
Put a hand on the velvet, but don't touch the glass,
Everything I am I made out of the past.
```

- Scene: The workshop and the vault. Precision, not glamour.
- Shots: Her at a jeweller's bench with a loupe to her eye, the magnified iris filling frame; a stone held in tweezers under a lamp; a wall of tiny drawers; her walking a vault corridor in a tailored suit; a hand stopped an inch from the glass by her own hand.
- Emotion / Lighting: Cold, exact, in command. Single desk lamp, hard shadow, steel and glass.

### [pre-chorus 2]

```
Two thousand degrees and a mile of stone,
Nobody heard a thing — I was down there alone.
Now the doors come off and the whole room turns,
Count it down and let it burn.
Here we go.
```

- Scene: The gala doors.
- Shots: Two heavy doors from the inside; her silhouette against them; the doors opening and every head in the room turning at once.
- Emotion / Lighting: The stop before the drop. Backlit white through the doorway, the room dark.

### [chorus 2]

(repeat)

- Scene: The gala at full size.
- Shots: The staircase; chandeliers throwing the same prism pattern as the shop case; her walking through a room that used to be a display; a slow-motion insert of a champagne glass refracting light exactly like a stone.
- Emotion / Lighting: Wide and cold and bright. Chandelier white, black marble, colour only from refraction.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: Light itself. No story, no people for the first half.
- Shots: A prism rotating on a black background, spectrum crawling across the frame; a slow filter-sweep shot of the arpeggio visualised as light moving down a corridor; the drums-out drop played on one held wide of the empty staircase; a snare-roll build cut on faces in the room; the shop's glass case, empty, one light still on.
- Lighting: Pure white through prism colour, then black, then everything.

### [bridge – piano only]

```
Do not get it wrong — I would not wish the weight,
I am not the girl who says the dark was great.
Nobody needs a mile of stone on their chest
To be worth the velvet, to be worth the rest.
I did not need the pressure to be worth the light,
But the pressure came, and I did not break, and that is mine.
So I will take the season and I will take the shine,
And I'll leave the door open for the next one in line.
```

- Scene: The stillest part of the video. Back in the shop, out of hours, in the gown.
- Shots: Her sitting on the floor of the empty shop in the gala gown with her shoes off, the case lit above her; the four-Cs poster still on the staff-room wall; her taking it down carefully and rolling it; the last line shot on a door being propped open with a foot.
- Emotion / Lighting: Honest, unglamorous, generous. One fluorescent strip and the case light; no prisms in this whole section.

### [final chorus]

(repeat, widest, with the cloth-and-case couplet varied)

- Scene: Both worlds in one frame.
- Shots: The gala staircase and the shop floor intercut on the bar; the case open with nothing in it; her at the top of the stairs turning under the chandelier as the refraction lands; the widest shot of the video, the whole room, every light on.
- Emotion / Lighting: Full release. Every prism firing, deep black behind it.

### [post-chorus 2]

(repeat, drop at full size)

- Scene: The four match cuts again, reversed and faster.
- Shots: Gown to uniform to gown; staircase to rock face to staircase; the loupe to the cloth to the loupe; ending on her hand, no jewellery on it.
- Emotion / Lighting: Impact, then a hard stop into the outro's fluorescent.

### [outro – back to the glass]

```
Night shift, glass case, cloth in her hand,
Somebody's wiping the glass where I stand.
I told her, "Ten years," and I meant every one,
Diamond season — and it's only begun.
```

- Scene: The shop again, present day, a new girl on the night shift.
- Shots: Macro of a cloth on glass exactly as in shot one, but a different hand; the new girl looking up; Mahima on the other side of the glass in the gown, saying the line; the final shot is the two of them either side of the case, the reflection putting them in the same frame, as the lights go down to one.
- Emotion / Lighting: Passed on. Fluorescent white and one warm case light.
