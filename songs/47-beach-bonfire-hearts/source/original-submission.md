# Beach Bonfire Hearts — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A beach bonfire on the Australian coast, a borrowed guitar, and the friend she has known since they were ten who, somewhere between the first log going on and the tide turning, becomes something more. Ten summers of almost, one look across the flames, and a walk down to the water to finally say it.

**Mood:**
Warm, golden, unhurried. Friends-to-lovers with a grin in it. Campfire singalong energy that turns private for one verse and then goes public again when the whole beach finds out.

**Message:**
"I've been keeping my hands in my pockets for years. Tonight I'm letting it show."

**Genre:**
Acoustic summer pop / campfire folk-pop with group harmonies. Think: Vance Joy x Ziggy Alberts x Lime Cordiale, with a touch of early Angus & Julia Stone in the bridge. Australian register: esky, servo, ute tray, mates, reckon, the southerly.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 102 BPM, 4/4, relaxed half-time feel in the verses
- Key: D major (open, campfire-friendly, capo-able)
- Final chorus lifts a key for the whole-beach singalong

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, easy and wry in the verses, big and joyful in the choruses, near-spoken and honest in the bridge
- Backing vocals: a circle of friends singing gang harmonies on every chorus, growing each time; wordless oohs in the instrumental; male and female voices on the final hook

**Instrumentation:**
- Steel-string acoustic guitar (the campfire guitar, pick noise and fret slides left in)
- Second acoustic guitar doubling from the pre-chorus; fingerpicked figure in the instrumental
- Cajón, shaker, tambourine, handclaps and foot stomps
- Warm upright-style bass
- Slide guitar for melodic answers
- Light ukulele-style strum and glockenspiel sparkle in the choruses
- Low warm pad under the bridge and outro
- Ambient textures: wave wash, fire crackle, a distant gull, a ute door

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: sunset gone, the fire lit, him on the far side of it like always
- Verse 1: the borrowed guitar, ten years of friendship, the first flicker
- Pre-chorus: one look over the flames, the thinking stops
- Chorus: the hook, sparks, hands out of pockets, the whole circle singing
- Verse 2: the kids gone, the ute headlights, his shoulder on hers, the missed chord
- Bridge: down at the water, the confession, "took you long enough"
- Final chorus: hand in hand, the whole beach knows, the brother yelling finally
- Outro: dawn, coals, one guitar, our song

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – one guitar, waves and fire, easy]

```
Sun went down behind the dunes an hour ago,
Esky full of servo ice and wood to throw.
Sand's still warm, the southerly's on its way,
You're on the far side of the fire, same as every day.
```

- Scene: A beach on the Australian coast just after dark. A bonfire being built up on the sand below the dunes, a ute parked up on the track, an esky, driftwood logs pulled into a circle.
- Shot: Wide, the fire small and new, friends arriving with armfuls of wood. Close-up on a bag of servo ice being tipped into the esky. She sits on a log, and across the flames, he is there, throwing wood on.
- Cut to: Her looking at him for a beat longer than she should, then looking at the fire.
- Emotion: Contentment with a question under it.
- Lighting: Last blue of dusk in the sky, orange firelight on faces, the ute's headlights off for now.

### [verse 1]

```
Your brother brought the guitar and he left it by the logs,
You picked it up and tuned it while the others walked the dogs.
Two chords and a shrug, a song we half knew,
And I'm singing at the fire but I'm singing it to you.
```

- Scene: The brother drops a battered acoustic guitar by the logs and walks off down the beach with two dogs and a couple of the others. He picks it up, tunes it by ear, plays two chords with a shrug.
- Shot: Close on his hands on the strings, the fire in the background. She starts singing along, then the shot finds her eyes: she's singing it to him.
- Cut to: Him not noticing, grinning at the guitar.
- Emotion: Fond, warm, a little private.
- Lighting: Firelight, sparks lifting.

```
Reckon we've been mates since the summer we were ten,
Beach cricket, peeling shoulders, I never looked back then.
But tonight the sparks go up and something in me turns,
Like the wood knows something first, and it's telling me it burns.
```

- Scene: A quick warm flashback: two kids playing beach cricket on the same beach, sunburnt shoulders, a tennis ball into the water. Back to the present, a log shifts and a column of sparks goes straight up.
- Shot: The flashback handheld and overexposed; the present a slow push-in on her face as the sparks rise and her expression changes.
- Cut to: The fire, close, the wood popping.
- Emotion: The realisation landing.
- Lighting: Flashback bright midday; present firelight and stars.

### [pre-chorus]

```
Everybody's laughing, passing round the last cold drink,
You look up over the flames and I forget to think.
One look, that's all it takes, I'm caught in it now,
I've been standing in the smoke and I'm working out how.
```

- Scene: The circle passing a can around, someone telling a story badly, everyone laughing.
- Shot: Over her shoulder to him across the flames. He looks up, mid-laugh, and holds her eyes for one beat.
- Cut to: Her, the smoke drifting across her, not blinking.
- Emotion: Caught.
- Lighting: Orange on him, smoke haze between them.

### [chorus – wide, everyone singing]

```
Beach bonfire hearts, burning slow,
Sparks going up where the stars all go.
I've been keeping my hands in my pockets for years,
Tonight I'm thinking I'll let it show.
Beach bonfire hearts, burning slow,
Salt in your hair and the tide out low.
Pass me the guitar, I'll play it till you know,
Beach bonfire hearts, burning slow.
```

- Scene: The circle sings the chorus with her, badly and loudly. Sparks against the stars. She reaches for the guitar and he passes it across the fire.
- Shot: A slow circle around the fire, every face lit. Tilt up from the sparks to the Milky Way. Close on her hands coming out of her hoodie pockets to take the guitar.
- Cut to: Her playing, looking at him, the group singing behind her.
- Emotion: Joy with a secret in it.
- Lighting: Full firelight, stars sharp, a little moon on the water.

### [verse 2]

```
Half past ten and the tide's turned, the little kids are gone,
Your sister's on the ute tray with the headlights on.
You slide across the log and your shoulder's touching mine,
Say, play the one you used to play, and I run out of time.
```

- Scene: Later. The families with kids have gone. His sister sits on the ute tray up on the track with the headlights throwing two beams across the sand. He gets up and moves round the fire to sit beside her on her log.
- Shot: The headlight beams across the beach, wide. Then close: his shoulder arriving against hers. Her looking down at the guitar.
- Cut to: Him, easy, asking for the old song.
- Emotion: Nervous, thrilled.
- Lighting: Headlights white behind them, fire orange in front.

```
So I play it and I miss a chord and you laugh out loud,
Same laugh from a hundred summers, but it's different now.
You lean in close and say, you've gone quiet, what's wrong,
And I say, nothing, hey, just listen to the song.
```

- Scene: She fumbles the chord, he laughs, she laughs too. He leans in close to ask what's up. She deflects.
- Shot: Close two-shot, the fire on one side of their faces, the headlights on the other. His head tilting in. Her looking at the strings.
- Cut to: Her mouth saying "nothing", her eyes saying the opposite.
- Emotion: Almost.
- Lighting: Split light, warm and white.

### [pre-chorus]

```
Everybody's singing, nobody's singing in key,
You've stopped singing altogether, you're just looking at me.
One look, that's all it takes, I'm caught in it now,
I've been standing in the smoke and I'm working out how.
```

- Scene: The whole circle is singing now, off-key and happy. He has stopped singing. He's watching her.
- Shot: Wide on the group singing; cut to him in profile, silent, then her noticing.
- Cut to: The two of them in the middle of the noise, not moving.
- Emotion: The look returned.
- Lighting: Firelight, the headlights now off.

### [chorus]

```
Beach bonfire hearts, burning slow,
Sparks going up where the stars all go.
I've been keeping my hands in my pockets for years,
Tonight I'm thinking I'll let it show.
Beach bonfire hearts, burning slow,
Salt in your hair and the tide out low.
Pass me the guitar, I'll play it till you know,
Beach bonfire hearts, burning slow.
```

- Scene: The second singalong, fuller. Someone throws the last big log on and the fire roars. She plays the chorus straight at him.
- Shot: Sparks from the new log filling the frame. The whole circle on their feet. Her playing, him watching, the friends singing around them.
- Cut to: His sister on the tray clocking it and grinning.
- Emotion: Everyone can feel it.
- Lighting: The fire at its biggest, the brightest the beach gets all night.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: The fire settles. The fingerpicked guitar and slide take over. She puts the guitar down and stands. She walks away from the circle toward the water, looking back once. He waits a beat, then follows.
- Shots: Her back to camera walking out of the firelight into the dark. The tide line, small waves, black water. Him standing up, his mate giving him a small push. Her footprints in wet sand. Him arriving beside her at the water's edge.
- Lighting: Firelight falling off to moonlight on the water; the fire a small orange point behind them.

### [bridge – one guitar and the voice, then the band returns]

```
Come down to the water where the firelight ends,
Where I can't hide behind the chords or the friends.
Ten summers of almost, I'm done playing it cool,
If this wrecks us I'm the fool, but I'd rather be the fool.
So I say it, I say all of it, I say it low,
And you say, took you long enough, and you kiss me slow.
```

- Scene: Two figures at the water's edge, the fire far behind them. She talks. He listens. Then he answers, and it's the answer she wanted.
- Shot: A slow static two-shot from the side, silhouettes against the moonlit water, her hands moving as she talks. Then close: his face, a smile breaking, his mouth saying "took you long enough." Then the kiss, slow, waves at their feet.
- Cut to: Far away, the circle round the fire going quiet and turning to look.
- Emotion: Everything.
- Lighting: Moonlight and starlight, cold blue-silver, the fire a warm dot behind.

### [final chorus – lifted key, every friend singing]

```
Beach bonfire hearts, burning slow,
Sparks going up where the stars all go.
I kept my hands in my pockets for years,
Tonight your hand's in mine and the whole beach knows.
Beach bonfire hearts, burning slow,
Salt in your hair and the tide out low.
Your brother's yelling, finally, from across the glow,
Beach bonfire hearts, burning slow.
```

- Scene: They walk back up the beach to the fire hand in hand. The circle erupts. His brother stands on a log and yells "finally." Everyone sings the chorus at them.
- Shot: The two of them walking back into the light, hands linked, the group rising. The brother on the log, arms wide. She laughs, hiding her face in his shoulder. The chorus sung by the circle, cutting fast between faces.
- Cut to: Her hand in his, close, firelit.
- Emotion: Relief, joy, embarrassment, the best kind.
- Lighting: Firelight, warm and full.

### [post-chorus – claps and cajón, a singalong]

```
Burning slow, burning slow,
Ten summers, one spark, and now we know.
Burning slow, burning slow,
Sing it round the fire till the embers go.
```

- Scene: The whole circle stamping and clapping, the guitar passed round, the song sung as a chant.
- Shot: Feet stamping in the sand, hands clapping, the guitar passing hand to hand across the fire, the two of them on one log, his arm round her.
- Cut to: The fire slowly settling, the sky beginning to lighten at the edges.
- Emotion: Community, warmth, tiredness setting in.
- Lighting: The fire lower, first grey light on the horizon.

### [outro – one guitar, dawn]

```
Sun's coming up behind the dunes, the fire's just coals,
Everyone's asleep in the tray with sand in their clothes.
You've got the guitar and you're playing our song,
Two chords and a shrug, and I sing along,
Sand in the strings and the whole sky gold,
Beach bonfire hearts, burning slow.
```

- Scene: Dawn. The fire is a ring of coals. Friends asleep in the ute tray under towels and hoodies. The two of them still awake by the coals, him with the guitar, playing the two chords from the start of the night.
- Shot: Wide on the dunes going gold. Close on the coals. The sleeping friends in the tray. Him playing, her head on his shoulder, singing softly. Final shot: the two of them from behind, looking at the sunrise over the water, the guitar between them.
- Emotion: Peace.
- Lighting: Gold dawn, the coals a faint red, everything soft.
