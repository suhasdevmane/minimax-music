# Polaroids in My Pocket — original submission

The song, direction and video concept exactly as submitted, before trimming to
the model's 6-minute cap and stripping stage directions from the lyric body.
Kept as source material.

**Concept:**
A couple sings together, flipping through old polaroids and memories: first meeting, first fight, first "I love you," moving in, long-distance, almost breaking up, choosing each other again. The chorus is simple, repeatable, and built to stick: *"We're still writing our story in polaroids and phone screens."*

**Genre:**
Modern acoustic-pop / indie-pop duet with cinematic build. Think Ed Sheeran x Lewis Capaldi x Bollywood-style emotional melody.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 92 BPM, 4/4
- Key: G major (easy for most singers, warm and hopeful)
- Final chorus can lift to A major if your generator supports modulation.

**Vocal setup:**
- Female lead: warm, intimate, slightly breathy in verses, strong in chorus.
- Male lead: soft, sincere baritone/tenor, harmonising in chorus.
- Both sing together on the final chorus and outro for an anthemic feel.

**Instrumentation:**
- Acoustic guitar (fingerstyle + strummed)
- Piano
- Warm bass (sub + light electric)
- Soft pads / strings in choruses
- Light percussion: shaker, kick, snare, finger snaps
- Subtle ambient textures (room noise, laughter, phone notification chimes as ear candy)

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: nostalgic, intimate
- Verses: storytelling, specific memories
- Pre-chorus: emotional tension, "are we still us?"
- Chorus: big, singable, viral hook
- Bridge: raw honesty, almost breaking up, then choosing each other
- Final chorus: triumphant, grateful, "we made it through"
- Outro: quiet, tender, "we're still here"

## Why this can go viral

- **Relatable couple narrative:** First meet, first fight, almost breaking up, choosing each other again. Almost every couple sees themselves here.
- **Repeatable hook:** "Polaroids and phone screens" is visual, modern, and easy to meme/duet. People can overlay their own photos.
- **Duet format:** Perfect for TikTok/Reels couple duets: one sings female lines, one sings male lines, then both on chorus.
- **Specific but universal details:** Grey hoodie, night train, 3 a.m. kitchen fight, list on the wall—specific enough to feel real, general enough to apply to many relationships.
- **Emotional arc:** Starts cute, goes through real pain, ends in grateful, mature love. That journey keeps people watching till the end.

## Video concept (couple-led, memory-driven)

**Main characters:**
- Girl: early–mid 20s, expressive, natural look, often in cosy clothes (hoodies, oversized shirts).
- Boy: early–mid 20s, warm smile, casual style (hoodies, tees, jackets).

**Visual motif:**
- Real or prop polaroids appearing in frame, transitioning into the memory they represent.
- Phone screens showing old chats: "good morning," "are you okay?", voice notes.
- Split screens: past vs present same locations (same bench, same kitchen, same door).

**Section-by-section direction (short version):**

- **Intro:** Girl finds a box of polaroids under the bed; flips through them; each photo briefly glows and cuts to a memory.
- **Verse 1 (her):** First meeting scene: café or rainy street, shared earphones, shy glances.
- **Verse 1 (him):** Night train or bus; him watching her sleep; close-ups of hands almost touching.
- **Pre-chorus:** Montage of small moments: cooking together, studying, laughing on a rooftop, sending good-morning texts.
- **Chorus:** Fast-cut montage of happy moments synced to the beat; overlay text: "polaroids & phone screens" in a simple font.
- **Verse 2 (him):** 3 a.m. kitchen fight; tense silence; him on the floor, her in the bathroom.
- **Verse 2 (her):** Reconciliation: hugging in the hallway, blurry selfie next day with puffy eyes.
- **Pre-chorus 2:** Both sitting on the floor, talking calmly, holding hands, crying but together.
- **Instrumental:** Memory montage: moving boxes, airport goodbye, video calls, reunion hug.
- **Bridge:** The "almost left" night: packed bag, sitting on the bed, honest conversation, writing the list on the wall.
- **Final chorus:** Present-day couple, stronger; dancing in the kitchen, walking in the rain, laughing on the same bench as the first photo.
- **Post-chorus:** Quick cuts of real couples (if you want UGC vibes) holding polaroids with their own captions.
- **Outro:** Girl puts most photos back, keeps a few in her pocket; couple sits together, looking at one last polaroid, smiling quietly.

**Viral challenge idea:**
Encourage couples to:
- Recreate one old photo in the same pose/place.
- Show a "before/after" polaroid transition on the chorus.
- Use the line: *"Every 'stay' when we could've gone"* with their own story in captions.
