# Slow Dance in the Kitchen — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A couple with no money for a night out turns their tiny kitchen into a ballroom. Rent has just come out, the toast is burnt, the smoke alarm is taped shut, and none of it matters. They dance in socks on the tile with the oven timer as a metronome, and somewhere between the sink and the stove one of them finally says "I love you" for the first time.

**Mood:**
Warm, playful, tender. Broke and happy. The kind of romance that lives in string lights and mismatched cups.

**Message:**
"We don't need a night out to have a night. Turn the radio low, we don't need a floor."

**Genre:**
Acoustic pop ballad / indie-folk duet. Think: Ed Sheeran x Norah Jones with a touch of Vance Joy; a slow dance in a small room, never a stadium.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 88 BPM, 4/4
- Key: G major (warm, open)
- No modulation; the lift is in width and harmony, not key. The final chorus gains the tambourine, full strings and both voices layered.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal (Singer A): young adult female, warm and amused in the verses, open and easy in the choruses.
- Singer B: male, soft baritone-tenor, sincere and a little sheepish in his verse, harmonising beneath her in the choruses.
- Both voices together on the pre-chorus count-in, every chorus, the "I love you" lines of the bridge, the post-chorus and the outro.

**Instrumentation:**
- Upright piano with the felt on (main spine)
- Warm nylon-string guitar (picking in verses, strumming in choruses, melody in the instrumental)
- Upright bass
- Brushed drums (brushes on snare, soft kick, shaker, tambourine in the final chorus)
- Warm string pad growing to full strings
- Glockenspiel (a sparkle in the second chorus)
- Ambient textures: refrigerator hum, kitchen timer ticking, a radio on the windowsill, small kitchen sounds

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: rent's out, blinds down, a bottle of the cheap stuff, just the two of them
- Verse 1 (her): socks on the tile, the timer as a metronome, "look at what we've got"
- Pre-chorus (both): hand here, chin here, count us in, in here it's slow
- Chorus (both): the hook, big and warm, a slow dance not a show
- Verse 2 (him): the night out he couldn't afford, the toast, eleven dollars and a feeling
- Instrumental: the guitar takes the chorus, they just dance
- Bridge: the sentence held behind the teeth, the count of three, "I love you" out loud
- Final chorus: fuller, flour on his shirt, a ring pull for a ring
- Post-chorus: "that's what the kitchen's for," swaying
- Outro: bottle empty, song nearly gone, "I'll take the kitchen if the kitchen comes with you"

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – felt piano, nylon guitar, fridge hum, timer ticking]

```
Rent came out on Friday, so we're staying in,
One bottle of the cheap stuff, two mismatched cups.
You pulled the blinds down on the world outside,
Now it's just the fridge light and the two of us.
```

- Scene: A tiny apartment kitchen at night. A phone screen shows a bank notification, then goes face-down on the counter.
- Shot: Close-up of a bottle of cheap red being unscrewed (no cork), poured into a chipped mug and a jam jar.
- Cut to: Him pulling the blinds down over the window; the street light outside vanishes.
- Emotion: A shrug, a smile — the night's not cancelled, it's just moved inside.
- Lighting: Fridge light and one string of warm fairy lights taped along the cabinets.

### [verse 1 – her]

```
Your socks keep sliding on the tile, you almost fall,
I'm laughing so hard I can barely stand.
The oven timer's ticking like a metronome,
So you set the bottle down and say, "come here."
```

- Scene: Black and white checkered tile, his mismatched socks skidding, a grab for the counter.
- Shot: Low angle on the socks; then her doubled over laughing against the fridge.
- Cut to: Extreme close-up of the wind-up oven timer, ticking. His hand setting the bottle down. His hand held out.
- Her reaction: Wipes her eyes, still laughing, takes the hand.
- Lighting: Warm, low, the fairy lights and the stove hood lamp.

```
There's string lights on the cabinet we never took down,
There's steam rising off a pot we both forgot.
We can't afford the place with the candles and the view,
But look at what we've got, look at what we've got.
```

- Scene: A pan of the kitchen: the fairy lights from last December still up, a pot boiling over on the stove.
- Shot: Steam curling up through the string lights.
- Cut to: A restaurant flyer on the fridge under a magnet — candles, a skyline view — and her eyes going from it to him.
- Emotion: The flyer is a joke to her now; she means "look at what we've got."
- Lighting: Steam catching the warm light; soft haze.

### [pre-chorus – both]

```
Put your hand right here, your chin on my shoulder,
I'll count us in, 1, 2, 3, here we go.
The whole world's out there and the rent is still due,
But in here it's slow, in here it's slow.
```

- Scene: She places his hand on her waist, his chin on her shoulder, correcting his frame like a dance teacher.
- Shot: Close-up of her mouth counting; their socked feet stepping on "three."
- Cut to: The blinds, the muffled city, then back to the two of them turning slowly.
- Emotion: Everything outside slows down.
- Lighting: Warm, steady; the fridge light flicks off as the door swings shut.

### [chorus – both, big and warm]

```
Turn the radio low, we don't need a floor,
Slow dance in the kitchen, that's what the kitchen's for.
Socks on the tile and the toast gone black,
Spinning by the sink, never going back.
```

- Scene: The slow dance proper: a small circle between the fridge and the sink.
- Shot: Him reaching to the windowsill radio and turning the dial down without letting go of her.
- Cut to: Socks on tile; the toaster popping two black slices; a spin by the sink with the tap still dripping.
- Emotion: Joy, unforced. Nobody is performing.
- Lighting: Fairy lights, stove lamp, a bluish glow from the toaster's coils.

```
We don't need a ballroom, don't need a band,
Just the hum of the fridge and your hand in my hand.
Turn the radio low, lock the front door,
Slow dance in the kitchen, that's what the kitchen's for.
```

- Scene: A wide of the whole kitchen, six feet by eight, the two of them filling it.
- Shot: Close-up of their hands, his thumb over her knuckles.
- Cut to: Her reaching out with one foot to push the front door shut, the latch clicking.
- Emotion: A private world sealed.
- Lighting: Warm, a little dim, the door's hallway light cut off as it closes.

### [verse 2 – him]

```
I know I promised you a night out somewhere nice,
White tablecloths and a waiter we could tip.
But the toast went black and you laughed till you cried,
Then you turned the music up and said, "let's skip it."
```

- Scene: Him at the counter, the restaurant flyer in his hand, a little embarrassed.
- Shot: Close-up of the flyer: white tablecloths, a candle. He puts it back under the magnet.
- Cut to: The burnt toast on a plate; her laughing so hard she has to sit on the floor; her reaching up to turn the radio up.
- Emotion: His apology becoming gratitude.
- Lighting: Stove lamp on his face; warm.

```
The smoke alarm's got tape across its mouth,
The chair beside the window only stands on three.
I've got 11 dollars and a feeling in my chest,
And the feeling's worth more than anything to me.
```

- Scene: A tilt up to the smoke alarm with a strip of masking tape over it; the wobbly chair propped against the wall.
- Shot: His wallet open on the counter: a ten and a one.
- Cut to: Him looking at her across the kitchen, hand flat on his chest without thinking.
- Emotion: Plain honesty.
- Lighting: Warm, close.

### [pre-chorus – both, her harmonies under him]

```
Put your hand right here, your chin on my shoulder,
I'll count us in, 1, 2, 3, here we go.
The whole world's out there and the rent is still due,
But in here it's slow, in here it's slow.
```

- Scene: This time he places her hand, her chin — he's learned the frame.
- Shot: Her surprised smile at being led.
- Cut to: The count on his mouth; the step on "three."
- Emotion: The roles swapped; the dance is theirs now.
- Lighting: Same warm kitchen; the fairy lights a touch brighter.

### [chorus – both, fuller, trading lines]

```
Turn the radio low, we don't need a floor,
Slow dance in the kitchen, that's what the kitchen's for.
Socks on the tile and the toast gone black,
Spinning by the sink, never going back.
We don't need a ballroom, don't need a band,
Just the hum of the fridge and your hand in my hand.
Turn the radio low, lock the front door,
Slow dance in the kitchen, that's what the kitchen's for.
```

- Scene: The dance gets braver: a dip by the oven, a spin that knocks a spoon off the counter.
- Shots: Her scraping the black off the toast with a knife, still dancing. Him dipping her, the string lights over her upside-down face. The two of them singing the hook at each other.
- Emotion: Play. The best night they've had in months, and it cost nothing.
- Lighting: Warm and full; steam haze; the fairy lights the brightest they'll be.

### [instrumental – nylon guitar takes the chorus, hummed duet]

- Scene: No lyrics. They just dance.
- Shots: A slow circle from above, socked feet on the checkered tile. Her forehead on his chest, eyes closed. His hand on the small of her back. The oven timer ticking in extreme close-up. The radio dial glowing. The pot lid rattling. A drop to near-quiet: the two of them barely moving, swaying.
- Lighting: Warm, low; the fairy lights and the stove lamp only.

### [bridge – piano and two voices, then both]

```
I've been holding a sentence behind my teeth,
Since you burned the toast in the middle of the week.
I've been practising in the mirror down the hall,
Every morning, every evening, never said it at all.
```

- Scene: They've stopped dancing but haven't let go. Her, quiet, looking at his collar instead of his face.
- Shot: Her lips pressed together, holding something in.
- Cut to: Him in the hallway mirror on a different morning, mouthing three words to himself, then stopping.
- Emotion: Two people who've both been carrying the same sentence.
- Lighting: The bridge drops the fairy lights to a single stove lamp; hallway memory in cool morning light.

```
So let's say it on the count, let's say it on 3,
1, 2, and you said it before me.
I love you, I love you, out loud by the stove,
And the timer went off like it already knows.
```

- Scene: She says "on three." He nods. She counts: one, two.
- Shot: His mouth opening before three; close-up of her face as she hears it.
- Cut to: Both of them saying it, over each other, laughing; the oven timer going off with a ding right on the beat.
- Emotion: Relief, joy, the whole song's turn.
- Lighting: The fairy lights come back on (he bumps the switch with his elbow), the kitchen warm again.

### [final chorus – both, fullest, tambourine and strings]

```
Turn the radio low, we don't need a floor,
Slow dance in the kitchen, that's what the kitchen's for.
Flour on your shirt and a ring pull for a ring,
Say it one more time, I'll say it back and sing.
We don't need a ballroom, don't need a band,
Just the hum of the fridge and your hand in my hand.
Turn the radio low, lock the front door,
Slow dance in the kitchen, that's what the kitchen's for.
```

- Scene: The dance again, bigger, both of them laughing, the pot finally turned off.
- Shots: A flour handprint on his shirt from her hand. Him twisting the ring pull off a can and sliding it onto her finger as a joke that isn't entirely a joke. Her holding the hand up to the string lights. Both singing the hook.
- Emotion: The happiest frame of the video.
- Lighting: Warm and full, every light in the kitchen on.

### [post-chorus – both, swaying]

```
That's what the kitchen's for,
Two left feet on a checkered floor.
That's what the kitchen's for,
Every night I'll ask you for one more.
```

- Scene: Slow sway, no steps now, just turning.
- Shots: Their feet from above on the checkered tile, out of step and not caring. Her mouthing "one more" and him nodding.
- Emotion: Settled.
- Lighting: The fairy lights dimming slightly as the night gets late.

### [outro – guitar, felt piano, fridge hum, unison]

```
The bottle's empty and the song's nearly gone,
The string lights flicker but you keep on holding on.
One day we'll have the candles and the view,
But I'll take the kitchen if the kitchen comes with you.
```

- Scene: The bottle upside down in the sink, empty. The radio song fading.
- Shots: One bulb on the fairy-light string flickering. Her hand still on his shoulder. The restaurant flyer on the fridge, and her not looking at it. Final shot: the two of them still swaying in the dim kitchen, the ring pull on her finger catching the last light, the timer on the counter, wide and locked-off, hold through the fade.
- Emotion: Contentment. Nothing is missing.
- Lighting: The stove lamp only, then the fridge light as the door drifts open, then fade.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the spin by the sink with the toast popping black behind them, vertical, with *"slow dance in the kitchen, that's what the kitchen's for"* on screen.
- **The kitchen slow-dance challenge:** couples post their own socks-on-tile slow dance to the chorus; the radio-turned-down gesture is the opening move.
- **The caption line:** *"We don't need a ballroom, don't need a band."*
- **The quote:** *"I'll take the kitchen if the kitchen comes with you."*
- **The turn:** the count of three where he says it before she gets to three, and the timer dings.
- **The ring pull:** the can tab slid onto her finger; an instant "not a proposal, but" edit.
