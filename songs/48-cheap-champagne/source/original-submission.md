# Cheap Champagne — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
Three friends on a fire escape with a seven dollar bottle, toasting the small wins nobody throws a party for: the rent going through, a job email that finally came, and a friend who got through a year that nearly took her. Being young and broke and completely certain, for one night, that you own the city.

**Mood:**
Warm, funny, a little defiant, and quietly moving. Not a hangover song and not a party song: a toast. The joy is earned by naming what the year cost.

**Message:**
"You do not get this part twice. Raise the cup while it is still cheap."

**Genre:**
Indie-dance pop. Think: Haim x Two Door Cinema Club x Robyn — a live disco bass, chorus-pedal guitar, handclaps, and a last chorus that turns into a room full of people shouting.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 114 BPM, 4/4
- Key: F major (warm, guitar-friendly, and low enough that the gang vocal can join)
- No modulation; the final chorus lifts by arrangement and by the gang vocal taking the hook.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, close and half-laughing in the verses, full and open in the choruses.
- Backing vocals: two friends rather than a choir on the toasts; six to eight voices in a room for the final chorus.

**Instrumentation:**
- Bright electric guitar through a chorus pedal (the signature; alone in the intro and outro, octaves in the last chorus)
- Round, busy, melodic disco bass
- Palm-muted second rhythm guitar, warm analogue synth pad
- Four-on-the-floor kick, tight snare, open hats, shaker, tambourine, handclaps
- Gang vocals, six to eight voices recorded in a room
- Textures: street ambience, a distant siren, an air conditioning unit, the metallic ring of a fire escape, a foil cap twisting, a cork, mug clinks

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: the corner store bottle, the window, no drums
- Verse 1: three small wins, one per couplet
- Pre-chorus: out over the sill, cups instead of glasses, the count
- Chorus: the toast, the city, the whole thing on loan
- Verse 2: the same fire escape a year ago, with nothing to celebrate
- Bridge: the future with matching glasses, and what it will not have
- Final chorus and outro: the gang vocal, then one guitar and a street

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro — one guitar figure, street ambience, no drums]

```
Seven dollars at the corner store for something with a foil top,
You went out the window first and held the bottle while I climbed,
And the whole city just sat there like it was waiting for us.
```

- Scene: A corner store at dusk, then a third-floor apartment window.
- Shot: A hand taking a bottle with a gold foil cap off a shelf near the door; a crumpled note going across a counter.
- Cut to: A sash window pushed up from inside, a friend climbing out backwards onto a fire escape and reaching back for the bottle.
- Emotion: Conspiratorial, unhurried, entirely happy.
- Lighting: Store fluorescents, then blue hour outside with the last orange on the buildings opposite.

### [verse 1 — soft kick, shaker, three small wins]

```
Rent went through on Thursday with two days left to spare,
First time in a year that the number didn't scare.
You got the email that you'd given up on getting,
Read it out to us twice, then you read it out again.
```

- Scene: Two victories, told in flashback inserts between shots of the three of them settling onto the steps.
- Shot: A banking screen going from red to black (composited), her hand over her mouth in a kitchen, two seconds.
- Cut to: A friend standing up in a café reading a phone out loud to nobody in particular, other customers looking over, delighted.
- Emotion: Disbelief that has not worn off yet.
- Lighting: Warm interior flashbacks, cool blue present.

```
And the third of us walked in tonight looking like herself,
Which is a sentence I could not have said in March.
So I took the twenty from the jar that says emergencies,
And I have decided this is one.
```

- Scene: The third friend arriving, and the jar.
- Shot: A door opening into the apartment; a young woman in the doorway, smiling properly, the other two going quiet for half a second before they move.
- Cut to: A glass jar on a kitchen shelf with a strip of masking tape across it (blank; the word composited), a twenty pulled out of it.
- Emotion: The line of the verse. Play the pause in the room absolutely straight.
- Lighting: Hallway light behind her in the doorway; kitchen lamp on the jar.

### [pre-chorus 1 — muted guitar, offbeat clap, one silent beat]

```
Out through the window, over the sill,
Two cups, no glasses, hold still.
The bottle's warm, the night is not,
Count to three and let it pop.
```

- Scene: Getting out onto the fire escape properly.
- Shot: Feet over a windowsill; two chipped mugs handed out one at a time; a hand steadying the rail.
- Cut to: A thumb working the foil cap, the cage twisting, all three faces waiting, held for one silent beat.
- Emotion: Comic tension, then release.
- Lighting: Blue hour, the window warm behind them, the street lamps just coming on.

### [chorus 1 — full indie-dance groove, handclaps]

```
Cheap champagne on the fire escape, tonight we're rich,
Seven dollars at the corner store and not one thing to fix.
Three flights over a city that we're never gonna own,
But you can see the whole of it from here, so it's on loan.
Cheap champagne on the fire escape, tonight we're rich,
To the rent, to the job, to the one who made it through.
Warm and flat and perfect and I wouldn't switch,
Cheap champagne on the fire escape, tonight we're rich.
```

- Scene: The toast, and the city behind it.
- Shot: The cork going, foam over a hand, all three laughing, handheld and close on the steps.
- Emotion: The best twenty minutes of the year and they know it while it is happening.
- Lighting: Street lamps, one warm window behind them, the city as a field of small lights.

### [post-chorus 1 — the toast, everyone answering]

```
To the small ones, to the small ones,
Here's to every small one that we won.
```

- Scene: Two hard cuts.
- Shot: Three mugs knocked together, macro; then all three drinking at once and pulling the same face at the taste.
- Emotion: The joke inside the joy.
- Lighting: Hard, close, warm.

### [verse 2 — warmer, the same steps a year ago]

```
This time last September we were out here with nothing,
Passing round the cheapest thing and calling it a night.
You were pulling doubles and I had a stack of letters
That I hadn't opened since the middle of July.
```

- Scene: The same fire escape, one year earlier, graded colder and emptier.
- Shot: The identical framing as the present-day wide, but two people instead of three, no lights strung, a plastic bottle between them.
- Cut to: A stack of unopened envelopes on a kitchen table, dust on the top one; a name badge and a work shirt over a chair.
- Emotion: Not tragic. Tired. The point is how ordinary it was.
- Lighting: Grey-blue, flatter, no warm window behind them.

```
Nobody announced it, nothing turned around at once,
It just got a little lighter every couple of months.
Same three people on the same three metal steps,
Same warm bottle and it tastes like something else.
```

- Scene: The year passing on one location.
- Shot: A four-shot dissolve on the identical fire escape framing: winter with snow on the rail; spring with the window shut; summer with a fan in the window; tonight with three people on it.
- Emotion: Recognition. This is the shot that makes the song more than a party.
- Lighting: Each season graded differently; the final frame is the warmest of the four.

### [pre-chorus 2 — three voices]

```
Out through the window, over the sill,
Two cups, then a third, hold still.
The bottle's warm, the night is not,
Count to three and let it pop.
```

- Scene: The second bottle, produced from a coat.
- Shot: A second foil cap, a third mug appearing from somewhere, all three counting on their fingers.
- Emotion: The night deciding to go on longer than planned.
- Lighting: Fully dark now; the window behind them is the main light.

### [chorus 2 — octave guitar, tambourine]

```
(the chorus again, bigger)
```

- Scene: Dancing on a fire escape, carefully.
- Shot: Three people dancing in a space too small for it, holding the rail, laughing at how little room there is.
- Emotion: Peak. Slightly reckless, entirely safe.
- Lighting: Warm from the window, cool from the street, the two colours meeting on their faces.

### [instrumental — guitar and bass feature, long build]

- Scene: The city at their level.
- Shots: A slow lateral drift across the building faces opposite, windows lit, other lives visible. A pigeon on the rail. The empty bottle standing upright on a step. The three of them lying back on the steps looking straight up, shot from directly above. A rising crane move up the front of the building through the build.
- Lighting: Deep blue and warm window gold; the crane shot ends on the sky.

### [bridge — guitar and voice alone]

```
One day one of us will have the kind of job
Where the glasses match and somebody else pours.
I'll be standing in a room that cost a fortune
Thinking of a metal step and a seven dollar cork.
You don't get this twice, so raise it while it's cheap,
Drink it while it still means everything to me.
```

- Scene: A flash forward, and back.
- Shot: Two seconds of a bright expensive room: matching glasses on a tray, someone pouring, a woman in a good coat holding one and looking past it. Do not show her face clearly.
- Cut to: Hard back to the fire escape, her looking at the chipped mug in her hand, close-up.
- Emotion: The turn. Gratitude with the future already in it.
- Lighting: The flash forward is cold, even and expensive; the fire escape is warm and uneven.

### [final chorus — gang vocal takes the hook]

```
(the chorus, with "Every window in the building lit")
```

- Scene: The building answering.
- Shot: A wide of the whole building face with windows lighting up one by one, and other people appearing at them.
- Emotion: The best kind of ending: they are not the only ones up.
- Lighting: The brightest frame of the video, entirely made of windows.

### [post-chorus 2 — shouted]

```
(the toast again, shouted)
```

- Scene: Mugs up on a fire escape, shouted at a street.
- Shot: From below, looking up at three silhouettes against a lit window, mugs raised.
- Lighting: Backlit, faces mostly dark, the gesture perfectly readable.

### [outro — one guitar, street ambience]

```
Bottle's empty, city's loud, and nobody wants to go in,
When they ask me for the best night I have ever had,
I'll say a metal step, three cups, and cheap champagne.
```

- Scene: Very late, nobody moving.
- Shot: The empty bottle on its side on a step, rolling an inch and stopping.
- Cut to: The three of them sat in a row on the steps, shoulders touching, saying nothing, the city noise coming up.
- Final shot: A slow pull back from across the street until the fire escape is one small lit detail on a huge dark building, held through the fade.
- Emotion: Contentment. Nobody wants to be the first to go inside.
- Lighting: Street lamps and one warm window; no grade tricks.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** three mugs knocked together on a fire escape with the city behind them, on *"tonight we're rich."*
- **The challenge:** the toast — name the small win nobody threw you a party for, raise whatever you are drinking out of, cut on *"to the small ones."*
- **The turn:** the four-season dissolve on the identical fire escape framing, empty to full.
- **The caption line:** *"You don't get this twice, so raise it while it's cheap."*
- **The last frame:** one lit fire escape on a huge dark building.
