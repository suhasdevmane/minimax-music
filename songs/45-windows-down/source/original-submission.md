# Windows Down — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A girl takes a car and a coast road on a Saturday with no destination. No one in the passenger seat, no plan, no notifications worth reading. The freedom she has been waiting for turns out to have been in the glovebox the whole time: keys, sunscreen, a playlist. It is a road-trip anthem about choosing a day for yourself and driving until the road runs out.

**Mood:**
Sunlit, loud, grinning. Pop-rock road anthem, windows-down-on-the-motorway energy, the last day of the holiday you never want to end.

**Message:**
"You don't need a reason and you don't need a map. Windows down, volume up, nowhere left to be."

**Genre:**
Pop-rock road anthem. Think: Harry Styles x Olivia Rodrigo's rock side x early Taylor Swift on the driving songs, with a festival-sized chorus and big floor toms.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 128 BPM, 4/4
- Key: E major (open, bright, guitar-friendly)
- Final chorus lifts a whole step to F♯ major for the sunset.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, cheeky and conversational in the verses, wide open and joyful in the choruses.
- Backing vocals: stacked harmonies on every hook, a gang-style shouted chant on the post-chorus, everyone-in-the-car.

**Instrumentation:**
- Driving electric guitars (clean strum in the intro, palm-muted chug in the verses, crunchy double-tracked power chords in the choruses)
- Live drum kit with big floor toms
- Eighth-note electric bass
- Lead guitar melody (instrumental break, final chorus)
- Tambourine, handclaps
- Low warm synth pad under the choruses
- Ambient textures: tyres on tarmac, a car door, an engine turning over, wind, a seagull

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: keys, sunglasses, the whole day empty, a grin
- Verses: the escape at dawn, then the petrol station and the beach
- Pre-chorus: the sky, the song coming on, turn it up
- Chorus: big, loud, singable, the hook shouted to the cliffs
- Bridge: quiet honesty, freedom was in the glovebox, then an invitation to whoever is stuck on a Tuesday
- Final chorus: sunset on the dashboard, key change, the biggest moment
- Outro: the tank says empty, the road ends where she'll go

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – clean guitar, tyre hiss, one grin]

```
Keys in the cup holder, sunglasses on,
Half a tank of anything and the whole day gone.
Tell the phone I'm "busy," tell the map to guess,
I'm not lost, I'm just somewhere I haven't been yet.
```

- Scene: A quiet residential street just after dawn, a cream vintage convertible parked at the kerb with the top down.
- Shot: Close-up of keys dropping into the cup holder, then her sunglasses sliding on in the rear-view mirror.
- Cut to: The fuel gauge at half, her thumb switching the phone to silent and tossing it into the glovebox.
- Emotion: A slow grin, no hurry, the first deep breath of the day.
- Lighting: Cool early morning, the sun not yet over the rooftops.

- Shot: A paper coffee cup wedged on the dashboard, steam curling; her finger scrolling a playlist on the car screen (UI composited).
- Cut to: A motorway exit sign flashing past (no readable text), her laughing and taking it late, the indicator ticking.
- Emotion: Mischief, the thrill of a wrong turn taken on purpose.
- Lighting: Pale gold breaking over the ring road.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** her standing up in the driver's seat at a lay-by, singing at the cliffs with "windows down, volume up, nowhere left to be" on screen.
- **The challenge:** the four post-chorus impact shots (foot down, top down, road, hand out the window) as a vertical template for people to film their own drives.
- **The caption line:** "Got a full tank of nothing and I've never felt so free."
- **The quote:** "You don't need a reason and you don't need a map."
- **The tide-line shot:** a name written in the sand and erased by a wave, an easy recreate for beach reels.
