# Ghost in My DMs — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes exactly as submitted,
before cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A girl is haunted by the memory of an ex who still pops up in her notifications, stories, and everyday life. They broke up, but he never really left—he's a ghost in her DMs, in her playlist, in her city. She knows she should move on, but every scroll, every song, every street corner pulls her back.

**Mood:**
Sad, nostalgic, modern heartbreak. Inspirational-pop meets late-night bedroom pop.

**Message:**
"You're gone, but you're everywhere. I'm trying to let go, but my phone won't let me."

**Genre:**
Emotional pop / bedroom pop with cinematic touches. Think: Olivia Rodrigo x Lewis Capaldi vibes, late-night drives, crying-in-the-car energy.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 92 BPM, 4/4
- Key: F♯ minor (moody, singable)
- Final chorus can lift to A major for extra emotional punch.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, intimate and breathy in verses, powerful and slightly raspy in choruses.
- Backing vocals: soft harmonies in choruses and final chorus for an anthemic feel.

**Instrumentation:**
- Piano (main spine)
- Warm sub-bass / soft 808s
- Atmospheric synths (pads, light arps)
- Light electronic percussion: soft kick, snaps, brushed snare, shaker
- Electric guitar with reverb (melodic lines in chorus/bridge)
- Strings in final chorus (violins, cello) for cinematic lift
- Ambient textures: phone notification sounds (very low), room tone, distant city noise

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: late night, scrolling, haunted by memories
- Verses: specific moments of pain, realisation, small steps of healing
- Pre-chorus: building strength, "I'm not that girl anymore"
- Chorus: big, singable, viral hook
- Bridge: raw honesty about the hard days, then full recommitment to self
- Final chorus: triumphant, grateful, "I'm better without you"
- Outro: calm, confident, "I'm finally me"

## Why this can go viral

- **Modern, relatable pain:** DMs, stories, likes, views—this is how breakups actually feel now.
- **Native slang:** "toxic, babe," "best life," "fit," "zoom right in," "Do Not Disturb"—sounds like real US/UK Gen Z / young millennial talk.
- **Strong hook:** "You're a ghost in my DMs" is visual, meme-able, and perfect for captions.
- **Visual potential:** Easy to pair with phone-screen POV, story screenshots, late-night scrolling, crying-in-the-car energy.
- **Emotional arc:** Starts haunted, ends choosing herself—perfect for "glow-up" and "moving on" content.
