# Cassette Tape Heart — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A woman in her twenties finds a mixtape somebody made her at nineteen, in a
drawer, with dead batteries in the player beside it. The whole first love
comes back in the physical facts of the object: sloping handwriting on the
card, hiss before the first track, a song that always skipped, a drawer that
gets closed again at the end.

**Mood:**
Nostalgic, widescreen, unsentimental at the last minute. Synthwave sadness
with a spine — the bridge turns the song away from the boy entirely.

**Message:**
"I don't want you back. I want the girl who thought a tape was a promise."

**Genre:**
Synthwave with analogue polysynths, gated reverb drums and a modern pop
vocal. Think: The Midnight x M83 x Taylor Swift's synth-pop era — huge
snares, real writing on top.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 100 BPM, 4/4
- Key: A minor
- No modulation. The final chorus lifts by adding a counter-melody, a second
  lead line and the biggest gated snare on the record.

**Vocal setup:**
- Singer A (female lead) throughout, solo.
- Backing: stacked female harmonies on the choruses, octave harmonies across
  the final chorus, wordless echoes in the outro.

**Instrumentation:**
- Wide detuned analogue polysynth pad (present bar one to the end)
- Driving octave analogue bassline
- Gated-reverb drum machine, huge snare on two and four
- Sixteenth-note arpeggio, always ticking
- Lead synth doubling the vocal an octave up, and the solo
- Electric piano in the second verse
- Choir pad from the second pre-chorus
- Textures: tape hiss under everything, a cassette lid clunk, a tape-stop, a
  spooling rewind, one deliberate ribbon dropout, a drawer sliding shut

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: hiss, a pad, a drawer, a girl she used to be
- Verse 1: how the tape was made, and what she did to it
- Pre-chorus: new batteries, the motor, four beats she would know anywhere
- Chorus: the thesis, full synthwave
- Verse 2: the car, the frost, the click at the end of the side
- Instrumental: lead solo, tape-stop, rewind, a bar of pure hiss
- Bridge: no drums; the song stops being about him
- Final chorus: biggest, and the bleed getting quieter
- Post-chorus: side A, side B, the click
- Outro: the drawer closes and the tape stays

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – hiss, pad, no drums]

```
Found it in a drawer with the batteries dead,
Your handwriting sloping across the white card.
A breath of static before anything starts,
And a girl I used to be sits down in the dark.
```

- Scene: A present-day bedroom, one lamp, a drawer open.
- Shot: Macro on a cassette lying in a drawer among chargers and old
  cables; a hand lifting it out; the battery hatch of a personal stereo
  opening onto corrosion.
- Cut to: The white card in the case, handwriting sloping downhill, never
  legible in frame.
- Emotion: Recognition arriving faster than she wants it to.
- Lighting: One warm lamp, deep shadow, cool blue window behind.

### [verse 1 – how it was made]

```
You made it in a bedroom with the radio on,
One finger on the button, waiting hours for a song.
You caught the deejay talking at the top of track four,
You swore, and you kept it, and I loved you all the more.
I played it till the ribbon wore as thin as a thread,
And the fourth song always skipped and I loved it instead.
```

- Scene: Memory, a teenage bedroom, VHS grain.
- Shots: A finger hovering over a record button on a stereo; a radio dial
  and a lit display; a boy's hands only, never his face, pressing record too
  late; a bedroom carpet with a stack of tapes on it; present-day cut of the
  ribbon itself, brown and thin, held up to a lamp.
- Emotion: Fondness with a joke in it.
- Lighting: Memory shots are warm and grainy with a magenta cast; present is
  clean and cold.

### [pre-chorus 1]

```
New batteries out of the back of the clock,
The lid clicks down and the motor kicks up.
Four beats of a drum that I'd know anywhere,
And I'm nineteen again and you're still standing there.
```

- Scene: The decision to play it.
- Shots: A wall clock taken down and its battery hatch opened; two
  batteries going into a personal stereo; the lid clicking down in extreme
  close-up; the spools starting to turn; her eyes closing on the fourth beat.
- Emotion: Knowing exactly what she is about to do to herself.
- Lighting: Lamp only, her face half-lit.

### [chorus 1 – full synthwave]

```
I've got a cassette tape heart, and you're on side A,
Wound tight in a drawer where I left you that day.
Everyone after you got put down on side B,
Recorded over you, and you still bleed through the seams.
Press rewind, press rewind, let the spools start to turn,
There's a hiss in the silence I could never unlearn.
I know how it ends and I press play anyway,
I've got a cassette tape heart, and you're on side A.
```

- Scene: The synthwave world opens: present and memory in the same frame.
- Shots: Her walking through her own flat while the teenage bedroom is
  visible through a doorway; a wall of magenta and cyan light; the spools
  turning in macro with a whole city reflected in the plastic window; her
  singing straight down the lens for the first time.
- Emotion: Enormous, and only half sad.
- Lighting: Full synthwave: magenta key, cyan rim, haze, one practical lamp
  left in for realism.

### [verse 2 – the car]

```
A car with a tape deck and a heater that lied,
Frost on the inside of the glass and your hand on the dial.
We drove past the water till the second side ran out,
And the click at the end of it was the loudest sound.
I never taped over side A and I don't know why,
I just wound it to the front and I put it back inside.
```

- Scene: Memory, a car at night in winter.
- Shots: Frost scraped from the inside of a windscreen with a sleeve; a
  dashboard tape deck with the tape going in; a hand on a heater dial, only
  the hand; headlights on a wet road beside water; the deck clicking at the
  end of the side and the cabin going silent; present-day cut of a pencil
  winding a spool back to the front.
- Emotion: The coldest and most beautiful sequence in the video.
- Lighting: Dashboard green, oncoming headlights, black water.

### [pre-chorus 2]

```
Thumb on the button, and I know I should stop,
The lid clicks down and the motor kicks up.
Four beats of a drum that I'd know anywhere,
And I'm nineteen again and you're still standing there.
```

- Scene: Present, the second play.
- Shots: A thumb on rewind held too long; the counter numbers spinning
  (composited); her sitting on the floor with her back to the bed.
- Emotion: Deliberate relapse.
- Lighting: Lamp plus the cold of a phone screen face-down and glowing.

### [chorus 2]

- Scene: Bigger, both worlds fully merged.
- Shots: The teenage bedroom furniture standing in her present-day flat; a
  wall of tapes; the two versions of her passing each other without looking.
- Emotion: Overwhelmed.
- Lighting: Deeper magenta, more haze.

### [instrumental – lead solo, tape-stop, rewind]

- Scene: The world stalls and rewinds, no lyrics.
- Shots: A tape-stop rendered literally, everything in the room slowing and
  dropping in pitch and slumping; a full rewind sequence played backwards,
  the car reversing, the frost re-forming, the drawer re-opening; a single
  bar of pure hiss with the frame going to black and only the arpeggio
  ticking; the lead synth solo shot as her walking a corridor of light.
- Lighting: The most stylised minute of the video, then a hard drop to
  nothing.

### [bridge – no drums, one pad]

```
I don't want you back, I want the girl that I was,
The one who thought a tape was a promise you could hold.
I want the bedroom carpet and the waiting and the hiss,
I want an afternoon spent making one thing good.
You were never the point, though I let you be for years,
You're just the sound of the last time that I was new.
```

- Scene: The song stops performing. All the synthwave lighting drains out.
- Shots: Her sitting on the floor of a plain, real, badly lit room with the
  tape in her hand; a slow push to her face; a single flashback frame of her
  own nineteen-year-old self on a bedroom carpet with headphones on, alone
  and happy, no boy in the frame at all; back to her, deciding something.
- Emotion: The turn. This is the only part of the video with no colour gel.
- Lighting: One overhead bulb. Deliberately unflattering and honest.

### [final chorus – biggest]

- Scene: The colour comes back, but warmer.
- Shots: Her standing up; the flat's windows going gold instead of magenta;
  the spools turning one more time in macro; her dancing alone with the
  headphones on, properly, not sadly; the widest frame of the video.
- Emotion: Grief that has become fondness inside four minutes.
- Lighting: Warm amber replacing magenta, cyan reduced to a rim.

### [post-chorus]

```
Side A, side B, and the click at the end,
Side A, side B, and I wind it again.
All that hiss, all that hope, that afternoon,
Side A, side B, and I let it run through.
```

- Scene: The tape playing to the end of the side on its own.
- Shots: The spools with one side nearly empty; the counter slowing; the
  mechanism clicking and stopping; her not reaching for it.
- Lighting: Warm and level.

### [outro – the drawer]

```
Batteries out and the drawer slides away,
Card in the case with the crease and the fray.
I won't wind you back for a long time, but stay,
I've got a cassette tape heart, and you're on side A.
```

- Scene: Putting it away, on purpose this time.
- Shots: Two batteries taken out and set on the desk; the card slid back
  into the case along its worn crease; the cassette placed in the drawer
  flat, not buried; the drawer sliding shut in macro; final shot, the closed
  drawer and the lamp switching off, the room lit only by the window.
- Emotion: Closure without disposal. She keeps it.
- Lighting: Warm lamp, then window blue, then nothing.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the lid clicking down and the spools starting to turn,
  cut on the first chorus hit.
- **The challenge:** the drawer test — post the object you have never been
  able to throw away, with the chorus over it.
- **The caption line:** *"Recorded over you, and you still bleed through the
  seams."*
- **The quote:** *"I don't want you back, I want the girl that I was."*
- **The visual gag with a heart in it:** the batteries taken out of a wall
  clock to play a mixtape.
- **The last image:** the drawer closing on a tape that is lying flat rather
  than buried.
