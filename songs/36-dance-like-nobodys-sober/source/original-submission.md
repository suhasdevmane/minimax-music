# Dance Like Nobody's Sober — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A wedding reception that has stopped behaving. Not a joke song about a bad
wedding — a real one about the specific joy of a room full of people who are
related to each other, or used to be, all dancing badly at the same time.
The reason it matters arrives in the bridge: this exact room will never
happen again.

**Mood:**
Loud, warm, sweaty, unembarrassed. Wit rather than comedy: the funny details
are true, and they are there to make the last verse and the bridge land.

**Message:**
"Nobody here is being cool tonight, and none of us will ever be in one room
like this again."

**Genre:**
Party pop with a live-band reception energy. Think: Dua Lipa x Meghan
Trainor, with the gang-vocal size of a Sigala record and the brass and
handclaps of a wedding band that has genuinely stopped caring.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 126 BPM, 4/4
- Key: D major (bright, open, easy for a whole room to shout in)
- No modulation. The final chorus is the loudest point of the record by
  arrangement and gang-vocal size alone.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, fast and conversational in the verses,
  belted in the choruses, shouted in the post-chorus chant, close and
  unguarded in the bridge.
- Backing vocals: gang vocals of twenty or more voices from the first
  chorus onward; a handful of unaccompanied voices close the outro.

**Instrumentation:**
- Four-on-the-floor kick, live-feel kick and snare in the choruses
- Slap-feel electric bass
- Wide supersaw synth stacks (the chorus signature)
- Bright plucked synth carrying the verses
- Brass: trumpet and trombone stabs, a counter-melody in the later choruses
- Stacked handclaps, tambourine, cowbell
- Upright piano: the bridge alone, and the outro
- Real crowd noise, whistles, shouts; car park room tone and a distant PA

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: outside in the car park, the door opening on a room already gone
- Verses: the reception coming apart, catalogued, then the people in it named
- Pre-chorus: the DJ, the request, the bass through the folding chairs
- Chorus and post-chorus: the hook shouted by the whole room, then the chant
- Instrumental: a band breakdown, then a hard drop to near silence
- Bridge: the beat gone, one piano, the reason the night matters
- Final chorus: the loudest thing on the record
- Outro: the car park at half five, a handful of voices, shoes in hand

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – filtered kick, room tone, a door opening]

```
String lights over the parking lot, band's loading in,
Somebody's grandma's already three glasses in.
The DJ says, one more, and the whole room roars,
And a hundred pairs of shoes hit the floor.
```

- Scene: Outside a reception hall at eleven at night, string lights strung between poles over a parking lot.
- Shot: A slow push toward the building; the music is muffled and distant until a fire door swings open and the sound and light spill out.
- Cut to: An older woman in her best coat on a plastic chair by the door, glass in hand, entirely delighted, not remotely apologetic.
- Emotion and lighting: Anticipation; cool blue car park against a hot amber doorway, the contrast doing all the work.

### [verse 1]

```
There's a napkin in the punch bowl and a fork on the floor,
The best man ran eleven minutes, then he ran some more.
Cousins from three time zones that nobody has seen
Since the summer that we all turned seventeen.
Table nine has given up on ever sitting down,
And the kids in socks go skidding past the sound.
```

- Shots: The room mid-collapse, still recognisably a wedding. A napkin sagging in a punch bowl. A fork on the carpet nobody picks up. A best man at a microphone with three cue cards and no plan, the room's polite faces holding on. Three cousins colliding in a hug by the bar. An entirely empty table nine with nine full drinks on it. Two children in socks skidding the length of the parquet and crashing into a curtain.
- Emotion and lighting: Affectionate chaos; warm tungsten uplighters, a disco ball throwing dots, real light not CGI.

```
My shoes are in a plant pot and I'm leaving them right there,
There's frosting on my elbow and I'm past the point of care.
Somebody's uncle does the shoulder thing he does,
And the whole room comes apart, and I'm in love with us.
```

- Shots: Mahima on the floor with the rest of them. A pair of heels standing upright in a potted ficus. A close-up of a smear of white frosting on her elbow and her not noticing. An uncle in a waistcoat doing one very specific shoulder move; a ring of people losing it around him. Mahima's face lit up, laughing at all of it.
- Emotion and lighting: The first big joy beat; hot amber, moving disco dots, handheld and close.

### [pre-chorus]

```
The DJ takes a request from a woman in a hat,
Plays it twice in a row and nobody minds that.
Bass in the folding chairs, the ceiling's coming loose,
Grab a hand, any hand, we've got nothing left to lose.
```

- Shots: The DJ booth at the end of the hall. A woman in an enormous hat leaning over the booth, shouting a request. The DJ nodding and reaching for the fader. A macro of a folding chair leg buzzing on the parquet with the bass. A ceiling tile visibly moving. Hands finding hands across the floor, nobody checking whose.
- Emotion and lighting: The build; the room lights starting to strobe on the snare roll.

### [chorus – whole-room gang vocal]

```
Dance like nobody's sober, sing like nobody's home,
Left foot, wrong foot, doesn't matter, it's our own.
Hands up, ceiling down, throw the whole night over,
Nobody's watching, and nobody's sober.

Dance like nobody's sober, sing like nobody's home,
Every song's our song when the speakers get blown.
Turn it up, hold on, this is what we came for,
Dance like nobody's sober till they throw us out the door.
```

- Shots: The floor at full capacity. A wide of forty people all doing something different at the same tempo. Hands up on the downbeat, the whole room. The disco ball dots crossing faces. A slow-motion beat of confetti coming out of a cannon somebody definitely was not licensed to fire. The bride's veil in motion at the centre of it.
- Emotion and lighting: Peak joy; saturated amber and magenta, strobe on the brass stabs, everything moving.

### [post-chorus – shouted chant]

```
Left foot, wrong foot, doesn't matter, doesn't matter,
Left foot, wrong foot, let the whole floor shatter.
Left foot, wrong foot, doesn't matter, doesn't matter,
Dance like nobody's sober, and nobody's sober.
```

- Scene: Four ultra-fast repeating shots built for short-form edits.
- Shots: Feet in socks on parquet; a hand hitting the air; a tray of drinks going past at speed; the disco ball. Same four, same order, on every repetition of the chant.
- Emotion and lighting: Pure release; hard strobe cuts.

### [verse 2]

```
The bride has got her dress hitched up above her knees,
Her mother's on the floor and her father's on his feet.
Two aunties who have not spoken since a funeral in June
Are holding both hands, screaming out the same tune.
The groom has lost his jacket and I think he's lost a shoe,
The photographer gave up an hour or two ago too.
```

- Scene: The specific people, one at a time, each held long enough to read.
- Shots: The bride with her dress bunched in one fist, dancing properly. Her mother on the floor doing something from 1983; her father standing on a chair filming it. Two women in their sixties holding both hands, howling a chorus at each other, one of them crying and grinning at once. The groom in shirtsleeves, one shoe on. The photographer sitting on the edge of the stage with the camera in her lap, watching instead of shooting.
- Emotion and lighting: The heart of the video — every one of these is a real person with a history; warm, close, handheld.

```
There's a cousin on a chair who should not be on a chair,
And a conga line has started and I don't know where.
The waiters have stopped pretending, they are in it now,
And somebody's grandma's leading it, don't ask me how.
```

- Shots: The conga line. A cousin standing on a folding chair with both arms up and no plan for getting down. A line forming from nothing, snaking around the cake table. Two waiters abandoning a tray and joining. The front of the line: the same older woman from the intro, coat off, leading twenty people, absolutely in charge.
- Emotion and lighting: Total surrender; wide tracking shot following the line all the way round the room.

### [pre-chorus 2]

```
The DJ's taking requests from a queue outside the booth,
The bride says, play it one more time, and that's the truth.
Bass in the folding chairs, the ceiling's coming loose,
Grab a hand, any hand, we've got nothing left to lose.
```

- Shots: A queue of six people waiting at the DJ booth. The queue, patient and serious, like it is a bank. The bride arriving at the front and being let straight in. The DJ laughing and reloading the same track. The room reacting before the drop lands.
- Emotion and lighting: Momentum; lights building again.

### [chorus]

(repeat)

- Shots: Bigger, more people, less floor. An overhead of the entire floor from the balcony or a ladder. The brass counter-melody mimed by two guests with imaginary trumpets. Mahima in the middle, arms up, head back.
- Emotion and lighting: Fuller and hotter; the strobe faster, more confetti underfoot.

### [post-chorus]

(repeat) — the same four chant shots, cut faster; hard, fast, saturated.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: The band breakdown. No lyrics.
- Shots: A drummer and a percussionist trading with the whole room clapping on the two and four. A synth lead over an overhead of the floor. Two guests attempting a lift and abandoning it, laughing. The cake table with one slice left. A hard cut to near-black: the lights dropping, the room breathing, a single kid asleep on a pile of coats.
- Emotion and lighting: Full band energy into sudden quiet; strobe into darkness.

### [bridge – no beat, one piano]

```
Half past one, and the room goes soft for a beat,
There's a kid asleep on a mountain of coats by our feet.
And I look at every face that I grew up beside,
And I know that this exact room will not happen twice.
So I take the nearest hand and I lift it in the air,
While the song is still playing and the floor is still there.
```

- Shots: The one still moment in the film. Mahima stopped in the middle of a moving room, everything around her slightly slowed. A child asleep face-down on a heap of coats on two pushed-together chairs. A slow pan across faces she has known her whole life, each one held for a beat: the aunties, the uncle, her mother, the bride. Her eyes going wet and her not letting it happen. Then her hand finding another hand and both of them going up.
- Emotion and lighting: The turn; the lights dropped to a single warm wash, everything else in shadow.

### [final chorus – loudest point of the record]

```
Dance like nobody's sober, sing like nobody's home,
Left foot, wrong foot, doesn't matter, it's our own.
Hands up, lights up, last ones on the floor,
Nobody's leaving, and nobody's sober.

Dance like nobody's sober, sing like nobody's home,
Every song's our song and the speakers have blown.
Turn it up, hold on, this is what we came for,
Dance like nobody's sober till they throw us out the door.
```

- Scene: Everything back, all at once, louder.
- Shots: The house lights coming up on a floor that refuses to clear. The bride and groom carried, badly. The grandmother back at the front. Confetti in the air a second time. A wide of the whole hall from the doorway, every single person in frame and moving.
- Emotion and lighting: Maximum; house lights, full colour, no shadows left.

### [post-chorus]

(repeat) — the four chant shots one last time, then the doors opening, ending on the last strobe frame before daylight.

### [outro – piano and a handful of voices]

```
Car park, half five, and the sky is going grey,
Shoes in one hand, cake in a napkin, on our way.
Somebody starts the chorus in the taxi line once more,
Dance like nobody's sober till they throw us out the door.
```

- Scene: Outside, half five in the morning.
- Shots: The car park in flat grey light, string lights still on and now pointless. Six people in evening wear sitting on a kerb. Shoes carried in hands; cake wrapped in napkins. Somebody starting the chorus in the taxi queue and four voices joining, ragged and unaccompanied. Final shot: the fire door swinging shut on the empty hall, confetti all over the floor.
- Emotion and lighting: Tired, happy, sentimental without saying so; flat dawn grey, one warm doorway.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** forty people with their hands up on the first *"dance like nobody's sober"*, disco dots across every face.
- **The challenge:** the wrong-foot cut — post the post-chorus chant and invite people to film the worst dancer at the best wedding they have been to.
- **The caption line:** *"This exact room will not happen twice."*
- **The quote:** *"Nobody's watching, and nobody's sober."*
- **The still:** the grandmother at the front of the conga line with her coat off.
