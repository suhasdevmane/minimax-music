# Butterflies Don't Lie — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
She has told her friends four separate times that it is nothing. Her mouth is doing an excellent job. Her body is running a completely different campaign — heart rate, ears, hands, knee — and her friends can read it from across a brunch table. The song is the gap between what she says and what she is obviously feeling, and the moment she finally stops arguing with it.

**Mood:**
Bright, funny, giddy, warm. Wit inside a real feeling, never a joke song. Candy-colored on the surface and genuinely nervous underneath.

**Message:**
"You can talk your way out of anything except your own nervous system."

**Genre:**
Bubbly synth-pop. Think: Carly Rae Jepsen x Dua Lipa's rhythmic verses x the plucky arpeggio world of a Robyn record, with a chorus written to be shouted at a table.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 122 BPM, 4/4
- Key: C major (open, bright, no shadow in it anywhere)
- No modulation; the final chorus lifts by arrangement — an octave synth line, stacked harmonies, crowd claps.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, light and conspiratorial in the verses as if leaning across a table, full and bright in the choruses, plain and unarmoured in the bridge.
- Backing vocals: stacked female harmonies in the choruses; a chanted group vocal on the post-chorus; the bridge is entirely unstacked.

**Instrumentation:**
- Plucky arpeggiated synth as the signature line, running the whole record and doubled an octave up in the choruses
- Rubbery sidechained synth bass, taking the melody when the arpeggio drops
- Four-on-the-floor kick with an open hat on the offbeat in every chorus; closed hi-hat and rim in the verses
- Warm electric piano under the bridge and outro
- Bright lead synth for the instrumental break and the post-chorus answer
- Finger snaps, handclaps, a shaker
- Textures: café ambience, cutlery, a low watch-buzz tick, a very quiet notification blip

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: the brunch table, the name said too casually, the first blush
- Verses: the evidence piling up — the watch, the group chat, the deleted message, the corner in the cold
- Pre-choruses: the argument, the excuses running out, the drums filtering away
- Chorus: the drop, the admission, joy
- Instrumental: the whole table on its feet, chopped vocal, a half-bar of silence
- Bridge: no drums, no armour, she says it
- Final chorus: widest arrangement, the ceiling does not fall in
- Outro: the same table, the same plate, a different face

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – plucky arpeggio, filtered kick, café ambience]

```
Table for four on a Saturday, sun through the glass,
I said his name like it was nothing and the whole table laughed.
Three iced coffees and a plate we're all pretending to share,
And I'm suddenly the only one who's going pink in here.
```

- Scene: A bright café at midday, four young women at a window table, sun coming through in slabs.
- Shot: Overhead of the table — three tall iced coffees, one shared plate of food nobody has touched, a sunflower in a jam jar.
- Cut to: Her mid-sentence, the name landing, and three heads turning at once in slow motion.
- Her reaction / Emotion: A performance of casualness that lasts about half a second.
- Lighting: Hard clean daylight, candy colors, saturated.

### [verse 1a]

```
I said it's nothing, honestly, we're only talking a bit,
He texted twice this morning and I haven't answered it.
Then somebody says his name a second time out loud,
And the sunflower on the table turns around to look at me.
```

- Scene: The defence — she keeps talking, the table keeps not believing her. Shots: her gesturing with a fork to make a very reasonable point; a friend's flat unimpressed blink; the phone face-down beside her plate with two unanswered messages (screen composited); the sunflower in the jar rotating a few degrees toward her, a single small unreal beat in an otherwise real video.
- Emotion: Confident, then not.
- Lighting: Daylight, warm, color-popped.

### [verse 1b]

```
My watch buzzes, tells me that my heart rate's high for sitting,
The waiter asks if I'm alright and I say I'm doing fine.
The group chat has the receipts and the receipts run long,
Somebody kept a screenshot of the things I said in June,
And they read the whole thing back to me in my own voice.
```

- Scene: The evidence. Shots: extreme close-up on a smartwatch flashing a high heart rate (UI composited); a waiter pausing with a jug, genuinely concerned; a phone held up across the table scrolling a group chat far too fast to read; her face going through several emotions in one continuous take while a friend reads aloud off a screen.
- Emotion: Caught, and enjoying being caught more than she will admit.
- Lighting: Same bright daylight; a slight push into saturation on the watch shot.

### [pre-chorus 1]

```
I can talk my way around it, I can argue it to death,
I had fourteen good excuses and I'm somewhere down to nine.
But my hands are on the table doing something on their own,
And nobody has to ask me, they can watch me lose the line.
```

- Scene: The last stand, drums filtering out under her. Shots: her building an argument with both hands; a tight macro on her fingers shredding the corner of a paper napkin without her noticing; her ears going pink at the tips; the whole table just watching, saying nothing.
- Emotion: Losing, and knowing it.
- Lighting: Daylight narrowing to a single warm shaft on the table as the room defocuses.

### [chorus 1 – the drop]

```
My mouth says maybe,
But butterflies don't lie.
I can hold it together in a room full of people,
I just can't hold it together on the inside.
Ask the goosebumps, ask the shaking in my knee,
Ask whoever's running things down there instead of me.
I get one vote in this body and I lose it every time.
My mouth says maybe,
But butterflies don't lie.
```

- Scene: The color turns up to maximum. Choreographed but loose — friends, café, street outside the window.
- Shots: All four of them dancing in the booth; her spinning out from the table into a sunlit doorway; a macro of goosebumps rising along a forearm; her knee bouncing under the table at double speed; a wide of the four of them crossing the street in a line, all on the beat.
- Emotion: Joy. No shame in it at all.
- Lighting: Peak saturation — yellows, pinks, a hard blue sky.

### [post-chorus 1 – chant]

```
Don't lie, don't lie, they never learned how,
Don't lie, don't lie, and they're telling on me now.
Don't lie, don't lie, they never learned how,
My mouth says maybe but the butterflies are loud.
```

- Scene: Four fast impact shots on the chant — a hand slapping the table, the sunflower, her grin caught mid-denial, three friends pointing at her at once.
- Emotion: Group energy. Lighting: hard bright, high contrast.

### [verse 2a]

```
Tuesday, he asks if I'm free and I typed out a no,
Sat and read it for a minute and I let it go.
Deleted every letter, wrote a yes with a little too much,
Took the mark off, put it back, then took it off again.
Forty minutes on a message that was two letters long.
```

- Scene: Alone in her room, the opposite of the brunch table. Shots: her lying across a bed with the phone above her face, blue evening light; extreme close-up of a thumb hovering (message composited); the same message typed, deleted, typed again; a clock on the wall moving forty minutes in three cuts while nothing else changes.
- Emotion: Private, ridiculous, tender.
- Lighting: Cooler and softer than the café — the only cool sequence in the video.

### [verse 2b]

```
Thursday he was early, on the corner in the cold,
Two coffees and my order right and I had never told him.
I had a whole speech ready about keeping this thing light,
Then my body walked me over there before I said a word.
```

- Scene: A street corner, early evening, cold enough to see breath.
- Shots: Kai on the corner with two takeout cups, checking the time, breath visible; her rounding the corner and stopping dead for one beat; a close-up of the cup with her order right; her feet starting to move before her face has decided anything; the two of them meeting, the speech not happening.
- Emotion: The moment the argument is lost for good.
- Lighting: Cold blue street with warm store windows behind them; her first warm-lit close-up of the sequence.

### [pre-chorus 2]

```
I could talk my way around it, I could argue it all week,
I had fourteen good excuses and I'm down to about two.
But my face went and answered while my mouth was still deciding,
And the girls don't say a word, they let me dig myself through.
```

- Scene: Back at the café, a different day, same table. Shots: her mid-argument again with a coffee cup for emphasis; a slow push-in on her face as the smile arrives without permission; three friends in a row, saying absolutely nothing, letting her keep going.
- Emotion: Comedy with real warmth underneath.
- Lighting: Bright daylight returning, the cool sequence over.

### [chorus 2]

Repeat of chorus 1 — bigger, out of the café and into the street.

- Scene: The same choreography moved outdoors — a market street, a bus stop, a crossing. The four of them moving through a market on the beat, her spinning past a fruit stall, the goosebumps macro reused tighter, a wide crane-style pull back over the crossing.
- Emotion: Bigger, freer. Lighting: full sun, maximum color.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: The room dancing, a chopped vocal, then one half-bar of nothing.
- Shots: The four friends dancing badly and joyfully in the café doorway; the waiter joining in for exactly one beat; a macro of ice cracking in a glass; her alone in frame, still, mid-crowd, one hand on her stomach; a hard cut to black for the silence.
- Lighting: Saturated, then a single frame of flat white.

### [bridge – electric piano, shaker, no drums]

```
Fine. Fine. You want it slow and out loud,
I like him. I like him. I have liked him since the fourth of March.
I like the way he listens with his whole entire face,
I like that he remembered something I said and didn't mean,
I like that he is not smooth about it, not at all.
So take the plate away, I'm not eating anything,
I have got a stomach full of something with a mind of its own.
```

- Scene: The confession, straight to her friends across the table, nothing left in the tank.
- Shots: A single unbroken medium close-up of her talking, no cuts for four lines; then three two-second inserts of him not being smooth — dropping a cup lid, laughing at his own joke, standing wrong; back to her; the plate being slid away from her by a friend without a word.
- Emotion: Relief. The armour is off and nothing bad happens.
- Lighting: Warm, soft, the busiest background of the video gone quiet behind her.

### [final chorus – widest, octave synth, crowd claps]

```
My mouth says maybe,
But butterflies don't lie.
I said it out loud in a room full of people,
And the ceiling didn't fall out of the sky.
Ask the goosebumps, ask the shaking in my knee,
Ask whoever's running things down there instead of me.
I got one vote in this body and I finally let it go.
My mouth said maybe,
But the butterflies were right.
```

- Scene: The biggest version — the café, the street, the crossing, everyone. Shots: the four of them dancing on the sidewalk with strangers clapping along; a low angle up at a clean blue sky and a ceiling that conspicuously does not fall; her walking toward the corner where he is waiting, still dancing; the two of them, finally, in the same frame in daylight.
- Emotion: Full release, unembarrassed.
- Lighting: The brightest in the video.

### [post-chorus 2 – chant]

Repeat of the chant, on the street.

- Scene: Four hard cuts — clapping hands, her shoes, the sunflower carried out of the café, their two coffee cups touching. Lighting: hard bright, high contrast.

### [outro – arpeggio alone, café ambience returning]

```
Table for four on a Saturday, sun through the glass,
I said his name like it was something and the whole table clapped.
Same iced coffee, same plate that we never share,
And I'm the only one still smiling like an idiot in here.
```

- Scene: The bookend. Same table, same overhead framing, one week later.
- Shots: The identical overhead of the table from the intro, matched exactly — except now there is a fourth cup and her phone is face-up; three friends applauding; final shot on her face, smiling and not hiding it, holding until the fade.
- Emotion: Happy, settled, still slightly ridiculous.
- Lighting: The same clean daylight as the intro, one stop warmer.

## Viral moments

- **The sunflower turn.** One small unreal beat in a real video — the flower rotating to look at her. Instantly loopable and the video's signature.
- **The hook.** *"My mouth says maybe, but butterflies don't lie"* is a caption on its own.
- **The line for captions.** *"I get one vote in this body and I lose it every time."*
- **The challenge.** The smartwatch high-heart-rate screenshot: post yours with the person's name blurred, cut on the chorus drop.
- **The quote.** *"I like that he is not smooth about it, not at all."*
- **The bookend.** Two identical overhead table shots, seven days apart, cut back to back.
