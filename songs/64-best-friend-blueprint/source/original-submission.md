# Best Friend Blueprint — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A friendship anthem as a manifesto. Nine years, two cities, one best friend
who shows up with snacks, tells the truth in the right order and takes her
phone off her at midnight. The last chorus swaps the roles.

**Mood:**
Euphoric, funny, unembarrassed. Loud enough for a birthday post, specific
enough to mean it.

**Message:**
"Everybody wants a love song. This one's for the girl who drove four hours."

**Genre:**
Bright uptempo pop. Think: Charli XCX plucked synths x Carly Rae Jepsen
euphoria, with a Lizzo-sized gang vocal at the end.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 118 BPM, 4/4. Key: F major (bright, sits high in her chest voice)
- No key change; the lift is a second clap layer, a gang vocal and a
  countermelody on the final chorus.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead: young adult female, fast and conversational in the verses, belted on
  the hook, near-spoken in the bridge.
- Backing: octave doubles and stacked harmonies, a chanted post-chorus, a
  real gang vocal on the last title line.

**Instrumentation:**
- Bright plucked synth carrying the hook end to end; staccato square-wave
  answers; a countermelody from chorus two; brass stabs on the downbeats
- Round low sine bass, four-on-the-floor kick, hats, rim clicks, toms
- Real hand claps recorded in a room, the second lead instrument of the record
- Textures: a filtered vocal chop of the title, white-noise sweeps, reverse
  claps, a car door, a kettle switch, a highway hum, an indicator tick

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: nine years counted off, dry and close, no reverb
- Verses: snacks as first aid, then the fight and eleven days of silence
- Pre-choruses: every other friend measured against her, coming up short
- Chorus and post-chorus: the hook, the phone confiscation, the chant
- Instrumental: the drive, the clap breakdown, the half-time drop
- Bridge and final chorus: the reversal, and the same words with the roles
  swapped; the outro repeats the intro count over a car that finally died

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro — filtered arpeggio, one clap, no kick]
```
Nine years, two cities and a group chat with a name,
Three phones, one charger, and a car that barely runs.
I have never had to explain myself to you,
Not one time, not once.
```
- Scene: A kitchen table at night, two mugs, two phones, one charger between them.
- Shot: Overhead of the table: two phones, one plug, a tangle of cable, four hands entering and leaving frame on the beat. Cut to: A driveway outside, a small old car with the interior light on and one door open.
- Emotion: Comfortable. Neither of them is performing for the other or for us.
- Lighting: One warm kitchen pendant, the rest of the room dark; blue night through the window.

### [verse 1 — kick and bass drop in]
```
You show up with snacks like it's a medical emergency,
Sour candy and a bag of something fried.
You don't ask me if I'm fine, you just start unpacking,
And you put the kettle on and let me cry.
You tell me the truth in the exact right order,
The nice part first, then the part I need to hear.
You said, that dress is not the problem, and you're right,
And I put on the other one and we got out of there.
```
- Scene: A doorstep arrival and a bedroom triage, played fast and funny.
- Shot: The friend on the doorstep with two carrier bags held up like evidence; the bags emptied across a duvet in one continuous overhead shot, everything landing on the beat. Cut to: A kettle switch, steam, Mahima crying and laughing at the same time; then a bedroom mirror, two dresses, a verdict delivered with one raised eyebrow, and both of them out the door.
- Emotion: The specific relief of somebody arriving who does not need to be briefed.
- Lighting: Hard practical bedroom lamps, warm and slightly messy, nothing styled.

### [pre-chorus 1 — kick out, claps and filter sweep]
```
Every friend I've ever made, I measured against you,
And every single one of them came up short.
You're not the bar, you're the whole entire building,
You're the reason I know what a person's worth.
```
- Scene: The measuring, made literal and slightly absurd.
- Shot: A pencil line on a door frame, the way parents mark height; several lines lower down, one much higher, all unlabelled. Cut to: A low angle on the friend standing in a doorway, shot to look enormous, deadpan, one hand on the frame.
- Emotion: Comic on the surface, entirely sincere underneath. Do not wink at camera.
- Lighting: Backlit from the hallway so she is a silhouette with an edge of gold.

### [chorus 1 — full kit, claps, gang energy]
```
You're the blueprint, best friend, everyone else is a copy,
Cheap ink, wrong size, and the second page is gone.
You take my phone at midnight, then the keys, then the wheel,
You tell me what I look like when I won't say how I feel.
Everybody wants a love song, I'm not writing one,
This one's for the girl who drove four hours in the dark.
Say it in a toast, say it on a card,
You're the blueprint, best friend, everyone else is a copy.
```
- Scene: The chorus is the two of them and a kitchen with the fridge open.
- Shot: Both of them dancing badly and fully committed in a small kitchen, fridge door open as the only key light, socks on linoleum, one of them on a chair. Cut to: A hand taking a phone off a table and putting it in a coat pocket; a set of keys sliding across a counter; a hand on a steering wheel at night.
- Emotion: Peak joy. This is the frame people screenshot and send.
- Lighting: Fridge light plus one warm bulb; deliberately unglamorous and instantly recognisable.

### [post-chorus 1 — clap and kick, vocal chop]
```
Blueprint, blueprint, everyone else is a copy,
Blueprint, blueprint, nobody else got it right.
Blueprint, blueprint, everyone else is a copy,
You're the original, honey, I got the only one.
```
- Scene: The chant, cut as a two-person routine.
- Shot: A locked-off head-on two-shot against a plain wall, both of them doing the same four-move clap routine, one beat behind the other on purpose. Cut to: The same routine on a beach, in an elevator, in a parking garage, one bar each, years apart, hair and clothes changing.
- Emotion: Silly, tight, obviously rehearsed over a decade.
- Lighting: Flat and even in every version so the only thing that changes is them.

### [verse 2 — rim clicks, sharper]
```
You told me the truth about him in a parking lot,
And I didn't speak to you for eleven days.
You never chased me down, you left the door unlocked,
And you saved me half of everything anyway.
Then I called you from a stairwell, you picked up on the first ring,
You didn't say I told you so, you said, I'm in the car.
Four hours in the dark with a bag of sour candy,
And you never once asked me how I let it get that far.
```
- Scene: The fight, the silence, and the drive.
- Shot: Two figures arguing in a parking lot between two cars, no audio implied, one walking away; then eleven quick cuts of a phone screen with no new messages, one per day, all composited. Cut to: A front door left visibly unlocked; a plate covered with foil on a counter; then a stairwell at night, a phone answered on the first ring, and headlights pulling out.
- Emotion: The most serious stretch in the song. Play the reconciliation as undramatic.
- Lighting: Sodium orange in the parking lot, cold blue for the silent days, warm dashboard green for the drive.

### [pre-chorus 2 — full clap build]
```
Every love I've ever lost, I got over in your kitchen,
And every version of me, you have kept them all.
You're not a chapter in the book, you're the spine,
And I would sign up for the whole nine years again.
```
- Scene: The kitchen as an archive.
- Shot: A slow pan across the friend's fridge door: nine years of photographs of the two of them, hairstyles and phones changing left to right. Cut to: The same kitchen table from the intro, seen from above, with a different crisis on it in each of four fast cuts.
- Emotion: The realisation landing rather than being announced.
- Lighting: Warm, domestic, the same pendant in every one of the four cuts.

### [chorus 2 — countermelody, brass stabs]
```
You're the blueprint, best friend, everyone else is a copy,
Cheap ink, wrong size, and the second page is gone.
You take my phone at midnight, then the keys, then the wheel,
You tell me what I look like when I won't say how I feel.
Everybody wants a love song, I'm not writing one,
This one's for the girl who drove four hours in the dark.
Say it in a toast, say it on a card,
You're the blueprint, best friend, everyone else is a copy.
```
- Scene: The kitchen dance again, but bigger and with the years layered in.
- Shot: The same kitchen composition, and on every second bar the two of them are a different age in the same frame position. Cut to: A bar sink, hair being held back, a glass of water arriving without being asked for; a phone taken firmly out of a hand at a table.
- Emotion: Bigger, louder, with the second half of the chorus now carrying the weight of verse two.
- Lighting: Identical fridge-light key in every age so the match cuts land.

### [post-chorus 2 — chant, harder]
```
Blueprint, blueprint, everyone else is a copy,
Blueprint, blueprint, nobody else got it right.
Blueprint, blueprint, everyone else is a copy,
You're the original, honey, I got the only one.
```
- Scene: The routine again, faster, in more places.
- Shot: Eight one-bar cuts of the same clap routine in eight locations, ending on a hotel corridor at three in the morning. Cut to: The two of them collapsing against a wall laughing, out of breath.
- Emotion: Escalation, then release.
- Lighting: Each location keeps its own real light; no grading is applied to unify them.

### [instrumental] — scene block only

- Scene: The four-hour drive, in full, with nobody talking.
- Shot: Wing mirror, indicator tick, empty interstate, a bag of sour candy passed sideways without a word; a service-station forecourt at two in the morning. Cut to: The clap breakdown staged as the two of them clapping on the hood of the parked car under a forecourt light; then a hard half-time drop to the car pulling away.
- Emotion: Companionable silence, which is the entire thesis of the song.
- Lighting: Dashboard green, oncoming headlights, one brutal white forecourt light.

### [bridge — electric piano, half feel]
```
Last year it was your turn and I got to hold the door,
I drove the four hours and I didn't say a thing.
And you looked at me like no one ever did that before,
And I thought, so that's what she's been doing all along.
If this is the great romance of my life, then fine,
Nine years, no contract, and nobody had to sign.
```
- Scene: The reversal. Same road, same car, other driver.
- Shot: The exact drive shots from the instrumental, reversed: Mahima at the wheel, the friend in the passenger seat with her forehead against the window. Cut to: The friend's face turning to look at her, and holding it, which is the shot the whole video is built toward.
- Emotion: The turn. Quiet, adult, no music-video crying.
- Lighting: Pre-dawn blue through the windscreen, the dashboard the only warm source.

### [final chorus — gang vocal, roles swapped]
```
You're the blueprint, best friend, everyone else is a copy,
Cheap ink, wrong size, and the second page is gone.
I take your phone at midnight, then the keys, then the wheel,
I tell you what you look like when you won't say how you feel.
Everybody wants a love song, so here's the only one,
For the girl who drove four hours and the girl who drove them back.
Say it in a toast, say it on a card,
You're the blueprint, best friend, everyone else is a copy.
```
- Scene: A room full of people, and the two of them in the middle of it.
- Shot: A birthday kitchen packed with friends, everyone singing the hook at the two of them; a toast raised; a homemade card held up with the writing turned away from camera. Cut to: The kitchen dance from chorus one repeated exactly, with fifteen people in it now instead of two.
- Emotion: The biggest, warmest moment on the record. Nobody is cool in this shot.
- Lighting: Every light in the flat on, fridge included; the brightest frame in the video.

### [post-chorus 3 — crowd chant]
```
Blueprint, blueprint, everyone else is a copy,
Blueprint, blueprint, nobody else got it right.
Blueprint, blueprint, everyone else is a copy,
Blueprint, blueprint, and I'm keeping you for life.
You're the original, honey, I got the only one,
Blueprint, blueprint, everyone else is a copy.
```
- Scene: The clap routine taught to the whole room and done badly by everybody.
- Shot: The head-on locked-off wall shot from post-chorus one, now with fifteen people in it, the two originals dead centre and perfectly in time. Cut to: Individual faces getting it wrong and laughing, three of them, one bar each.
- Emotion: Communal. The private joke becomes public and loses nothing.
- Lighting: Flat and even, as in every other version of this shot.

### [outro — arpeggio, one dry voice]
```
Nine years, two cities and a group chat with a name,
Three phones, one charger, and a car that finally died.
I have never had to explain myself to you,
And I'm not about to start tonight.
```
- Scene: The same table as the intro, at the end of the night.
- Shot: The overhead table shot from shot one, matched exactly: two phones, one charger, two empty mugs, both of them asleep with their heads down. Cut to: The old car on a driveway with a tarpaulin over it and grass growing round the tyres.
- Emotion: Settled, funny, permanent.
- Lighting: The same warm pendant, dimmed; first grey light at the window.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** two people dancing badly in a small kitchen lit only by an open fridge door. Free to recreate, instantly legible, and the single most postable shot in the catalogue.
- **The clap routine:** four moves, one bar, performed identically in nine locations across nine years and then taught to a whole room.
- **The challenge:** post the routine with your own best friend, one of you a beat behind on purpose, and caption it with the hook.
- **The quote:** *"If this is the great romance of my life, then fine."*
