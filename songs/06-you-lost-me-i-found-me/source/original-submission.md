# You Lost Me, I Found Me — original submission

The song, direction and video concept exactly as submitted, before cleanup
for the lyric body and trimming to the model's 6-minute cap. Kept as source.

**Theme:**
A breakup or difficult period that transformed her into a stronger person.
**Mood:** Inspirational pop / emotional anthem.
**Message:** "You lost me, but I found myself."

**Genre:**
Inspirational pop with cinematic build. Think: part ballad, part empowerment anthem.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 96 BPM, 4/4
- Key: A major (bright, strong, singable)
- Final chorus can lift to B major for extra lift if your generator supports modulation.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, warm and intimate in verses, powerful and clear in choruses, slightly raspy on big notes for emotion.
- Backing vocals: layered harmonies in choruses and final chorus for an anthemic feel.

**Instrumentation:**
- Piano (main spine)
- Warm sub-bass / soft 808s
- Atmospheric synths (pads, light arps)
- Drums: soft kick, snare, claps, shaker in verses; bigger kit in choruses
- Electric guitar with reverb (melodic lines in chorus/bridge)
- Strings in final chorus (violins, cello) for cinematic lift
- Ambient textures: room tone, breath, subtle crowd-like pad in final chorus

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: quiet, reflective, "I remember when I broke"
- Verses: specific moments of pain, realisation, small steps of healing
- Pre-chorus: building strength, "I'm not that girl anymore"
- Chorus: big, singable, viral hook
- Bridge: raw honesty about the hard days, then full recommitment to self
- Final chorus: triumphant, grateful, "I'm better without you"
- Outro: calm, confident, "I'm finally me"

## Why this can go viral

- **Clear, repeatable hook:** "You lost me, but I found me" is simple, emotional, and perfect for captions, Reels, and TikTok transitions.
- **Relatable story:** Breakup → pain → realisation → self-love. Millions of people live this; few songs say it this directly.
- **Strong female empowerment:** Not bitter, not revengeful—just calm, clear, "I'm better without you." That energy travels.
- **Visual potential:** Easy to pair with before/after clips: crying in the bathroom vs walking confidently, old relationship clips vs new life.
- **Singable melody:** Short phrases, clear rhymes, natural breath points—easy for singers and creators to cover.

## Suggested video / visual concept

**Main character:**
- Young woman, early–mid 20s, expressive eyes, natural look.
- Starts in darker, cooler tones; ends in warmer, brighter light.

**Visual arc:**

- **Intro / Verse 1:** Bathroom floor, dim light, tears, phone with old messages. Packing a bag quietly, leaving keys on the table. Walking out at night, streetlights, rain.
- **Pre-chorus 1:** Looking in the mirror, wiping tears, small but real decision in her eyes.
- **Chorus 1:** Montage: her deleting old chats, blocking number, throwing out old gifts. First solo coffee, first solo walk, first smile that looks real.
- **Verse 2:** Crying in the car, then slowly gaining strength: buying flowers, cooking for herself, dancing alone in the living room.
- **Pre-chorus 2:** Mirror scene again, this time with a genuine smile, maybe new haircut or style.
- **Instrumental:** Quick cuts: gym, journaling, working late, laughing with friends, traveling alone, sunset on a balcony.
- **Bridge:** Night, phone in hand, almost texting, then putting it down, deep breath, lying in bed, calm.
- **Final chorus:** Confident walk through the city, bright daylight, friends around, dancing, singing along, no fear.
- **Outro:** Passing the old street or building, pausing, then walking on with a small, peaceful smile. Final shot: her looking into the camera, calm, sure, "I'm home."

**Viral hook idea:**
- Use the line: "You lost me, but I found me" as text on screen.
