# I'll Be There in Every Tomorrow — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material. This is the improved
version of the submitted draft: the promise, the chorus and the bridge's
answered-prayer image are kept; the length is not.

**Theme:** A father's promise to his daughter across her whole life, from a
newborn in his arms to a grey-haired man watching her wedding. The closing
piece of the family set.

**Mood:** Tender, steady, quietly emotional. Heartfelt, never sentimental: the
feeling rests on restraint, breath and small specific images.

**Message:** "I cannot promise perfect days, but I can promise you my love
will always keep you warm."

**Genre:** Orchestral ballad finale, for a wedding, graduation or birthday film.
Sound: a close acoustic string score with a piano at its centre, a chorus that
opens like a slow hymn when the whole room joins in.

**Tempo & Key:** 74 BPM, 4/4, E-flat major, no tempo change: the lift is orchestral.

**Vocal setup:** One male lead, Singer A, a warm lived-in baritone close to the
microphone. A low male harmony a third below on each chorus; a wordless pad of
female and child-register voices on the final chorus.

**Instrumentation:** solo piano (opens, carries the verses); one held violin
under the intro; warm cello; full strings (chorus, bridge); upright bass for
woody low end; soft orchestral drum swell entering with the chorus; brass and
a wordless choir (bridge, final chorus); acoustic guitar under verse 2; closing
piano decay.

**Mood arc:** intro, a first heartbeat he has not yet heard, solo piano.
Verse 1, the arrival, warm cello and a soft wash, the promise forming. Chorus,
the big heart, strings, drum swell, low harmony. Verse 2, the grown daughter,
guitar under the voice. Pre-chorus, the quiet promise, strings only. Chorus,
fuller. Bridge, the summit, full orchestra and choir, the father older with
silver hair, the answered-prayer line. Final chorus, the largest sound on the
record. Outro, stripped back to piano and voice, the forever heart.

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

Male lead **Kai**: late thirties early on, sixties at the end, grey at the
temples. Daughter **Ellie**: a baby, a girl, then a grown woman at her wedding.

### [intro – solo piano, a first heartbeat]

```
I remember your first heartbeat
Before I knew your face,
A tiny promise growing
In a quiet, sacred place.
```
- Scene: A quiet hospital corridor before dawn. Kai, late thirties, sits on a plastic chair outside a closed door, hands clasped, a paper cup going cold.
- Shot: Close two-shot through the narrow window of the door, then a slow push in on his face in profile.
- Cut to: The nursery window at first light, a row of empty cots, the glass cold and grey.
- Emotion: Awe held in check. He is listening to something he cannot see yet.
- Lighting: Cool grey dawn from the corridor window, one warm practical lamp inside the door.

### [verse 1 – the arrival, cello and wash]

```
I wondered who you'd become one day,
What dreams would fill your eyes,
But nothing could prepare me
For the moment you arrived.
They placed you in my waiting arms,
So fragile, warm and new,
The world became a different world
The moment I saw you.
I held my breath and whispered,
I will give you all I can,
And in that very instant, girl,
I became a better man.
```
- Scene: The delivery room, morning. A nurse places a newborn into Kai's arms; he sits very still, afraid to move.
- Shot: Medium close on Kai's hands taking the weight, then a slow reveal of his face above the blanket.
- Cut to: The same man at a kitchen table late at night, a baby monitor glowing, his head in his hands and then lifting.
- Emotion: Overwhelmed, then steadied. Wonder that turns into a vow.
- Lighting: Soft window light in the delivery room, warm lamp at night, both low contrast.

### [chorus – strings, drum swell, low harmony]

```
I'll be there in every tomorrow,
In the sunshine and the rain.
I'll be there through every victory,
And beside you through the pain.
When you're dancing through your happiest days,
When you're searching for the way,
My love will be a steady light
That never fades away.
You are the dream I never knew
My heart was waiting for,
My precious little daughter,
You are what I'm living for.
And though the years may carry you
To places far from home,
You'll never face this life alone,
You'll never face it alone.
```
- Scene: A montage that moves through the early years in the same light. Ellie as a toddler on a living-room rug, Kai on the floor beside her, rain on the window behind them.
- Shot: Wide, static, then tracking slow along the rug. Each cut lands on a line.
- Cut to: Ellie's first day at school, Kai crouched at the gate, her hand in his, a wet pavement shining.
- Emotion: Open, plain, unguarded. The promise said out loud for the first time.
- Lighting: Warm daylight through rain, the sun breaking through on "sunshine".

### [verse 2 – the grown daughter, guitar and cello]

```
When you grow into a young woman
With a world beneath your feet,
When you find the dreams you're meant to chase
And people you will meet,
I won't hold you back from flying,
I won't ask you not to go,
I'll be proud of every distance
That your brave heart dares to know.
But if a dream should break apart,
If someone makes you cry,
If you feel the road is empty
And the stars forget to shine,
You can call the name of Daddy,
And I'll answer where I am,
No matter how old you become,
You'll always be my child.
```
- Scene: Ellie at seventeen, packing a car in a driveway for university, Kai leaning on the door frame with his arms folded and not stopping her.
- Shot: Medium two-shot across the car roof, then a static wide of the empty driveway after the car leaves.
- Cut to: A late-night phone call on a kitchen step, Ellie's face lit by the screen, Kai in the doorway in a dressing gown.
- Emotion: Letting go, and the promise that letting go is not leaving.
- Lighting: Hard late-afternoon sun in the driveway, a soft porch light at night.

### [pre-chorus – strings only]

```
I cannot promise perfect days,
Or shelter from the storm,
But I can promise you my love
Will always keep you warm.
```
- Scene: The hallway wall of family photographs, one by one, the frames catching the light as the camera passes.
- Shot: Slow dolly along the wall, rack focus from a frame of a baby to a frame of a graduate.
- Cut to: Kai alone at the wall, touching one frame with two fingers, then letting his hand drop.
- Emotion: Honest, a little humble. A promise with the limits admitted.
- Lighting: Warm hallway lamps, the frames picking up the glass glare.

### [chorus – fuller, harmony thickening]

```
I'll be there in every tomorrow,
In the sunshine and the rain.
I'll be there through every victory,
And beside you through the pain.
When you're dancing through your happiest days,
When you're searching for the way,
My love will be a steady light
That never fades away.
You are the dream I never knew
My heart was waiting for,
My precious little daughter,
You are what I'm living for.
And though the years may carry you
To places far from home,
You'll never face this life alone,
You'll never face it alone.
```
- Scene: A school hall for a graduation, then a dance floor in a warm room. Ellie in a cap and gown, then in a long dress under string lights.
- Shot: Slow orbit of the graduate in the hall, then a steadicam pass across the dance floor.
- Cut to: Kai in the crowd, watching, his hand over his mouth.
- Emotion: Pride that cannot be contained. The line "through the pain" lands on the old scar of a hard year.
- Lighting: Stage-warm light in the hall, string lights and candles at the dance.

### [bridge – the answered prayer]

```
And when my hair is silver,
And my footsteps have grown slow,
I'll still see the little girl
Who taught my heart to glow.
I'll still hear your laughter
In the quiet of the night,
And every memory of you
Will make the darkness bright.
You gave me more than happiness,
You gave my life a name,
You took my fear and tiredness
And turned them into flame.
You are my greatest blessing,
My answered prayer come true,
There is no love in all this world
Like the love I have for you.
```
- Scene: Kai, sixties now, grey at the temples, on a porch at sunset. Ellie comes up the steps carrying a small child on her hip, and he takes the child's hand.
- Shot: Wide establishing shot of the porch, then a slow move in on Kai's face as the bridge opens. Brass and choir enter here.
- Cut to: Flash of the nursery window at dawn from the intro, then back to the porch in full colour.
- Emotion: The summit. Gratitude said plainly, eyes wet, the voice fuller and open.
- Lighting: Low golden sun across the porch boards, long shadows, warm rim light on his silver hair.

### [chorus – final, largest]

```
I'll be there in every tomorrow,
In the sunshine and the rain.
I'll be there through every victory,
And beside you through the pain.
When you're dancing through your happiest days,
When you're searching for the way,
My love will be a steady light
That never fades away.
You are the dream I never knew
My heart was waiting for,
My precious little daughter,
You are what I'm living for.
And though the years may carry you
To places far from home,
You'll never face this life alone,
You'll never face it alone.
```
- Scene: The wedding. Ellie in white at the top of an aisle, then the first dance with Kai under warm light, a wide floor and a long table behind.
- Shot: Wide, moving slowly around the couple. Cuts to the faces of the guests on the big lines.
- Cut to: Kai's hand on her back for the first dance, then a last cut to the porch, the same sun, the same steps.
- Emotion: Full, unrestrained joy, with the grief of time running under it.
- Lighting: Warm, saturated wedding light: candles, string lights, golden hour through a tent wall.

### [outro – piano and voice]

```
So close your eyes, my baby girl,
Let tomorrow softly start,
No matter where your journey goes,
You'll always own my heart.
I'll be there in every tomorrow,
As I have been from the start,
My daughter, my joy, my little one,
You are my forever heart.
```
- Scene: Early morning in the nursery of the house Ellie grew up in, the cot long gone, a rocking chair by the window. Kai, sixties, sits in it. Ellie, grown, comes in and sits on the floor at his feet.
- Shot: Locked-off wide, the window filling with light, Kai in the chair, then Ellie's head coming to rest on his knee. The last line holds on the two of them.
- Cut to: Black, then the nursery window at dawn, the first frame of the intro, now full of light.
- Emotion: Calm and complete. No new information, only the promise kept.
- Lighting: Clean warm morning light through the nursery window, the room otherwise dim.
