# After Hours — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
The best hour of a night out is the one after the club. Nine people end up
in somebody's sister's flat two floors above a fryer, the big light goes
off, the kettle goes on, and everybody starts telling the truth. A UK
after-party record about the moment performance stops.

**Mood:**
Warm, shuffling, slightly delirious. Not a comedown song and not a rave
song — the in-between one. Funny in the verses, disarmingly sincere by the
bridge.

**Message:**
"Nothing happened here, and it was the truest hour of the week."

**Genre:**
UK garage / two-step with a modern pop top line and a London MC feature.
Think: Craig David x Disclosure x PinkPantheress, with the MC hosting the
room rather than showing off.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 132 BPM, 4/4, swung sixteenths
- Key: C minor
- No modulation; the final chorus lifts by arrangement — a counter-melody,
  two extra lines and the MC underneath.

**Vocal setup:**
- Singer A (female lead): intro, both sung verses, both pre-choruses, all
  choruses, chants, bridge and outro.
- Singer B (male MC): the third verse alone after the instrumental, plus low
  ad-libs under the final chorus.
- Backing: the lead's own vocal chopped and pitched, answering every chorus
  line and reassembled into a new phrase in the break.

**Instrumentation:**
- Shuffled two-step drums: tight closed hats, clipped kick, snare pushed off
  the grid
- Warm round sub-bass moving in octaves
- Organ stabs (the harmonic signature)
- Chopped and pitched vocal science
- Rhodes chord under the second verse
- Wide reverbed clap on the two and the four
- Filtered noise sweeps and reverse cymbals on every section change
- Ambience: strip-light hum, a heavy door, a kettle boiling, vinyl crackle,
  birdsong, a bin lorry, a shop shutter rolling up

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: the club emptying, nobody ready to go home
- Verse 1: the journey — the bus, the shutter, the stairwell, the flat
- Pre-chorus: the big light goes off and the truth sits down
- Chorus: the thesis, euphoric but small-room
- Post-chorus: the chant
- Verse 2: what people actually say at half four
- Instrumental: drum-edit science, a bassline feature, an organ breakdown
- MC verse: the host's side of it, the flat as a country
- Bridge: the balcony, first light, the drums gone
- Final chorus: widest, with the MC underneath
- Outro: full daylight, a bus stop, a message about next week

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – no drums, strip-light hum]

```
Strip lights up and the music is gone,
Doorman counting us out one by one.
Everybody's got a bed and nobody wants it,
Somebody's got a key in their pocket,
So the night is only halfway done.
```

- Scene: A small club at closing. The house strip lights come up and the
  room is suddenly ugly and honest.
- Shot: Wide of a sticky floor, abandoned cups, the DJ unplugging.
- Cut to: Mahima in the middle of the emptying room, coat over one arm,
  entirely unbothered by the lights.
- Emotion: Not disappointed. Waiting for the real part.
- Lighting: Flat white strip light, the worst light in the video, deliberate.

### [verse 1 – the journey]

```
Out on the pavement and my ears are still ringing,
Lost the cloakroom ticket and my mate is still singing.
Somebody says, "my sister's flat, it's two stops on the bus,"
So we're up on the top deck with the windows full of us.
```

- Scene: Pavement, then the night bus.
- Shots: A group spilling out onto a wet pavement; a cloakroom stub being
  patted for and not found; the front seats of a night bus top deck, six
  people across three seats, condensation on the glass; a hand drawing on the
  window.
- Emotion: Loud, silly, happy.
- Lighting: Sodium orange outside, hard fluorescent inside the bus.

```
Past the shutter coming down and the fella with the mop,
Two flights up above the fryers where the smell doesn't stop.
The hallway light's been broken since the year before last,
So we're going up on phone torch and we're going up fast.
```

- Scene: Arrival.
- Shots: A shop shutter rolling down to knee height; a man mopping inside;
  a narrow side door beside the shopfront; a stairwell in total dark lit by
  four phone torches swinging; feet on lino stairs; a hand on a bannister.
- Emotion: Conspiratorial.
- Lighting: Phone torches only — the darkest sequence in the video.

### [pre-chorus 1]

```
Big light off and the little lamp on,
Somebody's playing an old garage song.
The coats are all piled on the end of the bed,
And the truth gets a chair and it sits down instead.
```

- Scene: The flat, first thirty seconds.
- Shots: A hand hitting the wall switch, the room dropping from ceiling
  light to a single lamp; a phone going into a speaker dock; coats going onto
  a bed in a pile; a kitchen chair being carried into the front room and set
  down in the middle of it.
- Emotion: Settling, softening.
- Lighting: One warm lamp, everything else falling away.

### [chorus 1 – full two-step]

```
After hours, that's when the night gets honest,
Nobody's posing and nobody's promised.
Kettle going and the bassline low,
Say the thing that you couldn't say downstairs, then let it go.
After hours, when the shutters come down,
Half a flat, half a city, one sound,
You can tell me anything, I'm not going home,
After hours, that's when the night gets honest.
```

- Scene: The flat filling up and finding its shape.
- Shots: Nine people in a front room built for four; a kettle rising to the
  boil in close-up; a bassline felt through a floorboard, filmed as a glass of
  water trembling on a radiator; Mahima half-dancing in her socks with a mug.
- Emotion: Euphoric but low-volume — this is a happiness you have to be
  close to see.
- Lighting: Lamp, kitchen strip through the doorway, streetlight through
  the blind.

### [post-chorus – chant]

```
Four in the morning, four in the morning,
Nobody's leaving without saying something.
Four in the morning, four in the morning,
That's when the night gets honest.
```

- Scene: Chant, four fast cuts.
- Shots: A clock on the cooker (composited); socks on a radiator; a hand
  passing a mug; the front door being locked from inside.
- Lighting: Same, cut faster.

### [verse 2 – what gets said]

```
Half four and the tunes have gone soft and gone slow,
And my mate on the floor says a thing I didn't know.
Says his dad's been ill since the spring and he kept it in,
Says he couldn't say it sober so he says it with a grin.
```

- Scene: The quiet middle of the party.
- Shots: The volume knob turned down two clicks; a young man sitting on the
  floor with his back against the sofa, mug held in both hands, talking to the
  carpet; Mahima's face listening, not interrupting; his grin arriving in the
  wrong place and everybody understanding it.
- Emotion: The turn from funny to real. Do not sentimentalise it — nobody
  hugs.
- Lighting: Lamp low, faces half-lit, warm.

```
The girl in the doorway is handing in her notice,
Typing it out on her phone and reading it to all of us.
I have known this lot for years and I met them all tonight,
Everything you swallow at the bar comes up in this light.
```

- Scene: The kitchen doorway.
- Shots: A young woman leaning in the doorframe reading off her phone
  (screen composited) with a room of people cheering her on; a thumb hovering
  over send; Mahima looking around the room at faces she thinks she knows.
- Emotion: Reckless honesty, celebrated.
- Lighting: Kitchen strip behind her, lamp in front.

### [pre-chorus 2]

```
Big light off and the balcony door,
Somebody's crying and laughing at four.
The coats are all piled and the tea's going cold,
And nobody's leaving till somebody's told.
```

- Scene: The balcony door open, cold air coming in.
- Shots: Condensation clearing off a pane; someone laughing while wiping
  their eyes; a row of half-drunk teas going cold on a windowsill.
- Emotion: Tender, unembarrassed.
- Lighting: Lamp inside, blue-grey outside for the first time.

### [chorus 2]

- Scene: The same room, later and closer.
- Shots: Tighter frames than chorus one; the same glass of water on the
  radiator, now still; a whole room singing at a volume that would not wake a
  neighbour.
- Emotion: Peak warmth.
- Lighting: Lamp plus the first grey at the window.

### [instrumental – drum edit, bassline, organ breakdown]

- Scene: The room in motion, no lyrics.
- Shots: A close-up two-step foot shuffle on lino; a hand riding a volume
  fader on a laptop; a fridge opened and closed, the light across a face; the
  bathroom mirror with three people crowded into it laughing; a slow move down
  the hallway past coats and shoes to the front-room doorway.
- Lighting: Warm inside, cold in the hallway, the contrast the whole point.

### [MC verse – stripped beat]

```
Two flights, one lift that has not worked since I moved in,
Coat on the radiator, kettle doing overtime, tune in.
I do the door, I do the drinks, I do the peace talks,
I clock who is going to break before he even walks.
No performance in a room this small and this warm,
You get honest when your back is on the wall by the door.
I have heard things in this kitchen that a priest would bin,
Grey light through the blind and I am the one who lets it in.
Four in the morning is a country of its own,
Passport is a mug of tea, and nobody goes home.
```

- Scene: The flat's owner — Kai — walking his own party.
- Shots: A lift door with a laminated out-of-order note (composited); him
  taking coats at the front door; him making four teas at once; him crouching
  beside the man on the floor and saying nothing; him raising a blind with two
  fingers and grey light hitting the carpet.
- Emotion: Quiet hosting. He is the least loud person in the video and the
  one everything routes through.
- Lighting: He is always half in the kitchen strip and half in the lamp.

### [bridge – no drums, balcony]

```
Out on the balcony the sky's gone grey and gold,
Bin lorry grinding and a fox crossing the road.
Somebody's asleep in a parka in the hall,
Somebody's playing the first tune of them all.
I will keep this hour longer than the club or the queue,
Nothing happened here, and it's the truest thing I knew.
```

- Scene: First light on a small concrete balcony.
- Shots: Mahima leaning on the rail with a mug, low estate rooftops and a
  grey-gold sky; a bin lorry working the street below; a fox crossing between
  parked cars; back inside, someone asleep sitting up in a parka in the
  hallway; the first tune of the night starting again on the speaker.
- Emotion: Awe, without saying so.
- Lighting: The only natural light so far. It should feel like relief.

### [final chorus – widest]

- Scene: Everybody up one last time in full daylight through the blind.
- Shots: The room with the blind fully open and the lamp still on and now
  pointless; dancing in daylight, which always looks slightly absurd and here
  looks great; the MC ad-libbing from the kitchen doorway; the glass of water
  trembling again.
- Emotion: Full release, no comedown.
- Lighting: Daylight winning, lamp still burning.

### [post-chorus 2]

- Scene: The chant in daylight.
- Shots: Socks; mugs stacked in the sink; the lamp finally switched off
  because it is redundant; the front door unlocked.
- Lighting: Daylight only.

### [outro – full daylight, the street]

```
Bus stop, daylight, jacket in my hand,
Baker's open and the shutter's going up again.
Somebody messages, "same time next week,"
And I'm smiling at my phone on an empty street.
After hours, that's when the night gets honest.
```

- Scene: The street outside, morning.
- Shots: Mahima at a bus stop with her jacket over one arm; a bakery light
  on and a tray going into a window; the same shop shutter from verse one
  rolling upward; a group chat notification (composited) and her smile at it;
  final shot, the empty street with the shutter fully up and the flat window
  two floors above it, curtains still closed. Locked-off, hold.
- Emotion: Content, unhurried, alone and fine with it.
- Lighting: Full flat morning daylight — the same honesty as the club's
  strip lights in the intro, but now it suits her.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the wall switch going from big light to lamp, cut on
  the first chorus hit.
- **The challenge:** post the moment your night stopped being a night out
  and started being a conversation — the four-in-the-morning chant under it.
- **The caption line:** *"Nothing happened here, and it's the truest thing I
  knew."*
- **The MC clip:** *"Four in the morning is a country of its own / Passport
  is a mug of tea, and nobody goes home."*
- **The visual gag with a heart in it:** dancing in full daylight with a
  lamp still on.
- **The bookend:** the shop shutter comes down in verse one and goes up in
  the outro; the strip lights that are ugly at the start are kind at the end.
