# Your Name in Cursive — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A girl who has been writing a boy's name everywhere except out loud. It starts in the back row on the first week of September, blue pen, little loops in the margin where the notes should be. It goes into the steam on a bathroom mirror, onto the back of a café receipt, into wet sand, onto the inside of her wrist. Everybody can see it except him — and then she finds his notebook open on a table, and her own name is looping down his page in a hand that isn't hers. So she stops writing and says it.

**Mood:**
Private, tender, building. A slow-burn confession, not a heartbreak. It ends happy, and the happiness is earned by six minutes of not saying anything.

**Message:**
"I wrote your name in cursive, now I'm saying it out loud."

**Genre:**
Cinematic pop with a soft trap underpinning. Think: Sasha Alex Sloan's plainness x Taylor Swift's diary specificity x the string writing on a Labrinth cue. Felt piano, real strings, trap hi-hats underneath.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 92 BPM, 4/4
- Key: F minor (private, contained)
- Final chorus lifts to A♭ major — the relative major — on the line where she asks him to say it back. The whole song is built so that lift is the moment she speaks.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, close and secretive in the verses, open-throated in the choruses, near-spoken in the bridge.
- Backing vocals: stacked female harmonies in the choruses, a wordless line answering the piano in the instrumental, a chanted stack on the post-chorus.

**Instrumentation:**
- Close-mic'd felt piano (main spine, one looping figure)
- Warm sub-bass and a light kick
- Soft trap hi-hats, doubling in the pre-choruses, absent in the instrumental
- String section: violins, viola, cello (chorus one on, melody in the instrumental, highest on the final chorus)
- Soft synth pads under the pre-choruses
- Claps on the post-chorus
- Ambient textures: a pen scratching on paper, a page turning, a distant school corridor, vinyl-like noise under the last piano figure

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: September, the back row, the first time she writes it
- Verse 1: everywhere she has written it since, all of them private, all of them erasable
- Pre-chorus: her friends telling her to just say it
- Chorus: the hook, wide and bright, the decision more than the act
- Verse 2: the library, being caught, then the fog, the frost, the ink on the wrist
- Bridge: his notebook, her name in his handwriting, the pen put down
- Final chorus: modulated, and one line changed to ask him to say it back
- Post-chorus: chanted, no more margins
- Outro: the September classroom again, but the writing is on her face now

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – felt piano and a pen on paper, almost spoken]

```
First week of September, back row, blue pen,
I wrote it once and then I wrote it again.
Little loops in the margin where the notes should be,
A secret in my handwriting that only I could read.
```

- Scene: A classroom in early September, late-afternoon sun, the back row.
- Shot: Extreme close-up of a blue ballpoint moving in the margin of a lined page — the loops are real cursive but always shot too close, too soft or too oblique to be read.
- Cut to: Her face, chin on her hand, eyes on something off frame; then back to the page, where the loops have multiplied.
- Her reaction: A small private smile she puts away before anyone sees it.
- Lighting: Warm dusty afternoon through a high window, the rest of the room flat.

### [verse 1]

```
I wrote it in the steam on the bathroom glass,
Watched it run before the mirror went clear.
I wrote it on the back of a café receipt,
Folded it in my coat pocket like it was worth something here.
```

- Scene: Three private places, three erasures.
- Shots: A steamed bathroom mirror, a fingertip drawing, the letters immediately beginning to run and drip. A café receipt turned over on a table, a pen, then the receipt folded into quarters and pushed into a coat pocket.
- Emotion: The pleasure of a secret, and the small grief of every version disappearing.
- Lighting: Bathroom warm and fogged; café window light, cool and grey.

```
I dragged it in the sand with the side of my shoe,
Let the tide come and take it, cause the tide doesn't tell.
Every notebook I own has a page that's just you,
I've been spelling out the thing that I can't say too well.
```

- Scene: A flat beach at low tide; then her room at night.
- Shots: The side of a trainer dragging a long looping line in wet sand; a wide of the whole word from behind her, out of focus; the tide taking it. Cut to a stack of notebooks on her bed, each one falling open to a page that is nothing but the same loops.
- Emotion: Compulsion, half-embarrassed.
- Lighting: Flat silver beach light; her room lit by one desk lamp.

### [pre-chorus]

```
And my friends all say, "Just tell him, it's not that deep,"
But it's the deepest thing I know how to keep.
I've got a thousand little versions in a thousand little lines,
And not one of them has ever left this mouth of mine.
```

- Scene: A booth in a café or a bedroom floor, two friends talking over her.
- Shot: Her friends animated, gesturing, laughing; her half-smiling, not answering, turning a pen over and over in her fingers.
- Emotion: Affection and stubbornness. She is not going to say it.
- Lighting: Warm, ordinary, a little cluttered.

### [chorus – wide, bright, strings in]

```
I wrote your name in cursive,
Now I'm saying it out loud.
Every loop, every letter,
I'm not hiding it now.
I wrote it small, I wrote it soft,
I wrote it where nobody looks,
But you're not a secret anymore,
You're the title of my book.
I wrote your name in cursive,
Now I'm saying it out loud.
```

- Scene: The handwriting motif released into the world, at scale. This is the visual hook.
- Shots: The loops appearing in condensation, in flour on a counter, in chalk dust, in the pattern of streetlights sliding over a car ceiling — never legible, always the same rhythm of loops. Her walking through a city with the light rising on her face. A wide of her alone on a rooftop at golden hour, arms loose, head back.
- Emotion: Release. She has decided, even if she hasn't done it yet.
- Lighting: The brightest and widest the video gets so far; golden hour.

### [verse 2]

```
You caught me in the library, my hand across the page,
Asked me what I was drawing, I said, "Nothing, just a shape."
You laughed and said, "You're a terrible liar,"
Then your ears went red and you looked for an escape.
```

- Scene: A university library, long tables, green lamps.
- Shots: Her hand flattening over her page as he sits down opposite. Over-the-shoulder: his face, amused, close. Her covering the page harder. Then his ears going red, his eyes going back to his book too fast.
- Emotion: The first crack — he is not indifferent, and she doesn't notice.
- Lighting: Green desk lamps, deep shadow between the stacks.

```
Now it's fog on the bus window, one finger, one name,
In the frost on the car in the cold morning light,
On the inside of my wrist in a blue that stays the same,
I've been carrying you round like a word I can't say right.
```

- Scene: Winter. The secret has stopped being seasonal and started being constant.
- Shots: A finger drawing a loop on a fogged bus window, the city sliding past behind it. Frost on a windscreen at dawn, a fingertip, breath visible. Her wrist under a coat cuff, blue ink on the skin, half rubbed away and redrawn.
- Emotion: It's no longer a game. It's a weight.
- Lighting: Grey-blue winter light throughout; the wrist shot is the only warm frame.

### [pre-chorus]

```
And my friends all say, "Girl, he can see it on your face,"
But my face has never been the honest place.
It's my hands that give me up, it's my hands that know,
And my hands have been saying it for months now, so.
```

- Scene: Same friends, same booth, months later, coats on.
- Shot: Close-up on her hands the whole time — turning a cup, tapping the table, drawing on a napkin without looking at it while she talks about something else entirely.
- Emotion: She knows. She still isn't going to say it.
- Lighting: Warm interior, colder outside the window than the first pre-chorus.

### [chorus]

```
I wrote your name in cursive,
Now I'm saying it out loud.
Every loop, every letter,
I'm not hiding it now.
I wrote it small, I wrote it soft,
I wrote it where nobody looks,
But you're not a secret anymore,
You're the title of my book.
I wrote your name in cursive,
Now I'm saying it out loud.
```

- Scene: The chorus world again, bigger and more public: the same loops, now in places other people can see.
- Shots: Condensation on a bus shelter, a fogged shop window, the misted glass of a fridge door in a corner shop, a finger through dust on a car boot. Her walking faster, more openly.
- Emotion: More urgent than chorus one.
- Lighting: Evening, streetlights, saturated.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: No lyrics. The hats drop out. Slow and close.
- Shots: Her at her desk at night, the lamp on, writing and stopping. A slow drift over the open notebooks. Rain on a window with old loops still faintly on the glass. Her hand hovering over her phone, a message typed and not sent (composited). A table by a door, a coat over a chair, a notebook lying open on it — held two seconds longer than expected, the cut into the bridge.
- Lighting: One desk lamp, the rest of the room dark; the last shot lit only from a hallway.

### [bridge – bare, piano and voice, then building]

```
You left your notebook open on the table by the door,
I wasn't gonna look, but I looked, and there was more.
Little loops in the margin in a hand that wasn't mine,
Mine, in cursive, over and over, every page, every line.
So I'm done with the pen, I'm done with the glass,
I'm done writing down a thing that I could just ask.
You're standing right here and the room's gone still,
And if I don't do it now, then I never will.
```

- Scene: A hallway, a table by a door, his notebook open.
- Shots: Her seeing it from across the room. The page from her POV — the same rhythm of loops as her own margins, but in a different hand, never legible. Her turning one page, then another, then another, faster. Her hand going to her mouth. Then a pen being set down on the table, deliberately, and left there.
- Emotion: The turn. It was mutual the whole time, and both of them were writing instead of speaking.
- Lighting: Hallway light only, then warming as she turns around and he is standing there.

### [final chorus – modulated to A♭, fullest, one line changed]

```
I wrote your name in cursive,
Now I'm saying it out loud.
Every loop, every letter,
Say it back to me now.
I wrote it small, I wrote it soft,
I wrote it where nobody looks,
But you're not a secret anymore,
You're the title of my book.
I wrote your name in cursive,
Now I'm saying it out loud.
```

- Scene: The two of them in the hallway, then outside, then the whole world lighting up.
- Shots: Her mouth moving on the line — and the audio is the vocal, so the video does not need to fake it. His face. The two notebooks on the table together. Then wide: the street, the light, both of them walking.
- Emotion: Said. Out loud. Answered.
- Lighting: The modulation is matched by the grade — the whole frame goes a stop brighter on "say it back to me now."

### [post-chorus – chanted, claps]

```
Out loud, out loud,
No more margins, no more doubt.
Out loud, out loud,
Your name, out loud.
```

- Scene: Fast cuts of every writing surface from the whole video, in order, being wiped, washed, cleared or closed.
- Shots: The mirror wiped clear. The receipt unfolded and dropped. The tide over the sand. The notebook closed. The wrist under running water.
- Emotion: Delight. Nothing needs to be written down any more.
- Lighting: Bright, clean, high key.

### [outro – the September classroom again, one voice]

```
First week of September, back row, blue pen,
I wrote it once and then I wrote it again.
Now it's written on my face where the whole world can see,
And you're writing mine in cursive right next to me.
```

- Scene: A year later. The same room, the same back row, the same light.
- Shots: The same framing as shot one: a page, a blue pen, loops in a margin — then the frame widens for the first time to show two pages, two hands, two pens, side by side at the same desk.
- Emotion: Peace. The secret is a shared habit now.
- Lighting: The warm dusty September afternoon from the intro, unchanged. Hold and fade.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** a fingertip drawing loops on a fogged bus window with *"I wrote your name in cursive, now I'm saying it out loud"* on screen — endlessly reproducible with any phone.
- **The challenge:** write the name in steam, in sand, in frost, on a receipt, then wipe it — one clip per surface, cut to the post-chorus chant.
- **The caption line:** *"But my face has never been the honest place."*
- **The turn:** the notebook page in his handwriting — the shot that makes the comment section arrive.
- **The quote:** *"I've been carrying you round like a word I can't say right."*
- **The last frame:** two pens, two pages, one desk.
