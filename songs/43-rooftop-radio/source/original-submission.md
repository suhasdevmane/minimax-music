# Rooftop Radio — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A summer block party that nobody organised. Six friends carry one old transistor radio up to the roof of a walk-up building, and by midnight half the street is up there with them. Nothing is owned, nothing is booked, nothing is permitted — and it is the best night of the year.

**Mood:**
Euphoric, warm, communal. Sweat, tar, ice and strings. Joy without irony: this is a celebration record, not a party joke.

**Message:**
"We don't own the roof, the building, or the song. Tonight we own the sound."

**Genre:**
Disco-pop with a live string section. Think: Dua Lipa x Nile Rodgers x Jungle — clean funk guitar, octave bass, brass stabs, and strings doing the second hook.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 120 BPM, 4/4
- Key: B♭ major (bright, brass-friendly, sits well in her belt range)
- Final chorus modulates up a whole step to C major for the last lift.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, conversational and grinning in the verses, full-throated and open in the choruses.
- Backing vocals: stacked female harmonies on every chorus; crowd-style group vocals, half-shouted, on the post-chorus chant and the final chorus.

**Instrumentation:**
- Clean muted funk guitar (sixteenth-note chords, the rhythmic identity)
- Melodic disco bass guitar, octave jumps in the choruses
- Live string section: violins and violas high, cellos doubling the bass
- Brass stabs (trumpets and trombones, tight and short), electric piano, warm Rhodes pad
- Four-on-the-floor kick, bright snare, open hats, tambourine, congas, cowbell, handclaps, group vocals
- Textures: transistor radio tuning static, band noise, street ambience, a rooftop crowd bed

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: a radio tuned in a stairwell, then a hatch opening onto sky
- Verses: the setup, then the block arriving — cooler, chairs, lights, neighbours
- Pre-chorus: the antenna, the held breath, the countdown
- Chorus and post-chorus: the drop, the whole roof moving, the chant
- Bridge: nobody up here owns anything, and it doesn't matter
- Final chorus and outro: modulated, every window open, then dawn and dead batteries

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro — radio tuning, filtered strings, no drums]

```
Six flights up with a radio under my arm,
Tar still soft from the heat of the day.
Somebody propped the hatch with an empty paint can,
And the whole sky opened up our way.
Nobody called it, nobody planned it,
It just started playing and we stayed.
```

- Scene: A hot stairwell in a six-storey walk-up, late afternoon light through a wired-glass window.
- Shot: Close on a battered silver transistor radio held against her hip as she climbs; the dial glowing faintly.
- Her reaction / Emotion: Not excitement yet — relief. The heat, the climb, then air.
- Lighting: Stairwell dim and green-tinged; roof is warm gold, sun low, long shadows across soft tar.

### [verse 1 — funk guitar, bass, tambourine]

```
Landlord's gone to Miami till the end of July,
Nobody up here's asking who's allowed tonight.
A fresh pack of batteries, a dial that sticks,
One old silver radio and a hundred feet of bricks.
```

- Scene: The roof being turned into a room.
- Shot: A hand tearing open a battery pack; two batteries thumbed into the back of the radio; the dial rocked with a thumbnail.
- Emotion: Conspiratorial. Nobody is stopping this.
- Lighting: Golden hour, hard shadows, warm skin.

```
Girl from the fourth floor hauled a cooler up the stairs,
Somebody's cousin showed up with a stack of folding chairs.
We swept the broken glass and strung a line of lights,
And the water tank's a stage if you can climb it right.
```

- Scene: The build-out montage.
- Shot: A cooler dragged one step at a time, ice sloshing; folding chairs opened one after another on the beat; a broom pushing glass into a dustpan.
- Emotion: Everybody working, everybody laughing.
- Lighting: Sun dropping; the string lights come on and instantly matter.

### [pre-chorus — claps, rising strings, countdown]

```
Antenna wrapped in foil and pointed at the moon,
Hold it still, hold it still, there it is, that's the tune.
Everybody freeze, then everybody move,
On three, on two,
```

- Scene: The antenna problem, played as suspense.
- Shot: Extreme close-up: kitchen foil wound around a bent telescopic antenna, fingers adjusting it a millimetre at a time.
- Emotion: Comic tension, then release.
- Lighting: Blue hour; faces lit by string lights only.

### [chorus — drop, four-on-the-floor, brass, strings]

```
Rooftop radio, turn it up, let the whole block know,
We got no permit and we got nowhere else to go.
Antenna in the air like a hand in the air,
And the city's looking up at us like we put it there.
Rooftop radio, turn it up, let the whole block know,
Everybody dancing on a roof that isn't ours,
Half the song is static and we sing the rest out loud,
Six flights over Sunday, and tonight we are the sound.
```

- Scene: The roof going off.
- Shot: A tracking circle around the group as they break into dancing, the radio on an upturned crate in the middle like a fire.
- Emotion: Full release. Grinning, not posing.
- Lighting: String lights, a work lamp, the city's own glow. Warm against blue.

### [post-chorus — chant, claps, brass]

```
Turn it up, turn it up, let the whole block know,
Turn it up, turn it up, till the batteries go.
Hands on the water tank and feet down on the tar,
Turn it up, turn it up, that's how loud we are.
```

- Scene: Four hard cuts, one per line.
- Shot: A hand cranking the volume wheel; feet stamping on soft tar; palms slapping the wooden tank; the whole group jumping on the last beat.
- Emotion: Chant energy — this is the clip that gets duetted.
- Lighting: Hard flash of a phone torch across faces, then back to warm.

### [verse 2 — congas, second guitar, the block arrives]

```
The lady on the third floor leaned out to complain,
Then she caught the chorus and she asked us for a name.
Now she's up here with a plate and a folding paper fan,
Saying she danced to this one back before the block began.
```

- Scene: The complaint that becomes an invitation.
- Shot: A third-floor window sliding up; an older woman leaning out, unimpressed; the music reaching her.
- Emotion: The night's warmest turn. Everyone watches her.
- Lighting: String lights on grey hair; the best-lit face in the video.

```
The guy from the corner store brought ice up in a crate,
Two kids from the stairwell swore they'd only stay till eight.
At midnight the dial slipped and found a song from way back when,
And nobody knew how, but every one of us came in.
```

- Scene: The party doubling in size.
- Shot: A crate of bagged ice hoisted through the hatch; two kids sitting on the parapet eating ice pops, not leaving.
- Emotion: The goosebumps moment of the song.
- Lighting: Midnight blue, warm pools under the lights.

### [pre-chorus 2 — string glissando, siren]

```
A squad car turned the corner, slowed down and drove away,
Somebody's grandmother is teaching me to sway.
Everybody freeze, then everybody move,
On three, on two,
```

- Scene: The only threat in the video, and it dissolves.
- Shot: Straight down from the parapet: a patrol car rolling along the street, light bar dark; it pauses, it drives on. Then the roof frozen mid-dance, then laughing.
- Emotion: Relief, then tenderness.
- Lighting: A slow red-blue wash crossing the wall behind them, then gone.

### [chorus 2 — string counter-melody, gospel-tinged stack]

```
(the chorus again, bigger)
```

- Scene: The full roof, forty people now.
- Shot: Handheld from inside the crowd, spinning, faces flashing past; then windows opening down the street.
- Emotion: Communal, huge.
- Lighting: Warm and dense, phone torches held up like a small field of stars.

### [instrumental — bass and percussion breakdown, radio filter]

- Scene: The party from a distance, then from inside the speaker.
- Shots: A slow drone rise off the roof, the lit rectangle shrinking in a dark grid. The bass player of the block — a kid with a portable speaker — nodding on the parapet. A cowbell being hit with a spoon. The camera pushing all the way into the radio's mesh grille as the mix filters down to mono and thin, then blowing back out to full stereo as the roof reappears.
- Lighting: Blue and warm; the radio grille shot is a hard practical close-up.

### [bridge — Rhodes, brushed hats, honest]

```
None of us own a window with a view,
We split a rent that we can barely do.
But nobody lives above the sixth floor sky,
And nobody's charging extra for the night.
So play it for the ones who worked all week,
Play it for the ones who couldn't sleep,
Play it till the tar goes cool and the east turns gold,
Play it till the batteries go cold.
```

- Scene: The quiet middle of a loud night.
- Shot: Her sitting on the parapet with her back to the city, feet on the roof, a cup in both hands, catching her breath.
- Emotion: Gratitude. This is the line people screenshot.
- Lighting: The string lights only; everything else dark and soft.

### [final chorus — modulated, whole street]

```
(the chorus, up a whole step, with "Every window on the street is open now")
```

- Scene: The biggest shot in the video.
- Shot: A crane or drone move that starts tight on her on the water tank and pulls back and up until four other rooftops have people on them too, all facing this one.
- Emotion: Peak. Nobody up here is watching this through a phone.
- Lighting: Brightest of the video; warm gold against deep blue.

### [post-chorus 2 — chant]

```
(the chant again, everyone)
```

- Scene: Everyone on the beat.
- Shot: Four hard cuts again, but wide: forty feet, forty hands on the tank, one jump, one freeze.
- Lighting: Phone torches, gold, controlled chaos.

### [outro — mono radio, one string note, dawn]

```
Sun coming up behind the tank and the wires,
Somebody's asleep in a folding chair.
The radio is still going on the last of the batteries,
And the whole block knows, and the whole block knows.
```

- Scene: Morning on the roof.
- Shot: Grey-gold first light behind the water tank and the overhead wires; the string lights still on and now pointless.
- Final shot: The radio alone on the crate, dial glowing, volume low, static rising as the batteries die. Locked-off. Hold.
- Emotion: Contentment, a night that will be quoted for a year.
- Lighting: Clean cool dawn, no colour grade tricks.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** her on the water tank, arms out, the skyline behind, on *"let the whole block know."*
- **The challenge:** the pre-chorus freeze — everyone stands dead still around a speaker, then explodes on *"on three, on two."* Made for duets and group clips.
- **The heart moment:** the third-floor neighbour teaching the step, on *"saying she danced to this one back before the block began."*
- **The caption line:** *"We got no permit and we got nowhere else to go."*
- **The last frame:** a radio alone on a crate at dawn, dying batteries, still playing.
