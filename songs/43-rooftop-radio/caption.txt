Global Metadata

The pre-chorus tightens to claps, a rising string figure and a held breath on the countdown, then the chorus lands with the full four-on-the-floor kick, octave bass, brass stabs and a wide stacked hook, joyful and communal rather than triumphant. The second verse brings the neighbours in, adding congas, a shaker and a second guitar as the party grows, and the second pre-chorus is bigger with a siren-like string glissando where the squad car passes. The second chorus adds a soaring string counter-melody and a gospel-tinged backing stack. The instrumental break is a full disco breakdown: a bass guitar solo over the kick, a string riff answering it, a percussion section with cowbell and tambourine, and a filtered snippet of the hook pushed through a radio-band filter before a riser lifts everything back. The bridge drops to soft Rhodes, brushed hats and a single sustained string pad, tender and grateful, then rebuilds line by line into the final chorus, which modulates up a whole step with the biggest arrangement of the track, live drums, brass, full strings and layered crowd vocals. The last post-chorus is pure chant, and the outro thins back down to the small radio speaker and a single sustained string note at dawn.
