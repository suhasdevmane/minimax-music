# Never Yours to Break — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A man has been telling people he broke her. She answers, once, calmly, and the answer is a property argument: he was never the owner of the thing he claims to have destroyed. The house of glass on the hill is the whole video — the town waits all night for the sound of it coming down and the sound never arrives.

**Mood:**
Still, cold, enormous. Composure rather than fury. The power comes from how little she moves; the arrangement does the shouting she refuses to do.

**Message:**
"You were weather. Weather doesn't own the house."

**Genre:**
Cinematic pop with orchestral scale and a modern low end. Think: Lana Del Rey x Hozier x RAYE — felt piano and a full string section over an 808 that hits like a door closing.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 92 BPM, 4/4
- Key: E minor, the final chorus modulating up to G major
- The lift is the modulation plus the full choir and string section; the vocal never shouts, it holds.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, almost flat and unhurried in the intro, conversational and dry in the verses, held and open in the choruses.
- Backing vocals: stacked female harmonies in the choruses, a choir pad widening from chorus one, a low male-register vocal pad for weight under chorus two, the widest stack on the modulated final chorus.

**Instrumentation:**
- Felt piano (the spine, a single low note in the first bar and the last)
- Deep 808 and sub-bass from the first pre-chorus
- Full string section, cellos low and violins high
- Choir pad
- Timpani-style hits
- Soft trap hats under the pre-choruses, live-feel kick and snare in the choruses
- Ambience: distant thunder, rain on glass, one struck glass tone, birdsong at the end

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: the glass house, the storm arriving, the town waiting for the crash
- Verse 1: what he took, and the version of events he is telling
- Pre-chorus: the invitation — come and look at the wreckage yourself
- Chorus: the hook, wide and held, the property argument
- Verse 2: months later, a new version of his story, and what he actually took
- Bridge: the honest admission, then the refusal
- Final chorus: modulation to G, full choir and strings, sunrise
- Outro: morning gold across the floor, the town tired of waiting

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – single low piano note, thunder, rain on glass]

```
A house of glass at the edge of the hill,
And the town waited to hear it come down.
Thunder came in like it had something to prove,
Wind on the panes, rattling the ground.
I stood in the middle, hands at my sides,
And let the sky throw all that it had.
```

- Scene: A single-storey glass house alone on a bare hill at night, a storm rolling in.
- Shots: An extreme wide of the house from the valley, lit from inside, the sky black above it. A row of lit windows in the town below, curtains open, people watching. Rain hitting full-height glass in sheets.
- Cut to: Inside. She is standing dead centre in an empty room, hands loose at her sides, not flinching.
- Emotion: Absolute stillness. She is not braced; she is simply not moving.
- Lighting: Interior warm and low against a blue-black exterior. Lightning fills the room for single frames.

### [verse 1 – felt piano, sub-bass swell]

```
You came in like a season, you left like a bill,
Took the mirror off the wall, the salt off the sill.
Told your friends a version where I came apart,
Where you did the holding and I did the harm.
```

- Scene: The house by day, after him.
- Shots: A pale rectangle on a wall where a mirror used to hang. An empty ring in the dust on a windowsill where a bowl of salt stood. A door left half open onto an empty hallway.
- Cut to: A pub or a kitchen elsewhere in town, faceless, a man's hands gesturing while three people lean in — his version being told.
- Emotion: Detachment. She is describing, not pleading.
- Lighting: Flat overcast daylight through glass, no warmth.

```
I was never once the thing that you dropped,
I was the floor you stood on while you talked.
Every crack you point at, I put there myself,
Long before I heard your name from someone else.
```

- Scene: The floor of the glass house, and an older repair.
- Shots: Low angle along the floorboards toward her feet. A close-up of a hairline crack in one pane, filled and set long ago, the repair clearly older than the storm. Her thumb running along it once.
- Emotion: A flicker of ownership — this damage is hers, it predates him, and she is not ashamed of it.
- Lighting: Grey daylight, one warm interior lamp on for no reason.

### [pre-chorus]

```
You tell it like a rescue that went wrong,
Like the roof caved in the second you left.
Come and see it then, come stand in my doorway,
There's nothing on this floor to sweep up yet.
```

- Scene: An invitation, unanswered.
- Shots: The front door standing wide open onto the storm, nobody coming through it. A slow track down a corridor toward the open doorway. A wide of a completely clean, empty floor.
- Emotion: Dare rather than plea.
- Lighting: Storm light from outside, warm from behind the camera.

### [chorus – full cinematic width, held not shouted]

```
You call it a wreckage, I call it a home,
Windows intact and the lights still on.
Say that you broke me if it helps you sleep,
You never held a key, never held the deed.
I was standing here before you learned my name,
Still in the storm, and I'm not the one who changed.
Say it loud, say it twice, whatever it takes,
My heart was never yours to break.
```

- Scene: The house in full storm, every light on, holding.
- Shots: Extreme wide from the valley: the glass house blazing with light in the middle of the worst of it. Her walking the length of the house past every window, unhurried, turning lights on as she goes. A pane taking a branch strike and not breaking, in slow motion.
- Emotion: Composure at maximum scale.
- Lighting: Blazing interior gold against a black exterior. This is the widest contrast in the video.

### [verse 2 – piano and 808, strings low]

```
Heard you tell it a different way this winter,
That the girl in your version got quieter each year.
Funny, I've been loud in every room I walk in,
And none of them has watched me disappear.
```

- Scene: Winter, months later, elsewhere.
- Shots: A cold bright street, her walking into a room full of people and the room turning toward her. Her mid-laugh at a long table, entirely present, entirely visible.
- Cut to: A faceless figure telling the story again to a smaller group, fewer people leaning in this time.
- Emotion: Amusement. Not bitterness.
- Lighting: Hard winter daylight, then warm crowded interiors.

```
You took a jacket and a playlist and a Sunday,
And a couple of the words I don't say the same.
You never took the roof, never touched the wiring,
Never had a hand on the frame.
```

- Scene: An inventory, shot literally.
- Shots: An empty hanger swinging on a rail. A song being skipped on a car stereo without a flicker in her face. An unmade Sunday bed with the curtains open. Then, in hard contrast, the structural shots: the roof beam, a junction box, the steel frame of the glass house — untouched, solid, hers.
- Emotion: Precision. She has counted, and the count is small.
- Lighting: Warm domestic for the losses; cool architectural for the structure.

### [pre-chorus 2]

```
You tell it like a rescue that went wrong,
Like the ceiling came down when you were gone.
Come and see it then, come knock on the glass,
Same house, still standing, the storm already passed.
```

- Scene: The invitation repeated, on the other side of the weather.
- Shots: A knuckle rapping once on a pane from outside, no one visible behind it. The ceiling, intact, seen from directly below. The storm clouds pulling away over the hill in time-lapse feel.
- Emotion: The dare, now with proof behind it.
- Lighting: Storm breaking, first cold light on the horizon.

### [chorus]

(repeat)

- Scene: The chorus world, later in the same night, more powerful.
- Shots: The house from directly above, a lit rectangle on a dark hill. Her standing at the largest pane with the storm on the other side of the glass, inches away, unmoved. A long lens from the town: every window lit.
- Emotion: Settled.
- Lighting: More saturated, more contrast, a hint of dawn at the frame edge.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: The storm at its worst and the house not caring, no lyrics.
- Shots: Rain running down full-height glass in macro, the valley distorted behind it. Lightning lighting the entire interior white for a single frame, her silhouette dead centre. A tree going over on the hill outside. Her sitting down on the floor in the middle of the empty room, calm, while the storm peaks. One bar of near-silence: the room, still, water running off the roof.
- Lighting: Blue-black and white strobe, then one warm lamp.

### [bridge – half-time, piano and voice]

```
I won't pretend it didn't hurt, because it did,
Months I couldn't hear your name out loud.
Lost the sleep, lost the weight, lost a friend or two,
Sat outside a house I don't drive to now.
But hurting isn't ruin, a bruise is not a wound,
And nothing you carried out of here was mine.
You were weather. I have lived through harder weather.
The lights are on in every window down the line.
```

- Scene: The honest part, in a car and in the dark.
- Shots: Her sitting on the floor with her back against the glass, knees up, one lamp. A flashback, cold and handheld: her parked at night outside an ordinary suburban house, engine running, not getting out. A close-up of her face there, wrecked. Hard cut back to the present, composed.
- Emotion: The song's only admission, given without any hedging, then withdrawn from him.
- Lighting: Flashback sodium orange and harsh; present single warm lamp.

### [final chorus – modulation to G, choir and full strings]

```
You call it a wreckage, I call it a home,
Every light in every window, and I turned them on.
Say that you broke me if it helps you sleep,
You never held a key, never held the deed.
I was standing here before you learned my name,
Still in the sunrise, and I'm not the one who changed.
Say it loud, say it twice, whatever it takes,
My heart was never yours to break.
```

- Scene: Sunrise, the storm gone, the house standing.
- Shots: A drone rise from the valley to the house as the sun comes over the hill. Her walking through the house switching lamps off one at a time because she does not need them any more. A wide of the hill in gold with one tree down and the house untouched.
- Emotion: Triumph without volume.
- Lighting: The first warm exterior light in the whole video.

### [post-chorus – stripped beat, chant]

```
Never yours, never yours,
Not the windows, not the walls, not the doors.
Never yours, never yours to take,
Never yours, and it never was yours to break.
```

- Scene: A hard rhythmic list.
- Shots: Cut on the beat — a window, a wall, a door handle, a floorboard, the frame, the roofline. Each one held for exactly one beat, each one hers.
- Emotion: Finality.
- Lighting: Clean morning, flat and bright.

### [outro – single piano note, sunlight]

```
A house of glass at the edge of the hill,
And the town got tired of waiting for the fall.
Morning came in gold on the floor of the hall,
And it never was your house at all.
```

- Scene: Morning. Nothing has happened, which is the point.
- Shots: The town below, curtains closing, people going back to bed. Gold light moving across the floorboards of the hall in real time. Final shot: the exact opening extreme wide of the house on the hill, now in sunlight, one tree down beside it, everything else exactly as it was.
- Emotion: Quiet, absolute.
- Lighting: Warm low morning sun, long shadows.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the pane taking a branch strike and not breaking, in slow motion, on *"my heart was never yours to break."*
- **The knife line:** *"You never held a key, never held the deed."*
- **The caption line:** *"You were weather. I have lived through harder weather."*
- **The challenge:** the standing-still edit — one locked-off shot, one person, the storm doing everything and the person doing nothing, cut to the first chorus.
- **The quote:** *"Every crack you point at, I put there myself."*
