# Soft Girl, Steel Spine — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
Softness read as weakness, and the correction. A woman who wears pastel and cries in the car and feels every word in the room — and who is also the hardest person in the building. She does ballet in the morning and boxing after dark, and neither is a costume. The song refuses the choice: she is not soft *despite* being strong, and not strong *despite* being soft. She is both, at the same time, and the two things are the same muscle.

**Mood:**
Calm, certain, unbothered. Not angry — she has nothing to prove, she is simply correcting the record. Pastel and concrete. Confidence without a single insult in it.

**Message:**
"Soft girl, steel spine — don't confuse the two."

**Genre:**
Trap-pop with a melodic-rap second verse. Think: SZA's composure x Doja Cat's pocket x Ariana Grande's top-line sweetness over 808s and felt piano. The rap verse is dry and unhurried, not aggressive.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 94 BPM, 4/4
- Key: F♯ minor (dark under a bright top-line, which is the whole point)
- No modulation; the final chorus lifts through layered harmonies, brass-like stabs and a raised top-line.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, close and half-whispered in the intro, conversational and quietly amused in verse 1, rhythmic and pocketed in the melodic-rap second verse, belted and proud in the choruses, near-spoken in the bridge.
- Backing vocals: stacked harmonies on every chorus; layered male and female harmonies on the final hook; a clipped chant on the post-chorus with throw delays on the words *soft* and *steel*.

**Instrumentation:**
- Felt piano (the 6 a.m. motif; the spine of the bridge and outro)
- Deep 808 with long glides; sub-bass drone
- Rolling trap hi-hats, snappy clap, tight hats under the rap verse
- Bright synth pluck counter-melody (choruses)
- Brass-like synth stabs (second and final choruses)
- Rising string pad (instrumental break, final chorus)
- Pitched vocal chop of the hook (instrumental, post-chorus)
- Textures: hand wraps being pulled tight, a coffee cup set down, a car door closing

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: 6 a.m., pink nails and black coffee, wrapping her hands
- Verse 1: everything people misread; her mother and her coach, and doing both
- Pre-chorus: go ahead, underestimate
- Chorus: the correction, big and singable
- Verse 2 (melodic rap): the two disciplines, the bathroom stall, the deal closed with a shaking hand
- Instrumental: half-time switch, then a bare piano note
- Bridge: spoken to every girl who cried in the stairwell and then walked in and nailed it
- Final chorus: fullest arrangement, "say it louder now"
- Outro: the same 6 a.m., both things still true

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – felt piano, sub drone, half-whispered]

```
Pink nails, black coffee, 6 a.m.,
Wrapped my hands before I said amen.
Cried in the car, fixed my face in the glass,
Then I walked in like the whole room asked.
```

- Scene: A dark kitchen before dawn. Pastel manicure against a black mug.
- Shot: Macro on pink nails around a black coffee cup; the cup set down on concrete worktop.
- Cut to: Pink hand wraps being pulled tight around a wrist, one loop at a time, in near darkness.
- Her reaction: A long breath, eyes closed, then open.
- Lighting: One window's worth of blue pre-dawn, one warm bulb. Pastel against dark.

### [verse 1]

```
They see the lashes and the pastel set,
Think I'm a pushover, easy bet.
Called me sensitive like it's a flaw,
Baby, sensitive is how I saw it all.
```

- Scene: A glass-walled office corridor, mid-morning.
- Shot: Her walking past a meeting room; through the glass, two faceless colleagues glance and go back to talking.
- Cut to: Macro on lashes, a pastel-pink blazer sleeve, a soft manicure resting on a hard steel handrail.
- Emotion: She hears it and keeps walking. No reaction shot — that's the point.
- Lighting: Cool daylight through glass, one pastel object in every frame.

```
I feel everything, that's the gift,
I know the room before the mood shifts.
I'll hold your hand and I'll hold my ground,
Softest voice in here, but I don't back down.
```

- Scene: A meeting already in progress. She is reading it, not speaking.
- Shot: Slow pan across the table on her eyeline — a jaw tightening, a pen stopping, someone leaning back.
- Cut to: Her hand under the table taking a colleague's hand for one second; then the same hand flat on the table as she starts to talk.
- Emotion: Reading the room as a skill, filmed as a skill.
- Lighting: Flat overhead office light; she is the only warm thing in the frame.

```
Mom said, "keep your heart wide open,"
Coach said, "keep your guard up," so I did both of them.
Lavender candle next to a heavy bag,
Both of them mine and neither one's an act.
```

- Scene: Two flashbacks, cut against each other on the beat.
- Shots: A kitchen table, an older woman's hands over hers. A gym, a coach lifting her elbows into a guard.
- Cut to: A wide of her home gym: a lavender candle burning on a concrete ledge, a heavy bag hanging two feet away.
- Emotion: No contradiction. The shot holds long enough for the audience to stop finding it strange.
- Lighting: Flashbacks warm and grainy; the home gym lit by the candle and one cold overhead.

### [pre-chorus]

```
So go ahead, underestimate,
Watch me smile while I take the plate.
Yeah, I cry, and then I win,
Silk on the outside, steel within.
```

- Scene: A boardroom door, from the outside.
- Shot: Her hand on the handle, a beat, then the push.
- Cut to: The room turning to look; a small polite smile; her setting a folder down on the table.
- Emotion: Total composure.
- Lighting: Cold room, warm hallway behind her, so she arrives with light on her.

### [chorus – big, proud, singable]

```
Soft girl, steel spine, don't confuse the two,
I can break down, then break through.
Petal on the outside, iron in the root,
Kindness ain't a weakness, kindness is the proof.
Soft girl, steel spine, say it with me now,
I bend, I bloom, I never bow.
You thought the tears meant I was through,
Soft girl, steel spine, don't confuse the two.
```

- Scene: The two worlds cut against each other at full speed.
- Shots: A ballet studio at dawn, pastel light on a wooden floor, her at the barre. A boxing gym, concrete walls, her hitting the bag. A pointe shoe ribbon tied. A hand wrap pulled tight. A slow développé. A straight right.
- Cut to: The two shots matched on shape — the line of a leg extended, the line of an arm extended.
- Emotion: Pride, not defiance.
- Lighting: Studio pastel and gold; gym concrete and cold blue. The cut between them is the video's whole grammar.

### [verse 2 – melodic rap, dry and pocketed]

```
Studio at six, bag by the door, gloves in the pocket,
Ballet for the posture, boxing for the way I drop it.
Pointe shoes pink, hand wraps pink, that was not an accident,
Soft is the uniform, the discipline's the evidence.
```

- Scene: One duffel bag, unpacked on a bench.
- Shot: Macro, in order: pointe shoes, hand wraps, a mouthguard case, a folded pastel blazer.
- Cut to: Her at the barre in the studio; hard cut to her at the bag in the gym, same posture, same spine.
- Emotion: Matter-of-fact. This is a résumé, not a boast.
- Lighting: Studio dawn; gym overheads; the bag on the bench lit by a locker-room strip light.

```
Glitter on the mouthguard, gloss on the grin I bring,
They saw the sparkle, slept on it, then felt the swing.
Cried in the third stall, eleven minutes, no sound at all,
Blotted, buttoned, walked back in and I ran the whole call.
```

- Scene: The mouthguard, then the bathroom.
- Shot: Extreme close-up of a glittered mouthguard being pushed in; a grin around it.
- Cut to: A pair of pastel heels under a bathroom stall door, still. A phone face-down on the cistern. Then hands at a sink, blotting under the eyes with a folded tissue; a blazer button done up.
- Then: The same woman at the head of a table, entirely composed, running the room.
- Emotion: The eleven minutes are real and so is the room. Neither cancels the other.
- Lighting: Bathroom harsh and green-white; the boardroom warm and controlled. The hardest lighting change in the video.

```
Mascara did the running, honey, I did the math,
Shook through the signature and still signed it fast.
They asked me for quiet, so I handed them polite,
Politeness is a razor if you angle it right.
```

- Scene: The signing.
- Shot: Macro on a hand holding a pen, visibly shaking, above a contract; then the signature going down in one clean movement.
- Cut to: A handshake; her face, pleasant and unreadable; the faceless other party's shoulders dropping.
- Emotion: The shake is left in. It is not a weakness shot, it is a cost shot.
- Lighting: Late-afternoon window light across the paper.

### [pre-chorus]

```
So go ahead, read me wrong,
I've been writing the end of this song.
Yeah, I cry, and then I win,
Silk on the outside, steel within.
```

- Scene: The corridor again, walking out.
- Shot: Tracking from behind, the glass meeting rooms sliding past, the faceless colleagues now watching.
- Cut to: Her reflection in a lift door as it closes on her.
- Lighting: The corridor lights coming on behind her as she passes, one by one.

### [chorus]

(repeat)

- Scene: The two worlds again, bigger and faster, and now they start to bleed into each other.
- Shots: Her at the barre in hand wraps. Her at the heavy bag in a leotard and a wrap skirt. Pastel light in the concrete gym for the first time.
- Emotion: The two rooms are becoming one room.
- Lighting: The grades cross-contaminate — gold in the gym, cold blue at the studio window.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: The half-time switch. No lyrics.
- Shots: Slow motion: chalk dust off a hand. Slow motion: a bag swinging back toward camera. Her sitting on the studio floor unlacing a pointe shoe, a bruised foot. Her sitting on a gym bench unwrapping her hands, the pink wrap pooling. Then a bare white frame and a single piano note — a lavender candle blown out.
- Lighting: Everything soft and slowed, then a hard drop to one light.

### [bridge – near-spoken, piano and pad, then the kit]

```
To every girl who cried in the stairwell,
Then walked in the room and nailed it,
Who felt every word and still stayed kind,
Who got called weak for a heart that size.
```

- Scene: A concrete stairwell. Not her — other women, one per line.
- Shots: A young woman sitting on a stair landing, head down, phone in hand. Another straightening a collar in a stairwell window's reflection. Another pushing through a fire door into bright office light.
- Emotion: The song widens out from her for the only time.
- Lighting: Stairwell green-grey and unflattering; the doorway light on the other side is warm.

```
Let them call it soft, let them call it sweet,
Soft is the part they can't defeat.
Cry it out, then stand up straight,
Steel don't bend from a little weight.
```

- Scene: She joins them.
- Shots: Her on the same landing, sitting with one of them; then both standing; then a line of four women walking up the stairs together, not in slow motion, just walking.
- Emotion: Solidarity without a single triumphant gesture.
- Lighting: The kit returns; the stairwell lights warm through as they climb.

```
You can be the softest thing in the building,
And still be the one holding up the ceiling.
```

- Scene: The building itself.
- Shot: A low wide from the ground floor of an atrium, looking straight up through the floors to the ceiling, the women small at the top.
- Cut to: Her face, close, lit warm, looking up.
- Lighting: The brightest frame of the video so far.

### [final chorus – fullest arrangement, "say it louder now"]

```
Soft girl, steel spine, don't confuse the two,
I can break down, then break through.
Velvet on the glove, but the punch is true,
Kindness ain't a weakness, kindness is the proof.
Soft girl, steel spine, say it louder now,
I bend, I bloom, I never bow.
You thought the tears meant I was through,
Soft girl, steel spine, don't confuse the two.
```

- Scene: One space, not two. The studio and the gym have merged into a single room: wooden floor, concrete wall, barre on one side, heavy bag on the other.
- Shots: Her moving between them without a cut — barre to bag in one continuous camera move. Pastel light and cold light in the same frame. A pointe shoe and a boxing glove side by side on the floor.
- Emotion: The argument is over.
- Lighting: The fullest, warmest, widest light of the video.

### [post-chorus – clipped chant]

```
Soft, soft, steel, steel,
Cry, then close the deal.
Soft, soft, steel, steel,
Pink on the wrap, but the hook is real.
```

- Scene: Four hard cuts on the chant.
- Shots: Pink nails. A fist in a wrap. Mascara under an eye. A left hook connecting with the bag.
- Shot length: half a second each, on the word.
- Lighting: Alternating pastel and concrete, one per cut.

### [outro – felt piano, sub drone, calm]

```
Pink nails, black coffee, 6 a.m.,
Wrap my hands and go again.
Cried in the car and still won the day,
Both of those are me, they're not going away.
Don't confuse the two, don't confuse the two,
Soft girl, steel spine, I'm both, it's true.
```

- Scene: The opening kitchen, the next morning.
- Shots: The same macro of pink nails and the black mug. The same wraps, pulled tight. The car mirror, no tears this time, just a look. The front door.
- Final shot: Her walking out of frame into daylight, the door left open behind her, the pointe shoes and the gloves both in the bag on her shoulder. Locked-off, hold through the fade. No text.
- Lighting: Blue pre-dawn turning to full morning across the section.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the match cut between the barre and the heavy bag, same line, same spine, *"soft girl, steel spine, don't confuse the two"* on screen.
- **The challenge:** the two-worlds transition — one outfit, one spin, and you land in the other life. Pastel to concrete, or the reverse.
- **The caption line:** *"Kindness ain't a weakness, kindness is the proof."*
- **The quote:** *"You can be the softest thing in the building, and still be the one holding up the ceiling."*
- **The rap clip:** *"Cried in the third stall, eleven minutes, no sound at all, / Blotted, buttoned, walked back in and I ran the whole call."*
- **The last frame:** the pointe shoes and the gloves in the same bag, going out the same door.
