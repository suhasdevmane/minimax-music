# Lipstick on Your Collar — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A woman leaves a lipstick print on her boyfriend's collar on purpose, every morning, so the whole office knows he's taken. It's not jealousy, it's pride played as a game: she wants him to catch his reflection in the lobby doors and remember who he's going home to. By the end he's in on it, wearing the mark like a badge, and he brings home a flower for her hair so the claim runs both ways.

**Mood:**
Playful, confident, flirty, warm. Sixties girl-group sass with a modern phone-in-hand wink. Possessive in the way a private joke is possessive.

**Message:**
"I don't need a ring to make it official. Just a kiss of red on you, and that's a signal."

**Genre:**
Retro doo-wop modernised into pop-R&B. Think: Amy Winehouse x Doja Cat x Bruno Mars, with the baritone sax of a sixties record and the trap hats and 808 of a current one.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 108 BPM, 4/4, swung
- Key: F major (bright, brassy, snap-along)
- Final chorus lifts a semitone to F♯ major for the walk-in.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, close, amused and conversational in the verses, big and bright in the choruses with a rasp at the top.
- Backing vocals: girl-group ooh and ahh stacks from the pre-chorus on; male and female harmonies on the last hook; a call-and-response post-chorus.

**Instrumentation:**
- Walking upright-style bass
- Dry finger snaps (the spine)
- Baritone sax (the signature voice, verse fills, chorus riff, instrumental lead)
- Doo-wop piano with triplet chords
- Muted electric guitar on the offbeats
- Tambourine and handclaps
- Trap hi-hats ticking above the swung kit; an 808 sub under the choruses
- Textures: a lipstick cap click, a door closing, a soft laugh

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: the doorway kiss and the secret she planted
- Verse 1: the plan explained, cheeky and certain
- Pre-chorus: the elevator ride, the mirrors, the blush she can feel from two miles away
- Chorus: big, bright, the whole floor gets a look
- Verse 2: the lunchtime texts, the office reaction, he likes it
- Bridge: honest, half-time, it was never about them; then he brings the flower home
- Final chorus: both marks worn, the two of them walking in together, key lift
- Outro: the same morning a week later, she does it again, brighter

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – snaps and bass, a kiss at the door]

```
7:45, you're buttoning up,
Coffee going cold in your favourite cup.
One kiss at the door before you go,
I left a little something you don't even know.
```

- Scene: A sunlit apartment hallway in the morning, sixties styling: a mustard kitchen, a rotary-style phone next to a smartphone.
- Shot: He buttons a white shirt in the hallway mirror; she leans in the kitchen doorway in a silk robe, hair in rollers, red lip already on.
- Cut to: The kiss at the door, her hand on his collar; a tight insert of the red print she leaves just below the collar edge; he doesn't notice.
- Her reaction: A slow, satisfied smile at the closed door.
- Lighting: Warm morning sun through a kitchen window, gold and cream.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the macro of the red print on white cotton at the doorway (intro), with *"lipstick on your collar, so they know you're mine"* on screen.
- **The challenge:** the collar-print reveal: couples film the doorway kiss, the collar, and the partner's reaction at work; the elevator-mirror shot is the second frame people will copy.
- **The caption line:** *"I don't need a ring to make it official."*
- **The quote:** *"You smile like you just remembered who you're going home for."*
- **The turn:** the flower behind the ear, *"now it's only fair,"* and the walk-in together the next morning.
- **The outro loop:** *"do it again, and make it a brighter red"* returns to the opening frame, so the video loops cleanly.
