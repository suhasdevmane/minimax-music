# Your Place or Mine — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
The end-of-the-night decision. The club has shut, the Tube is closed, it's four in the morning on a London corner and there are two front doors in two postcodes. She plays the whole thing as a flirty negotiation she has already won: whichever door they pick, he's hers tonight. A male rap feature answers from the taxi rank, cheerfully admitting he lost the argument the second she walked in. The turn in the bridge is that it was never about whose place; it's about who is still there making tea in the morning.

**Mood:**
Cheeky, confident, warm. Lights-up-at-the-club energy, taxi-rank drizzle, a coin in the air, a kitchen at sunrise. Playful and PG-13 all the way through.

**Message:**
"Your place or mine? Either way, you're mine tonight."

**Genre:**
House-pop / piano house with a UK rap feature. Think: Jessie Ware x Joel Corry, a bit of PinkPantheress in the vocal chops, a Dave-style dry London flow on the feature, Sunday-morning-after warmth in the outro.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 122 BPM, 4/4
- Key: F minor (sunny minor, house-piano friendly)
- No key change; the lift in the final chorus comes from the piano in octaves, doubled bass and the male harmonies underneath.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, close and amused in the verses, big and joyful in the choruses, near-spoken in the bridge.
- Singer B: male London rapper, dry and conversational, one verse after the instrumental, low harmonies and ad-lib shouts on the final chorus.
- Backing vocals: stacked female harmonies on the choruses, gang-style chant on the post-chorus, filtered pitched vocal chops of the hook.

**Instrumentation:**
- Stabbed piano house chords (main spine, filtered in the intro, octaves in the final chorus)
- Four-on-the-floor kick, rolling sub-bass, bouncing bass line
- Shuffled hi-hats, open hat, claps
- Pitched, filtered vocal chops of the hook
- Warm organ pad (second verse, bridge)
- Brass-style synth doubling the piano (second and final chorus)
- Ambient textures: crowd murmur, cloakroom ticket tear, muffled club thump, drizzle, black cab idling, taxi door thump, a coin spinning and landing, a kettle boiling

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: lights up, the queue, the look
- Verse 1: the chicken shop corner, the Tube's shut, she's already decided
- Pre-chorus: laying out the terms
- Chorus: the hook, big, sunny, certain
- Post-chorus: the chant
- Verse 2: the back of the black cab, the negotiation gets funnier and closer
- Instrumental: the taxi ride, the build
- Rap verse: he admits defeat, happily
- Bridge: the coin in the air, the turn, it was never about the postcode
- Final chorus: she tells the driver her street
- Outro: kettle on, morning, he's still there
