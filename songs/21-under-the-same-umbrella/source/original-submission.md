# Under the Same Umbrella — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A monsoon downpour hits a market street at dusk. She has six blocks to the bus stop and no umbrella. A stranger with one black umbrella offers half of it. They walk the six blocks together, share a chai under a tarp, and by the time the bus arrives neither of them wants to get on it. Two strangers, one umbrella, and a goodbye that never quite happens.

**Mood:**
Warm, rain-soaked, playful, cinematic. The joy of being dry in the middle of a flood. A first meeting that already feels like a memory.

**Message:**
"The rain took the whole city, and it gave me you."

**Genre:**
Bollywood-pop fusion. Think: Arijit Singh x Prateek Kuhad in duet, the string swell of an A.R. Rahman monsoon scene over a modern pop bass, a Mumbai indie-pop groove with tabla and a sitar-like hook. Cinematic but light on its feet.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 98 BPM, 4/4
- Key: D minor (warm, filmi, singable)
- The final chorus lifts higher with a brighter string voicing; no full key change, the lift comes from the arrangement and the harmonies.

**Vocal setup:**
- Singer A: young adult female lead, warm and conversational in the verses, glowing and open in the choruses.
- Singer B: male, a warm easy tenor with a smile in it, takes the second verse, harmonises beneath her in the choruses.
- Backing: stacked harmonies in the choruses, both voices together on the post-chorus chant.

**Instrumentation:**
- Sitar-like plucked strings (the hook motif)
- Soft piano
- Tabla (the heartbeat of the song)
- Deep modern bass with sub weight
- Cinematic strings (swelling in choruses, drone in the bridge)
- Handclaps (pre-chorus, post-chorus)
- Acoustic guitar under the male verse
- Warm synth pad
- Ambient textures: monsoon rain on a canvas awning, traffic horns, a bus engine idling, a single bus horn

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: the sky opens, everyone runs, an umbrella appears
- Verses: the first blocks, the chai stall, the walking getting slower
- Pre-chorus: puddles clapping, shoulders closer, don't look at the time
- Chorus: big, glowing, the only ones dry in the whole city
- Bridge: the bus arrives, the decision, the bus leaves without them
- Final chorus: neither says goodbye, she's not going to the bus stop anymore
- Post-chorus: let it pour
- Outro: the umbrella still over her, the name still unsaid

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – rain, a pluck and piano, her voice close]

```
The sky cracked open right over the market,
Every rickshaw ran for the shade of a tree.
I was counting how far I would have to run,
When a stranger held a black umbrella over me.
```

- Scene: A crowded Indian market street at dusk. The first fat drops hit, then the downpour. Vendors pull tarps, rickshaws swerve under a banyan tree.
- Shot: Mahima under a shop awning, counting the street ahead, dupatta over her head, about to run.
- Cut to: A black umbrella opens beside her, held by a man she's never seen. He tilts it over her.
- Her reaction: Surprise, a half-laugh, a glance up at the umbrella, then at him.
- Lighting: Blue-grey rain light, neon shop signs turning the wet street gold.

### [verse 1 – her]

```
You said, "which way," and I pointed anywhere,
The gutters running gold with the light from the signs.
Your left sleeve was soaking on the side that wasn't covered,
And you smiled like you didn't even mind.
```

- Scene: The first block. The umbrella barely covers two people.
- Shot: Two-shot from the front, walking, his left shoulder dark with rain, her side dry.
- Cut to: Her noticing his soaked sleeve, then his face; he shrugs.
- Emotion: Curious, amused, a little guilty about the sleeve.
- Lighting: Gold gutter reflections, neon reds and greens, rain in every frame.

```
Six blocks to the bus stop, one bus every hour,
The chai stall's got a tarp and a pot that never sleeps.
You asked me for my name, I said, "ask me at the corner,"
Some things go better slow when the whole street's running deep.
```

- Scene: A chai stall under a blue tarp, steam rising, a pot boiling.
- Shot: Mahima pointing down the street with six fingers, then at the stall.
- Cut to: Him asking, her tilting her head, "ask me at the corner."
- Emotion: Flirtatious, a game beginning.
- Lighting: Warm bulb under the tarp, blue rain outside it.

### [pre-chorus – tabla doubles, claps enter, both voices trading lines]

```
Every step, the puddles clap under our feet,
Every step, your shoulder gets closer to mine.
Don't look at the sky, don't look at the time,
Just walk, just walk, just walk.
```

- Scene: The walk, low angle on feet splashing through puddles in rhythm.
- Shot: Feet, then shoulders touching under the umbrella, then the two of them refusing to look at the sky.
- Cut to: A wristwatch she covers with her hand.
- Emotion: Building, playful urgency, nobody wants this to speed up.
- Lighting: Neon reflections under their feet.

### [chorus – wide, glowing, both voices]

```
Under the same umbrella, we're the only ones dry,
Two strangers in a monsoon, let the whole town go by.
The rain's got the city, the city's got us,
Six blocks is a lifetime when you're waiting on a bus.
Under the same umbrella, count the corners, one, two,
I'm halfway to the bus stop, and I'm halfway to you.
```

- Scene: The street in full flood, everyone else running, the two of them walking slowly in a dry circle.
- Shot: Slow-motion wide, the crowd streaking past, the umbrella still.
- Cut to: Overhead of the black umbrella moving through a sea of colour. Two corners passing, her counting on her fingers.
- Emotion: Pure joy in the middle of chaos.
- Lighting: Saturated, gold and neon, rain lit from behind.

### [verse 2 – him, acoustic guitar under]

```
I only had the one umbrella, I was headed the other way,
But you looked like you'd run for it, and I couldn't watch that.
The chai wallah gave us two cups for the price of one,
Said the rain does this to strangers, and he tipped his cap.
```

- Scene: A flashback to thirty seconds before the intro: Kai walking the opposite direction, seeing her about to run, turning around.
- Shot: His point of view of her under the awning, then his feet turning.
- Cut to: Present, the chai stall. The chai wallah handing over two small glasses, holding up one finger, tipping his cap.
- Emotion: His confession, sweet and a little sheepish.
- Lighting: Warm stall bulb, steam, rain sheeting off the tarp edge.

```
Your dupatta's dripping blue all down my white shirt,
It's the best that shirt has ever looked, I swear.
Three blocks in, you've stopped checking where the bus stop is,
And I'm walking slower, like I don't want to get there.
```

- Scene: Her wet blue dupatta has bled colour onto his white shirt.
- Shot: Close-up on the blue stain, then him looking down at it and grinning.
- Cut to: Her not looking down the street anymore, just at him. His feet slowing.
- Emotion: Flirt, ease, both of them stalling.
- Lighting: Neon on wet cotton, warm.

### [pre-chorus – both voices in unison]

```
Every step, the puddles clap under our feet,
Every step, your shoulder gets closer to mine.
Don't look at the sky, don't look at the time,
Just walk, just walk, just walk.
```

- Scene: Repeat of the walk, but now they are shoulder to shoulder with no gap.
- Shot: The umbrella tilting so the rain hits them both a little.
- Cut to: A clock on a shop wall, both of them looking away from it.
- Emotion: Closer, more certain.
- Lighting: Deeper dusk, more neon.

### [chorus]

```
Under the same umbrella, we're the only ones dry,
Two strangers in a monsoon, let the whole town go by.
The rain's got the city, the city's got us,
Six blocks is a lifetime when you're waiting on a bus.
Under the same umbrella, count the corners, one, two,
I'm halfway to the bus stop, and I'm halfway to you.
```

- Scene: The chorus montage, bigger: a flooded crossing, a temple bell, a wedding band caught in the rain, kids jumping in puddles.
- Shot: The umbrella passing all of it, the two of them laughing.
- Cut to: Corner four, corner five, her counting.
- Emotion: Bigger joy, the city as a party.
- Lighting: Fully saturated, string lights on a shop front, rain lit gold.

### [instrumental – sitar-style solo, strings, then rain alone]

- Scene: Wordless montage of the last block. A dog shaking off water. A bicycle bell. The umbrella spinning once. A drop of rain on her eyelash. A last shared look before the bus stop comes into view.
- Shot: Slow motion, the closest and most romantic frames of the video.
- Cut to: The bus stop, and the sound of a bus. The music drops to rain.
- Lighting: Golden, slow, then colder as the bus's headlights sweep over them.

### [bridge – half-time, piano and string drone, trading lines]

```
There's the bus stop, there's the bus, there's the door swinging wide,
And the driver's on the horn like he's got somewhere to be.
You could keep the umbrella, I could walk home in the rain,
Or the next one's in an hour, and the chai is still on me.
```

- Scene: The bus stop. The bus pulls in, its door folds open, the driver leans on the horn.
- Shot: Mahima looking at the open door, then at the umbrella, then at him.
- Cut to: Him offering the umbrella handle to her, then pointing back down the street toward the chai stall.
- Emotion: Tense, tender, the whole song hangs here.
- Lighting: Harsh bus interior light spilling out, cold, against the warm street behind them.

```
Say the word and I'll stay, say the word and I'll stay,
I don't know your name, but I know I don't want to go.
So the bus pulls away with an empty seat by the window,
And we're standing in the flood with nowhere left to go.
```

- Scene: Neither moves. The driver shrugs, the door folds shut.
- Shot: Extreme close-up on her mouth about to speak, then her hand not taking the handle.
- Cut to: The bus pulling away, an empty seat by the window sliding past, rain on the glass. The two of them left standing on the kerb under the umbrella.
- Emotion: Relief, a laugh, they both chose it.
- Lighting: Bus tail lights red on the wet road, then just the street neon again.

### [final chorus – highest, fullest, both voices, strings soaring]

```
Under the same umbrella, let the bus roll on by,
Two strangers in a monsoon, and neither says goodbye.
The rain's got the city, the city's got us,
Who needs a lifetime when you're missing every bus.
Under the same umbrella, count the corners, one, two,
I'm not going to the bus stop, I'm just going with you.
```

- Scene: They turn around and walk back into the flooded street, the wrong way, laughing.
- Shot: Wide from behind, the umbrella heading away from the bus stop into the neon.
- Cut to: The chai wallah seeing them return and already pouring two more.
- Emotion: Triumphant, giddy, a decision made.
- Lighting: The brightest, most colourful frames of the video.

### [post-chorus – claps, tabla flourishes, both voices chanting]

```
Let it pour, let it pour, we're not going anywhere,
Let it pour, let it pour, there's a whole night to spare.
Let it pour, let it pour, on the market, on the street,
The only ones dry from our heads to our feet.
```

- Scene: A dance, almost. Two people spinning under one umbrella in a flooded square, the crowd around them clapping in doorways.
- Shot: Overhead spin, then a low angle on splashing feet.
- Cut to: Both of them lifting the umbrella and letting the rain hit their faces for one second, then covering up again, laughing.
- Emotion: Release, celebration.
- Lighting: Rain lit from behind like sparks, neon everywhere.

### [outro – rain, a pluck, piano, her voice soft, his beneath]

```
The sky's still open right over the market,
The rickshaws are still hiding under the tree.
I never did tell you my name at the corner,
You just kept the umbrella over me.
You just kept the umbrella over me.
```

- Scene: Late now. The market is quieter, the rain steady, the same banyan tree, the same rickshaws.
- Shot: The two of them sitting on the chai stall bench under the tarp, the umbrella still open over her even though they are under cover.
- Cut to: His mouth asking the question again, her shaking her head, smiling. Final shot: the umbrella from above, the two heads beneath it, the rain, fade.
- Emotion: Warm, private, unfinished in the best way.
- Lighting: One warm bulb, blue rain, the last neon sign switching off.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** an overhead of one black umbrella moving through a sea of colour while everyone else runs. Caption: "Under the same umbrella, we're the only ones dry."
- **The challenge:** the umbrella-share. Two people, one umbrella, film the walk; the person on the wet side has to keep smiling. Post with the chorus.
- **The line for captions:** "Six blocks is a lifetime when you're waiting on a bus."
- **The bridge moment:** the bus door open, the driver on the horn, and neither of them moving. Made for a slow-zoom edit.
- **The quote:** "I'm not going to the bus stop, I'm just going with you."
- **The post-chorus:** "Let it pour" as a monsoon-season sound for rain videos.
