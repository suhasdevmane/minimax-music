# Pretty and Powerful — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
The false choice. She is told, in a hundred small ways, that she can be decorative or she can be taken seriously, and she refuses the trade. He raps the outside view — a man who was raised on the same false choice and unlearned it — and neither of them cancels the other out.

**Mood:**
Gold and black. Heavy, confident, unhurried. Swagger without a sneer; the song never punches down at anyone, it just declines the question.

**Message:**
"Pretty and powerful. Don't make me choose — I was never going to."

**Genre:**
Hip-hop pop with cinematic brass and a stadium chant. Think: Doja Cat x Megan Thee Stallion structure with the brass weight of a Kanye-era soul-sample chorus, sung lead and a relaxed male feature.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 100 BPM, 4/4
- Key: G minor (heavy, room for brass)
- No modulation; the final chorus lifts by doubling the brass an octave up and layering the chant underneath.

**Vocal setup:**
- Singer A: young adult female, dry and melodic in the verses, belted in the choruses.
- Singer B: male rapper, low register, relaxed pocket, slightly behind the beat. Two full rap verses, the title doubled an octave below in the choruses, one spoken answer in the bridge.
- Backing: stacked female harmonies on the hook, gang vocals on the chant.

**Instrumentation:**
- Sliding 808 and hard rimshot (the spine, first verse to last bar)
- Cinematic brass section: one low swell in the intro, stabs on the raps, full section on the choruses
- Tight trap hi-hats with rolls under the rap verses
- Fat clap layer and a floor-tom pattern for the chant; warm electric piano on the third verse and the bridge
- Rising strings and choir pad on the pre-choruses; gang vocals on the chant
- Ambience: warehouse room tone, a metal door, heel strikes on concrete, a skipping rope, a camera shutter

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: cold warehouse, one brass swell, the question and the refusal
- Verses: her account of being asked the wrong questions, his account of unlearning the same assumption
- Pre-chorus: two things can be true at once, drums out, one silent bar
- Chorus: the widest moment, full brass, both voices on the title; the post-chorus is a crowd chant
- Instrumental: brass riff, heel-strike breakdown, filtered rise
- Bridge: electric piano, the years she cut herself in half, his one spoken answer
- Final chorus and outro: both voices, then back to the empty room

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – cold room, one brass swell]

```
Warehouse mirror, cold floor, gold light,
Heels in one hand, a contract in the other.
They keep asking me which one is the real one.
Both of them. Run it up.
```

- Scene: An empty concrete warehouse before anyone arrives, a full-length mirror propped against a pillar.
- Shot: Her bare feet on cold concrete, heels hooked on two fingers in one hand, a black folder in the other; a shaft of gold through a high window.
- Cut to: The male lead in the doorway at the far end, backlit, saying his line without moving.
- Emotion / Lighting: Unhurried. Nothing to prove yet. Cold blue concrete cut by one gold shaft.

### [verse 1 – her, dry and melodic]

```
They put my face on the cover of the deck,
Left my name off the line where the credit gets checked.
He got the forecast question, I got the small talk,
"Which salon, which shade, which designer, which walk?"
So I named the margin before they could ask,
And I let the number do the talking, then I laughed.
Pretty was never a place I was hiding,
It's the door I walk through — it is not the building.
```

- Scene: A black glass boardroom, mid-presentation, and the corridor outside it.
- Shots: A printed pitch deck with her photograph on the cover and a credits line she is not on (composited); the room turning toward a faceless man for the numbers question; her answering anyway; a slow push-in as the table recalculates.
- Cut to: Her walking the corridor afterwards, folder under her arm, laughing once at nothing.
- Emotion / Lighting: Amused, not wounded. Hard boardroom daylight, black glass, one gold reflection.

### [verse 2 – his rap, beat drops]

```
Watch her walk in the warehouse, whole room recalibrate,
Gold on the collar, but the mind is what intimidates.
They clocked the glow and they slept on the resume,
Now they in the back row taking notes on how she runs the day.
She ain't a muse, she the architect, she drafted the design,
Y'all built a hallway, she just widened up the line.
Call her pretty, cool, but say the whole sentence:
Pretty and the payroll and the plan and the presence.
```

- Scene: The warehouse runway, now lit. He walks it toward camera while the crew builds around him.
- Shots: Him at the head of the runway, hands loose, delivering to the lens; behind him a crew laying lighting track; a wide of her walking the same runway in the opposite direction, the two passing without acknowledging each other.
- Cut to: An architect's roll of drawings unrolled on a trestle table, her hand flattening it.
- Emotion / Lighting: Vouching, not explaining. Gold work lamps, hard shadows, black floor.

### [pre-chorus]

```
Two things are true at once, they always were,
Don't hand me a mirror and call it a career.
I can be the picture and the one who takes it,
I can be the plan and the one who makes it.
```

- Scene: The mirror from the intro, now upright, with her in front of it and a camera on a tripod beside it.
- Shots: Her reflection; her hand turning the tripod camera to face the mirror so both are in frame; the drums dropping out and one silent bar on a held still frame.
- Emotion / Lighting: The held breath before the drop. Gold key, deep black fill.

### [chorus – widest, both voices]

```
Pretty and powerful, don't make me choose,
I'll take the heels and the hard-earned bruise.
Gold in my ears and the numbers in my hand,
Look good, close it out — that was always the plan.
You want the gloss or you want the grind?
Both of them living in the same mind.
Pick a lane for me, but I already do,
Pretty and powerful, don't make me choose.
```

- Scene: The runway at full power, cut against the boardroom and the ring.
- Shots: Her walking the lit runway in a black blazer and gold hoops; hard cuts on the beat to the same walk in the boardroom and the same walk into an empty boxing ring; brass hits synced to lights igniting overhead.
- Emotion / Lighting: Full release. Gold and black, high contrast, practical lamps only.

### [post-chorus – crowd chant]

```
Gold and black, gold and black,
Front of the room and I'm not moving back.
Pretty. Powerful. Both at once.
Don't make me choose.
```

- Scene: The crew becomes the crowd.
- Shots: Twelve people in black stamping the chant on the concrete, heel strikes visible; her at the front, unmoving; him at the side, arm raised on the last word.
- Emotion / Lighting: Communal, physical. Overhead lamps swinging slightly.

### [verse 3 – her, warmer groove]

```
Fifth floor glass and my name on the wall,
Same faces, same questions — different tone in the call.
I do not owe this table a plainer face,
And I don't shrink my laugh to fit a smaller space.
They said pick one, so I picked the two,
Then I hired the girl they said was "too much" too.
```

- Scene: The same boardroom, later in the story. Her name is now on the glass.
- Shots: A signwriter finishing her name on the door (composited); the same faceless people, now leaning in; her laughing at full volume and nobody flinching; a younger woman in a bright coat being shown to a chair at the table.
- Cut to: The two of them shaking hands, the older woman at the head of the table watching.
- Emotion / Lighting: Warm, settled, generous. Late-afternoon gold through black glass.

### [verse 4 – his second rap, stripped beat]

```
I'll be honest, I was taught that a woman had to trade,
Look good or get respected — only one of them pays.
Then I sat in the back while she carried a whole floor,
Charts up the wall and a warehouse on tour.
Told my little sister, "Hey, don't lower the light,"
Nobody dims the sun to make the moon look right.
So say it all together, don't split it in two,
Pretty and powerful — that is just her, that's the truth.
```

- Scene: Him alone in the empty warehouse at the end of the day, then one shot of him elsewhere.
- Shots: Him seated on a flight case, jacket off, talking straight to camera; a wide of the wall of charts behind him; a single warm cutaway of him on a doorstep with a teenage girl, saying the "don't lower the light" line to her, no music-video gloss.
- Emotion / Lighting: Honest, quiet, no swagger on this verse. One work lamp, everything else dark.

### [pre-chorus 2]

```
Two things are true at once, they always could,
Don't call it a lucky face — it was hours and good.
I can be the picture and the one who takes it,
I can be the plan and the one who makes it.
```

- Scene: The mirror again, this time with the crowd behind her in the reflection.
- Shots: Her framing herself in the tripod camera; the reflection filling with people; the silent bar again on a held frame, but wider.
- Emotion / Lighting: Bigger, less private. Choir enters, lights raise a stop.

### [chorus 2]

(repeat)

- Scene: The three worlds cut faster and start to bleed into each other.
- Shots: A runway light rig above the boardroom table; a boardroom chair on the runway; the ring ropes lit gold; her walking through all three in one continuous-feeling move.
- Emotion / Lighting: Momentum. Saturated gold, black everywhere else.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: The brass riff, the heel-strike breakdown, the rise. No story, all texture.
- Shots: Twelve pairs of heels stamping in unison on concrete, shot at floor level; a skipping rope turning in slow motion in the empty ring; brass players in silhouette in a corner of the warehouse; a hand flattening a contract; the lights killing one by one until only the mirror is lit.
- Lighting: Gold reducing to a single lamp, then a filtered rise as everything comes back.

### [bridge – electric piano, near-spoken]

```
For years I cut myself in half to fit the room,
Wore the quiet one to work, saved the loud one for the moon.
Grew up on lip gloss and long division,
Nobody told me I had to pick a religion.
Beauty is not the rent I pay to keep the skill,
Both of them mine, and I'm not choosing. I never will.
```

- Scene: The mirror, alone, the whole crew gone. This is the stillest part of the video.
- Shots: Her sitting on the floor in front of the mirror, blazer over her shoulders; a childhood-desk insert of a maths exercise book beside an open lip gloss; him off to the side, out of focus, saying his one line; her turning to the lens on the last line.
- Emotion / Lighting: Unguarded, then decided. One lamp, long shadow, everything warm.

### [final chorus]

(repeat, both voices, brass an octave up)

- Scene: Everything at once, at full crowd size.
- Shots: The runway packed on both sides; the boardroom table carried onto the runway by the crew; her walking its length; him on the ropes of the ring with the chant behind him; a crane pulling up over the whole warehouse with every lamp lit.
- Emotion / Lighting: Triumphant and shared. Gold saturating, black floor reflecting it.

### [post-chorus 2]

(repeat, gang vocals)

- Scene: The chant at full size, everyone facing camera.
- Shots: The crowd stamping; the younger woman from verse three at the front now; the two leads on either side of frame, not centre.
- Emotion / Lighting: Handed over. All lamps up, dust in the beams.

### [outro – back to the empty room]

```
Warehouse mirror, cold floor, gold light,
Heels on, folder closed, both of them right.
Don't make me choose.
Never had to choose.
Pretty and powerful.
```

- Scene: The warehouse empty again, exactly as the intro framed it.
- Shots: Her putting the heels on, standing, closing the folder; the mirror holding her reflection alone; the final shot is the intro's first frame repeated with her walking out through the gold shaft and the lamp going dark behind her.
- Emotion / Lighting: Settled. Cold concrete, one gold shaft, then black.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the runway walk with the brass hit landing as the overhead lamps ignite one by one, *"pretty and powerful, don't make me choose"* on the step.
- **The challenge:** the gold-and-black chant. Film four seconds of yourself stamping the *"gold and black, gold and black"* heel pattern and cut on the fourth beat; it works as a transition into any outfit change.
- **The caption line:** *"Pretty was never a place I was hiding. It's the door I walk through, it is not the building."*
- **The rap clip:** *"Y'all built a hallway, she just widened up the line."* **The quote card:** *"Nobody dims the sun to make the moon look right."*
