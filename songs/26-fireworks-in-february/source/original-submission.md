# Fireworks in February — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A boy set off fireworks in an empty retail parking lot in the second week of February because months earlier, on a bus, she had said she had never seen one up close. No holiday, no birthday, no anniversary, no reason at all except that he remembered and he could. The song is about the difference between an occasion and a gesture.

**Mood:**
Cold air, warm heart. Amused in the verses, wide open in the choruses. Not a big romantic ballad — a small true story told at radio tempo.

**Message:**
"Anybody can do December. You did February."

**Genre:**
Modern radio pop with synth bells and punchy drums. Think: Taylor Swift's 1989-era production x Maggie Rogers's warmth x a Sasha Sloan lyric — big major-key chorus, small specific detail.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 112 BPM, 4/4
- Key: G major throughout, no modulation
- The lift is arrangement only: bells stack an octave per chorus until the final one is two octaves and strings

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: female, conversational and slightly amused in the verses, fully open in the choruses
- Backing vocals: stacked female harmonies from the first chorus, an octave layer added in the second, full stack plus an answering low harmony in the final
- The bridge is voice and piano only

**Instrumentation:**
- Bright synth bells (the signature figure, first bar to last)
- Punchy programmed drums with a live-feeling kick and snare in the choruses
- Muted plucked bass from verse one
- Sustained warm pads under the choruses; soft upright piano alone in the bridge
- Cinematic strings for the build out of the bridge and the final chorus, and a wide synth lead answering the bell hook in the instrumental
- Ambience: parking-lot room tone, wind, a car door, a lighter, a car heater

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: a dead month, a text with no explanation, a big coat
- Verse 1: the drive, the empty lot, the trunk opening
- Pre-chorus: cold shoes, slow smoke, something going off in her chest first
- Chorus: the sky, the gesture, the argument of the song
- Verse 2: the receipt, the safety video, the security guard, his face in the light
- Instrumental: the display at full size
- Bridge: what everybody else does with occasions, and why it is worse
- Final chorus: widest arrangement, the trade she would make
- Post-chorus: the month chanted
- Outro: ash on the windshield, the heater, the drive home

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – bells and room tone, no drums]

```
Second week of February and the whole town's asleep,
Nothing on the calendar and nothing left to keep.
You said, "wear the big coat," and you wouldn't tell me why,
Half past ten on a Tuesday in the deadest month alive.
```

- Scene: A small town at half past ten on a February Tuesday. Nothing is open.
- Shot: Static wide of a wet main street with every window dark, one traffic light cycling to nobody.
- Cut to: Her pulling a heavy coat off a hook by the door and reading a text she doesn't answer.
- Emotion: Curiosity without expectation. She thinks they're getting food.
- Lighting: Sodium orange outside, one cold interior lamp; breath visible the moment she steps out.

### [verse 1 – the drive and the trunk]

```
We drove past the strip mall and the shuttered garden store,
Parked between the carts where the lot light doesn't go anymore.
I said, "is this a kidnapping," you said, "more or less,"
And your breath came out white and you laughed at my face.
You popped the trunk open and I stood there in the wind,
Two flat boxes, a lighter, and a bucket of sand.
You said, on a bus in November I had mentioned it once,
That I'd never seen one close, and you'd been holding that since.
```

- Scene: The drive out to a dead retail lot, and what is in the trunk.
- Shot: Passenger-side profile through the windshield, the strip mall sliding past; a shuttered garden center with its shade cloth flapping.
- Cut to: The car parked alone in a huge empty lot beside a corral of shopping carts, one broken lot light above it; then the trunk lifting to reveal two flat fireworks boxes, a lighter and a builder's bucket of sand.
- Her reaction / Emotion: Confusion, then the penny dropping, then a laugh she doesn't finish.
- Lighting: Headlights and one working lot light; everything else black. Breath white in both faces.

### [pre-chorus – before the first one]

```
And the cold got into my shoes and I didn't care,
And the smoke came down slow through the frozen air.
Something in my chest went off before the first one flew,
I have never been the kind of girl this happens to.
```

- Scene: The last few seconds before the first firework goes up.
- Shot: Her feet in thin shoes on frozen asphalt, then a slow tilt up to her face.
- Cut to: The first fuse catching, him walking backwards fast, smoke rolling low across the ground.
- Emotion: Anticipation with something underneath it she hasn't named yet.
- Lighting: One point source on the ground, faces mostly dark, smoke catching the lot light.

### [chorus 1 – the sky]

```
Fireworks in February, just because you could,
No birthday, no reason, no occasion, nothing owed.
You lit up an empty parking lot like a town square,
And I finally know what people mean by care.
Fireworks in February and the sky went gold,
Twenty-eight days of nothing and you made one hold.
Anybody can do December, baby, anyone would,
You did fireworks in February, just because you could.
```

- Scene: The display, and the empty lot lit like a town square for one person.
- Shot: Wide low from the far side of the lot with the two of them tiny under an enormous sky; then a shot from directly beneath, gold falling toward the lens.
- Cut to: The whole lot in gold light — carts, painted lines, puddles — and her spinning once with her arms out.
- Emotion: Wide-open happiness, disbelief, and being genuinely seen.
- Lighting: Everything motivated by the fireworks. The lot goes gold, then black, then gold again on the beat.

### [verse 2 – the receipt, the guard, his face]

```
There was a receipt in the glove box for a hundred and ten,
More than your insurance will forgive you again.
You had watched a whole video on where to stand back,
And you made me hold the sparkler while you lit the first pack.
The security guard came out and then he just stayed,
Arms folded on the curb like a kid at a parade.
And I didn't look up once at the sky going white,
I was watching your face turning gold in the light.
```

- Scene: The evidence of how much planning this took, and who else showed up.
- Shot: The glove box open, a crumpled receipt on top of the manual; his phone face-up on the dash with a paused safety video.
- Cut to: A security guard coming out of a service door with a flashlight, deciding not to say anything, and sitting down on the curb with his arms folded; a sparkler in her fist held out at arm's length.
- Her reaction / Emotion: She stops watching the sky. The last two lines are her looking only at him.
- Lighting: Flashlight beam, then firework gold across his face; she stays half in shadow so the audience follows her eyeline.

### [pre-chorus 2 – same words, colder]

```
And the cold got into my shoes and I didn't care,
And the smoke came down slow through the frozen air.
Something in my chest went off before the first one flew,
I have never been the kind of girl this happens to.
```

- Scene: The same four beats, later in the night and colder.
- Shot: Repeat the feet-to-face tilt from the first pre-chorus in identical framing so the repetition reads.
- Cut to: The guard on the curb now holding a sparkler too, and not making eye contact with anyone.
- Emotion: Warmth arriving in the middle of physical cold.
- Lighting: More smoke in the air than the first time; the beams from the lot light now visible.

### [chorus 2 – bigger]

```
Fireworks in February, just because you could,
No birthday, no reason, no occasion, nothing owed.
You lit up an empty parking lot like a town square,
And I finally know what people mean by care.
Fireworks in February and the sky went gold,
Twenty-eight days of nothing and you made one hold.
Anybody can do December, baby, anyone would,
You did fireworks in February, just because you could.
```

- Scene: The display at full size, both of them under it.
- Shot: Slow-motion at ninety-six frames of the two of them on the hood of the car looking up, one shared coat over both shoulders.
- Cut to: Reflections — the whole display in a puddle between the painted lines, then in the windshield with their faces behind it.
- Emotion: Settled, not giddy. The night has become a memory while it is still happening.
- Lighting: Gold on wet asphalt, blue-black sky, the coldest-looking warm image in the video.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: No lyrics. The display given the whole screen.
- Shots: A long lens on a single shell going up and opening; the empty carts lit gold and going dark; the guard walking back to the service door and stopping to watch one more; her sparkler burning down to her glove; the two of them small at the bottom of a very tall frame.
- Emotion: The plain pleasure of watching something burn brightly for no reason.
- Lighting: Entirely fireworks-motivated, with the lot light as the only constant.

### [bridge – piano and voice, no drums]

```
People wait for a reason, they save it for a date,
Put a circle in red on a square on a page.
Wrap it in ribbon and they leave it by the tree,
And forget halfway through what the whole thing should mean.
So don't buy me diamonds and don't book me a flight,
Just tell me to get in the car on a nothing kind of night.
```

- Scene: The argument of the song, cut against everybody else's version of romance.
- Shot: Three cold flash inserts — a wall calendar with one date circled in red, a wrapped box under a dead tree, a jewellery case being opened in a lit shop window.
- Cut to: Back to the lot, the two of them sitting on the curb beside the guard, all three quiet, the last smoke drifting.
- Emotion: Not bitter. Just certain.
- Lighting: The inserts are clean, bright and lifeless; the lot is warm, dark and specific. The contrast is the point.

### [final chorus – strings, widest]

```
Fireworks in February, just because you could,
No birthday, no reason, no occasion, nothing owed.
You lit up an empty parking lot like a town square,
And now every February I know what's coming there.
Fireworks in February and the sky went gold,
Twenty-eight days of nothing and you made one hold.
I'd trade every New Year's for the way you stood,
Doing fireworks in February, just because you could.
```

- Scene: The biggest and last of the display, and one flash-forward.
- Shot: Drone rising from the lot until the car is a dot and the display is level with the lens.
- Cut to: A two-second flash-forward — the same lot, a different February, her arriving first with the bucket of sand.
- Emotion: The gesture becoming a tradition.
- Lighting: Full gold, then a single held frame of the two of them in silhouette against the last shell.

### [post-chorus – the month]

```
February, February,
Nothing on the calendar and everything in the sky.
February, February,
Twenty-eight days and you picked the coldest night.
```

- Scene: The chant, over the lot emptying out.
- Shot: Locked-off wide of the lot with smoke drifting across it and the two of them walking back to the car.
- Cut to: The guard raising a hand without turning around as he goes back inside.
- Emotion: Quiet comedown, still elated.
- Lighting: Only the lot light and the headlights now; the sky black again.

### [outro – bells, pad, heater]

```
Ash on the windshield and the heater coming on,
Your hand on the gearshift and the radio off.
Nine more Februaries and I still know the sound
Of the first one going up over an empty lot.
```

- Scene: The drive home, and the last line spoken from years later.
- Shot: Ash settling on the windshield; the wipers clearing it in one pass; his hand on the gearshift; the radio staying off.
- Cut to: Final shot — the empty lot from the rear window, one working light, smoke still in the air, holding as the car pulls away.
- Emotion: Contentment, and the beginning of a memory she will keep.
- Lighting: Dash glow on both faces, warm heater light, everything outside blue-black.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the whole lot going gold on the downbeat of *"fireworks in February"* — a hard black-to-gold cut that reads at any size.
- **The knife line:** "And I didn't look up once at the sky going white / I was watching your face turning gold in the light."
- **The caption line:** "Anybody can do December. You did February."
- **The challenge:** post the empty-lot-to-gold cut and invite people to post the smallest thing somebody did for them for no reason at all.
- **The security guard** sitting down on the curb with his arms folded, and later holding a sparkler, is the detail that makes the video feel true rather than staged.
