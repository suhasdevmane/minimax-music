# Made of Sunlight — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A call-and-response love song between two people in a small beach town. The whole song is one question passed back and forth — "what are you made of?" — and two people answering it with the things around them: salt, sand, engine oil, mango skin, the drum at the end of the road. It is a love song with no obstacle in it. Nothing goes wrong. That is the point.

**Mood:**
Joyful, warm, unhurried, communal. Golden-hour happiness that does not apologise for itself. Orange and blue as an emotional register, not just a colour grade.

**Message:**
"Ask me what I am made of and I will keep giving you the same answer."

**Genre:**
Afrobeats-pop with highlife guitar and a crossover pop chorus. Think: Burna Boy x Tems x Calvin Harris's sunlit records — log drums and horns underneath, a chorus a stadium in any country can sing back on first hearing.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 106 BPM, 4/4
- Key: F major, bright and open, no modulation needed
- The lift comes from arrangement, not key change: brass and group vocals stack chorus by chorus until the final one is twice the width of the first

**Vocal setup:**
- Singer A, female lead: verse one, the calling half of every hook line
- Singer B, male: verse two, the answering half of every hook line
- Group backing vocals: a street-party crowd on the chorus and post-chorus, deliberately imperfect
- The two leads only sing full unison in the bridge and the final chorus

**Instrumentation:**
- Log drums (the foundation, round and low)
- Bright plucked highlife-leaning electric guitar (the melodic signature)
- Round electric bass locked to the log drums; shakers, woody hand percussion and clapped offbeats
- Three-piece brass section (stabs growing into sustained lines) and a talking drum through the break
- Warm electric piano pad under the choruses and bridge
- Ambience: evening surf, a far-off radio, children in the street

**Mood arc:**
- Intro and verse 1: evening arriving, one hand held out, her teasing the first answer
- Pre-chorus and chorus: the drum arrives, the street leans, the road sings the exchange back
- Verse 2: the next morning, his answer, the market and the mangoes
- Instrumental: the street dance at full size
- Bridge: the power cuts, and nothing stops
- Final chorus: widest arrangement, both voices in harmony
- Post-chorus: the chant, orange and blue
- Outro: two voices, one guitar, the surf again

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – guitar, one shaker, surf]

```
Orange on the water and the blue coming down,
Somebody's speaker going wild at the end of the town.
You put your hand out flat and you said, "come on, walk,"
And the whole evening changed the way it talked.
```

- Scene: The main road of a beach town at golden hour, the sea at the end of it.
- Shot: Wide, low, the sun sitting on the water, the road glowing orange, silhouettes far off.
- Cut to: A speaker on a plastic crate outside a shop, the cone visibly moving; then his open hand entering frame, held flat.
- Emotion: Anticipation, ease, nothing urgent yet.
- Lighting: Full golden hour, deep blue shadow on the sea side of the frame.

### [verse 1 – her, teasing]

```
You came down the beach road with your shirt undone,
Two plastic chairs, a cooler and the last of the sun.
The barber's radio was older than the both of us,
But you had the whole street moving with your hands up.
I said, "what are you made of," and you laughed and said, "guess,"
So I started at your hands and I gave it my best.
Sea glass, engine oil, and a laugh like a bell,
And whatever else it is, it wears on you well.
```

- Scene: He arrives carrying too much, entirely unbothered, and the street picks up his step without being asked. Then the two of them drop into the chairs at the edge of the sand and she starts guessing.
- Shot: Tracking behind him down the middle of the road; a barber shop doorway with a battered radio and two old men nodding; a close two-shot in the chairs.
- Cut to: Three fast inserts on her three answers — a piece of green sea glass turned in her fingers, an oil-dark knuckle, his head going back laughing.
- Her reaction / Emotion: She is amused before she is anything else, and then she looks a beat too long.
- Lighting: Sun behind him, flare allowed, faces held up with bounce; low sun raking both faces in the chairs.

### [pre-chorus – the drum arrives]

```
Then the drum comes in and the drum comes in,
And the whole town leans the way the evening leans.
Put your hand in my hand, let the horns begin,
Ask me what I'm made of, I will answer again.
```

- Scene: The street dance starting — not on a cue, just gathering.
- Shot: A drummer's hands on a log drum, then a whip-pan to the road filling with people.
- Cut to: Her hand going into his, low frame, both stepping off the sand into the road.
- Emotion: The switch from two people to a whole town.
- Lighting: Sun gone, the first strings of bulbs coming on above the road.

### [chorus 1 – call and response, the whole road]

```
You're made of sunlight, I'm made of you,
Made of the salt and the sand and the blue.
Say it out loud where the whole street hears,
Say it over log drums, say it in my ear.
You're made of sunlight, I'm made of you,
Made of the drum and the dust and the truth.
Whatever I am, I was made in your hands,
So I'm made of you.
```

- Scene: The full street dance with both leads inside it, children copying the step at the edges.
- Shot: Circling handheld around the two of them, crowd blurred orange behind; alternating tight singles on the call and the answer in matched framing.
- Cut to: A drone rising slowly from head height to roof height, the road a river of movement; a low shot of feet in sand with dust lifting through the light.
- Emotion: Pure joy, no irony anywhere in it.
- Lighting: Bulb strings and headlights, deep blue sky above, orange street below — the whole grade in one frame.

### [verse 2 – him, the morning after]

```
You came in from the market with the mangoes and the rain,
Orange on your fingers, half the ocean in your hair.
The auntie at the fish stall said we move like one song,
And I have not stopped humming it, so she was not wrong.
You asked me what I'm made of, so I'll answer you now,
Since the night you said my name I have not put it down.
Cold glass, warm asphalt, and the sound of your feet,
And a street that plays your name on every beat.
```

- Scene: Morning, the market, a short warm shower of rain. He finally answers the question she asked the night before.
- Shot: Her walking in from a doorway, bag on her hip, hair wet, holding a mango; a fish stall under a tarpaulin with an older woman laughing and pointing; him in a doorway with a cold bottle against his neck, looking straight down the lens for the only time in the video.
- Cut to: Three inserts on his three answers — condensation running down glass, bare feet on hot wet road, a speaker cone pulsing.
- Emotion: Domestic delight, then sincerity arriving inside a joke.
- Lighting: Flat bright morning, colours saturated by wet ground, one hard bounce and real shadows.

### [pre-chorus 2 – both, trading half lines]

```
Then the drum comes in and the drum comes in,
And the whole town leans the way the evening leans.
Put your hand in my hand, let the horns begin,
Ask me what I'm made of, I will answer again.
```

- Scene: Evening again, the dance reassembling faster than the first time.
- Shot: Match-cut from the empty morning road to the same framing at dusk with the drummers already in it.
- Cut to: The brass players walking into shot mid-phrase, instruments up.
- Emotion: The town knows what happens next and is not waiting.
- Lighting: Golden hour again, a shade deeper than the first.

### [chorus 2 – bigger]

```
You're made of sunlight, I'm made of you,
Made of the salt and the sand and the blue.
Say it out loud where the whole street hears,
Say it over log drums, say it in my ear.
You're made of sunlight, I'm made of you,
Made of the drum and the dust and the truth.
Whatever I am, I was made in your hands,
So I'm made of you.
```

- Scene: The dance at full size, the whole road, both leads at the centre of it.
- Shot: A long steadicam push straight down the middle of the crowd, people parting, the leads at the end of it; four ordinary faces singing the answer half.
- Cut to: The hook line only in slow motion at ninety-six frames, then hard back to real time; a blue neon over a shop reflected in a puddle with dancing legs crossing it.
- Emotion: Two people alone inside a hundred people.
- Lighting: Bulbs, brake lights, one blue neon; warm key with a blue rim from the sea side.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: No lyrics. The street dance as its own set piece, and a town that does this every week.
- Shots: A talking drum answering the log drums, the drummer's face; the brass section playing to each other rather than the crowd; a rooftop wide of the whole road with the sea black beyond; children at the front doing the step better than the adults; a boy carrying two crates of bottles straight through the middle without spilling one.
- Emotion: Ordinary, repeatable, communal happiness.
- Lighting: Bulbs and headlights, the last blue leaving the sky.

### [bridge – the power cuts]

```
When the power cuts and the whole street goes dark,
You are still the warmest thing out here in the yard.
I have got you memorised right down to the scar,
I could find you in a blackout by the sound of your heart.
So let the generators cough and the lanterns come out,
We were never dancing for the bulbs anyhow.
```

- Scene: A blackout. Everything stops for exactly one second and then does not stop at all.
- Shot: Hard cut to black on the downbeat with the drums still going, then phone torches and one lantern coming up under her face; her fingers finding a small scar on his forearm in the dark.
- Cut to: A generator kicked awake, a cough of smoke, one string of bulbs stuttering back on at the top of frame.
- Emotion: The first quiet of the whole song, then defiant joy.
- Lighting: Near-black, then one practical source with hard falloff, then bulbs returning in stages.

### [final chorus – both in harmony, full width]

```
You're made of sunlight, I'm made of you,
Made of the salt and the sand and the blue.
Say it to the palms and the plastic chairs,
Say it to the children who are still out there.
You're made of sunlight, I'm made of you,
Made of the drum and the dust and the truth.
Ten thousand mornings and I still choose,
I'm made of you.
```

- Scene: Full power back, the whole road lit, and the vow said inside the party rather than away from it.
- Shot: Drone climbing from inside the crowd to above the roofline with the sea behind; the two plastic chairs from verse one now empty in the sand as the leads dance past them.
- Cut to: An older couple at the edge of the dance doing exactly the same step, forty years on, held just under two seconds.
- Emotion: Release, then the future arriving in one image.
- Lighting: Every practical on, deep blue sky above, faces fully lit.

### [post-chorus – the chant]

```
Oh, the orange and the blue,
Oh, the orange and the blue,
Everything I am, I am made of you.
Oh, the orange and the blue.
```

- Scene: The crowd chant with the simplest possible choreography.
- Shot: Locked-off wide of the road, everyone on the same two-count step, arms up, repeated in identical framing so the repetition reads.
- Cut to: Three tight cutaways of individual faces singing the chant, cut on the beat.
- Emotion: Something a stranger can join on the second bar.
- Lighting: Orange lights, blue sky, flat and clean.

### [outro – two voices, one guitar]

```
Sunlight, sunlight, walking down the beach road,
Sunlight, sunlight, and the evening going gold.
Ask me in a hundred years, I will tell you the truth,
I'm made of you.
```

- Scene: The two of them walking back down the beach road, the dance a warm blur far behind.
- Shot: Long lens from far ahead, both walking toward camera down the empty end of the road.
- Cut to: Final shot — the chairs and the cooler left in the sand, the sea beyond, held to the fade.
- Emotion: Calm, settled, deliberately unfinished.
- Lighting: Last blue, one warm bulb still burning behind them.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the call and the answer cut on alternating tight singles — "you're made of sunlight" / "I'm made of you" — built for two people lip-syncing the two halves in one frame.
- **The challenge:** the post-chorus two-count step, deliberately the easiest choreography in the catalogue, filmed as a locked-off wide so the whole move is learnable from a single clip.
- **The caption line:** "Ask me what I'm made of, I will answer again."
- **The blackout:** a hard cut to black on the downbeat of the bridge with the drums still playing — the most shareable second in the video.
- **The forty-years-on couple** at the edge of the final chorus, on screen for under two seconds, is the frame people will pause on.
