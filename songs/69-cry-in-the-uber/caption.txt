Global Metadata
Basic Attributes: bpm is 90. key is F minor, and scale is minor. Sad Pop and cinematic downtempo, piano-led with deep sub-bass and real rain in the texture, a small British heartbreak recorded in the back of a moving car.
Global Emotional Progression: The track opens with rain on glass, a wet-road hiss and a distant siren, a single felt-piano figure and no drums at all, the lead voice tight and holding something back. The first verse brings a soft heartbeat kick and a low sub-bass that sits under everything like an engine, brushed hats moving at the speed of windscreen wipers, the voice conversational and cracked at the edges. The pre-chorus removes the kick and leaves piano, sub and breath for two bars of held tension. The chorus opens with wide strings and a warm choir pad, the drums still restrained, more of a swell than a drop, the melody rising into the top of the chest voice and staying there. The second verse is quieter than the first, the piano more sparse, a cello line entering under the small kindness of a stranger, and the second pre-chorus is heavier with a slow rising filter. The second chorus adds a second string layer and stacked harmonies, the biggest sound so far but never loud. The instrumental break is the piano figure over passing traffic and rain, a lone cello answering it, then a long filtered swell and a bar where only the rain remains. The bridge drops almost to nothing, close voice and felt piano and one held sub note, and rebuilds slowly across its last three lines as the crying stops and the strings return. The final chorus is the fullest arrangement of the record with strings, choir, a live-sounding kit and the widest harmonies, released rather than triumphant, and the post-chorus is a soft counting tag over piano and pad. The outro removes everything but piano and a single close vocal, a door, a kettle, and silence.
