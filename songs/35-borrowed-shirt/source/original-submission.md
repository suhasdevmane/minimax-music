# Borrowed Shirt — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
The morning after, told entirely through ordinary objects. She is in his
shirt with the sleeves rolled twice, making the coffee too sweet on purpose,
and the song never leaves the apartment. What it is actually about is that
this is the fourth Sunday in a row and neither of them has said a word about
what that means — until he tells her to keep the shirt, which is the same
thing said quieter.

**Mood:**
Warm, slow, unhurried, entirely tender. Sensual but never explicit: the
intimacy is a chipped mug, a hair tie on a wrist, a kiss on a shoulder.

**Message:**
"Nobody said anything big. He just told me to keep the shirt."

**Genre:**
Bedroom R&B with neo-soul warmth. Think: Daniel Caesar x SZA, with the
domestic softness of a Cleo Sol record and the loose, behind-the-beat pocket
of an early Frank Ocean ballad.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 84 BPM, 4/4
- Key: B♭ major (warm, unhurried, sits low in her chest voice)
- No modulation. The final chorus lifts by arrangement only: low strings and
  a saxophone shadowing the melody.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, extremely close to the microphone, breath
  audible, almost spoken in the bridge.
- Backing vocals: stacked breathy harmonies in the choruses; a wordless line
  drifting across the instrumental break; full stacks on the post-chorus.

**Instrumentation:**
- Soft electric piano (the spine, first bar to last)
- Rounded sub-bass with a fretless feel
- Muted electric guitar answering the vocal
- Brushed programmed kit sitting behind the beat, rimshot instead of snare
- Shaker and light tambourine from verse two
- Warm analogue synth pads
- Low strings and a saxophone under the final chorus only
- Vinyl crackle and a run-out groove; domestic room tone, a kettle, a street

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: the record at the end of its side, nobody getting up
- Verses: the apartment inventoried object by object, then the pattern revealed
- Pre-chorus: the small evidence of a life beginning to overlap
- Chorus: the hook, wide and warm rather than loud
- Instrumental: piano and guitar talking over the crackle
- Bridge: the quietest point of the record, the shirt folded and given back
- Final chorus: the same joy with the question answered
- Outro: the run-out groove again, nobody moving

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – crackle, one chord, voice almost alone]

```
Needle at the end of the record, going round,
Making that soft little scratch instead of sound.
Curtains half open on a street still asleep,
And your shirt on my shoulders, three buttons deep.
```

- Scene: A one-bedroom apartment mid-morning, curtains half drawn, dust in the light.
- Shot: Macro on a turntable, the needle riding the run-out groove, the platter turning.
- Cut to: Her bare feet crossing a wooden floor; a pan up to the shirt, sleeves rolled twice.
- Emotion and lighting: Contentment with nothing performed in it; low warm sun through a half-open curtain, everything else soft shadow.

### [verse 1]

```
The kettle's doing that thing where it rattles the lid,
You're still under the blanket, doing what you did,
Which is nothing, beautifully, one arm hanging loose,
Face down in the pillow like you've got no excuse.
```

- Scene: The kitchen and the bedroom, one open doorway between them.
- Shots: Close on a kettle lid shivering on the boil. A wide from the kitchen through the doorway: a shape under a blanket, one arm hanging off the mattress. Her leaning on the door frame watching him sleep with a mug in both hands.
- Emotion and lighting: Amused, unguarded; warm bedroom shadow against a bright kitchen.

```
I roll the sleeves up twice and they swallow my hands,
Smells like cedar and the rain that we walked in.
I take the ugly mug, the one with the chip,
Make the coffee far too sweet so you'll steal a sip.
```

- Scene: The kitchen counter.
- Shots: Macro on her rolling a cuff twice; the sleeve still past her knuckles. Her pulling the collar to her face for one breath. A chipped mug lifted from a shelf of matching ones. Two spoons of sugar going in, deliberately, a small private smile.
- Emotion and lighting: Domestic mischief; hard morning light across the counter, steam catching it.

### [pre-chorus]

```
There's a watch face down on the windowsill,
There's a shoe by the door lying where it fell.
Nothing here is tidy, nothing here is planned,
And I've never felt this easy standing where I stand.
```

- Scene: A slow walk through the apartment, object by object.
- Shots: A watch lying face down on a sill. One shoe on its side by the door where it was kicked off. A towel over a chair back. Her stopping in the middle of the room, eyes closed, just standing in it.
- Emotion and lighting: Ease; a single slow track through warm light and dust.

### [chorus – wide and warm, not loud]

```
I'm wearing your shirt and nothing else is on my mind,
No plans, no phone, no reason to be on time.
The sunlight's doing slow laps across the floor,
And I'm not thinking past the kitchen door.

Small and ordinary and holy as it gets,
Toast and butter, two spoons, and the radio set.
Say my name low, say it one more time,
I'm wearing your shirt and nothing else is on my mind.
```

- Scene: The apartment as one continuous unhurried morning.
- Shots: Her turning slowly in the middle of the floor, shirt tails moving. A time-lapse of the sun crossing the boards. Butter going onto toast. Her phone face-down and forgotten on the arm of a sofa. Him appearing in the doorway with his hair wrecked.
- Emotion and lighting: The happiest the song gets; wide golden light, no cool tones anywhere.

### [verse 2]

```
Fourth Sunday in a row that I've woken up here,
Which is not an accident, let's be clear.
There's a hair tie on your wrist that is not yours,
And my charger's got a home in your bedside drawer.
```

- Scene: The evidence that this has happened before.
- Shots: A calendar on a wall with four small pencil marks (composited, illegible as text). A black hair tie around his wrist as he reaches for the mug. A bedside drawer sliding open on a charger, a lip balm, a paperback with a folded corner.
- Emotion and lighting: The realisation arriving quietly; warm interior, one shaft of light on the drawer.

```
I nearly ask the question at the counter, then I don't,
I ask about the toast instead and you say, sure, both.
You come in half asleep and you kiss my shoulder,
Not my mouth, my shoulder, and somehow that's bolder.
```

- Scene: The counter, both of them standing.
- Shots: Her mouth opening to say something and closing again; a beat of held silence. Two slices going into the toaster. Him crossing behind her, one hand on her hip in passing, and a kiss on the shoulder through the shirt fabric. Her eyes closing for exactly one beat.
- Emotion and lighting: The most intimate frame in the video and nothing is uncovered; soft window light, shallow focus.

### [pre-chorus 2]

```
There's a watch lying face down where you left it,
There's a list on the fridge and my milk is on it.
Nothing here is tidy, nothing here is planned,
And I've stopped counting mornings on one hand.
```

- Scene: The fridge door.
- Shots: The watch again, same sill, this time with his hand reaching past it for the window latch. A shopping list magnet-pinned to the fridge, two handwritings on it (composited, illegible). Her noticing the second handwriting is hers.
- Emotion and lighting: Quiet astonishment; flat kitchen light, one warm reflection off the fridge door.

### [chorus]

(repeat)

- Scene: The same morning, wider and more lived-in.
- Shots: The two of them eating toast standing up. A record being flipped. Her sitting on the counter, feet swinging, him between her knees, foreheads touching, both fully dressed and laughing.
- Emotion and lighting: Warmer, fuller; the sun higher, the room brighter.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: The apartment doing nothing for sixteen bars.
- Shots: The record turning, macro, the arm tracking in. Steam off two mugs on a windowsill. His shirt on her in a long mirror, seen from behind, her tugging the collar straight. A cat or a plant on the sill, whichever the location gives; something alive and ignored. Her bare feet and his socks side by side on the floor.
- Emotion and lighting: Pure texture, no story; slow drifting camera, warm and grainy.

### [bridge – voice, one piano and the crackle]

```
I fold it on the bed like I'm handing it back,
Sleeves squared and the collar smoothed flat.
You look at the shirt and you look at my face,
And you say, that one is yours now, it lives at your place.
So I don't make a speech and I don't ask for proof,
I just put it back on, and that's the whole truth.
```

- Scene: The bedroom, late morning, the bed unmade.
- Shots: Her folding the shirt on the duvet with real care, squaring the sleeves, smoothing the collar flat. Holding it out to him. Him looking at the shirt, then at her, and shaking his head once. Her face doing something complicated and warm. Then her putting it back on, one button at a time, and neither of them saying anything else.
- Emotion and lighting: The turn; the quietest, softest light in the film, curtains half drawn again.

### [final chorus – strings low, saxophone shadowing]

```
I'm wearing your shirt and nothing else is on my mind,
No plans, no phone, no reason to be on time.
The sunlight's doing slow laps across the floor,
And I'm not thinking past the kitchen door.

It's not a big love song, it's a Sunday and a shine,
Your shirt, my coffee, and the rest of it is fine.
Say my name low, say it one more time,
I'm wearing your shirt and nothing else is on my mind.
```

- Scene: The best part of the day, still indoors.
- Shots: Both of them dancing badly and barely, in the kitchen, no music playing on screen. The shirt in motion. Her laughing with her head back. A slow pull-back to a wide of the whole flat with the two of them small in it.
- Emotion and lighting: Full, settled joy; the brightest the apartment gets.

### [post-chorus]

```
Nothing else, nothing else, nothing else is on my mind,
Just the kettle and the light and you taking your time.
Nothing else, nothing else, nothing else is on my mind,
Your shirt on my shoulders and the whole day mine.
```

- Scene: Four short repeating beats.
- Shots: The kettle, the light on the floor, his hand around a mug, her collar. Cut on the phrase each time, same order, like a refrain.
- Emotion and lighting: Restful; unchanged golden light.

### [outro – the run-out groove]

```
Needle at the end of the record, going round,
Neither of us getting up to fix the sound.
Your shirt on my shoulders, three buttons deep,
And the best part of Sunday is the part we don't speak.
```

- Scene: Back to the first frame.
- Shots: The needle in the run-out groove, macro, still turning. A wide of the two of them on the sofa, her legs across his lap, neither moving to fix it. Final shot: her head back, eyes shut, the shirt collar open, the record scratching softly.
- Emotion and lighting: Complete stillness; late-morning light gone soft and even.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** her turning slowly on the floorboards in the shirt on *"nothing else is on my mind."*
- **The challenge:** the borrowed-shirt cut — post the chorus and invite people to film the item of clothing they never gave back.
- **The caption line:** *"He said keep it. That was the whole conversation."*
- **The quote:** *"Small and ordinary and holy as it gets."*
- **The still:** the folded shirt on the duvet, sleeves squared, being handed back.
