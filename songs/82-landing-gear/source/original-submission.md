# Landing Gear — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
The last hour of a long-distance relationship. Eleven months apart, a night flight home, and the descent. She is on the plane, not waiting at the gate — the whole song happens between the cabin lights coming up and the arrivals doors opening. The landing gear dropping is the moment her body understands before her head does.

**Mood:**
Nervous, bright, physical. Not longing — longing is over. This is the ninety minutes after longing ends and before arriving starts, which is the most alive anyone ever is.

**Message:**
"I got good at missing you and I am putting that skill down forever."

**Genre:**
Widescreen pop with rock guitars. Think: Taylor Swift's stadium pop x The 1975's guitar shimmer, with the piano-driven lift of a Coldplay final chorus.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 108 BPM, 4/4
- Key: D major, bright and open, the guitars ringing in the key's natural voicings
- Final chorus modulates up a whole step to E for the run through arrivals

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead: female, close and tired in the verses, full-throated and joyful in the choruses, near-spoken in the bridge.
- Backing: stacked harmonies from chorus one, a crowd-sized vocal wall on the modulated final chorus, wordless echoes in the outro.

**Instrumentation:**
- Piano (the spine: a countdown note in the intro, hammered eighths in the choruses)
- Two electric guitars: one palm-damped and dry, one wide and overdriven with long delay
- Live drum kit, big room sound, from the first chorus
- Bass guitar, driving, following the kick
- Warm synth pads (intro and outro)
- Low strings under the pre-choruses and the modulation
- Handclaps, tambourine, floor toms
- Ambience: cabin air, a seatbelt chime, the mechanical shudder of the gear, terminal room tone

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: altitude, cabin lights, four hours left on the map
- Verse 1: one aircraft row in close detail, the speech she wrote and hid
- Pre-chorus: descent, the city under the wing, the gear coming down
- Chorus: the release, the biggest sound so far, eleven months named
- Verse 2: the landing, the tarmac wait, the terminal, the moving walkway
- Bridge: the honest accounting of a year of calls
- Final chorus: the run, the dropped bag, modulated
- Outro: over the ocean, the map, the arrival

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – pad, one piano note, cabin air]

```
Cabin lights came up on somewhere dark,
Four hours left on a little plastic map.
I have been counting this down since a January airport,
And the whole year folded into one gray seat.
```

- Scene: A wide-body cabin at night, most passengers asleep.
- Shot: The reading lights coming up in sequence down the aisle, slow dolly along the ceiling line.
- Cut to: A seatback screen — blank in camera, the flight map composited in the edit — a tiny plane crawling over an ocean; then her face lit by it.
- Her reaction / Emotion: Awake, wired, patient in the way only someone at the end of something is patient.
- Lighting: Cabin dim, one overhead spot on her, the screen glow cold on her cheek.

### [verse 1]

```
I know this row by heart, I have flown it in my head,
Twenty-six A, the wing, the same square of sky.
There's a kid asleep on his mother in the aisle,
And a man beside me watching something with the sound off.
I wrote you a speech on the back of a receipt,
Then I read it once and I put it in my shoe.
Because everything I practiced is going to leave my mouth
The second that those doors go the other way.
```

- Scene: The row itself, in unglamorous detail.
- Shots: The wing outside the window against black; a small boy asleep across his mother two seats up; a man's screen flickering with no sound, his headphones unplugged in his lap.
- Cut to: Her hands unfolding a curled receipt covered in tiny handwriting (composited), reading it once, then bending to tuck it into her sneaker under the seat.
- Her reaction / Emotion: Self-aware, half-embarrassed, entirely certain it will not survive contact.
- Lighting: One overhead reading light, everything else in cabin blue.

### [pre-chorus]

```
The seatbelt light, the little chime, the tray table up,
The city coming up like a spilled box of light.
Something in the floor lets go and starts to lower,
And my whole body knows the sound before I do.
```

- Scene: Descent.
- Shots: The seatbelt sign illuminating (composited icon on a blank lit panel); a tray table clicking up; her palm flat on the cabin wall.
- Cut to: The window — a city coming up through cloud, sodium orange in scattered grids; then a low exterior shot of the gear doors opening and the wheels swinging down and locking.
- Her reaction / Emotion: The physical jolt. Her eyes close for one beat on the sound.
- Lighting: Cabin lights down for landing, the window the only bright thing in the frame.

### [chorus – full band, wide]

```
Landing gear down, my heart's coming home,
Eleven months of nothing but a voice on a phone.
Six thousand miles folding into one hallway,
And you at the end of it and nothing in the way.
I have been up here so long I forgot the ground,
Now the wheels find the runway and the year comes down.
Pull the window shade up, let the morning come on,
Landing gear down, my heart's coming home.
```

- Scene: The chorus is not literal. It is the eleven months compressed into eight lines.
- Shots: Fast intercuts — a phone propped on a pillow at three in the morning; a coat on the back of a chair in an empty apartment; a suitcase living permanently open; a calendar with a circled square (composited).
- Cut to: Back to the window, the runway lights streaming past underneath, the wheels touching, the shade going up on a white morning.
- Emotion: Enormous. The first shot in the video with any width to it.
- Lighting: The flashbacks are cold and lamp-lit; the runway is a hard sodium streak; the morning through the shade is blinding.

### [verse 2]

```
We touched and the whole cabin clapped like we had done it,
Then we sat on the tarmac for another twenty minutes.
The aisle stood up too early like the aisle always does,
And for once I was standing with them, bag against my chest.
A booth, a nod, a corridor with nothing on the walls,
A moving floor that I refuse to stand still on.
There's a woman with a placard and a driver on his phone,
And the frosted doors keep opening for other people.
```

- Scene: The landing and the terminal walk. Comedy, then tension.
- Shots: The cabin applauding; a wide of the plane parked in the middle of nowhere with the engines spooling down; the whole aisle standing shoulder to shoulder going nowhere.
- Cut to: A passport booth (all screens and stamps composited), a bare corridor, a moving walkway with her overtaking everyone on it; a woman holding a blank placard; a driver scrolling; the frosted arrivals doors opening and closing on strangers.
- Her reaction / Emotion: Impatience turning into something closer to fear. The last hundred metres are the longest.
- Lighting: Airport fluorescent, flat and unkind, warming only as she gets closer to the doors.

### [pre-chorus 2]

```
The strap in my fist, the escalator taking its time,
A sign says arrivals like it is a normal word.
Somebody hugs somebody just ahead of me,
And I am running before I decide to run.
```

- Scene: The last thirty seconds before the doors.
- Shots: Her fist white on the bag strap; an escalator from a low angle, her feet not staying still on it; an overhead arrivals sign (composited).
- Cut to: A stranger reunion just ahead of her — two people colliding — then her face changing, then her legs.
- Her reaction / Emotion: The decision made in the body first. This is the shot the song exists for.
- Lighting: Fluorescent, then the warm daylight spill from the arrivals hall windows.

### [chorus 2]

```
Landing gear down, my heart's coming home,
Eleven months of nothing but a voice on a phone.
Six thousand miles folding into one hallway,
And you at the end of it and nothing in the way.
I have been up here so long I forgot the ground,
Now the wheels find the runway and the year comes down.
Pull the window shade up, let the morning come on,
Landing gear down, my heart's coming home.
```

- Scene: The run through arrivals, held and stretched.
- Shots: Tracking with her at full speed, bag banging her hip, other passengers blurring past; slow-motion inserts of the strap slipping, a shoe striking tile.
- Cut to: The crowd at the barrier, faces scanning, none of them his; then a gap in them.
- Emotion: Panic and joy running the same speed.
- Lighting: Big daylight windows, lens flare across the frame, everything overexposed at the edges.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: Twenty bars, no lyrics. The year, told properly, in the middle of the run.
- Shots: Two clocks on one wall set to different cities; a video call frozen mid-buffer; her falling asleep on a couch with a phone still lit on her chest; a birthday cake with a laptop propped in front of it.
- Cut to: A filtered drop to near-silence over a single held frame of the arrivals doors, then a tom rebuild over her running again.
- Emotion: The cost, acknowledged, then dropped.
- Lighting: All flashbacks cold and lamp-lit; the return to the present is the brightest cut in the film.

### [bridge – piano and voice, half-time]

```
We did it well, we did the calls, we did the time,
We split the difference on the hours and the sleep.
I learned your weather and you learned mine,
I fell asleep to your morning more nights than I can count.
But nobody ever hugged a screen and got it back,
And I am done being good at missing you.
```

- Scene: She stops. Dead centre of the hall, people flowing around her.
- Shots: A locked-off wide, her completely still in a moving crowd, bag at her feet.
- Cut to: The two clocks again, one of them being taken down off a wall; a weather app for a city that is not hers (composited).
- Her reaction / Emotion: The only still moment in the second half. Grateful and finished with it.
- Lighting: Daylight from above, the crowd motion-blurred, her sharp.

### [final chorus – modulated, biggest sound]

```
Landing gear down, my heart's coming home,
No more goodnight at the wrong end of a phone.
Six thousand miles folding into one hallway,
And your arms in the doorway and nothing in the way.
I dropped the bag before I got to where you stood,
And the year hit the floor and it broke open good.
Pull the window shade up, let the morning come on,
Landing gear down, my heart's coming home.
```

- Scene: The arrival.
- Shots: The gap in the crowd; him, seen properly for the first time in the whole video; the bag hitting the floor mid-stride and staying there.
- Cut to: The collision, held long, both of them turning; the abandoned bag alone on the tile with people stepping around it.
- Emotion: Full release. No restraint left anywhere in the frame.
- Lighting: Blown-out daylight, warm, flares, the most overexposed shot in the film.

### [post-chorus – chanted]

```
Coming home, coming home,
Feet on the floor and I'm not on my own.
Coming home, coming home,
Every mile I flew was a mile of the way home.
```

- Scene: The rest of the hall, unbothered, carrying on.
- Shots: A wide from high above: two people motionless in the middle of a moving terminal.
- Cut to: Small details — her feet off the ground; his hand on the back of her head; someone nearby smiling and looking away.
- Emotion: Public and private at once.
- Lighting: Same big daylight, softened as the camera pulls back.

### [outro – pad and piano, calm]

```
Somewhere over the ocean I stopped being scared,
Somewhere over the ocean the year let go.
The little plane on the little map is gone now,
And I'm standing where the map was trying to go.
```

- Scene: One last return to the plane, then out.
- Shots: The dark cabin from the intro, her asleep against the window, the map on the seatback with the little plane most of the way across (composited).
- Cut to: The final shot — the two of them walking out of the terminal into daylight, the bag now carried by him, the doors closing behind them, locked-off, held through the fade.
- Emotion: Settled. Nothing left owed.
- Lighting: Cabin blue, then clean white daylight through the exit doors.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the exterior shot of the gear doors opening and the wheels locking down, cut hard to *"landing gear down, my heart's coming home."*
- **The caption line:** *"I am done being good at missing you."*
- **The challenge:** the dropped bag — invite long-distance couples to post their own arrivals-hall footage, phone-shot and shaky, cut to the modulated final chorus.
- **The quote card:** *"Nobody ever hugged a screen and got it back."*
- **The shareable ten seconds:** the still, locked-off bridge shot of her motionless in a moving crowd.
