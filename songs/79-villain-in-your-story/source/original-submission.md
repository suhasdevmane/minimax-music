# Villain in Your Story — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A woman arrives at a London gala where a version of her has got there first. He has been telling the story all week and in his telling she is ruthless, cold, the one who burned it down. She does not argue with the casting. She takes it, wears it in a black dress on a marble staircase under red light, and points out the only detail he left out: the narrator of that story is the man who lost.

**Mood:**
Dark, controlled, dry. Not furious — amused, and slightly bored of being described. British register throughout: the wit is understatement, and the hardest lines are delivered flattest.

**Message:**
"Every story has a narrator. In his, I'm the villain. In mine, I'm the lead."

**Genre:**
Dark pop with cinematic scale and a rap-sung second verse. Think: RAYE x Billie Eilish x Little Simz — a half-time groove, staccato choir hits, a sub-heavy cinematic bass, and one verse where the singing stops and the bars start.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 98 BPM, 4/4
- Key: D minor throughout, no modulation
- The chorus is deliberately slow-burning rather than explosive: it should read as an entrance, not an eruption.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, British delivery, low and dry in the verses, held rather than belted in the choruses, almost spoken in the bridge.
- **Verse two is rap-sung by the same lead** — tight sixteenth-note phrasing, internal rhyme landing on the offbeats, fast and precise, pitched but spoken in feel. No male feature; this is her verse.
- Backing vocals: staccato choir hits on the chorus downbeats, a wordless low chant beneath the hook, stacked harmonies doubling in the second chorus, the choir returning one voice at a time through the bridge.

**Instrumentation:**
- Deep distorted cinematic bass (drone in the intro, moving line under every chorus)
- Single struck piano
- Staccato choir, building to a full crescendo in the break
- Pizzicato and sustained strings
- Timpani and a brass swell
- Finger snaps and tight side-stick, with a hard trap hat pattern under the rap verse
- Ambience: a room of conversation cutting to silence, camera shutters, a heel on marble

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: the reputation that arrived before she did
- Verse 1: the polite room, and what the actual crime was
- Pre-chorus: the casting accepted, and the mirror on the landing
- Chorus: the hook, wide and slow, an entrance
- Verse 2: the rap-sung verse — the briefing, the maths, the crown and the bank
- Bridge: who is holding the pen
- Final chorus: fullest arrangement, controlled triumph
- Outro: heels in hand, sunrise over the river

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – bass drone, one struck piano note, choir stab every two bars]

```
There's a version of me round Mayfair tonight,
Doing terrible things in somebody's mouth.
Apparently I'm ruthless, apparently I'm ice,
Apparently I burned a perfectly good house.
Black dress zipped, called a cab off the Strand,
Walked in on time with a drink in my hand.
```

- Scene: London at night, and a gala already in progress.
- Shots: A wet Mayfair street from a moving black cab, lit windows going past. Inside a townhouse: small clusters of people talking, one of them clearly telling a story, hands moving, faces leaning in.
- Cut to: A dressing mirror, a black dress being zipped from behind by her own hand, gold at her ears, her looking herself over once and being satisfied.
- Emotion: Amusement. She has heard the story and she is not going to correct it.
- Lighting: Deep blue street, warm interiors, one hard practical over the mirror.

### [verse 1 – half-time snaps, side-stick, sub-bass]

```
They're polite when they think you can't hear,
Same room, same champagne, same knives.
I was quiet for years, I made myself small,
Then I asked what I'm worth and I ruined our lives.
```

- Scene: The party, from her point of view.
- Shots: A tray of champagne moving through a crowd. Two people mid-sentence going quiet as she passes and starting again after. A wide of the room where every face is turned very slightly the same way.
- Emotion: Cold clarity. She hears all of it and keeps walking.
- Lighting: Warm chandeliers with a hard red uplight creeping in from the staircase.

```
That's the charge sheet, if you want it read,
I stopped apologising for the room that I fill.
I never took a thing that wasn't mine,
I just stopped giving mine away, and I never will.
```

- Scene: A flashback, cool and flat, and a return.
- Shots: Two seconds, desaturated: her in a meeting room saying a number out loud and the room's temperature dropping. Her signing something. Her walking out of an office at night with a box.
- Cut back to the party: her taking a drink from the tray and not thanking anybody for it.
- Emotion: The crime, stated plainly, and it is not a crime.
- Lighting: Flashback fluorescent and grey; present warm and red.

### [pre-chorus]

```
Put me in the black hat, put me in the wrong,
Give the tragic bit to you, I'll take the song.
There's a mirror on the landing, it doesn't say cruel,
It says girl who finally stopped losing to you.
```

- Scene: The turn of the staircase.
- Shots: Her stopping at the half-landing in front of a tall old mirror. Her reflection, held. A slow push in on the reflection until, for two frames only, the light on her hair reads as a crown — never a prop, always an effect of the chandelier behind her.
- Emotion: Recognition, not vanity.
- Lighting: Chandelier from behind, red uplight from below, her face the only warm thing.

### [chorus – choir hits, cinematic bass, an entrance not an eruption]

```
Tell it how you tell it, I'll allow it,
Paint me in the red light, make it loud.
Every story needs a shadow at the party,
And I wear it better than you wore the crown.
I'll be the villain in your story, the hero in mine,
Same girl, two books, and I'm fine with the line.
I'll be the villain in your story, the hero in mine.
```

- Scene: The descent.
- Shots: Her coming down the marble staircase in red light, slow, one hand on the rail. The room below turning toward her in waves. Camera flashes from the press line at the bottom, going off out of rhythm. Her shadow thrown huge up the wall behind her.
- Emotion: Total composure. She never speeds up.
- Lighting: Red uplight on marble, white flash punctuation, deep shadow everywhere else.

### [verse 2 – rap-sung, snaps, trap hats, bass alone]

```
Cameras on the staircase, I don't blink, don't dip,
Red light on the marble, got a smirk on my lip.
Briefing all week, got a script, got a spin,
Mate in the corner doing PR for your sin.
Call me calculating, that's a compliment, mate,
Did the maths on my worth and sent you the rate.
You liked me when I whispered, loved me when I shrank,
Now the girl in the glass has a crown and a bank.
Spell it properly when you take my name in vain,
Villain's just a woman who declined the blame.
```

- Scene: The floor of the party, moving fast, cut on the sixteenths.
- Shots: Flashbulbs freezing her face mid-blink and her not blinking. A man in a corner talking to three people, cropped at the chin, gesturing at the staircase. Her walking straight through the middle of the room. A close-up of a smirk held one beat too long. Her signature going onto something in a side room while the party carries on through a doorway behind her.
- Emotion: Fast, precise, unbothered. The only section where she moves quickly.
- Lighting: Hard flashes over red, high contrast, near-black shadow.

### [pre-chorus 2]

```
Put me in the black hat, keep me in the wrong,
Give the tragic bit to you, I've got the song.
There's a mirror on the landing, it doesn't say cruel,
It says girl who finally stopped bleeding for you.
```

- Scene: The mirror again, later.
- Shots: The same half-landing, the same mirror, the party louder behind her. The reflection, and this time she holds her own eye contact without the push-in.
- Emotion: Settled. The first time was recognition; this is confirmation.
- Lighting: Same setup, one stop brighter.

### [chorus]

(repeat)

- Scene: Repeat of the descent, from below.
- Shots: Low angle from the foot of the stairs looking up as she comes down. The press line from behind her. The room parting without anyone deciding to.
- Emotion: Ownership of the room.
- Lighting: More saturated red, more flash.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: The party at full noise and her outside it, no lyrics.
- Shots: The room from a balcony above, everyone moving, nobody looking up. Her stepping out onto a stone terrace with the door closing on the sound. A single drink set down on a balustrade. The city over the rooftops. One bar of complete silence: her face in profile, the party a warm rectangle behind glass.
- Lighting: Red interior seen through glass, cold blue exterior, one warm window.

### [bridge – piano and voice, calm and almost spoken]

```
Every story's got a narrator and it's you,
Course I come out badly when you hold the pen.
Stayed small and grateful, I'd be lovely in your book,
And I'd be sat here with nothing all over again.
Write it. Print it. Put my name in bold.
Read worse about myself and slept just fine.
Some of us are villains for an hour in one telling,
And the lead in every other, all of the time.
```

- Scene: The terrace, alone.
- Shots: Her leaning on the balustrade with her back to the party, close-up, no red light on her at all for the first time. A hand-held camera drifting slightly, the only unsteady shot in the video. Her looking back through the glass at the man telling the story, with something close to sympathy.
- Emotion: The song's only soft moment, and it is directed at him.
- Lighting: Cold blue, one warm spill from the doorway.

### [final chorus – fullest arrangement, controlled triumph]

```
Tell it how you tell it, I'll allow it,
Paint me in the red light, make it loud.
Every story needs a shadow on the staircase,
And I wear it better than you wore the crown.
I'll be the villain in your story, the hero in mine,
Call me cold, call me clever, call it what you like,
I'll be the villain in your story, the hero in mine.
```

- Scene: Back in, and out the front door.
- Shots: Her walking back through the party without stopping. The staircase behind her, empty, still lit red. Her collecting her coat. The front door opening onto a blue London street and the noise cutting off behind it.
- Emotion: Finished with the evening on her own schedule.
- Lighting: Red into blue, the hardest colour cut in the video.

### [post-chorus – bare bass and snaps, chant]

```
Villain, villain, say it like a curse,
Been called a lot of things and villain isn't worse.
Villain, villain, in your telling of the night,
Hero in mine, and I'm sleeping alright.
```

- Scene: The street.
- Shots: Cut on the snaps — a heel on wet pavement, a cab light, a coat pulled closed, her reflection in a dark shop window walking level with her.
- Emotion: Unhurried, private, done.
- Lighting: Sodium and blue, wet ground, no red.

### [outro – drone, one voice, sunrise]

```
Black cab down the Strand and the city looking new,
Heels in my hand and the sky going blue.
Somewhere in your version I'm still doing the crime,
And I'm the hero in mine.
```

- Scene: Dawn, going home.
- Shots: The back seat of a black cab going down the Strand, the city getting lighter. Her barefoot on a bridge with her heels hooked on two fingers. Final shot: her walking away from camera over the bridge into early sun, the city ahead of her, nobody else in frame.
- Emotion: Peace, and a private joke she is still enjoying.
- Lighting: The first daylight in the video, cold and clean turning gold.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the two frames on the half-landing where the chandelier reads as a crown in the mirror, on *"the hero in mine."*
- **The knife line:** *"Villain's just a woman who declined the blame."*
- **The caption line:** *"Same girl, two books, and I'm fine with the line."*
- **The challenge:** the staircase descent — one slow walk down any stairs in red light, cut on the first chorus, captioned with whatever people have been called.
- **The quote:** *"Course I come out badly when you hold the pen."*
