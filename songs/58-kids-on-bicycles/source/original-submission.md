# Kids on Bicycles — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
The summer four children were nine years old and four streets were the
entire world. Told from adulthood but almost never from adulthood's
vocabulary: the verses stay inside the summer, and only the bridge steps
outside it, in a car, in the rain, years later.

**Mood:**
Warm, unhurried, and then quietly devastating. Indie folk with a group
vocal, not a children's song — the register is an adult remembering
precisely, without softening anything.

**Message:**
"We didn't know it was the best of it. We just knew it was ours."

**Genre:**
Indie folk with fingerpicked acoustic guitar, glockenspiel and a small
string section. Think: The Lumineers x Fleet Foxes x Gregory Alan Isakov —
group vocals, real room, no polish where polish would lie.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 92 BPM, 4/4
- Key: C major
- No modulation. The lift is the string section entering fully on the final
  chorus and the group vocal getting bigger every time it comes round.

**Vocal setup:**
- Singer A (female lead) throughout, solo on all verses and the bridge.
- A communal group vocal sings the hook under the lead in every chorus, and
  grows each time; a low male voice joins from the second chorus.
- Wordless group vocal in the instrumental build.

**Instrumentation:**
- Fingerpicked steel-string acoustic (the spine, first bar to last)
- Warm upright-sounding bass
- Second strummed acoustic, choruses only
- Glockenspiel: single notes in the verses, the melody in the break
- Small string section, cello first, then violins
- Brushed snare and soft floor tom, the only drums; tambourine on the last
  chorus
- Mandolin-style tremolo layer from the second chorus
- Textures: sprinkler, screen door, birdsong, a card ticking in a spoke, a
  bicycle bell, a freewheel, a far-off ice cream truck, rain on a windscreen

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: cut grass, a hose, a screen door, a street waking up
- Verse 1: the ramp, the crash, the hose, the dog, the ice cream truck
- Pre-chorus: chalk, sprinkler, two dollars, a father whistling
- Chorus: the hook, group vocal, strings enter
- Verse 2: the four houses they were going to buy, and the moving truck
- Instrumental: glockenspiel and violin, a build, then one sustained note
- Bridge: present day, a car, spring rain, the street now
- Final chorus: fullest arrangement, and a hope rather than a memory
- Post-chorus: the chant, hand percussion
- Outro: back to the opening figure, four bikes on their sides, gold light

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – one guitar, summer ambience, no drums]

```
Cut grass and hot tar and a hose in the sun,
Screen door banging and the day just begun.
Somebody's mother calling somebody in,
And the whole street waking up again.
```

- Scene: A suburban dead-end street, early on a hot morning in the
  nineteen-nineties.
- Shots: A sprinkler ticking across a lawn in macro; heat shimmer over black
  tar; a garden hose lying in the grass still running; a screen door swinging
  shut on its spring; a wide of the street with four bikes leaning on four
  different fences.
- Emotion: Anticipation. Nothing has happened yet and everything is about to.
- Lighting: Hard early sun, deep green grass, blown highlights on the tar.

### [verse 1 – the ramp]

```
We built a ramp from a plank and a brick,
Argued an hour over who would go quick.
I went last and I went too slow,
Took the gravel on the drive below.
```

- Scene: A driveway. Four nine-year-olds and one bad idea.
- Shots: A plank balanced on a stack of bricks; four kids arguing with their
  whole bodies; one bike hitting the ramp too slowly and stopping halfway;
  gravel and a knee; a hand offered down into frame.
- Emotion: Comic seriousness. These are the most important negotiations of
  their lives.
- Lighting: Overhead midday sun, short shadows, glare off concrete.

```
Nobody cried and nobody told,
Ran it under the hose and called it cold.
The dog two doors down knew all four of us by sound,
And the ice cream truck was the only clock in town.
```

- Scene: The repair, and the geography of the street.
- Shots: A knee held under a running hose; a face doing an excellent job of
  not crying; a dog behind a chain-link fence going off at a specific
  freewheel sound; heads turning in unison at a chime two streets away; four
  bikes leaving frame at speed in the same direction.
- Emotion: Loyalty, expressed entirely through not telling anyone.
- Lighting: Sun through hose spray, small rainbow, no filter needed.

### [pre-chorus 1]

```
Chalk on the driveway and a sprinkler on the lawn,
Two dollars between four of us and the whole day long.
Somebody's father whistling at the door,
And we knew what it meant and we rode one more.
```

- Scene: The economy and the law of the street.
- Shots: Chalk on concrete, hands filthy with it; four kids running through
  a sprinkler fully dressed; coins counted out on a kerb; a man in a doorway
  putting two fingers in his mouth and whistling; four kids hearing it and
  deliberately doing one more lap.
- Emotion: Small, delicious disobedience.
- Lighting: Late afternoon, longer shadows, gold beginning.

### [chorus 1 – group vocal, strings enter]

```
We were kids on bicycles, racing the streetlights home,
Handlebars and hollering, and nobody rode alone.
Cards in the spokes making engines out of air,
Nine years old and the whole wide world was four streets square.
We were kids on bicycles, and the only rule we knew
Was be up on the porch by the time the yellow came through.
We didn't know it was the best of it, we just knew it was ours,
We were kids on bicycles, racing the streetlights home.
```

- Scene: The ride. This is the shot everyone will remember.
- Shots: Four bikes abreast down the middle of an empty road at dusk, filmed
  from the front at knee height; a playing card clipped to a fork with a
  clothes peg, macro, ticking; hands off handlebars for exactly two seconds;
  a streetlight beginning to warm up from orange to full; the four of them
  hitting a porch at a dead run.
- Emotion: Total joy, and the camera never gets ahead of them.
- Lighting: Golden hour into blue, the streetlights the only artificial
  source and only just coming on.

### [verse 2 – the plan and the truck]

```
We swore we'd buy four houses in a row,
Cut a gate in every fence so we could go
Through each other's back yards for the rest of our lives,
And nobody laughed at it, nobody thought twice.
```

- Scene: The plan, made in complete seriousness.
- Shots: Four kids sitting on a kerb pointing at four different houses; a
  finger drawing a gate in the air on a fence panel; chalk on a fence marking
  where the gate would go; four faces, all of them meaning it.
- Emotion: Sincerity with no irony available yet.
- Lighting: Warm, low, backlit.

```
Then a moving truck came in the last week of June,
And a bike went in the back of it too soon.
We rode three abreast and the gap was loud,
And nobody said it, and we rode round and round.
```

- Scene: The end of it.
- Shots: A removal truck on the street, ramp down; a small bike being lifted
  into the back and set on its side; a hand on a car window from the inside;
  then three bikes riding abreast with an obvious empty space on the left;
  the same corner taken again and again as the light fails.
- Emotion: Grief without a single word for it. Nobody in this sequence
  speaks.
- Lighting: Overcast for the truck, then dusk. The only section of the
  summer that is not sunlit.

### [pre-chorus 2]

```
Chalk washing off in the rain on the lawn,
Three bikes on the corner and a fourth one gone.
Somebody's father whistling at the door,
And we came in early and we didn't know what for.
```

- Scene: The first rain of the season.
- Shots: Chalk drawings running in rainwater down a driveway; three bikes on
  a corner in the wet; the same man in the same doorway whistling; three kids
  coming in immediately, which they have never done before.
- Emotion: The summer ending, felt but not understood.
- Lighting: Flat grey, wet concrete, warm light from inside the houses.

### [chorus 2]

- Scene: The ride again, but with the space in it.
- Shots: The same front-facing knee-height frame with three riders instead
  of four; the card in the spoke; the streetlight coming on; the porch, three
  pairs of shoes.
- Emotion: The same joy with something missing that nobody names.
- Lighting: Dusk, cooler than chorus one.

### [instrumental – glockenspiel, violin, build]

- Scene: The summer as a montage, no lyrics.
- Shots: A bike wheel spinning upside down on the grass, slow motion; a
  bicycle bell struck twice; sun through a spinning spoke pattern; the street
  from directly above with four bikes lying on their sides in a rough circle;
  everything dropping to a single wide of the empty road as one note holds.
- Lighting: The most beautiful two shots of the video live here: the spoke
  flare and the overhead.

### [bridge – present day, no drums, rain]

```
I drove down that street in the spring, in the rain,
Slowed to a crawl and I sat there again.
The ramp is long gone and the fences came down,
And the streetlight is white and it burns all night now.
But I know the sound of a card in a spoke,
And a part of me is nine, and she never came in.
```

- Scene: Now. A car, spring rain, the same street.
- Shots: A windscreen with a wiper crossing it, the street beyond in soft
  focus; adult Mahima in the driver's seat, engine off, hands still on the
  wheel; the driveway where the ramp was, plain concrete; the fences between
  the four gardens gone, one continuous lawn; a white LED streetlight, on in
  daylight; a single flash frame of a card in a spoke.
- Emotion: The only section from outside the summer. Underplay it — no
  crying, one long look.
- Lighting: Grey spring daylight, rain on glass, the cold white of the new
  streetlight.

### [final chorus – fullest]

- Scene: Back inside the memory, and the video does not apologise for it.
- Shots: The four-abreast ride restored, all four riders, at full golden
  hour; the group vocal visualised as the whole street's kids joining the
  ride, ten bikes, then twenty; a wide crane of the road full of children on
  bicycles; the streetlight coming on over all of them.
- Emotion: Hope rather than memory. This is why the last chorus changes its
  words.
- Lighting: The brightest, warmest gold of the video.

### [post-chorus – chant]

```
Ride till the yellow comes on,
Ride till somebody calls,
Ride like the summer is long,
Ride like it never ends at all.
```

- Scene: The chant, hand percussion, fast cuts.
- Shots: Feet on pedals; a bell; a card in a spoke; a porch light coming on;
  a screen door held open.
- Lighting: Gold into blue.

### [outro – back to the opening figure]

```
Cut grass and hot tar and a hose in the sun,
Four bikes on their sides where the pavement runs.
The light on the corner is warming to gold,
And nobody's going in, and nobody's old.
```

- Scene: The street at the very end of a day, empty of people.
- Shots: The sprinkler again; four bikes lying on their sides on a verge,
  wheels still turning slightly; the corner streetlight warming to gold, the
  old orange kind; final shot, a locked-off wide of the empty street in gold
  light with the four bikes in it and children's voices audible off frame.
  Hold until the audio fades.
- Emotion: Not sad. Held.
- Lighting: The last gold of the day, warm and long.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** four bikes abreast down the middle of the road at
  dusk, shot from the front at knee height.
- **The challenge:** the card in the spoke — post a childhood-street photo
  or the sound of a card ticking in a wheel, with the chorus over it.
- **The caption line:** *"We didn't know it was the best of it, we just knew
  it was ours."*
- **The knife line:** *"We rode three abreast and the gap was loud."*
- **The quote:** *"And a part of me is nine, and she never came in."*
- **The two images that will travel on their own:** sun flaring through a
  spinning spoke pattern, and the overhead of four bikes lying on their sides
  in a circle.
