# That Summer Song — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A song that was inescapable one summer comes on again years later over a supermarket ceiling speaker, and the woman standing in the freezer aisle is nineteen for three minutes. It is not about wanting him back. It is about wanting the version of herself who lived inside that summer, and finding out she never lost her.

**Mood:**
Warm, bright, aching. Sunlight remembered under fluorescent light. Happy-sad in the specific way a chorus you loved at nineteen is happy-sad at twenty-six.

**Message:**
"I don't want you back. I want the girl who thought a song could hold the world, and she is still here."

**Genre:**
Nostalgia pop with bright chorused guitars and claps. Think: Taylor Swift x The 1975 x Maggie Rogers — summer-radio guitars, a modern pop low end, and a lyric that names objects instead of feelings.

**Tempo & Key:**
110 BPM, 4/4, C major, open and singable in a car. The final chorus lifts with strings and a soft horn pad rather than a modulation; the post-chorus harmonies sit a third higher.

**Vocal setup:**
Lead: young adult female, conversational and close in the verses, full and slightly raspy in the choruses. Backing: stacked harmonies through every chorus, widest on the last; a hummed melody line closes the outro.

**Instrumentation:**
- Bright chorused electric guitar (the spine)
- Ringing twelve-string doubling the choruses
- Round warm electric bass
- Hand claps and tambourine
- Soft kick, brushed shaker, rim click
- Wide analogue synth pads; electric piano on verse two
- Strings and soft horn pad on the final chorus only
- Acoustic guitar in the outro
- Ambient: fluorescent hum, trolley wheels, late-shop murmur, tape flutter on the memory sections

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: the aisle, the ice, four chords from the ceiling
- Verse one: the summer in objects — borrowed car, stuck disc, blue slush
- Pre-chorus: the present pulls at her sleeve and she refuses to leave
- Chorus: both times at once, memory and freezer light in the same bar
- Verse two: the life she has now, and the wedding she scrolled past
- Bridge: years of skipping the song, then the night she lets it finish
- Final chorus: she stops reaching backwards, because the girl is already here
- Outro: out through the doors, humming, into the car, gone

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro — clean guitar, fluorescent hum, no drums]

```
Aisle five, quarter past nine,
A bag of ice going soft in my hand,
Four chords come down through the ceiling,
And the cold floor turns into sand.
```

- Scene: A late-night supermarket, chest freezers, white light, almost nobody in.
- Shot: Extreme close-up of a bag of ice sweating in her hand, condensation over her knuckles, then a slow tilt to her face as she hears it.
- Cut to: The ceiling speaker grille, dust on it, held two seconds too long. Then the vinyl floor becoming sand under her shoes.
- Emotion: Recognition landing before she can defend against it.
- Lighting: Flat cold fluorescent, green in the shadows.

### [verse 1 — warm, the borrowed car]

```
That June we had a borrowed car and thirty dollars,
No air conditioning, so we rode with the windows down.
One disc stuck in the player since the winter before,
Same eleven songs carried us out of that town.
You knew the second verse before the radio did,
I learned it off your mouth at dusk.
Blue slush on our teeth, sunscreen on the seatbelt,
A whole July that nobody could rush.
```

- Scene: Hard match cut into summer. A two-lane road, heat shimmer, a beat-up sedan.
- Shot: Her arm out flat riding the airflow; the dashboard with a disc jammed half out of the slot and a finger jabbing eject to no effect.
- Cut to: A gas station forecourt at dusk — his mouth in close-up shaping the words, her mouthing them a half-second behind. Blue tongues, a greasy seatbelt buckle, the car parked on gravel with both doors open.
- Emotion: Broke, free, unhurried, invincible.
- Lighting: Blown-out gold, lens flare, tape flutter on the grade. He is never fully in frame.

### [pre-chorus 1 — snap back, claps enter]

```
Somebody's cart, somebody's kid asking for cereal,
A green apron stacking bags of ice.
I could pay and walk out to the lot,
But I stand in the cold and I don't think twice.
```

- Scene: Present, hard cut, no transition.
- Shot: A trolley wheels through the foreground; a child's arm tugs a sleeve out of focus; a shop worker in a green apron loads the freezer chest behind her.
- Cut to: Her hand on the trolley handle, not moving. Her feet, planted. A push-in on her face.
- Emotion: Choosing, knowingly, to stay inside the feeling.
- Lighting: The green apron is the only colour in the frame.

### [chorus 1 — the two worlds cut on the bar]

```
They're playing that summer song and I'm right back there,
Salt on the dashboard, wind doing what it wants with my hair,
Same cheap speakers, same wound-down glass,
Same three long minutes we thought would last.
Half of me is holding a basket in the light,
Half of me is nineteen and out of my mind,
I don't want you back, I want the girl
Who thought a song could hold the world.
```

- Scene: Aisle and road cut together, one bar each, getting faster.
- Shot: Salt crust on a black dashboard; hair whipping at speed; a blown paper speaker cone vibrating; glass wound fully into the door.
- Cut to: The hero image — a wide of her alone in the aisle while her reflection in the freezer glass is the nineteen-year-old, dancing. Then high and wide: the whole shop, one woman in it.
- Emotion: Total surrender, and joy inside it. This is the thesis.
- Lighting: Summer overexposed, aisle cold; the reflection is warm and she is not.

### [verse 2 — the good man, the wedding]

```
A good man's in the lot with my keys in his hand,
Texting his mother, warm, and nothing at all like you.
He wouldn't know this song if it played all night,
And I love that about him, and it stings a little too.
Somebody posted your wedding last fall and I scrolled it,
Tapped a heart with my thumb and I slept fine.
Funny how a chorus knows where to find me,
Right between the frozen peas and the wine.
```

- Scene: Through the shop window to the parking lot, then a flashback to a phone in bed.
- Shot: A man leaning on a car, keys looped on one finger, thumbing a message, entirely at ease. He has a face; he is the present. Her watching him through the glass with real affection, her reflection laid over him.
- Cut to: A wedding post on a screen, no clear faces, her thumb tapping a heart and scrolling on. Then present, symmetrical: she stands dead centre between frozen peas and a shelf of wine.
- Emotion: Love that is calm, and the small grief of calm. Genuine peace, ambushed anyway.
- Lighting: Warm sodium outside, cold white inside, the window a hard line between them.

### [pre-chorus 2 — the ice going]

```
The ice is going soft and there's water on my sleeve,
The girl in the green apron says the doors close at ten.
A whole life waiting under the streetlight,
And I'm standing in this aisle being nineteen again.
```

- Scene: Time pressure entering the frame.
- Shot: Meltwater running off the bag down her forearm, soaking her cuff, dripping to the floor.
- Cut to: The shop worker gesturing at a clock; then through the automatic doors, the man under a streetlight, patient, the brightest thing in frame.
- Emotion: Caught between two good things.
- Lighting: The doors are the exit and the light source at once.

### [chorus 2 — deeper in]

```
They're playing that summer song and I'm right back there,
Salt on the dashboard, wind doing what it wants with my hair,
Same cheap speakers, same wound-down glass,
Same three long minutes we thought would last.
Half of me is holding a basket in the light,
Half of me is nineteen and out of my mind,
I don't want you back, I want the girl
Who thought a song could hold the world.
```

- Scene: The same structure, but the summer footage is now things she had forgotten.
- Shot: A motel pool at midnight; the two of them asleep in the back seat at a rest stop at dawn; a hand writing on a paper cup on the car roof.
- Cut to: A bonfire on gravel, sparks going up, him only a silhouette. Present: the reflection spins and, for the first time, her real foot moves.
- Emotion: Enjoying it rather than surviving it.
- Lighting: The summer grade gets warmer and grainier with each cut.

### [instrumental — guitar solo, then one bar of shop tone]

- Scene: The shop empties out around her.
- Shot: A long dolly down the whole freezer aisle at her walking pace, the doors fogging one by one as she passes.
- Cut to: The shop worker wheeling the empty pallet away; her sitting on the edge of a low display with the basket on the floor, eyes closed, listening; an overhead of the whole shop floor with one person in it.
- Final beat: One bar of near silence, the overheads dropping a bank, a static close-up of her face and nothing but fluorescent hum.
- Lighting: Cold, and one degree darker than anywhere else in the video.

### [bridge — half-time, guitar and voice, then the turn]

```
For years I skipped it, thumb on the button before the drums,
Changed the station, left the room, walked out.
Tonight I stood in the freezer light and let it finish,
And nothing broke, and the ice machine hummed some more.
It was never your song, it was never even ours,
It was cheap gas, long light, the girl I used to be,
And she's not gone, she's only quieter now,
She's out in the lot with a good man, waiting on me.
```

- Scene: Every previous avoidance, then the breaking of the habit.
- Shot: A thumb hitting skip on a steering wheel; a hand twisting a kitchen radio dial; her leaving a bar mid-song with her coat half on. Then a locked wide where she does not move at all.
- Cut to: The ice machine's compressor, vibrating, ordinary, indifferent. The reflection stops dancing and looks straight out at her; then the reflection is simply her, present day.
- Emotion: Relief. Not triumph, relief.
- Lighting: Single overhead bank, hard shadows, warming as the drums return.

### [final chorus — strings, horns, the biggest harmonies]

```
They're playing that summer song and I'm right back there,
Salt on the dashboard, wind doing what it wants with my hair,
Same cheap speakers, same wound-down glass,
Same three long minutes we thought would last.
All of me is standing in the freezer light,
And the girl I've been missing has been here the whole time,
I don't want you back, I have got the girl
Who thought a song could hold the world.
```

- Scene: She walks the length of the shop with the song at full size.
- Shot: Tracking backwards ahead of her, ice bag swinging; one last half-second summer cut; the ceiling speaker shot heroically from below.
- Cut to: Her stopping to look at her own present-day reflection on purpose; then the checkout, unloading the basket, laughing once at herself.
- Emotion: Owning it. No tears anywhere in this section.
- Lighting: The shop is graded one degree warmer than it has been all video, the only cheat in the piece.

### [post-chorus — chant over claps]

```
Turn it up, turn it up over the frozen aisles,
Let it play, let it play to the end of the line,
I'm not sad, I'm not sorry, I'm standing here
With one whole summer of mine.
```

- Scene: The supermarket as a stage for four bars.
- Shot: Symmetrical wide, dead centre of the aisle, her walking straight at camera with the paper bag.
- Cut to: A low angle past the ice chest, fluorescents streaking overhead; a final nod to the ceiling speaker.
- Emotion: Playful, unembarrassed.
- Lighting: Clean and bright, no green anywhere.

### [outro — clean guitar, hum, stop]

```
Out through the doors with a paper bag on my arm,
Humming the second verse under the lot lights,
He asks what took so long, I tell him nothing much,
Just a song I used to know, and I get in.
```

- Scene: Out of the building, across the lot, into the car.
- Shot: The automatic doors parting, warm night air, her hair moving for the first time in a present-day frame.
- Cut to: Her crossing the lot humming, bag on her hip; him opening the passenger door; her shrugging as she gets in.
- Final frame: Locked-off wide of the lot, the car pulling out, a different song faintly on their radio. No text.
- Emotion: Content, and unfinished in a good way.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the freezer glass holding two reflections, her now and her at nineteen, under *"Half of me is nineteen and out of my mind."*
- **The caption line:** *"I don't want you back, I want the girl who thought a song could hold the world."*
- **The challenge:** the aisle-freeze — stop dead wherever you are the second your summer song comes on, then cut to a photo from that summer.
- **The quote card:** *"It was never your song, it was never even ours."*
- **The comment bait:** the peas-and-wine frame — name the song that does this to you, and the year.
