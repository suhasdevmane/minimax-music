# Sugar on My Tongue — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A cheeky, sweet-tooth flirt in a candy-coloured diner after hours. She talks about him like dessert and makes him earn it: he pretends he doesn't do dessert, she catches him eyeing the cherry, and by the end of the night he's passed every small test — held the door, let her have the last sip, didn't rush — and she decides he's earned a taste. Playful, confident, PG-13.

**Mood:**
Sugar rush. Bouncy, bright, grinning. She is in charge of the pace the whole time and enjoys it.

**Message:**
"You're sweet, and I've got a sweet tooth — but sugar, you've got to earn it."

**Genre:**
Bouncy dance-pop with a retro-diner twist. Think Dua Lipa x Doja Cat's playful side x a pinch of Meghan Trainor's diner-pop bounce, with kiss-sound percussion and bass drops.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 118 BPM, 4/4
- Key: C minor (cheeky, groovy)
- Final chorus lifts to the relative major, E♭, for the sugar-rush payoff.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, teasing and conversational in the verses, big and bright in the choruses, half-spoken and grinning in the bridge.
- Backing vocals: stacked call-and-response in the post-chorus chant ("sweet tooth, sweet tooth"), layered harmonies on the final chorus, a filtered "sugar" vocal chop in the instrumental.

**Instrumentation:**
- Round, bouncing synth bass (the spine) with sub-bass drops on the chorus
- Four-on-the-floor kick with kiss-sound percussion on the backbeat
- Plucky bright synth lead and stabbing chord synths
- Clean retro rhythm guitar, wah guitar in verse 2, a jukebox-style guitar lick in the break
- Finger snaps, handclaps, tom fills
- Ear candy: jukebox hum, straw slurp, cherry-stem pop, cash-register ding, neon buzz

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: after hours, jukebox humming, he walks in
- Verse 1: the booth, the milkshake, the lie about dessert
- Pre-chorus: the count to three
- Chorus: sugar on my tongue, sweet tooth tonight, but you've got to earn it
- Verse 2: he slides closer, the napkin, the fries, the closing sign, she makes him wait
- Bridge: the verdict, he passed the test
- Final chorus: the lift, she gives it away on her terms
- Outro: neon dims, she walks out with him

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – jukebox hum, walking bass, snaps]

```
Cherry on the counter, neon in the glass,
Jukebox humming something slow and I'm not gonna ask.
You walked in like Friday with your collar turned up,
Sit down, honey, I'm about to fill your cup.
```

- Scene: A candy-pink diner after closing. Chrome stools, a lit jukebox, one waitress wiping down at the far end.
- Shot: A single maraschino cherry on the counter, pink neon reflected in a glass, then her at the counter in a red top, red lips, twirling the cherry stem.
- Cut to: The door chime. He walks in, collar up, shakes off the cold. She doesn't turn around; she watches him in the mirror behind the counter.
- Her reaction: A slow smile at the mirror. She taps the stool next to her without looking.
- Lighting: Pink and teal neon, warm tungsten over the counter, the rest dim.

- Scene: The corner booth. A milkshake, two straws, his and hers.
- Shot: She slides a straw toward him. He talks, hands moving, about nothing. Close on her eyes, on his mouth, on the straw she's not drinking from.
- Cut to: Her nodding along with zero attention, chin on her hand.
- Emotion: Amused, predatory in the cutest possible way.
- Lighting: Booth lamp warm on their faces, neon rim light on her hair.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the cherry held between two fingers and pulled away on *"but sugar, you gotta earn it"* — the whole song in one gesture.
- **The challenge:** the count-to-three at the end of the pre-chorus (*"one, two, and I'm looking at you"*) cut to the drop — a duet-style transition clip where creators count on their fingers and cut to their own reveal.
- **The caption line:** *"I don't give it away, I decide."*
- **The chant:** *"Sweet tooth, sweet tooth, honey, don't rush"* — four bars for lip-syncs with a blown kiss on each kiss-sound hit.
- **The quote:** *"You're sweet like the frosting, but I want the cake."*
- **The wipe:** the thumb on his lip in verse 2 — the slow-motion moment for couple edits.
