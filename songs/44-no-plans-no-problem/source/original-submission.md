# No Plans, No Problem — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A London Saturday that nobody organised. No booking, no itinerary, no reservation — a bus taken in the wrong direction, a market, a canal, a speaker on a bicycle, and by evening a towpath full of strangers dancing. The unplanned day beats every planned one she has had all year.

**Mood:**
Sunlit, easy, generous. Not a rave and not a joke: a warm record about the pleasure of an empty diary. UK register throughout — spelling, slang and street detail all London.

**Message:**
"The best days never make it into the calendar. They just find you on the way."

**Genre:**
Afrobeats with Afroswing rap-sung verses. Think: Wizkid x NSG x Ms Banks — log drums, a round moving bass, a whistle synth on the hook, and verses delivered just behind the beat.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 104 BPM, 4/4
- Key: E major (warm, guitar-friendly, sits under her spoken range)
- No modulation; the final chorus lifts by arrangement, not by key.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female. The verses are **rap-sung** in an Afroswing flow — conversational, rhythmic, landing on internal rhymes, sitting behind the beat, melody returning only in the last two bars of each verse. The choruses are fully sung and open.
- Backing vocals: stacked female harmonies on the chorus; call-and-response ad-libs answering the rap lines; a crowd of voices on the post-chorus chant and the final hook.

**Instrumentation:**
- Log drums (the identity of the record), shuffled shaker, rimshot, congas, talking drum
- Round, warm, constantly moving melodic bass
- Clean muted guitar figure played high and dry
- Whistle synth doubling the hook, soft electric piano, bright plucked guitar
- Horn line answering the vocal from the second chorus
- Textures: street ambience, market crowd, canal water, gulls, distant traffic

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: just woken up, curtains shut, no drums, one guitar figure
- Verses: the rap-sung travelogue — the bus, the market, the towpath
- Pre-chorus: the phone battery dying and nobody caring
- Chorus: the wide, melodic hook with the whistle synth
- Bridge: the honest contrast with a colour-coded year
- Final chorus and outro: the fullest arrangement, then guitar and one shaker

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro — guitar figure, one bass note, no drums]

```
No alarm, no reason, just a Saturday,
Sunlight on the wall and the curtains still drawn.
Somebody is typing in the group chat asking what we're doing,
And the honest answer is, we haven't got a plan at all.
```

- Scene: A small London flat, mid-morning, curtains shut and the day already hot behind them.
- Shot: A hard line of sunlight lying across a wall and a bare shoulder; the room otherwise dim.
- Cut to: A phone face-up on a duvet, screen lit, three dots typing (UI composited), nobody reaching for it.
- Emotion: Not lazy — free. The particular pleasure of owing nobody anything.
- Lighting: One blade of hard sun, everything else soft shadow.

### [verse 1 — log drums, rap-sung flow]

```
Half eleven, curtains drawn, no alarm, no shame,
Group chat asking what's the plan, there ain't one, that's the game.
Meet me by the station, bring your appetite and time,
We're not booking, we're not queueing, we're just letting the day decide.
```

- Scene: Getting out of the house with no destination.
- Shot: Curtains yanked open on the downbeat, the room blowing out white for a frame.
- Cut to: Trainers going on at the door, keys off the hook, a denim jacket over one shoulder.
- Emotion: Momentum without urgency. She walks like the day has already been won.
- Lighting: Blown-out daylight, saturated, no filter.

```
Missed the train on purpose so we took the bus instead,
Top deck, front seat, all of Kingsland Road ahead.
Somebody's shoes are brand new, somebody's aunt is on the phone,
Somebody swears they know a spot, they don't, but we go on.
```

- Scene: The top deck of a double-decker, front seats, four friends.
- Shot: The front window of the top deck, road unspooling, shot over their shoulders from behind.
- Cut to: Brand-new white trainers being lifted away from a scuffing foot; a friend loudly on the phone to an aunt; the whole row laughing.
- Emotion: The comedy of a group with no leader.
- Lighting: Sun through a scratched bus window, flaring across faces.

### [pre-chorus 1 — hats and claps, the lift]

```
No reservation, no itinerary, no rush,
Phone on ten percent and I am not topping up.
Wherever we end up is exactly where we should be,
Turn it up a little bit and drop that beat on me.
```

- Scene: The bus stop, the decision that there is no decision.
- Shot: Her phone battery indicator, low and red (composited), then her sliding the phone into a back pocket without acting on it.
- Emotion: Release. The last thread to the outside world going slack.
- Lighting: Street-level sun, hard shadows, moving crowd.

### [chorus 1 — whistle synth, full groove]

```
No plans, no problem, just vibes and my people,
Nowhere to be by nine and nothing to prove.
No plans, no problem, no map and no timing,
Just a speaker, a pavement and a reason to move.
Feet doing something that could pass for a dance,
Best day of the summer and it came by chance.
No plans, no problem, just vibes and my people,
Nothing in the diary, so tell me what we do.
```

- Scene: The four of them owning a pavement.
- Shot: A tracking walk-and-dance straight down a busy street, the camera walking backwards ahead of them.
- Emotion: Delight, unforced. Nobody is performing for a camera in the fiction.
- Lighting: High summer sun, deep colour, real London street.

### [post-chorus 1 — chant]

```
No plans, no problem, no plans, no problem,
Just vibes and my people and a whole day free.
```

- Scene: Two hard cuts on the chant.
- Shot: Four hands going up together at a crossing; then all four turning to walk the other way at once, mid-crossing, for no reason.
- Lighting: Hard, bright, high contrast.

### [verse 2 — congas and talking drum, the market]

```
Two o'clock, we hit the market and the street is full of smoke,
Jerk chicken, roti, plantain and a mango for the road.
Auntie on the corner says she'll do it for a fiver,
Takes the fiver, gives me change, and then she calls me daughter.
```

- Scene: A busy street market at its loudest hour.
- Shot: Smoke rising off a grill in the sun; a fork turning chicken; steam off a tray of rice.
- Cut to: A five-pound note handed across a stall and change pressed back into her palm along with an extra piece of fruit.
- Emotion: The warmth of being called daughter by a stranger. This is the heart of the verse.
- Lighting: Smoke-filtered sunlight, gold and grey, faces in and out of haze.

```
Down beside the water there's a speaker on a bike,
Playing something old enough that everybody likes.
Kid starts moving, then his mum, then the barber from the shop,
Now the towpath is a dance floor and there's nobody to stop us.
```

- Scene: A canal towpath in the late afternoon.
- Shot: A bicycle leaning on a bollard with a battered speaker bungee-corded to the rack.
- Cut to: A chain reaction, one shot per person: a boy of about eight starts moving; his mother joins; a barber in an apron comes out of a shop and joins; a jogger stops and joins.
- Emotion: The best two seconds of the video — the moment strangers become a party.
- Lighting: Late gold, long shadows on the water, everything warm.

### [pre-chorus 2 — warmer, ice cream queue]

```
It's proper warm, the ice cream queue is halfway round the block,
Phone on four percent and I am not topping up.
Wherever we end up is exactly where we should be,
Turn it up a little bit and drop that beat on me.
```

- Scene: The heat of the day at its peak.
- Shot: A queue snaking from an ice cream van, everybody squinting, one child already dripping.
- Cut to: Her phone, four percent (composited), then her switching it off entirely and pocketing it.
- Emotion: Committed now. The day owns her.
- Lighting: Overexposed highlights, heat shimmer off the pavement.

### [chorus 2 — horns answering]

```
(the chorus again, with a horn line answering each hook line)
```

- Scene: The towpath dance, in full.
- Shot: A long lateral track along the water beside twenty people dancing in a loose line.
- Emotion: Communal, easy, no showing off.
- Lighting: Gold on brick, reflections breaking on the canal.

### [instrumental — percussion and guitar, talking drum]

- Scene: The day breathing.
- Shots: A talking drum played by a man who simply had one with him. Hands passing a paper plate of food along a wall. A narrowboat going by with someone waving from the tiller. Her sitting on the edge of the towpath with her feet over the water, laughing at something off frame. A wide from a bridge above, the whole towpath moving.
- Lighting: Deep gold turning to blue at the edges of the frame.

### [bridge — muted guitar, drums almost gone]

```
I had a whole year of calendars and colour-coded weeks,
Alarms to go to the gym and reminders to eat.
But today was never written down and look at what it gave,
The best ones don't get booked, they only find you on the way.
```

- Scene: The one still moment.
- Shot: Her alone on a bench with the party still going behind her, out of focus, catching her breath.
- Cut to: Two-second inserts of the life she is not living today: a laptop calendar grid of coloured blocks (composited), a gym bag by a door, an alarm going off in an empty room.
- Emotion: Recognition, not regret. She is not against plans; she is just not in one.
- Lighting: The sun properly low now, orange on one side of her face.

### [final chorus — horns, group vocal, fullest]

```
(the chorus, with "Half of the towpath in a kind of a dance")
```

- Scene: The biggest shot of the video.
- Shot: A drone lift straight up off the towpath, revealing how far along the water the dancing goes.
- Cut to: The four friends in the middle of it, arms round shoulders, singing the hook into each other's faces.
- Emotion: Peak. The day at its absolute best, and everyone knows it while it is happening.
- Lighting: Last direct sun of the evening, hitting the water and coming back up under their chins.

### [post-chorus 2 — street chant]

```
(the chant again, everyone)
```

- Scene: The whole towpath chanting.
- Shot: Two wide cuts, twenty people, hands up on the first line and a jump on the second.
- Lighting: Blue creeping in at the edges, gold in the middle.

### [outro — guitar and one shaker]

```
Half eleven tomorrow and the curtains will be drawn,
Group chat will be asking me the same.
And I'll tell them what I told them here today,
No plans, no problem, come and find me anyway.
```

- Scene: Sunday morning, the same flat, the same blade of light.
- Shot: The same wall and the same line of sun as the first shot, one day later.
- Cut to: The phone lighting up on the duvet with the group chat again, and her hand reaching over and turning it face-down, smiling.
- Final shot: Her pulling the curtain open onto a bright ordinary street, holding it, no music cue, just the guitar. Locked-off.
- Emotion: Contentment and a promise. It will happen again next Saturday.
- Lighting: Clean morning, no grade tricks.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** four friends walking straight at camera down a busy street doing four different steps, on *"just vibes and my people."*
- **The challenge:** the chain reaction on the towpath — one person starts, then a parent, then a shopkeeper, then a stranger. Film your own chain and cut on the hook.
- **The heart moment:** the market stall, the fiver, the change and *"then she calls me daughter."*
- **The caption line:** *"Nothing in the diary, so tell me what we do."*
- **The quote:** *"The best ones don't get booked, they only find you on the way."*
