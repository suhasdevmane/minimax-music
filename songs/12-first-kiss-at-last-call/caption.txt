Global Metadata

Global Emotional Progression: The track opens on the sound of a bar in full swing, a jukebox bleeding through a wall, a single palm-muted electric guitar and a hi-hat counting the room in, the lead voice dry and amused rather than tender, a night that has not decided what it is yet. The first verse rides a tight, springy rock groove, bass and muted guitar carrying the story while the drums stay on the rim, restless and full of near misses. The pre-chorus pulls the guitars into open chords, the snare climbs, the room noise falls away and a single cymbal swell drags everything upward. The chorus explodes into full distorted guitars, an enormous live snare, tambourine on the backbeat and gang vocals shouting the title back, bright and major and slightly reckless. The second verse drops back to bass, drums and a clean guitar arpeggio, more detailed and more nervous, and the second pre-chorus is louder and shorter, cutting the music away to almost nothing on the last line before the second chorus lands with an added octave guitar line and doubled gang vocals. The instrumental break is sixteen bars of a lead guitar melody over a four-on-the-floor stomp with handclaps and a half-time breakdown that strips to bass, kick and crowd noise. The bridge falls to a single clean guitar and voice with the room sound gone, the most honest moment in the song, and rebuilds with a rising tom pattern into the final chorus. The final chorus is the widest arrangement of the track with a second guitar harmony, a full gang-vocal crowd and the tambourine doubled, followed by a chanted post-chorus that the whole room can shout. The outro pulls everything back to one clean guitar, a shaker and the lead voice, ending on a warm unresolved chord and the sound of a door closing on an empty street.
