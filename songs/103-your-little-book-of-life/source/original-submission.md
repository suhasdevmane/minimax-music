# Your Little Book of Life — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, improved
for the lyric body. Kept as source material. The lyric body in this file is
the cleaned version that lives in `lyrics.txt`.

**Theme:** A father sings to his young daughter about the small things she
does every day that he would otherwise walk past: a ladybug on a garden
wall, a spoon used as a microphone, a blanket fort with a hand-lettered
sign. He writes her life as a book she keeps writing, and he is the reader
who keeps every page. The song is about a child's everyday wonder and what
it teaches a tired grown-up about where joy actually is.

**Mood:** Warm, playful, lightly tired, tender without being sentimental.
Morning sunlight and a kitchen full of noise, not a hospital or a goodbye.

**Message:** "Sometimes joy is just a blanket fort at the ending of the day."

**Genre:** Gentle folk-pop. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar, a soft upright
bass and light hand percussion; the sound of a warm living room, not a
stage. Reference comparisons describe sound only: an unhurried acoustic
singer-songwriter with a close, dry vocal and a small-room feel, and a
modern family-album pop sound with claps, glockenspiel and a soft organ pad.

**Tempo & Key:** 76 BPM, 4/4, C major. The song stays in major throughout
and lifts only in dynamics at the chorus and final chorus, never in key.

**Vocal setup:** Lead male baritone (Singer A, Kai's voice), close and
conversational in the verses, open-throated in the chorus. A single low
male harmony a third beneath the lead thickens each chorus. A soft wordless
pad of female and child-register voices joins the final chorus. Airy unison
doubles drift under the outro.

**Instrumentation:**
- Fingerpicked acoustic guitar (the spine from intro to outro)
- Soft upright bass
- Light hand percussion and shaker (verses)
- Ukulele-like plucked guitar (first verse, bright and playful)
- Claps and a steady strum (choruses)
- Glockenspiel (third verse and the final chorus run)
- Soft organ pad and warm bass (second verse)
- Slow piano and strings (bridge)
- Bright brass accent and wordless choir (final chorus)
- A ladybug's small wing sound under the first line; a page turning in the bridge

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: bare guitar and room tone, a small morning observation.
- Verse 1: bright and playful, the shaker enters, the spoon and the blanket.
- Chorus 1: the title line lands clearly, full strum, claps and harmony.
- Verse 2: quieter, the blanket-fort mood, the organ pad under the voice.
- Verse 3: the widest view, the glockenspiel, the world made new.
- Pre-chorus: a small reflective pause, guitar only, the father's own past.
- Chorus 2 and 3: the hook returns with the father's voice fuller.
- Bridge: piano and strings, the father almost speaking, the pages to come.
- Final chorus: every layer returns, brass, a rainbow in a father's heart.
- Outro: guitar and voice alone, washed in a long soft reverb tail.

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

Characters: **Kai**, the father, a man in his late thirties with short dark
hair, a warm tired face, a cardigan and a mug of tea. **Ellie**, the
daughter, about four, wavy dark hair, a yellow raincoat and a plush bear.

### [intro — a small morning]

```
Today you found a ladybug
Beside the garden wall,
You watched it climb a blade of grass
And wondered if it'd fall.
```

- Scene: A back garden in early morning, a low brick wall with wet grass.
  Ellie in her yellow raincoat crouches close to the wall, the plush bear
  tucked under one arm.
- Shot: Macro close-up of the ladybug climbing a blade of grass, then a
  slow pull back to Ellie's face, eyes wide, watching.
- Cut to: Kai, standing in the back doorway with his mug of tea, smiling
  at the scene without wanting to interrupt it.
- Emotion: Quiet attention and small wonder, the father noticing what he
  would have missed.
- Lighting: Soft early sun low over the garden, cool shadows on the wall,
  a faint mist on the grass.

### [verse 1 — the biscuit, the spoon, the kitchen]

```
You gave it half a biscuit,
You gave it room to fly,
Then waved until it disappeared
Like a plane across the sky.
Yesterday you wore your shoes
Upon the wrong two feet,
You marched around the kitchen
To your own imaginary beat.
You called the spoon a microphone,
The blanket was a train,
And suddenly our little home
Was full of sun again.
```

- Scene: The kitchen. Ellie on the floor with a biscuit held out to the
  ladybug's wall, then a dance in mismatched shoes, then a spoon held to
  her mouth like a microphone.
- Shot: Medium shot of Ellie waving goodbye at the garden door; then a
  low-angle tracking shot of her marching in her shoes on the wrong feet;
  then a close-up of the spoon at her lips.
- Cut to: Kai at the counter laughing into his mug, the blanket draped over
  a chair behind him, a train made of cushions.
- Emotion: Playful and bright, a father charmed by something ridiculous.
- Lighting: Warm kitchen light, the morning sun through the window, a few
  yellow highlights from the raincoat hanging by the door.

### [chorus 1 — the title line]

```
You're writing your own little book of life,
With every laugh and every surprise.
Every new question, every new day,
You paint a thousand colours my way.
You're my sunrise, my morning light,
The reason my tired heart feels alive.
My beautiful girl, wherever you go,
You make the whole world brighter than you know.
```

- Scene: The living room, the whole family space in use. Ellie spins with
  her arms out, the bear in one hand. Kai sits on the arm of the sofa, a
  cardigan sleeve pushed up, joining in on the harmony.
- Shot: Wide shot of the room, Ellie spinning in the centre; push-in on
  Kai singing with his eyes closed; a close-up of the bear's button eye on
  the beat.
- Cut to: Ellie stopping dizzy, laughing, pointing at the window where the
  sun is.
- Emotion: Open and warm, the chorus carried by feeling rather than volume.
- Lighting: Full afternoon light across the room, soft and golden, no harsh
  highlights.

### [verse 2 — the castle, the princess, the blanket fort]

```
You built a castle out of pillows,
A kingdom on the floor,
You pinned a sign upon the chair,
Daddy, knock before the door.
I bowed before the princess
And asked if I could stay,
You said, only if you promise
To be silly every day.
So I became a dancing bear,
A dragon and a king,
And you laughed until your little cheeks
Were brighter than the spring.
I learned that joy is not a treasure
Hidden far away,
Sometimes it's just a blanket fort
At the ending of the day.
```

- Scene: A blanket fort built from the sofa cushions and two dining chairs.
  A hand-lettered sign pinned to the chair reads DADDY KNOCK. Kai kneels
  at the entrance in his cardigan and is let in as a visitor.
- Shot: Overhead shot of the fort on the floor; a close-up of the sign; a
  medium shot of Kai bowing as a dancing bear, then as a dragon with a
  cardboard crown; a close-up of Ellie's laughing face inside the tent.
- Cut to: The two of them lying inside the fort, the bear between them,
  a torch lit under the blanket.
- Emotion: Quieter and cosy, the blanket-fort mood, a shared secret.
- Lighting: Warm lamp light through the fabric, the inside of the fort
  glowing amber, the room outside dimmer.

### [chorus 2 — the hook returns]

```
You're writing your own little book of life,
With every laugh and every surprise.
Every new question, every new day,
You paint a thousand colours my way.
You're my sunrise, my morning light,
The reason my tired heart feels alive.
My beautiful girl, wherever you go,
You make the whole world brighter than you know.
```

- Scene: The garden in afternoon. Ellie runs in circles on the lawn with
  the bear held up, Kai follows at a walk with his tea, the chorus playing
  across the lawn and the open back door.
- Shot: Tracking shot following Ellie across the grass; a wide shot of
  Kai from the path; a close-up of the bear's ear bouncing on the beat.
- Cut to: Ellie stopping by the garden wall, pointing at the ladybug
  again, the first visual call-back to the intro.
- Emotion: Bright and open, a family sharing a plain afternoon.
- Lighting: Warm afternoon sun, long shadows across the grass, soft sky.

### [verse 3 — names, clouds, pebbles]

```
You learned to say the names of things,
Then changed them just for fun.
The fridge became a mountain,
The ceiling was the sun.
You gave a name to every cloud
And every passing plane,
You made a friend of every sound,
The thunder, wind and rain.
You make a celebration
From the smallest thing you find,
A pebble can be precious,
A puddle can be kind.
You show me that the world is new
When seen through loving eyes,
There are miracles in ordinary days
Beneath familiar skies.
```

- Scene: A sequence of small discoveries. Ellie names the fridge a mountain
  and climbs a chair to reach it. She names clouds from the garden with
  Kai lying beside her on the grass. A pebble in her palm, a puddle in the
  driveway she stamps in.
- Shot: Low-angle shot of Ellie climbing the kitchen chair toward the fridge
  door; a wide shot of the two on the lawn looking up at the sky; a macro
  close-up of the pebble; a puddle reflecting the sky with her boot
  stamping into it.
- Cut to: Kai's face in close-up, looking at her with his tired eyes softer
  than before, the glockenspiel note landing on him.
- Emotion: Wonder and gratitude, the father seeing the world again through
  her eyes.
- Lighting: Clean, bright daylight, a glockenspiel-bright sky, a sparkle on
  the puddle and the pebble.

### [pre-chorus — a small reflective pause]

```
I used to chase tomorrow,
Always running toward the next,
You taught me how to hold the moment
And be thankful for the best.
```

- Scene: Kai alone at the kitchen table after the children's bedtime
  routine. The tea has gone cold. Ellie's drawing sits on the table, and
  the plush bear is on the chair beside him.
- Shot: Static close-up of Kai's hands around the cold mug, then a slow
  push-in on his face as he looks toward the hallway, a light on in her room.
- Cut to: Ellie's drawing of a ladybug and a sun, held up to the light.
- Emotion: Reflective and grateful, a small honest pause, not sad.
- Lighting: Single warm lamp over the table, the rest of the kitchen dim,
  the hallway light a thin line at the edge of the frame.

### [chorus 3 — the hook, more intimate]

```
You're writing your own little book of life,
With every laugh and every surprise.
Every new question, every new day,
You paint a thousand colours my way.
You're my sunrise, my morning light,
The reason my tired heart feels alive.
My beautiful girl, wherever you go,
You make the whole world brighter than you know.
```

- Scene: Ellie in her bedroom in her pyjamas, a row of plush toys on the
  shelf and the bear in her arms. Kai sits on the edge of the bed, one
  hand on the blanket, and sings the chorus to her quietly.
- Shot: Medium close-up of the two of them on the bed; a slow close-up of
  the shelf of toys; a shot of Ellie's hand drawing on the wall of her
  bedtime notebook.
- Cut to: Ellie's sleepy face, the lamp dimming, her eyes closing.
- Emotion: Intimate and tender, the hook sung softly close to the microphone.
- Lighting: A small bedside lamp, warm and low, a soft glow on the blanket.

### [bridge — the pages to come]

```
One day you'll turn the pages
Far beyond the ones we know,
You'll write about your dreams
And the places you will go.
There may be roads I cannot follow,
There may be skies I cannot see,
But every chapter of your heart
Will always matter to me.
I'll keep the little memories,
Your drawings, words and shoes,
The tiny hands that held mine
When you had the world to choose.
And when you read the story
Of the girl you grew into,
I hope you see between the lines
How proud I am of you.
```

- Scene: A bookshelf in the hallway. Kai takes down a battered picture book
  and a small stack of Ellie's drawings tied with string. A page turns on
  the beat. The bookshelf is the one thing in the house that holds the
  whole story.
- Shot: Static close-up of the books and drawings on the shelf; a close-up
  of a page turning under Kai's thumb; a slow shot of the shoes by the door,
  two small pairs, one of them on the wrong feet.
- Cut to: Kai's face in soft focus as he reads the last line to himself,
  the lamp in the hall behind him.
- Emotion: Tender, almost spoken, the father falling back toward speech
  before the final build.
- Lighting: Single hall lamp, hard shadows across the books, a cold flash
  only on the photograph of Ellie at four.

### [chorus 4 — the final chorus, every layer]

```
You're writing your own little book of life,
With every brave and beautiful line.
Every new dream, every new start,
You leave a rainbow in your father's heart.
You're my sunrise, my morning light,
The reason my tired heart feels alive.
My beautiful girl, wherever you go,
You make the whole world brighter than you know.
```

- Scene: The whole family in the garden at golden hour: Ellie in the yellow
  raincoat over her pyjamas, the bear on her shoulder, Kai and a
  neighbour's child in the background running with bubbles. Everyone in
  the frame, every layer of the song playing.
- Shot: Wide sweeping shot of the garden, a slow crane up over the wall;
  a close-up of Ellie's face in the sun; a flash of light through the
  bubbles, a rainbow in the spray from the hose.
- Cut to: Kai, looking at the rainbow, then at Ellie, his cardigan open
  in the warm air.
- Emotion: Full and bright, the chorus sung open-throated, the rainbow
  landing on the line about a father's heart.
- Lighting: Golden hour, warm, the garden in full colour, a bright rainbow
  across the hose spray.

### [outro — guitar and voice]

```
So keep on dancing, keep on dreaming,
Keep your wonder shining through.
Life became a better story
The day it gave me you.
```

- Scene: Night. The back door is open to the dark garden and the lights of
  the kitchen. Ellie asleep on the sofa in her yellow raincoat, the bear
  under her arm. Kai sits beside her with the mug, the last of the tea.
- Shot: Slow static wide of the sofa and the open door; a close-up of
  Ellie's sleeping face, a small smile; a close-up of Kai's hand on the
  raincoat sleeve.
- Cut to: The garden wall in the dark, the ladybug's blade of grass, a
  single leaf moving in the breeze, then the frame fades.
- Emotion: Calm and reflective, the father quiet, with the last line sung
  simply.
- Lighting: Kitchen lamp light spilling onto the sofa, the garden dark
  blue beyond the door, the sofa in warm amber.
