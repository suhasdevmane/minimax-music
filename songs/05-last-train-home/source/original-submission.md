# Last Train Home — original submission

The song concept, lyrics and video direction exactly as submitted, before
trimming to the model's 6-minute cap and cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as
source material.

**Theme:**
Two strangers meet on the last train home night after night. They never exchange names at first—just glances, small smiles, shared silence. Over weeks, they start leaving little notes, sharing songs, talking about their days. They fall in love without ever saying "I love you." Then one night, one of them doesn't show up. The other keeps riding the last train, hoping. In the end, they meet again—not on the train, but by choice.

This is a modern, cinematic love story about timing, courage, and the moment you stop waiting and start walking toward someone.

**Genre:**
Cinematic indie-pop / soft electronic ballad with a slight nocturnal vibe.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 98 BPM, 4/4
- Key: E minor (moody but singable)
- Final chorus can lift to G major for hope.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult, gender-neutral or female-leaning, intimate and slightly breathy in verses, strong and clear in choruses.
- Optional harmony: a second voice (male or female) in the final chorus and outro.

**Instrumentation:**
- Piano (main spine)
- Warm sub-bass / soft 808s
- Atmospheric synths (night-city pads, subtle arps)
- Light electronic percussion: soft kick, snaps, brushed snare, shaker
- Electric guitar with reverb (melodic lines in chorus/bridge)
- Ambient textures: distant train sounds, station announcements (very low, as ear candy)

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: lonely, nocturnal, curious
- Verses: small moments, growing connection
- Pre-chorus: "what are we?" tension
- Chorus: big, emotional, longing
- Bridge: absence, doubt, decision
- Final chorus: hope, courage, movement
- Outro: quiet resolution, new beginning

## Why this can go viral

- **Fresh theme:** Not another "we're together" or "we broke up" song. This is "we almost were, then we chose to be."
- **Strong visual story:** Train, notes, song titles, 2:13 a.m., same seat—very easy to turn into a music video, short film, or TikTok series.
- **Relatable emotion:** Almost-love, timing, courage, the moment you decide to walk toward someone.
- **Repeatable hook:** "On the last train home / We built a world no one knows" is simple, cinematic, and memorable.
- **UGC potential:** People can post their own "last train" stories, notes they've kept, songs they've shared.

## Video concept (short version)

**Main characters:**
- Protagonist (any gender): young adult, thoughtful eyes, casual night clothes (hoodie, jacket, scarf).
- Love interest: similar age, black hoodie, headphones, quiet presence.

**Visual style:**
- Night city, neon lights, empty platforms, reflective glass, rain on windows.
- Cool blues, deep greens, warm interior train lights.
- Handheld but smooth, intimate close-ups.

**Key scenes:**
- Intro: Protagonist boarding the last train, same seat, seeing love interest by the door.
- Verse 1: Small glances, dropped pen, first "thanks," note left in seat pocket.
- Chorus: Montage of nights: exchanging notes, song titles, smiles, almost-touching hands.
- Bridge: Love interest absent, protagonist riding alone, looking at every face.
- Final chorus: Daytime or early evening platform, they meet by choice, walk away together.
- Outro: Their hands clasped, walking out of the station, no train in frame.

**Viral hook idea:**
- Show a note with lyrics: "Still here. Still tired. Still trying."
