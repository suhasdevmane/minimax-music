# Sunburn and Strawberries — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Register: Australian.** UK spelling throughout, Australian idiom used
naturally and sparingly — *cactus, sunnies, thongs, footy, punnet, hot chips,
zinc, arvo, jetty* — and Australian coastal detail: a highway strawberry
stall, a car park full by eight, a Mr Whippy van doing the same eight bars, a
jetty queue with a dare in it, a coast road home with the seats still wet.

**Theme:**
One beach day, start to finish, that quietly becomes the day everybody will
be telling wrong for the next twenty years. Nothing dramatic happens. That is
the point: the best day of a summer never announces itself, and you only find
out which one it was in April.

**Mood:**
Fast, bright, sunburnt, affectionate. The verses are funny because they are
accurate. The bridge is the only reflective passage and it earns its place by
being the only one.

**Message:**
"You don't get told which day it is until it's gone."

**Genre:**
Summer pop with a surf-rock spine. Think: Vance Joy x Dua Lipa, with the
spring-reverb guitars of a sixties surf record and the gang handclaps and
whistled hook of a modern festival single.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 118 BPM, 4/4
- Key: A major (open, bright, ideal for twin surf guitars in harmony)
- No modulation. The final chorus is the biggest arrangement on the record,
  with the whistle doubled in octaves.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, quick and chatty in the verses, belted in
  the choruses, close and plain in the bridge.
- Backing vocals: stacked harmonies in the choruses; a chanted post-chorus;
  the whole band whistling in the instrumental break.

**Instrumentation:**
- Reverb-drenched surf guitar with heavy spring reverb (the signature,
  present in the first bar and the last)
- Bright live kit, walking electric bass
- Stacked gang handclaps, structural rather than decorative
- Whistled counter-melody over the hook, doubled in octaves by the end
- Rising organ in the pre-choruses, organ pad in the bridge
- Baritone guitar in the later choruses
- Tambourine, shaker, cowbell
- Acoustic guitar and one tambourine for the outro
- Room tone: a car door, gravel, tyre noise, gulls, surf, beach crowd

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: half five in the morning, the boot packed, strawberries bought
- Verse 1: the drive and the arrival, catalogued fast and accurately
- Pre-chorus: the small chaos, the jetty queue, the dare
- Chorus: the hook, twin guitars and the whistle
- Post-chorus: the chant, guitar and claps only
- Verse 2: the day refusing to end, the tide, the van, the good light
- Instrumental: a surf guitar solo and a whistling breakdown
- Bridge: no drums, one guitar, the only reflective passage on the record
- Final chorus: the same day, now understood
- Outro: the coast road home, headlights, half a punnet left

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – one surf guitar, car door, gravel]

```
Alarm at half five and the kettle's not on,
Four of us awake and the boot's already done.
Punnet of strawberries from a stall on the road,
And a whole day in front of us and nowhere to go.
```

- Shots: A dark suburban street before dawn, one house with a kitchen light on. A boot slamming shut on towels, a footy and a folded chair. Four people getting into a small car, nobody talking yet. A roadside stall with a hand-painted sign turned away from camera and a punnet of strawberries changing hands through a car window.
- Emotion and lighting: Quiet excitement; blue pre-dawn, then the first orange light hitting the windscreen on the highway.

### [verse 1]

```
The back window's stuck so we cop it all the way,
Radio's cactus so we sing to fill the day.
Sunnies on your head and your feet up on the dash,
And a bag of hot chips going cold in the back.
```

- Shots: A rear window jammed half open with the wind tearing through the car. A car stereo with a blank dead display (composited). Four mouths singing something badly, all at once. Bare feet up on a dashboard with sunglasses pushed onto a forehead. A paper bag of chips on a back seat with a hand reaching into it.
- Emotion and lighting: The best part of any beach day is the drive; saturated film look, blown highlights, sun flaring straight down the lens.

```
Car park's full by eight and the sand is already hot,
Thongs off, straight in, and we don't check the spot.
Your brother's launched the footy and it's landed in the drink,
And a seagull's got a plan and it's closer than you think.
I put zinc across your nose and you just let me do it,
You don't say a word about it, but I know that you knew it.
```

- Shots: A beach car park entirely full at eight in the morning. Rubber thongs abandoned in a line on burning sand. A football sailing out over the shallows and everybody watching it land. A seagull sidling up to an unattended bag, in profile, unhurried. Mahima drawing a stripe of white zinc across Kai's nose with one finger while he stands perfectly still and does not look away.
- Emotion and lighting: The first flirt beat, and it is entirely wordless; hard morning sun, hot sand, high contrast.

### [pre-chorus]

```
Salt in everything, sand in the bread,
Somebody's towel is on somebody's head.
The jetty's got a queue and the queue's got a dare,
And the whole day's going nowhere and I don't care.
```

- Shots: A sandwich being unwrapped and visibly full of sand, eaten anyway. Somebody asleep with a towel over their whole head. A queue of teenagers on a wooden jetty, one of them counting down. A wide of the beach from the water looking back, everything small and bright.
- Emotion and lighting: The build; the camera getting faster, the light getting harder.

### [chorus – twin guitars and the whistle]

```
Sunburn and strawberries, that's my kind of summer,
Red on my shoulders and red on the back of my hands.
There's an ice-cream van doing the same eight bars,
And a mile of nothing but water and sand.

Sunburn and strawberries, that's my kind of summer,
Salt in my hair and my thongs going west.
I'll be peeling by Wednesday and I'd do it all again,
Sunburn and strawberries, and today was the best.
```

- Shots: Mahima running into the water with the whole group, filmed from behind at knee height. Red shoulders in close-up with a strap line already showing. Strawberry-stained fingers held up to camera. An ice-cream van parked on the grass above the sand with a queue at the window. A wide of a long empty beach with six tiny figures in it.
- Emotion and lighting: Peak joy; the most saturated grade in the film, deep blue water, white sand, red skin.

### [post-chorus – whistle and claps]

```
Sunburn and strawberries, sunburn and strawberries,
That's my kind, that's my kind of summer.
Sunburn and strawberries, sunburn and strawberries,
Salt on my mouth and the sun going under.
```

- Shots: Four repeating fast beats: feet hitting wet sand, a strawberry going into a mouth, a football spinning, the ice-cream van window. Same four, same order, every time the chant comes round.
- Emotion and lighting: Pure release; hard cuts on the clap.

### [verse 2]

```
Two o'clock and the tide has come a long way in,
Half our stuff is floating and we're laughing at the win.
Your mum's on the phone about coming back for tea,
And you say, maybe, in a voice that means, not me.
```

- Shots: A tide line visibly halfway up a pile of belongings. A thong floating away and nobody chasing it. Somebody laughing so hard they sit down in the shallows. Kai on the phone with one hand over his other ear, saying maybe in a way that means no.
- Emotion and lighting: The day refusing to end; the light going gold, shadows lengthening across the sand.

```
Ice-cream van rolls up and plays the same eight bars,
Eleven of us queue with the coins we found in cars.
You take the one with bubblegum stuck on its nose,
And you eat it in the water and you don't come in close.
Four o'clock, the good light, and nobody has moved,
And a nothing sort of Saturday has got something to prove.
```

- Shots: The ice-cream van's speaker, macro. A palm full of coins scraped out of a car's cup holder. Eleven people in a queue with wet hair. A gaudy ice-cream with a bubblegum nose held up in the sun. Kai eating it standing chest-deep in the water, twenty metres out, grinning back at shore.
- Emotion and lighting: The best twenty minutes of the film; the good light, low sun, everything backlit and glowing.

### [instrumental]

- Shots: The surf guitar solo carried by the beach itself: a long slow track along the water line past everybody's abandoned stuff. The jetty jump finally happening, shot from below in the water, three bodies in the air at once. A drum-and-tambourine breakdown cut to a whistling group walking up the sand. A hard drop to a single guitar: one wide of the empty beach with the tide fully in.
- Emotion and lighting: Full energy into a held breath; fast cuts, then one very long still frame.

### [bridge – no drums, one clean guitar]

```
You don't get told which day it is until it's done,
You just wake up in April and you know that it's gone.
So I'm keeping the sand in my shoe and the strawberry stain,
And the photo where your eyes are shut and mine are the same.
We'll be telling this one wrong for the next twenty years,
And nobody will fix it, and nobody cares.
```

- Shots: A two-second flash of a cold grey April street, a coat, a bus stop, no beach — the only cold frame in the film. Back to the beach: Mahima sitting on a towel with her arms around her knees, watching the others, not in it for one moment. A shoe with sand still in it. A strawberry stain on the hem of a shirt. A phone held up for a group photo where at least three people have their eyes shut (composited screen).
- Emotion and lighting: The turn; the same golden light, held longer, the camera stopping moving for the first time.

### [final chorus – biggest arrangement, whistle in octaves]

```
Sunburn and strawberries, that's my kind of summer,
Red on my shoulders and red on the back of my hands.
Ice-cream van's gone and the car park's clear,
And a mile of nothing but water and sand.

Sunburn and strawberries, that's my kind of summer,
Salt in my hair and my sunnies gone west.
I'll be peeling by Wednesday and I'd do it all again,
Sunburn and strawberries, and today was the best.
```

- Shots: The empty car park with one car left in it. The whole group carrying everything up the sand at once, badly. A last run into the water fully clothed. A drone pull-back from the six of them on an empty beach at sunset.
- Emotion and lighting: Enormous and warm; the sun on the water, long shadows, the last of the gold.

### [post-chorus]

(repeat)

- Shots: The same four chant beats, now in sunset light instead of morning.
- Emotion and lighting: Same rhythm, different hour, and that is the whole idea.

### [outro – acoustic guitar, one tambourine, tyre noise]

```
Headlights on the coast road and the seats are all wet,
Somebody's asleep with a towel on their head.
Half a punnet left and it's going hand to hand,
Sunburn and strawberries, and a whole lot of sand.
```

- Shots: Headlights on a coast road at dusk, the ocean going dark on one side. The car interior: wet seats, sandy footwells, one person asleep under a towel. A punnet with six strawberries left being passed forward between seats. Final shot: the car's tail lights going round a headland and out of frame, the beach empty behind it.
- Emotion and lighting: Tired, sunburnt, completely happy; last light and headlights, the saturation dropping for the first time.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the run into the water from behind at knee height on the first *"sunburn and strawberries."*
- **The challenge:** the zinc cut — post shots of the zinc stripe going across a nose and invite people to film the moment they knew a day was going to be the one.
- **The caption line:** *"You don't get told which day it is until it's gone."*
- **The quote:** *"A nothing sort of Saturday has got something to prove."*
- **The still:** the jetty jump, three bodies in the air, shot from the water.
