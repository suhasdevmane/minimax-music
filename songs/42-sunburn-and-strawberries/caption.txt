The first verse rides a fast, sunny groove with palm-muted guitar, a walking electric bass and a tambourine on the offbeat, the vocal quick and chatty, cataloguing a car full of people on a highway before eight. The pre-chorus lifts with a snare build, a rising organ and a doubled clap pattern, everything opening two bars early. The chorus is huge and bright, twin surf guitars in harmony, a full live kit, gang handclaps, stacked vocal harmonies and a whistled counter-melody sitting on top of the hook. A whistle-led post-chorus follows with the guitar and claps alone under a chanted vocal. The second verse comes back with more percussion, a shaker and a cowbell, and a warmer bass tone as the day extends past everybody's plans. The second chorus adds a baritone guitar line and a wider harmony stack. An instrumental break of sixteen to twenty-four bars is a full surf guitar solo over the clap pattern, a drum-and-tambourine breakdown with the whole band whistling, and a hard drop to a single guitar. The bridge cuts the drums entirely to a clean reverbed guitar, a soft organ pad and the lead vocal close and plain, the one reflective passage on the record. The final chorus returns as the biggest and brightest arrangement on the track with every layer present and the whistle doubled in octaves, and the post-chorus repeats. The outro drops to acoustic guitar, a single tambourine and one voice, headlights on a coast road, fading on tyre noise and a whistle.
