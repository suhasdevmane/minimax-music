# Confetti in My Hair — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
The morning after the best party of the year, told from inside the wreckage. Confetti still in her hair, a shoe in a plant pot, six people around a kettle nobody has the energy to pour. Halfway through, the song rewinds and we learn what the party was for: one of them was leaving in the morning.

**Mood:**
Warm, funny, then suddenly moving. A celebration record that earns its joy by admitting what the year cost. Not a hangover joke — the humour is in the detail, the feeling is real.

**Message:**
"Leave the mess. The mess is the proof."

**Genre:**
Party pop with a live brass section. Think: Lizzo x Bleachers x Jack Antonoff production — real drums played hard, gang vocals recorded in a room, brass answering every hook line.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 126 BPM, 4/4
- Key: C major (open, brass-friendly, singable by a room full of people)
- No modulation; the final chorus lifts by arrangement and by the gang vocal taking over.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, croaky and amused in the verses, full and open in the choruses.
- Backing vocals: six to ten real voices in a room rather than a polished choir; they join on the first post-chorus and take almost all of the final chorus.

**Instrumentation:**
- Live brass: two trumpets, trombone, baritone saxophone (stabs, a full break feature, a final counter-line)
- Live drums played hard and loose, bright snare, tambourine, handclaps
- Palm-muted electric guitar and melodic electric bass
- Clean electric piano (opens the song, carries the bridge, closes the outro)
- Textures: empty-apartment room tone, morning birds, distant traffic, a kettle, a confetti cannon, a room of laughter

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: sideways morning light, no drums, one ringing chord
- Verse 1: the inventory of the wreckage, comic and close
- Pre-chorus: the kettle, the squinting, the shared memory
- Chorus: relief rather than triumph, brass and gang vocals
- Verse 2: rewind to the party, and the reason for it
- Bridge: the year that took a lot, and the first night nobody had to try
- Final chorus and outro: the widest arrangement, then a jar on a shelf

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro — one electric piano chord, room tone, no drums]

```
Morning came in sideways through a curtain nobody closed,
Landed on a room that looks like it went ten rounds.
I sat up on the carpet and I felt it in my hair,
Little gold and pink and silver, still there.
```

- Scene: A city apartment at eight in the morning, curtains half open, everything still where it was dropped.
- Shot: A slow tilt down a shaft of hard morning light through drifting dust onto a carpet covered in confetti.
- Cut to: Mahima sitting up on the floor between a sofa and a coffee table, blanket half on, hair flattened on one side.
- Emotion: Amused disbelief. Not regret — nowhere near regret.
- Lighting: Flat hard morning sun, blown-out window, deep shadow on the other side of the room.

### [verse 1 — dry kick, shaker, the inventory]

```
Eight in the morning and the living room's a crime scene,
There's a sock inside the fruit bowl and I don't know what that means.
The tall one's on the sofa with a cushion on his head,
The quiet one is stacking paper plates instead.
```

- Scene: The wreckage, catalogued like evidence.
- Shot: A locked-off wide of the whole living room in one frame, everything visible: cups, streamers, a paper crown on the tile.
- Emotion: Deadpan. Nobody speaks for the first thirty seconds of the video.
- Lighting: Unforgiving daylight, no fill, everything a little too honest.

```
There's a slice of cake with one bite gone beside the chair,
And the balloons are half the height they were last night in here.
My mascara took the pillowcase down with it in the night,
And I've never been so glad to see a mess in daylight.
```

- Scene: The small archaeology of a good night.
- Shot: Macro on a slice of cake with a single bite taken out of it, fork still in the frosting.
- Emotion: The turn from comic to fond.
- Lighting: Sun creeping further across the floor as the verse goes on.

### [pre-chorus 1 — claps, a bar of silence]

```
Kettle's on, and nobody is talking,
Six of us just squinting at the light.
Somebody laughs and then everybody's laughing,
Because we all just remembered the same thing.
```

- Scene: The kitchen, six people, no conversation.
- Shot: A slow pan along six faces in a row at a counter, all squinting, all holding mugs, none talking.
- Emotion: The specific comedy of a group who all remember the same thing at once.
- Lighting: Backlit by the kitchen window, faces half in silhouette.

### [chorus 1 — brass, gang vocals, the drop]

```
Woke up with confetti in my hair, and I don't even care,
Sun through the curtains like it's proud of what went on in here.
There's a shoe in the plant and a hat on the lamp,
And a phone number written up the back of my hand.
Woke up with confetti in my hair, and I don't even care,
Nobody is touching a broom in this apartment soon.
Let the floor keep the evidence, let the kitchen keep the proof,
Woke up with confetti in my hair, and I don't even care.
```

- Scene: The apartment goes from still life to musical.
- Shot: A tracking move through the flat as everyone starts moving at once — dancing badly, in yesterday's clothes, holding mugs.
- Cut to: The hero frame — Mahima at the window with the light behind her, shaking her hair out and confetti coming off it in slow motion.
- Emotion: Full release. The relief of a good morning after a good night.
- Lighting: Hard sun turned into an asset: the confetti catches it.

### [post-chorus 1 — chant, claps]

```
Confetti in my hair, confetti in my hair,
Little bit of last night stuck to everywhere.
Confetti in my hair, confetti in my hair,
And I don't even care, no, I don't even care.
```

- Scene: Four fast cuts on the chant.
- Shot: Confetti pulled out of hair; a shoe lifted out of a plant pot; a hat lifted off a lamp; six mugs raised at once.
- Lighting: Bright, punchy, high contrast.

### [verse 2 — the rewind, and the reason]

```
Rewind to about eleven when the neighbors came up too,
And somebody's little brother played a keyboard like he knew.
Someone fired the cannon early, aimed it up into the fan,
So it snowed on us for twenty minutes longer than it planned.
```

- Scene: The night itself, warm and dense, shot completely differently from the morning.
- Shot: A hard cut to night: warm tungsten, string lights, bodies, motion blur, a handheld camera inside the crowd.
- Cut to: A confetti cannon fired straight up into a spinning ceiling fan; the fan flinging confetti across the whole room in a slow-motion wide.
- Emotion: Peak party joy.
- Lighting: Warm, orange, over-lit in the middle and dark at the edges — the exact opposite of the morning grade.

```
She was leaving in the morning for a city ten hours north,
So we made the whole night bigger than the rest of it was worth.
Then we stood there in the kitchen with the big light switched off,
And nobody said goodbye out loud, we just held on a bit too long.
```

- Scene: The reason for the party.
- Shot: A suitcase standing by the front door, half in shadow, party still going behind it out of focus.
- Cut to: The kitchen with the big light off, six people in a circle holding on to each other, nobody talking, shot from the doorway and held longer than is comfortable.
- Emotion: The turn of the whole song. Play it completely straight.
- Lighting: Only the light from the hallway; faces mostly in shadow.

### [pre-chorus 2 — laughter and tears in the same phrase]

```
Kettle's on again, still nobody's talking,
Six of us, and one of us is packed.
Somebody laughs and then everybody's laughing,
Then we're crying, then we're laughing, and that's that.
```

- Scene: Back to the morning kitchen, but now we know.
- Shot: The same pan along six faces as the first pre-chorus, matched exactly, except one of them has a coat on and a suitcase at her feet.
- Cut to: The laugh, then a face crumpling, then the laugh again, all on one person, one continuous take.
- Emotion: Both at once. Do not choose.
- Lighting: Identical to pre-chorus one, deliberately.

### [chorus 2 — brass counter-line, higher stack]

```
(the chorus again, bigger)
```

- Scene: The flat dancing again, but the suitcase is in frame now.
- Shot: The same tracking move as chorus one, re-blocked so the suitcase is in the background of every part of it.
- Emotion: Joy with the clock running.
- Lighting: The sun has moved; the room is warmer than it was.

### [instrumental — brass feature, drum breakdown]

- Scene: The clean-up that does not happen.
- Shots: A broom leaning untouched against a wall. A dustpan with three pieces of confetti in it, abandoned. Two of them lying on the floor looking up at the ceiling fan, still turning. Someone asleep in the bath fully clothed with a cushion. A slow push-in on the suitcase by the door.
- Lighting: Late morning, softer, the hard edge gone off the light.

### [bridge — electric piano and voice, no drums]

```
It was a year that took a lot out of all of us,
A bad spring and a worse July.
Last night was the first night in a long time
That nobody in this room had to try.
So I'm not sweeping yet and I'm not washing out my hair,
I'll keep the whole night on me for as long as it will stay.
```

- Scene: The honest moment.
- Shot: Mahima alone in the bathroom doorway, looking at herself in the mirror, confetti still in her hair, not moving.
- Emotion: Gratitude with an edge of grief. The line people screenshot.
- Lighting: Cool bathroom light against the warm flat behind her.

### [final chorus — gang vocal takes over]

```
(the chorus, with "And her ticket folded up where her jacket used to hang")
```

- Scene: The send-off.
- Shot: All six carrying the suitcase down the stairwell together, badly, laughing, the camera going down ahead of them.
- Cut to: The street: a car door, a hug that lasts a beat too long, confetti coming off somebody's collar onto the pavement.
- Emotion: The biggest and the saddest at once.
- Lighting: Full daylight outside for the first time in the video.

### [post-chorus 2 — chant]

```
(the chant again, all of them)
```

- Scene: The five of them walking back up.
- Shot: Five people on a stairwell, chanting, one flight at a time, handheld from below.
- Lighting: Stairwell window light, hard and bright.

### [outro — electric piano and tambourine]

```
Sweeping up on Sunday and I found one in my shoe,
Little gold circle from a night I'm not gonna lose.
Put it in a jar on the shelf beside the door,
Woke up with confetti in my hair, and I don't even care.
```

- Scene: Sunday, the flat clean.
- Shot: A broom finally moving; a shoe turned upside down and one gold disc falling out into her palm.
- Final shot: The clean, quiet, empty living room in evening light with the jar on the shelf, locked-off, held through the fade.
- Emotion: Peace. The night is kept, not mourned.
- Lighting: Soft evening, warm, low.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** her at the window shaking confetti out of her hair in slow motion, backlit, on *"and I don't even care."*
- **The challenge:** the six-faces pan — a line of friends squinting over mugs, nobody talking, then all laughing at once. Recreate it with your own group the morning after.
- **The turn:** the kitchen with the big light off, six people holding on, on *"nobody said goodbye out loud."*
- **The caption line:** *"Let the floor keep the evidence, let the kitchen keep the proof."*
- **The last frame:** a single piece of confetti dropped into a jar by the door.
