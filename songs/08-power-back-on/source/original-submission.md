# Power Back On — original submission

The concept, sound direction, lyrics and per-section video direction exactly
as submitted. Kept as source material.

A cinematic story about two people rebuilding an abandoned night-time city
power station into a place of energy — symbolising how we can restart our
own inner power. Designed for gym sessions, running, lifting, confidence
edits, comeback videos, and "main-character energy" reels. A 136 BPM main
groove suits strength and higher-intensity training ranges, while the bridge
briefly strips down for recovery before the final push.

## Concept

**Storyline:**
A woman and man enter an old, shut-down power station before dawn. The city above is tired, dim, and disconnected. She carries a broken neon sign; he carries a rusted key. As they train, run, and move through the building, every effort restores the lights: one lamp, one floor, one entire city block at a time.

By the final chorus, the building glows, the doors open, and they run into sunrise—not because somebody saved them, but because they restarted themselves.

**Core message:**
You do not need to wait for motivation to arrive. Move first. Your energy comes back when you do.

**Viral hook:**
> "Power back on, power back on— / I was down, but I was never gone."

## Sound direction

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| Genre | Cinematic trap-pop, motivational hip-hop, stadium-rock chorus |
| Tempo | 136 BPM, 4/4 |
| Key | E minor; final chorus lifts to G major |
| Female vocal | Strong, clear alto-to-belt delivery; intimate verses, explosive hook |
| Male vocal | Gritty rhythmic rap; confident spoken moments; harmonies in choruses |
| Production | 808s, live kick/snare, stadium claps, pulsing synth bass, distorted guitar, brass hits, choir pads, cinematic strings |
| Energy arc | Low battery → first spark → disciplined motion → power surge → calm control → full release |
