# Unfollowed — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
The day she finally unfollows him — and the sky does not fall. No confrontation, no paragraph, no final look. One button on a Tuesday afternoon, and then a completely ordinary week that turns out to be the best one she has had in a year.

**Mood:**
Dry, adult, quietly funny in places. Not a revenge song and not a sad song: the emotional event is the absence of an emotional event.

**Message:**
"I didn't make a scene. I took my name off a room I'd been cleaning, and the day just carried on."

**Genre:**
Contemporary pop with a piano spine, snaps and trap hi-hats. Think: Sasha Alex Sloan x SZA x Gracie Abrams — conversational writing, restrained production, one half-rapped bridge that changes the temperature.

**Tempo & Key:**
100 BPM, 4/4, A minor. No modulation. The lift in the final chorus comes from strings, a choir pad and the widest harmonies of the record rather than a key change — the song's whole argument is that nothing dramatic happens.

**Vocal setup:**
Lead: young adult female, low, dry and nearly muttered in the verses, open but never shouted in the choruses. The bridge is the same singer half-rapped, tight and conversational over hi-hats, lifting back into melody for its last two lines. Backing: stacked harmonies from the first chorus, widest on the last.

**Instrumentation:**
- Close-mic upright piano (the spine)
- Warm round sub-bass
- Finger snaps on the backbeat, and tight trap hi-hats that double in speed late in verse two
- Plucked synth and brushed percussion on verse two; low choir pad from chorus two
- Strings on the final chorus and post-chorus only
- Ambient: kitchen room tone, a kettle click, distant traffic, one small interface sound

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: an ordinary Tuesday, a thumb above a name; verse one, the months of rehearsing a two-second act
- Pre-chorus: no speech, no paragraph, no ceremony; chorus, the world stubbornly continuing
- Verse two: a park, a dress, a haircut, a name said at dinner
- Bridge: half-rapped — the museum she was running, and why she closed it
- Final chorus: the same words, warmer, and the quiet reads as clear rather than empty
- Outro: cold coffee, a new fringe in the glass, the truth

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro — piano only, one small click]

```
Tuesday, nothing special, coffee going cold,
My thumb above your name in the usual place,
One small button and one small sound,
And nothing at all happened to my face.
```

- Scene: A small kitchen, mid-afternoon, weekday light. No styling, no candles, nothing cinematic.
- Shot: A mug on a table with a skin forming on the coffee; her thumb hovering over a lit blank phone screen (all UI composited).
- Cut to: A locked-off close-up of her face for the length of the button press. She does not cry, flinch or smile. The shot holds two beats past comfortable.
- Emotion: The deliberate anticlimax the whole song is built on.
- Lighting: Flat, honest daylight through a kitchen window. No fill.

### [verse 1 — snaps and hats enter]

```
I rehearsed it for a season like a speech,
Told my sister I would do it in the spring.
Then I'd sit up past midnight with my thumb above the glass,
And put the phone back down and not do a thing.
You were never even cruel, that was the worst of it,
You just got quieter until the quiet was the norm.
I kept your tab open like a window in a storm,
Let the cold come in and I called that keeping warm.
```

- Scene: The months before, told in repeated failures.
- Shot: The same phone-in-hand framing at night, four times, with different pyjamas and different weather in the window each time.
- Cut to: Her sister on a video call mid-sentence, unimpressed and kind; then a real window left open in a rainstorm, the curtain soaking, nobody closing it.
- Emotion: Embarrassment at how long a two-second act took.
- Lighting: Cold blue night, and one warm lamp she keeps not turning off.

### [pre-chorus 1 — hats drop out]

```
No speech today, no closing line,
No paragraph I wrote and never sent,
Just a thumb, a quiet little sound,
And a whole year finally spent.
```

- Scene: The unsent drafts.
- Shot: A notes app full of long unsent paragraphs, scrolling (composited); her selecting all and doing nothing about it.
- Cut to: The kitchen again, the same mug, the same light, her hand flat on the table.
- Emotion: Refusing the ceremony.
- Lighting: Unchanged from the intro on purpose.

### [chorus 1 — the world continues]

```
Unfollowed, unbothered, un-yours,
And the sky did not fall down on me,
The kettle clicked, the bus came on time,
The dog next door barked at the tree.
I thought that it would feel like losing,
It feels like setting a heavy bag down.
Unfollowed, unbothered, un-yours,
And nothing in the world made a sound.
```

- Scene: Proof of an ordinary world, shot like evidence.
- Shot: A kettle switch popping up; a bus arriving at a stop with its indicator board (composited) on time; a neighbour's dog barking at a tree with nothing in it.
- Cut to: Her stepping out of her front door and putting a heavy tote down on the step for a second, then picking it up lighter.
- Emotion: Relief mistaken for anticlimax, then recognised as relief.
- Lighting: Bright, unglamorous afternoon. The frames are wider than the verses.

### [verse 2 — the ordinary week]

```
I took my coffee to the park at half past one,
Watched a kid start a war with a pigeon and win.
Sat there till the light went orange on the water,
And nobody came to ask me where I'd been.
Bought a dress in a colour you would call a lot,
Cut a fringe I am going to have to live with for a while.
Somebody said your name at dinner on the Friday,
I passed the bread and answered with a stranger's smile.
```

- Scene: A week of small, unremarkable, entirely hers.
- Shot: A park bench, a takeaway cup, a toddler charging a pigeon and the pigeon losing; the light going orange across a boating lake.
- Cut to: A changing-room mirror and a very loud dress; a hairdresser's chair, a fringe being cut, her seeing it and laughing at herself once.
- Emotion: Low-grade, real happiness. Nothing performed.
- Lighting: Warm afternoon in the park, tungsten in the salon, candlelit at the dinner table.

### [pre-chorus 2 — resolved]

```
No paragraph, no closing line,
No last look at a face I know by heart,
Just the same small sound in a different week,
And a photograph with your half torn apart.
```

- Scene: The dinner table and, later, a shoebox.
- Shot: Her at dinner mid-conversation, a name landing, her face doing almost nothing.
- Cut to: A printed photo of the two of them, her folding it in half so only she is showing, and standing it on a shelf that way.
- Emotion: Not erasure. Editing.
- Lighting: Warm, low, domestic.

### [chorus 2]

```
Unfollowed, unbothered, un-yours,
And the sky did not fall down on me,
The kettle clicked, the bus came on time,
The dog next door barked at the tree.
I thought that it would feel like losing,
It feels like setting a heavy bag down.
Unfollowed, unbothered, un-yours,
And nothing in the world made a sound.
```

- Scene: The same evidence shots, now with her inside them instead of watching them.
- Shot: Her making the tea rather than filming the kettle; her on the bus by the window; her actually greeting the dog.
- Cut to: The new dress on a hanger on a door frame, catching light.
- Emotion: Occupancy. She lives here now.
- Lighting: The same afternoon, one stop brighter.

### [instrumental — piano, then snaps, then a bar of silence]

- Scene: The flat, quietly reorganised.
- Shot: A slow pan across a shelf where the folded photograph now sits between other things, not hidden and not featured.
- Cut to: Her moving a chair, opening a window on purpose this time, and closing it again; a hairdryer, a laugh, a phone face-up and ignored.
- Final beat: One full bar of silence on a static shot of the empty kitchen, no people in frame.
- Lighting: Late golden hour crossing the floor.

### [bridge — half-rapped, dry and close]

```
Let me be honest, I was running a museum,
One exhibit, one man, and the only guide was me.
Every post became a plaque, every photo a display,
And I gave myself the tour at one and two and three.
I called it keeping up, I called it being kind,
It was homework on a person who had already resigned.
And the truth is not dramatic, it is boring and it's plain,
You don't get a lightning bolt, you get a Tuesday and some rain.
So I didn't write a paragraph, I didn't make a scene,
I just took my name off a room I'd been cleaning.
Not a punishment, no flex, not a quiet little war,
Just a door I had been holding, and I'm not holding it anymore.
```

- Scene: The museum, literalised — and this is the only stylised sequence in the video.
- Shot: An empty white gallery at night, one exhibit on the wall lit by a single picture light, her walking the room in a coat.
- Cut to: Her turning the picture light off; a heavy gallery door she has been holding open for someone, which she lets swing closed; then straight back to the real kitchen, no transition.
- Emotion: Wit, self-awareness, no bitterness. Delivered to camera on the last two lines.
- Lighting: Gallery: hard pools of light in black. Kitchen: the same flat daylight as the intro.

### [final chorus — strings, choir pad, widest harmonies]

```
Unfollowed, unbothered, un-yours,
And the sky did not fall down on me,
The kettle clicked, the bus came on time,
The dog next door barked at the tree.
I thought that it would feel like losing,
It feels like the first clean morning in a year.
Unfollowed, unbothered, un-yours,
And the quiet is not empty, it is clear.
```

- Scene: A genuinely good morning, the first in the video shot at dawn.
- Shot: Windows open, curtains moving, the flat full of air; her in the loud dress with the new fringe, barefoot.
- Cut to: Her walking a street she has been avoiding, past whatever used to be a landmark, without slowing down.
- Emotion: Clear, not triumphant. Never a fist in the air.
- Lighting: Early, blue-to-gold, the brightest sequence in the piece.

### [post-chorus — snaps and hats only]

```
Unfollowed in the morning, unbothered by the night,
Un-yours by the time the streetlights came on,
Unfollowed, unbothered, un-yours,
And the day just carried on.
```

- Scene: One day compressed into four bars.
- Shot: A single locked-off frame of her kitchen window from morning to streetlights, time-lapsed, with her passing through it four times.
- Cut to: The streetlights coming on outside, filmed from the same chair.
- Emotion: Ordinary time, which is the win.
- Lighting: The whole day, in one frame.

### [outro — piano alone]

```
Coffee going cold on a table by the window,
A fringe I'm still getting used to in the glass,
Somebody will ask me and I'll tell them it went fine,
And it will be the truth, at last.
```

- Scene: Back where it started, on purpose.
- Shot: The identical framing as the intro — same mug, same table, same light — but she is in the loud dress and the fringe, and the phone is not in the shot.
- Cut to: Her reflection in the dark window glass, touching the fringe, mildly amused by it.
- Final frame: The mug, the table, the empty chair opposite. Held until the piano stops. No text.
- Emotion: Settled. Not healed, settled.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the locked-off face during the button press, where absolutely nothing happens, under *"Unfollowed, unbothered, un-yours."*
- **The caption line:** *"I thought that it would feel like losing. It feels like setting a heavy bag down."*
- **The challenge:** the anticlimax cut — film your face for the two seconds after you do the small thing you have been putting off for a year, unedited.
- **The quote card:** *"I just took my name off a room I'd been cleaning."*
- **The comment bait:** the folded photograph on the shelf, and the dog barking at an empty tree.
