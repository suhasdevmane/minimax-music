# Vows I Didn't Rehearse — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
A woman writes her vows over four months, drafts them, rehearses them to a mirror, folds the paper into her sleeve — and then stands up at a small back-garden wedding and says something else entirely. The song is the something else. It is not about forgetting the words; it is about the words being somebody else's, and the true ones arriving without permission.

**Mood:**
Warm, funny, exposed. A room laughing and crying in the same ten seconds. No swelling until it has earned it.

**Message:**
"Every word I wrote down was somebody else's first."

**Genre:**
Acoustic ballad / modern folk-pop. Think: Noah Kahan's plain-spoken detail x Phoebe Bridgers' room-sound intimacy, with the chorus lift of a Brandi Carlile record.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 84 BPM, 4/4. Key: G major, guitar-natural, the whole record playable by one person
- Final chorus lifts a whole step to A with strings, a small choir and a high capoed guitar figure

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead: female, almost speaking in the verses, unforced and sincere in the choruses, entirely bare in the bridge.
- Backing: four or five warm voices join the last chorus and the post-chorus, sounding like guests rather than session singers.

**Instrumentation:**
- Fingerpicked steel-string acoustic guitar (the whole foundation, first note to last)
- Piano (from the first pre-chorus, harmonic centre in the choruses)
- Small string section: two violins, viola, cello; solo cello in the break
- Upright double bass, low and soft
- Brushed snare and light kick, never louder than the guitar
- High capoed guitar figure (final chorus)
- Ambience: garden room tone, chairs, birdsong, a distant dog, a seated crowd — never faded out

**Mood arc:**
- Intro and verse 1: four months of drafts, then the paper failing in her hands
- Pre-chorus: a dog loose, a chair blown over, a whole garden waiting
- Chorus: the vows themselves, small and specific and unquotable
- Verse 2: what she actually says, including the thing she refuses to repeat
- Bridge: the perfect version of today that would have been a lie
- Final chorus and outro: the lift, the paper in the grass, the drawer it lives in now

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – one guitar, room sound, almost spoken]

```
I wrote it out in April, I rewrote it in June,
Read it to the mirror till the mirror got bored.
Folded it in fours and I slid it in my sleeve,
And I never looked at it again.
```

- Scene: A bedroom over four months, compressed.
- Shot: A kitchen table with index cards, a pen, a laptop pushed aside; the same table with more cards; the same table with almost none.
- Cut to: Her at a mirror in an ordinary room, mouthing the words with no sound, then stopping mid-sentence and looking at herself; then a single sheet folded in half, in half again, and slid inside a sleeve.
- Her reaction / Emotion: Diligent, then quietly defeated by her own writing. She knows before the wedding that it is not right.
- Lighting: Spring window light for the drafts, a warm bedside lamp for the mirror, morning white for the folding.

### [verse 1]

```
Here's what the paper said, it said that you are kind,
Which is true and useless, everyone's kind on paper.
It said something about oceans, it said something about time,
It was good, it was fine, it would have made them cry.
But my hands went stupid and the paper went soft,
And I looked up and the whole speech left the yard.
```

- Scene: The ceremony, standing. The paper is a character and this is its whole scene.
- Shots: The folded sheet coming out of her sleeve; a macro of her own handwriting on it (composited); her thumb working a corner until the fibres go furry.
- Cut to: The paper visibly softening and creasing in her fist; a wide of thirty people on folding chairs on grass waiting; her eyes coming up off the page.
- Her reaction / Emotion: The exact second a plan stops working. Not panic — recognition.
- Lighting: Late-afternoon garden light, low and long, the paper the brightest object in every frame it is in.

### [pre-chorus]

```
So the wind took a folding chair, the dog got loose,
And your mother has a tissue she has not used yet.
Everybody's waiting on the girl with the paper,
And the girl with the paper's got nothing left.
```

- Scene: The garden refusing to be solemn.
- Shots: A white folding chair going over in the wind at the back of the rows and nobody getting up to fix it; a small dog trailing a lead across the grass between the chairs.
- Cut to: An older woman in the front row holding a folded tissue in both hands, not using it yet; then the whole seated crowd, silent, patient, looking at her.
- Her reaction / Emotion: Aware of every one of them, and out of material.
- Lighting: Same warm garden light, the dog and the chair catching sun, the crowd slightly in shade.

### [chorus – piano, cello, double-tracked voice]

```
These are the vows I didn't rehearse,
The ones that came sideways, the ones that came first.
You do the dishes when I'm three days low,
You say my name like it's somewhere to go.
I don't have a metaphor, I don't have a line,
I've got a man in a rented suit and the rest of my life.
Every word I wrote down was somebody else's first,
So these are the vows I didn't rehearse.
```

- Scene: The vows, illustrated with the smallest possible evidence.
- Shots: A kitchen sink at night, his hands in it, her sitting on the counter behind him not talking; a hallway, him saying her name from another room and her head lifting.
- Cut to: Back to the garden, her not reading, hands empty at her sides; a close-up of a rented suit's sleeve label still faintly visible at the cuff.
- Emotion: Steady. The first time in the video her voice does not shake.
- Lighting: The kitchen flashback is one warm overhead in a dark room; the garden is unchanged.

### [verse 2]

```
So I told them about the Tuesday the car gave up,
How you walked two miles back with a coffee going cold.
I told them what you said in the hospital hallway,
Which I'm not repeating, that one's ours to keep.
I told them that you sing badly and you sing anyway,
I said I've watched you choose me on days I was hard to choose,
And that's the only promise I know how to make.
```

- Scene: What she actually says, played as flashbacks that get more private as they go.
- Shots: A car on a shoulder with the hood up and a small figure walking a long straight road toward it carrying two cups; a hospital corridor, shot from floor level, two pairs of shoes side by side on plastic chairs and nothing else — no faces, no dialogue.
- Cut to: A car interior, him singing badly and confidently at a red light; then the garden, and the crowd's faces changing as she goes.
- Her reaction / Emotion: The hospital shot is the only one she does not narrate. The camera respects that and leaves before anything happens.
- Lighting: Flat road daylight; cold hospital fluorescent; warm windscreen sun; the garden getting lower and more golden each time we return to it.

### [pre-chorus 2]

```
The man with the script has let it fall to his side,
And the folding chairs have stopped their little noise.
Somebody's crying in the back who isn't even family,
And you're laughing at me with your whole face.
```

- Scene: The room stops moving.
- Shots: The officiant, an ordinary friend in a jacket, letting a printed script drop to his side and just listening; thirty people completely still on chairs that were creaking a minute ago.
- Cut to: A stranger at the back, a plus-one, crying openly and slightly embarrassed about it; then Kai, laughing with his whole face and not wiping his eyes.
- Her reaction / Emotion: She sees him laughing and it steadies her rather than derailing her.
- Lighting: The lowest, warmest garden light of the film — the golden fifteen minutes, used here and nowhere else.

### [chorus 2]

```
These are the vows I didn't rehearse,
The ones that came sideways, the ones that came first.
You do the dishes when I'm three days low,
You say my name like it's somewhere to go.
I don't have a metaphor, I don't have a line,
I've got a man in a rented suit and the rest of my life.
Every word I wrote down was somebody else's first,
So these are the vows I didn't rehearse.
```

- Scene: The same chorus, now with the whole garden inside it.
- Shots: A slow arc around the two of them with the seated crowd wheeling past behind; her hands, empty, open, moving while she talks.
- Cut to: The paper, dropped and forgotten, lying in the grass beside her shoe.
- Emotion: Released. The song's centre of gravity moves from the paper to her hands.
- Lighting: Golden, flaring at the edges of the arc.

### [instrumental]

- Scene: Sixteen bars, solo cello and guitar, and the garden simply existing.
- Shots: The jar of wildflowers on a card table; the loose dog lying down at the end of a row; the blown-over chair still on its side; the tissue, finally used.
- Cut to: The paper in the grass with an ant walking across it; a wide of the whole garden from the back fence with two small figures at the front.
- Emotion: Held breath, and a lot of affection for a very ordinary yard.
- Lighting: The sun dropping visibly across the four shots, ambience rising almost over the cello.

### [bridge – guitar and voice alone]

```
There's a version of today with the paper read out perfect,
Clean and quotable, a good line for a frame.
And it would have been beautiful and it would have been a lie,
Because nothing about us has ever come out clean.
So take the mess, take the sentence that fell over,
Take the girl who lost her place and found it out loud.
```

- Scene: The alternative version, shown once and dismissed.
- Shots: A two-second insert of the same wedding shot like a commercial — the paper read smoothly, everyone posed, the light too perfect, colour slightly cooler; then a hard cut back to the real one, warmer and messier.
- Cut to: Her mid-sentence, having genuinely lost her thread, stopping, starting again badly, and carrying on.
- Her reaction / Emotion: The most exposed passage in the film. No music underneath and nothing to hide behind.
- Lighting: The false version is even and cold; the real version is one-sided golden with half her face in shadow.

### [final chorus – modulated, choir, strings]

```
These are the vows I didn't rehearse,
The ones that came sideways, the ones that came first.
I will not be easy and I will not be far,
I'll be the light left on when you pull in the yard.
I don't have a metaphor, I don't have a line,
I've got a man in a rented suit and the rest of my life.
For better, for worse, for the paper in the dirt,
These are the vows I didn't rehearse.
```

- Scene: The end of the ceremony and the start of everything after it.
- Shots: A porch light on at dusk seen from a car pulling into a yard, headlights swinging across it; back to the garden, the two of them holding both hands.
- Cut to: The crowd standing, chairs scraping, the dog running through the middle of it; the paper still in the grass with feet stepping around it.
- Emotion: Full, communal, unpolished. The only shot in the film with everyone moving at once.
- Lighting: Last of the gold going blue at the edges, the first practicals coming on in the house behind.

### [post-chorus – guests singing along]

```
Not a word of it written, not a word of it wrong,
I found it in my chest where it had been all along.
Not a word of it written, not a word of it wrong,
And the paper's in the grass and I'm still going on.
```

- Scene: The reception starting itself in the same garden.
- Shots: Folding chairs being pushed back into a rough circle; a hand pressing a palm flat against a sternum on the second line, held.
- Cut to: The paper being trodden into the grass by somebody dancing, unnoticed.
- Emotion: Communal, easy, slightly out of tune, which is correct.
- Lighting: Blue hour, house lights and one string of bulbs over the table.

### [outro – one guitar and the room]

```
They found it in the grass by the gate,
Three drafts and an arrow and a line crossed out twice.
It lives in a drawer and I've never read it since,
I already said the true one out loud.
```

- Scene: After, and much later.
- Shots: A hand picking a damp, grass-stained sheet up by the gate at night; a macro of the page — drafts, an arrow, a line crossed out twice (all composited).
- Cut to: A years-later kitchen drawer opening, the same page flat under other papers, and closing again; the final shot is her at a sink in that later kitchen, not looking at the drawer, a porch light on behind her, locked-off, held through the fade.
- Emotion: Settled. The paper is kept and never needed.
- Lighting: Gate at night lit by one house window; the later kitchen warm and ordinary.

## Viral moments

- **The hook frame:** the paper going soft in her fist, cut to *"these are the vows I didn't rehearse."*
- **The caption line:** *"Every word I wrote down was somebody else's first."*
- **The challenge:** the unread-vows challenge — invite people to post the moment their own speech went off-script, wedding footage, best-man speeches, eulogies, cut to the final chorus.
- **The quote card:** *"I've got a man in a rented suit and the rest of my life."*
- **The shareable ten seconds:** the officiant letting the script fall to his side and simply listening.
