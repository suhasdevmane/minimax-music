# Golden Hour on Your Skin — original submission

The song, direction and scene-by-scene video notes as submitted, before
cleanup for the lyric body. Kept as source material.

**Theme:**
The twenty minutes before sunset when everything about him looks like a photograph she wants to keep. Not a big moment, not a proposal, not a fight — just a rooftop, a field, a car bonnet, and a boy lit from the side. She keeps falling for him at the same time every day, and the song is her admitting the light is only half of it.

**Mood:**
Warm, weightless, quietly amazed. Late-summer-evening energy. In love and slightly worried it's running out, then not worried at all.

**Message:**
"It was never really the sun. Golden hour is just the hour I remember to look at you."

**Genre:**
Dreamy pop / sun-drenched bedroom pop. Think: Clairo x Kali Uchis x early Lorde, with a harp where a guitar would usually go. Lens-flare music.

**Tempo & Key:**
- 96 BPM, 4/4
- Key: E major (bright, open, easy on the ear)
- No modulation; the final chorus lifts through arrangement, harmonies and a soaring synth lead rather than a key change.

**Vocal setup:**
- Lead vocal: young adult female, soft and close in the verses, half-laughing, opening into a warm easy belt on the chorus.
- Backing vocals: stacked harmonies in the choruses, wordless echoes in the instrumental and outro, male and female layers on the last hook.

**Instrumentation:**
- Shimmering, slowly modulating synth pad (the bed of the whole song)
- Soft 808 and a warm round sub-bass
- Plucked synth (verse melody)
- Harp flourishes, glissandi and arpeggios (the signature sound)
- Soft four-on-the-floor kick, warm clap, light shaker, finger snaps
- Soaring synth lead (final chorus)
- Warm cinematic strings (bridge turn and final chorus)
- Textures: room tone, a faint breeze, a soft camera-shutter click

**Mood arc:**
- Intro: twenty minutes to go, the sky turning honey, her watching him
- Verses: rooftop details, then a field and a back road; a summer in small frames
- Pre-chorus: the light is running out; please leave slowly
- Chorus: wide, golden, easy; the photograph being taken
- Bridge: winter imagined, the grey light at four, then the turn: it was never the sun
- Final chorus: fullest, warmest, every line certain
- Outro: the sun goes under, the streetlights come on, she carries it with her

## Lyrics + Video Direction (scene-by-scene)

### [intro – soft, close, over pad and a single harp gliss]

```
Twenty minutes till the sun goes down,
The whole sky turning honey brown.
You're leaning on the car with your eyes half closed,
And I swear the light knows something I don't know.
```

- Scene: A city rooftop car park, late evening, the sun low and huge.
- Shot: Wide on the sky first, honey and orange; then Kai leaning on the bonnet of a dusty old car, eyes half closed, sun on the side of his face.
- Cut to: Mahima a few steps away, watching him, a small smile she isn't hiding.
- Emotion: Unhurried, quietly amazed.
- Lighting: Full golden hour, lens flare, heavy film grain.

### [verse 1]

```
Seven forty on a Thursday, rooftop, no plans,
Warm can of lemonade sweating in your hands.
You're telling me a story that you've told before,
But the way the sun hits your jaw, I want to hear it more.
```

- Scene: The same rooftop, closer.
- Shots: Kai's hands around a sweating can of lemonade; him mid-story, gesturing, laughing at his own joke; the sun on his jawline; Mahima not really listening, just looking.
- Emotion: Fond, half-laughing, completely in love.
- Lighting: Low sun from the side, long shadows across the concrete.

```
Dust in the air like it's floating on purpose,
Every little freckle coming up to the surface.
I've got a hundred pictures that I never take,
Cause a camera never gets you the way I do, babe.
```

- Scene: The rooftop, macro.
- Shots: Dust floating in a shaft of sun; the freckles on his cheek and forearm; Mahima raising her phone to take a picture, then lowering it without taking it; her eyes instead.
- Emotion: A decision to look rather than record.
- Lighting: Backlit dust, warm haze.

### [pre-chorus]

```
And I know it only lasts a little while,
The light, the heat, the way you smile.
But if this is all I ever get to keep,
Then let me stand here till it sinks beneath the street.
```

- Scene: The rooftop edge, the sun touching the skyline.
- Shots: The sun visibly lower between two buildings; Kai's smile; Mahima stepping up beside him at the edge; their shadows stretching all the way across the roof.
- Emotion: Aware it's ending, choosing to stay.
- Lighting: The sun half behind the buildings, the flare at its strongest.

### [chorus – wide, golden, easy]

```
Golden hour on your skin,
And I'm falling all over again.
Every day the sun comes down,
And every day you pull me in.
Golden hour on your skin,
Like the light was made to fit you in.
I've loved you in the dark, I've loved you in the noon,
But nothing hits like you at half past seven in June.
Golden hour on your skin,
And I'm falling all over again.
```

- Scene: Montage of golden hours: the rooftop, a field, a back road, all at the same twenty minutes.
- Shots: Kai's arm in the sun; Mahima falling back onto the car bonnet laughing; him pulling her in by the hand; a quick dark flash of them in a kitchen at night and a flat noon flash of them in a supermarket, both fine, then back to the gold; a hard cut on "half past seven in June" to a slow-motion frame of him turning toward her.
- Emotion: Joy, ease, the feeling of a photograph being taken.
- Lighting: Everything golden, flare across the lens on the hook.

- Emotion: Playful, a whole summer opening up.
- Lighting: Lower and redder than the rooftop, the grass glowing.

- Scene: A lens-flare montage with no lyrics.
- Shots: Slow motion of long grass, dust, hair in a car window, a hand out of the window riding the air, the sun flaring between Kai's fingers held up to it, Mahima's face lit from the side with her eyes closed; then the sun dropping behind the skyline and the frame going briefly blue and quiet before the bridge.

### [bridge – near-spoken, then the turn]

```
And when the winter comes and the sky goes grey at four,
And the light's a cold thing coming through the door,
I'll close my eyes and it's July again,
Rooftop, back road, you and the honey light, and then
```

- Scene: Imagined winter: a grey flat, a window, four in the afternoon.
- Shots: Mahima in a jumper at a window, the light flat and grey; a cold slab of light on the floor through a door; her closing her eyes; a warm flash of the rooftop, then the back road, then the honey light.
- Emotion: Missing something that hasn't gone yet, then remembering it.
- Lighting: Desaturated grey for the winter, the warm flashes cutting in.

```
You'll look at me the way you're looking now,
And I'll fall, I don't know how, but I'll fall somehow.
Cause it was never really the sun, it was you,
Golden hour's just the hour I remember to look at you.
```

- Scene: Back on the rooftop, present, the last light.
- Shots: Kai looking at her, the look held; Mahima's face changing as she understands it; the turn line on her face, not on the sunset; then the real, present, golden Kai.
- Emotion: The realisation, warm and certain.
- Lighting: Warm again, the strings entering.

### [final chorus – fullest, soaring synth lead, all harmonies]

```
Golden hour on your skin,
And I'm falling all over again.
Every day the sun goes down,
And every day I let you in.
Golden hour on your skin,
Every line and every grin.
I've loved you in the dark, I've loved you in the noon,
But nothing hits like you at half past seven in June.
Golden hour on your skin,
And I'm falling all over again.
```

- Scene: Every location in one chorus, the best frames of each.
- Shots: The rooftop, the field, the bonnet, cut on the beat; Kai's grin; the two of them dancing badly on the roof; her finally taking the photo, on purpose, on "every line and every grin"; a wide from behind of both of them at the rooftop edge with the sun going.
- Emotion: Certain, joyful, nothing held back.
- Lighting: The warmest frames in the video.

### [post-chorus]

```
Falling, falling, all over again,
Falling, falling, all over again.
Turn your face into the light, don't move,
Let me keep this one of you.
```

- Scene: The rooftop, the last minute of sun.
- Shots: Mahima directing Kai into the light with her hands, him obeying and laughing; her holding up the phone; the photo taken; the still.
- Emotion: Playful, then the still frame lands like a heartbeat.
- Lighting: The very last gold.

### [outro – blue hour, slow harp]

```
The sun's gone under, the sky's gone blue,
Streetlights blinking on, nothing left to do.
But you're still glowing like the sun forgot to leave,
Guess I carry golden hour with me.
```

- Scene: The rooftop after sunset, blue hour.
- Shots: The sky gone blue, the streetlights below blinking on one by one; the two of them sitting on the bonnet, his arm around her; a close-up of Kai's face still warm in the blue, as if lit from inside; the final shot: Mahima's phone in her lap showing the golden photo, then her looking up at the real him.
- Emotion: Content, quiet, kept.
- Lighting: Blue hour, the phone screen the only gold left in the frame.
